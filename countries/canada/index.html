Fellowships in Canada

941 medical and surgical fellowships in Canada across 25 specialties. Eligibility, visa and deadline details from official pages.

The Fellowship Portal lists 941 fellowships in Canada at 24 institutions. The main training cities are Toronto, Montreal and Vancouver. 72% say they accept international medical graduates and 64% describe a visa route. The most common length is 1 year. Every listing links to the programme's official page, where you should confirm dates and requirements.
