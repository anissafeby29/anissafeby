Fellowships in Ireland

29 medical and surgical fellowships in Ireland across 13 specialties. Eligibility, visa and deadline details from official pages.

The Fellowship Portal lists 29 fellowships in Ireland at 18 institutions. The main training cities are Dublin, Dublin (placements in accredited hospitals across Ireland) and Dublin (posts across Irish training hospitals). 21% say they accept international medical graduates and 28% describe a visa route. The most common length is 12 months. Every listing links to the programme's official page, where you should confirm dates and requirements.
