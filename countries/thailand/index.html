Fellowships in Thailand

106 medical and surgical fellowships in Thailand across 19 specialties. Eligibility, visa and deadline details from official pages.

The Fellowship Portal lists 106 fellowships in Thailand at 16 institutions. The main training cities are Bangkok, Chiang Mai and Khon Kaen. 51% say they accept international medical graduates and 2% describe a visa route. The most common length is 1 year. Every listing links to the programme's official page, where you should confirm dates and requirements.
