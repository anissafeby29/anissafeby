Fellowships in Europe (multi-country)

45 medical and surgical fellowships in Europe (multi-country) across 12 specialties. Eligibility, visa and deadline details from official pages.

The Fellowship Portal lists 45 fellowships in Europe (multi-country) at 41 institutions. The main training cities are Various European host centres, Host centre in an ESC member country other than the applicant's own and Host centre chosen by applicant. 71% say they accept international medical graduates and 4% describe a visa route.
