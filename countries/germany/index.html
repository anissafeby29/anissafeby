Fellowships in Germany

73 medical and surgical fellowships in Germany across 13 specialties. Eligibility, visa and deadline details from official pages.

The Fellowship Portal lists 73 fellowships in Germany at 63 institutions. Every listing links to the programme's official page, where you should confirm dates and requirements.
