Privacy and optional analytics

Updated 29 October 2026.

This public professional profile works without Google Analytics. Analytics remains off until you choose Allow analytics in the preferences on the profile page.

If allowed, Google Analytics uses cookies and browser identifiers to measure visits. The integration sends a generic page title, the site origin, and the origin of an external referring website, such as https://www.google.com/. Referring paths, search strings, URL fragments, contact details and form contents are excluded. Google advertising storage, ad user data, ad personalisation and Google signals remain disabled.

Your granted or declined choice is stored on this device for up to 180 days. You can withdraw at any time using Privacy and analytics preferences on the profile page. Withdrawal disables collection, clears accessible Google Analytics cookies and reloads the page to stop the loaded tag. Declining does not limit access to the profile.

Google Analytics may process technical device and network information when you allow it. This analytics choice does not authorise sending health information or contact requests. Google Privacy Policy.
