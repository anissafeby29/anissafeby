Overview

A one-year emergency radiology fellowship with a dedicated emergency radiology group, time at Cincinnati Children's and an internationally recognised stroke programme.

Institution policyUCMC GME contract year runs 1 July - 30 June (2026-27 compensation year 1 July 2026 - 30 June 2027)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of an ACGME-accredited DR residency in the US or abroad, and ABR board certification/eligibility at the start of training.
International applicants
ECFMG certificate requested if applicable; ABR eligibility required.
Visa support
Institution policyUC Health requires incoming trainees entering the US for the first time to be J-1 (ECFMG) eligible; no new H-1B sponsorship since October 2025, but H-1B transfers from other institutions accepted
Licence requirement
Institution policyOhio permanent medical licence or State Medical Board of Ohio training certificate required; USMLE Step 3 required for entry at PGY-4 or above

How to apply

Deadline
Not stated on official page
Application method
