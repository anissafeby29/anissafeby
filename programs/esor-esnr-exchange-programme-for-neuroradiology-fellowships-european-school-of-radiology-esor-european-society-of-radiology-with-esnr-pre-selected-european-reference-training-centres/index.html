ESOR/ESNR Exchange Programme for Neuroradiology Fellowships

European School of Radiology (ESOR) / European Society of Radiology with ESNR — pre-selected European reference training centres

Overview

Three-month ESOR exchange programme that complements subspecialty training in neuroradiology with mentored modular training at a pre-selected European reference centre.

The programme

Training type
Exchange / short-term subspecialty fellowship
Duration
3 months (intense modular training)
Start
Second half of the year, arranged with the training centre (2026 cycle)
Positions
Five places
Accreditation
ESR/ESOR programme run with the ESNR; mentored by an assigned ESNR tutor
Department
ESOR Exchange Programmes for Fellowships

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Radiologists within the first three–four years after board certification (certification no more than 4 years old) who wish to subspecialise; fluent English; active ESR and ESNR memberships; previous selectees not eligible; one programme/topic only
International applicants
Open to ESR members (ESR membership is available to radiologists worldwide); candidates may only apply to training centres outside their own country of residence/work
Visa support
Selected candidates must apply for their own work permits and insurance; training centres will provide assistance (long visa waiting times should be considered)
Licence requirement
Not stated on official page

How to apply

Deadline
Application deadline: March 23, 2026 (2026 cycle); next cycle not yet announced
Application method
MyESR portal → 'Exchange Programmes for Fellowships': choose subspecialty and first/second/third choice training centre; upload one PDF (motivation letter, ESOR-format CV, board certification, two signed letters of recommendation)

Funding

Funding
Grant jointly provided by ESR/ESOR and ESNR; training offered free; fellow covers own expenses and health insurance
