Overview

AO-funded short-term craniomaxillofacial surgery fellowship placement at Erasmus University Medical Centre (Rotterdam), a department performing about 750 surgical cases a year.

Focus

Placement year after selection

Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Graduate of a training programme in a CMF specialty; completed an AO CMF MFT or other hands-on AO CMF course; at least two years' AO membership; under 40 in year of application (exceptions possible); AO Access 'Breaking Biases' pathway completed
International applicants
Yes – AO global programme; fellows from any country are placed by AO regional fellowship coordinators (membership and course prerequisites apply)
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Scrub-in: Yes; additional licence/requirement to scrub in: medical and/or dental school diploma required. Dominant OR language: Dutch (team English level (3)/5). Documents required: CV, passport copy, vaccination status, at beginning: test for MRSA

How to apply

Deadline
2027 call closed; 2028 call opens spring 2027
Application method
Online application to the AO Foundation during the open call (host centres allocated by AO); not directly to the hospital

Funding

Funding
AO CMF fellowship stipend (75–85 fellowships supported yearly)
