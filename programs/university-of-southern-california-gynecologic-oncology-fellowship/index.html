Overview

University of Southern California's Los Angeles programme appears in the Society of Gynecologic Oncology's national participating-program list for advanced multidisciplinary training in gynaecologic cancer care.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years (36 months: 1 year research, 2 years clinical)
Start
Not stated on official page

Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Fully accredited by the ACGME
Department
Division of Gynecologic Oncology, Department of Obstetrics and Gynecology, Keck School of Medicine of USC / Los Angeles General Medical Center

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed ACGME-accredited OBGYN residency; must meet Los Angeles General Medical Center GME eligibility; CREOG and USMLE scores required
International applicants
Not stated on official page
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 (ECFMG) processed through the Keck/LAC+USC Training Program Liaison; Keck/USC and LAC+USC do not sponsor H-1B visas; O-1 and TN not accepted for trainees
Licence requirement
California medical licence required by the start of fellowship
