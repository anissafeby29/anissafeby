Overview

A clinical academic training program preparing candidates for a career focused on leukemia and myeloid neoplasms, with inpatient, outpatient, transplantation and research exposure.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1-2 years
Start
July 1 (flexible)
Positions
1-2 annually
Accreditation
Institution policyUniversity of Ottawa clinical fellowship - does not lead to any Canadian (RCPSC/CFPC) certification
Department
Department of Medicine, Division of Hematology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Preference for prior Internal Medicine and Hematology training in a Canadian, British, Australian, New Zealand or US program
International applicants
Candidates not meeting University of Ottawa training criteria must complete a PEAP (pre-entry assessment) before starting
Visa support
Institution policyinternational trainees need an IRCC work permit (LMIA-exempt); the uOttawa PGME/immigration advisor provides the documents needed to apply (CPSO letter of eligibility and LMIA-exemption number)
Licence requirement
Institution policyCPSO postgraduate education (educational) certificate of registration required, renewed annually; internationally trained clinical fellows must complete a 4-12 week Pre-Entry Assessment Program (PEAP); three-year licensure limit for clinical fellowships

How to apply

Deadline
Institution policydocuments uploaded online between 1 May and 30 September for a start the following July
Application method
Contact Leslie Goulding; Fellowship Director Dr. Michael Kennah

Funding

Funding
Institution policyinternational fellows without Canadian status must be funded by an official home-country sponsor (government agency or health-related charity; private funds not accepted) unless fellowship funding is available through an Ottawa hospital
