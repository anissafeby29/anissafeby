Vanderbilt University Medical Center

Overview

A two-year CCEP programme covering the full spectrum of catheter ablation including epicardial, left atrial appendage management, laser lead extraction and device implantation.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of a three-year cardiovascular medicine fellowship before starting
International applicants
IMGs apply with ECFMG status report
Visa support
J-1 or H-1B visa applicants asked to send DS-2019 and visa status (page lists both visa types)
Licence requirement
Institution policyHouse staff train under a Tennessee Board of Medical Examiners licence exemption obtained by GME; full Tennessee licence needed for moonlighting and for non-ACGME clinical fellows with a secondary faculty appointment
