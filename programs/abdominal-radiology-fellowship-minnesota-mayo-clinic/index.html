Overview

Subspecialty training in the imaging and interventional aspects of abdominal imaging, with special emphasis on diseases of the gastrointestinal and genitourinary systems.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Board certified or eligible for certification, with completion of a four-year accredited radiology residency program.
International applicants
Foreign medical graduates generally not eligible unless they have completed at least one year of postgraduate clinical training in an approved US or Canadian program (occasional exceptions). Open to US citizens, permanent US immigrants and international applicants; graduates of medical schools outside the US and Canada must hold ECFMG certification (visa sponsorship information via ECFMG).
Visa support
Institution policyMayo Clinic School of Graduate Medical Education supports ECFMG J-1 and H-1B visas (Mayo selects visa type). Since the Sept 2025 $100,000 H-1B fee, Mayo will not file new H-1B petitions that trigger the fee - H-1B only for change/extension of status (USMLE Steps 1-3 required); otherwise ECFMG J-1 (Steps 1-2, ACGME-accredited/recognised programme). F-1 OPT accepted for first year for US-school graduates
Licence requirement
Institution policyMinnesota-based MCSGME trainees must hold a Minnesota residency permit, and all eligible trainees must obtain and maintain a full Minnesota medical licence (USMLE/COMLEX/MCCQE final exam deadlines apply); Mayo reimburses fees

How to apply

Deadline
Interviews January–March each year (SCARD timeline)
Application method
Recruit (third-party application service); no matching program services used. Requires letter from residency program director.
