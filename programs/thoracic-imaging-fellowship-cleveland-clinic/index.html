Thoracic Imaging Fellowship

Cleveland Clinic

RadiologyCardiothoracic imagingClinical Fellowship
Destination
USA
Cleveland, Ohio, United States

Overview

A 12-month fellowship offering comprehensive training in thoracic CT, MR and radiography with integral chest intervention and biopsy rotations; combined cardiothoracic pathways available.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
12 months (1 July – 30 June)
Start
1 July
Positions
2 per year
Accreditation
Non-ACGME/non-standard fellowship
Department
Imaging Institute, Cleveland Clinic (Section of Thoracic & Cardiac Imaging)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed a US diagnostic radiology residency; ABR board certified or eligible before commencing.
International applicants
All applicants must have completed a US diagnostic radiology residency and be ABR board certified or eligible before starting; foreign applicants must also be board eligible in their country of origin, ECFMG certified and have passed USMLE Steps 1, 2 and 3.
Visa support
Institution policyH-1B (requires USMLE Steps 1-3 passed; Step 3 results by 30 April for NRMP programmes) and J-1 (ECFMG); O-1 for fellows case by case; no permanent-residence sponsorship for trainees
Licence requirement
Applicants encouraged to obtain an Ohio medical license and DEA number before starting.
