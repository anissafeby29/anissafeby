Advanced Noninvasive Cardiac Imaging Fellowship

Northwestern University Feinberg School of Medicine (Northwestern Memorial Hospital / McGaw Medical Center)

Overview

A two-year GME-recognised programme: advanced and interventional echocardiography in year one, cardiac CT and MRI in year two.

The programme

Focus
Cardiac imaging (multimodality)
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
2 years (year 1 advanced/interventional echo; year 2 cardiac CT and MRI)
Start
1 July
Positions
2 per year
Accreditation
GME-recognised (non-ACGME)
Department
Division of Cardiology, Bluhm Cardiovascular Institute

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed ACGME CVD fellowship or international equivalent; ABIM certified/eligible; full Illinois licence by 1 July; ECFMG certification and appropriate visa sponsorship
International applicants
Yes – international-equivalent CVD training accepted with ECFMG certification
Visa support
Valid visa required if applicable; visa types per McGaw Medical Center GME policy (not specified on page)
Licence requirement
Full Illinois medical licence before starting
