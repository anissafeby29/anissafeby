Overview

Vanderbilt University Medical Center's Nashville programme is included in the American Urogynecologic Society's 2026 national guide to Urogynecology and Reconstructive Pelvic Surgery fellowships.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years for OBGYN pathways; confirm urology-entry options
Start
Early August 2027 for the published cycle; confirm with programme
Positions
1 position in the published 2026 cycle
Accreditation
ACGME (since 2013); recognised by AUGS
Department
Department of Obstetrics and Gynecology, Vanderbilt University Medical Center

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed eligible Obstetrics and Gynaecology or Urology residency; ERAS, NRMP and institution-specific requirements apply
International applicants
Institutional GME requirement: holders of a foreign medical degree need a valid ECFMG certificate; no programme-specific policy stated
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 via ECFMG; H-1B considered case by case by programme with GME; no new O-1 applications supported
Licence requirement
Institution policyHouse staff train under a Tennessee Board of Medical Examiners licence exemption obtained by GME; full Tennessee licence needed for moonlighting and for non-ACGME clinical fellows with a secondary faculty appointment

How to apply

Deadline
Participated in the 2026 ERAS and NRMP cycle; confirm the next cycle
Application method
ERAS and NRMP Match

Funding

Funding
Institution policyPaid on VUMC stipend scale (effective 1/1/2026): PGY4 $81,933, PGY5 $84,801, PGY6 $87,769, PGY7 $90,841
