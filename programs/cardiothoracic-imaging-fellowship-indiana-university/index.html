Overview

A one-year non-ACGME fellowship with a comprehensive thoracic and cardiac imaging curriculum for paediatric and adult patients and a required research project.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed, or completing, a four-year diagnostic radiology residency within the United States or Canada.
International applicants
Institution policyYes - international graduates need ECFMG certification (or unrestricted US licence or Fifth Pathway) and US work authorisation; J-1 preferred
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 (ECFMG) is the preferred training visa; J-2 with EAD, F-1 (OPT), H-1B or O-1 may be considered under special circumstances by the Program Director with GME approval (extra costs borne by the programme)
Licence requirement
Must be able to obtain licensure in the state of Indiana.
