Overview

A one-year non-ACGME fellowship with customisable modules in IMPT, Gamma Knife/linac SRS, SBRT, LDR/HDR brachytherapy, hyperthermia and GammaPod, with protected research time.

The programme

Training type

1 per year

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of ACGME- or RCPSC-accredited radiation oncology residency; US applicants board-eligible at start
International applicants
Exceptional international applicants who completed a radiation oncology residency may be considered depending on certification/licensing
Visa support
No — candidates must be legally authorised to work in the US without an employer-sponsored visa
Licence requirement
Maryland physician licence and Maryland Controlled and Dangerous Substance licence before start
