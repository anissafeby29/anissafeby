Overview

Joint urogynecology and urology training in complex pelvic reconstruction, female urology, neurourology and pelvic pain, supported by clinical or basic-science research.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
2 years
Start
Annual academic intake
Positions
1 fellow per year, alternating: OBGYN-trained fellow recruited in odd years, urology-trained fellow in even years
Accreditation
ACGME (ABU/ABOG)
Department
Division of Gynecology, Department of Obstetrics & Gynecology (joint with Stanford Urology)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed eligible Obstetrics and Gynaecology or Urology training; accreditation requirements apply
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs welcome to apply; must hold a valid ECFMG certificate and be able to obtain Medical Board of California licensure; non-ACGME fellowships need ACGME Non-Standard Training recognition before J-1 sponsorship
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 (ECFMG) is the primary visa; H-1B permitted only in some circumstances with GME approval; O-1 rarely, varies by department
Licence requirement
Institution policyMedical Board of California Postgraduate Training License required within 180 days of starting an ACGME programme (USMLE Steps 1 & 2 CK passed); non-ACGME programmes require a full California licence by the start date

How to apply

Deadline
Recruitment alternates by year (2025: gynecology fellow, last posted interview date 18 April 2025; 2026: urology fellow). Next cycle not posted.
Application method
Not stated on official page
