Overview

One-year ACGME-accredited palliative medicine fellowship training since 2000 at UAB Hospitals, the Birmingham VA Medical Center and Children's of Alabama.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
July (must be available by 1 August)
Positions
5 per year (4 adult track, 1 pediatric track)
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited
Department
Division of Gerontology, Geriatrics and Palliative Care

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Must be eligible for an Alabama medical licence, Alabama Controlled Substance Certificate and federal DEA registration; all USMLE steps completed at application; no minimum USMLE score
International applicants
IMGs accepted with a valid ECFMG certificate
Visa support
Division can sponsor J-1 visas; H-1B not sponsored unless the fellow is currently within the US
Licence requirement
Must be eligible for Alabama medical licence and Alabama Controlled Substance Certificate

How to apply

Deadline
Applications accepted through ERAS June through May
Application method
ERAS only; participates in ERAS signalling; 3 letters of recommendation

Funding

Funding
Salary and benefits per UAB PGY level
