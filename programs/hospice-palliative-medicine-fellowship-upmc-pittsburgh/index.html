Overview

Prepares physicians for leadership in palliative care clinical work, education and research across inpatient, outpatient, home hospice and long-term care settings.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed ACGME- or AOA-accredited residency in anesthesiology, EM, FM, IM, neurology, OB/GYN, pediatrics, PM&R, psychiatry, radiation oncology or surgery; board-certified/eligible; interest in an academic or scholarly palliative care career.
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs from non-LCME schools must hold ECFMG certification before entry; non-US citizens need employer-sponsored visa (J-1/H-1B per programme)
Visa support
Institution policyUPMC ME programmes may (not required to) offer J-1 via ECFMG and/or H-1B; programme can offer either or both; H-1B requires USMLE Steps 1-3 passed by match date
Licence requirement
Institution policyPennsylvania graduate medical training licence required (within 60 days of contract start)
