Pre-Hospital and Transport Medicine Fellowship

University of Toronto (affiliated teaching hospitals)

Emergency MedicineEmergency medical servicesClinical Fellowship
Destination
CAN
Toronto, Ontario, Canada
Open official programme page ↗

Overview

A one-year fellowship preparing fellows for EMS leadership, covering EMS system design, medical control, dispatch, transport and EMS research under the Director of Air or Land EMS programmes.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
1 July
Positions
1–3 per year
Accreditation
University of Toronto clinical fellowship (non-certifying)
Department
Division of Emergency Medicine, Department of Medicine, University of Toronto

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed RCPSC or CCFP-EM emergency medicine training, or equivalent training in country of origin approved by CPSO; board certified in EM in country of origin before start
International applicants
Yes — equivalent international EM training accepted if CPSO-approved
Visa support
Institution policyinternational clinical fellows need an IRCC work permit; the PGME Office issues an LMIA-exempt offer of employment number and supports the work permit application
Licence requirement
Must meet qualifications for a CPSO educational licence

How to apply

Deadline
1 September
Application method
Institution policyapply to the departmental Fellowship Program Director; sponsored international applicants apply through their sponsoring agency to the PGME Office

Funding

Funding
Institution policyclinical fellows must be funded (department or sponsoring agency); FEAC recommends pay at least equal to PGY1 on the PARO salary scale, and foreign nationals must be paid at a rate commensurate with a Canadian in the same role
