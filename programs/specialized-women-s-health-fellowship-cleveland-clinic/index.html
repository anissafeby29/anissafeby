Overview

A two-year interdisciplinary fellowship designed to create the women's health leadership of the future, based in the Center for Specialized Women's Health.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed an ACGME- or RCPSC-accredited family medicine or internal medicine program; board eligible or board certified
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs eligible with a current valid ECFMG certificate; Cleveland Clinic holds ACGME Non-Standard Training recognition so approved non-accredited fellowships can take ECFMG J-1 trainees
Visa support
Institution policyH-1B (requires USMLE Steps 1-3 passed; Step 3 results by 30 April for NRMP programmes) and J-1 (ECFMG); O-1 for fellows case by case; no permanent-residence sponsorship for trainees
Licence requirement
Able to obtain an Ohio State medical license

How to apply

Deadline
At least 30 days before interview dates or by January 1 for the subsequent academic year
Application method
Application and supporting materials in a single email; 2026 interviews September 25, October 30, November 13
