Fetal Medicine and Prenatal Diagnosis Fellowship Programme

KK Women's and Children's Hospital (KKH, SingHealth)

Overview

Twelve-month programme covering prenatal diagnosis of fetal anomalies, monochorionic twins, fetal growth restriction, invasive procedures and fetal therapy.

The programme

Focus
Maternal-fetal medicine (fetal medicine)
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
12 months
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Two positions per year
Accreditation
Fetal Medicine Fellowship Certification
Department
Department of Maternal Fetal Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
English proficiency (TOEFL paper-based 600); M Med (O&G), MRCOG, MRANZCOG or ACOG Board equivalent; recognised O&G specialist in country of practice with prior advanced ultrasound training; accreditation by MOH, SMC and KKH; currently practising in country of origin
International applicants
Yes - for foreign/local doctors interested in a subspecialty career in fetal medicine
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Temporary Registration for training with the Singapore Medical Council (SMC)
