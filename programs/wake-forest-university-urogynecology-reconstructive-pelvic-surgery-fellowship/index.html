Overview

Wake Forest University's Winston-Salem programme is included in the American Urogynecologic Society's 2026 national guide to Urogynecology and Reconstructive Pelvic Surgery fellowships.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years for Obstetrics and Gynecology physicians (including 12 months research); 2 years for Urology physicians
Start
Early August 2027 for the published cycle; confirm with programme
Positions
1 position in the published 2026 cycle
Accreditation
ACGME
Department
Female Pelvic Health division spanning the Departments of Urology and Obstetrics and Gynecology, Wake Forest University School of Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed eligible Obstetrics and Gynaecology or Urology residency; ERAS, NRMP and institution-specific requirements apply
International applicants
Foreign medical graduates must submit ECFMG certification with the ERAS application; no other policy stated
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Not stated on official page

How to apply

Deadline
2026 cycle: ERAS/NRMP; interviews 1 and 2 April 2026. Next cycle not yet posted.
Application method
ERAS application and NRMP Match registration

Funding

Funding
Competitive salary based on completed residency PGY level; 2026-27 stipends PGY-5 $83,137, PGY-6 $86,264, PGY-7 $89,390; health/dental/vision insurance and CME budget
