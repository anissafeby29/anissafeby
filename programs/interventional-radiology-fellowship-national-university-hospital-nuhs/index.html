Overview

Interventional Radiology fellowship (6 or 12 months, multimodality exposure) in NUH's Department of Diagnostic Imaging (≈150,000 studies/year).

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
6 or 12 months
Start
January or July intake
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
NUH clinical fellowship (SMC requirements)
Department
Department of Diagnostic Imaging

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Postgraduate medical degree from a recognised institution; at least 5 years' experience working in a radiology department; English proficiency (TOEFL/IELTS-type tests if medical education not in English, per SMC)
International applicants
Yes — must be formally sponsored by their government, regional health authority or home institution
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Singapore Medical Council registration (temporary registration for clinical fellowship)

How to apply

Deadline
Apply at least 18 months before preferred attachment date
Application method
Download NUH fellowship application form; submit with CV, photo and supporting documents 18 months before preferred start

Funding

Funding
No financial commitment by the hospital or department; fellows must be sponsored by home government/health authority/institution
