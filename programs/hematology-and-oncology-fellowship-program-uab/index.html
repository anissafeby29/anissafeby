Overview

A comprehensive three-year haematology and oncology fellowship at UAB offering hands-on clinical training across benign and malignant haematology and solid tumours with a multidisciplinary approach.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Eligible for Alabama medical licence and controlled-substance certificate (all USMLE steps completed at time of application); eligible for federal DEA registration; valid ECFMG certificate for IMGs; three letters of recommendation
International applicants
IMGs accepted with a valid ECFMG Standard Certificate (programme page)
Visa support
Division page: sponsors J-1 visas; H-1B only if the fellow is currently within the US. Apply page: 'This program sponsors J-1 and H-1B visas' (programme pages)
Licence requirement
Valid unrestricted Alabama medical licence, Alabama Controlled Substance Certificate and federal DEA certificate before starting

How to apply
