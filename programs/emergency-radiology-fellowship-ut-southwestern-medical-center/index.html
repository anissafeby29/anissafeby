Overview

A one-year fellowship split between Parkland Memorial Hospital (the busiest ER in the US) and Clements University Hospital, with 7 emergency radiology blocks and 6 elective blocks.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Applicants matriculated in an ACGME-accredited DR residency or RCPSC/CFPC-accredited Canadian programme; IMGs who completed a radiology residency are also welcome to apply.
International applicants
International medical graduates who completed a radiology residency are welcome to apply; UTSW only offers J-1 visas.
Visa support
J-1 (ECFMG) only; H-1B not routinely offered.
Licence requirement
Must be eligible for Texas medical licensure.

How to apply

Deadline
Not stated on official page
Application method
Department fellowship application form, three letters of recommendation (one from current Program Director) and original medical school transcript sent by mail or email to the Radiology Education Office.

Funding

Funding
Salary by PGY level (2025: PGY-6 $86,503; PGY-7 $91,451; PGY-8 $95,787) plus benefits.
