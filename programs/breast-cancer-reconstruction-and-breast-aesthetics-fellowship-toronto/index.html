Overview

A one-year programme for graduate plastic surgeons providing high-level clinical experience in all types of breast reconstruction and breast surgery across Women's College Hospital and UHN.

The programme

Focus
Breast reconstruction / aesthetic
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (two 6-month rotations)
Start
2 July
Positions
2
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Division of Plastic, Reconstructive and Aesthetic Surgery

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Fully completed certification from a recognised plastic surgery training programme and credentialed to work as a plastic surgeon
International applicants
One of the two positions is usually reserved for an international trainee
Visa support
University of Toronto PGME Office assists non-Canadian fellows in obtaining a work permit
Licence requirement
Must be licensed by the College of Physicians and Surgeons of Ontario (CPSO), with CMPA coverage

How to apply

Deadline
Applications for 2026–27 are closed
Application method
Institution policyapply to the departmental Fellowship Program Director; sponsored international applicants apply through their sponsoring agency to the PGME Office

Funding

Funding
Institution policyclinical fellows must be funded (department or sponsoring agency); FEAC recommends pay at least equal to PGY1 on the PARO salary scale, and foreign nationals must be paid at a rate commensurate with a Canadian in the same role
