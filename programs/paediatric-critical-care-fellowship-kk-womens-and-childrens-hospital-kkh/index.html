Paediatric Critical Care Fellowship

KK Women's and Children's Hospital (KKH)

PaediatricsCritical careClinical Fellowship
Destination
SGP
Singapore
Open official programme page ↗

Overview

Fellowship in a 16-bed tertiary multidisciplinary CICU with about 1,700 admissions a year, including ECMO, CRRT, HFOV and post-cardiac surgery care.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
12 months, with option to extend another 12 months for advanced training
Start
Two intakes a year: January and July
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Children's Intensive Care Unit (CICU)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Recognised postgraduate degree in paediatrics, emergency medicine or anaesthesia with significant acute paediatric experience; fluent in English (TOEFL/IELTS if instruction not in English); valid SMC registration; BCLS and conscious sedation credentialing; advanced paediatric resuscitation certification before start
International applicants
Yes - KKH clinical fellowships are offered to foreign medical doctors; clinical fellows need a supporting letter from their government, regional health authority or home institution stating their position is reserved until return
Visa support
Valid Training Employment Pass issued by the Ministry of Manpower required; processing time at least 12 months
Licence requirement
Temporary Registration with the Singapore Medical Council (SMC) required (SMC fees S$300 application + S$550 practising certificate)

How to apply

Deadline
Institution policyno fixed deadline - apply by email to Education.Office@KKH.com.sg at least 12 months before the intended start (processing time at least 12 months)
Application method
Email Education.Office@KKH.com.sg; KKH application fee S$620 + GST for successful applicants

Funding

Funding
Institution policyself-funded - KKH application fee, training fee, SMC temporary registration, Training Employment Pass, malpractice insurance and all personal expenses are borne by the applicant (home-country support letter required)
