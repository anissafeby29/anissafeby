Overview

A two-year fellowship preparing pediatricians for academic careers in general pediatrics through the Population Health Science Scholars Program.

The programme

Training type
Research Fellowship
Duration
2 years
Start
1 July
Positions
Not stated on official page

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Board eligible/certified in pediatrics; graduate of an accredited US residency; active New York State licence before matriculation
International applicants
US citizen or foreign national with permanent-residence visa required (HRSA T32 requirement)
Visa support
No - permanent US residence required
Licence requirement
Active New York State medical licence before matriculation
