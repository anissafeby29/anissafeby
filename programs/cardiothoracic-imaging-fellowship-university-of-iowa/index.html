Overview

A one-year non-accredited fellowship offering broad clinical experience in both thoracic and cardiovascular imaging.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed radiology residency and USMLE Steps 1-3 before applying; able to obtain an Iowa resident medical licence; preferably preparing for ABR certification
International applicants
Institution policyGraduates of non-US/Canadian schools eligible with a valid ECFMG certificate (or full unrestricted US licence, or Fifth Pathway); visa trainees accepted at programme discretion
Visa support
Institution policyAcceptable visas: J-1 (ECFMG), J-2 with EAD, H-1B, H-4 with EAD, E-3 (Australian citizens), F-1 OPT (first residency year only); no department is required to accept visa trainees; J-1 requires USMLE Steps 1-2 passed, H-1B requires Steps 1-3 passed before offer
Licence requirement
Institution policyMust meet Iowa licensure requirements (acceptance of visa trainees is contingent on meeting all Iowa licensure requirements)

How to apply

Deadline
Not stated on official page
