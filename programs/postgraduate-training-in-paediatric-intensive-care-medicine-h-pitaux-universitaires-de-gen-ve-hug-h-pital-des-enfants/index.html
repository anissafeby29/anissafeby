Overview

SIWF-registered postgraduate training in paediatric intensive care at HUG's multidisciplinary PICU, a centre for highly specialised medicine caring for patients from extreme prematurity to 16 years, including congenital heart disease and paediatric liver transplantation.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
Individualised training plan; includes optional 6-12 month rotations in partner centres (e.g. HUG adult ICU, anaesthesia, paediatric cardiology, CHUV PICU)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Prior postgraduate training in paediatrics strongly desired; for chef de clinique posts prior intensive care or anaesthesia experience is essential; French (spoken and written) at C1 level required (HUG institutional obligation)
