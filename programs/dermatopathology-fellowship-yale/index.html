Overview

One-year ACGME dermatopathology training with one of the largest academic dermatopathology groups in the US.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (optional second research year depending on funding)
Start
Institution policyYale-New Haven Hospital resident/fellow appointments run for one year beginning 1 July and ending 30 June (sample appointment agreement)
Positions
1 per year (2027 position filled; 2028 applications accepted 1-30 June 2026)
