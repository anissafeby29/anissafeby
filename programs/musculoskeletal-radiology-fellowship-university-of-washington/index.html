Overview

A 12-month non-ACGME musculoskeletal fellowship, founded in 1992, with advanced hands-on clinical training in MSK diagnostic imaging and procedures including sports medicine, tumour and orthopaedic trauma cases.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of an ACGME-accredited radiology residency in the US or RCPSC-accredited program in Canada (or equivalent radiology residency in another country with ECFMG certification); preference for ABR-certified or eligible candidates. Must be in the ABR certification process by fellowship start.
International applicants
Equivalent residency in another country accepted with ECFMG certification.
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Able to obtain an unrestricted Washington State medical licence by 1 July 2028.
