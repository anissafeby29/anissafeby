Overview

Immersive one-year laryngology fellowship in airway, voice and swallowing disorders (about 225 major cases), with the fellow providing tertiary laryngology services at Zuckerberg San Francisco General in the final six months.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
OHNS residency in an approved US centre or equivalent training outside the USA; commitment to an academic laryngology career; ability to obtain a California medical licence
International applicants
Equivalent OHNS training outside the USA accepted, provided California licensure can be obtained
Visa support
Institution policyIMGs in graduate medical training hold J-1 (Alien Physician) visas sponsored by ECFMG, with the UCSF ISSO as Training Program Liaison (request 6 months before start); H-1B for clinical trainees only via exception request
Licence requirement
California medical licence
