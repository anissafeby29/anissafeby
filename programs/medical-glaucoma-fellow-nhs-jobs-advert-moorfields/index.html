Overview

Medical glaucoma and laser fellowship including paediatric, uveitic, normal-tension and angle-closure glaucoma clinics across Moorfields sites.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
In ophthalmology training or recently completed higher specialist training
International applicants
Yes - applicants from outside the EU may obtain GMC registration and work visa via RCOphth Dual Sponsorship
Visa support
RCOphth Dual Sponsorship scheme; Skilled Worker sponsorship considered
Licence requirement
Full GMC registration with licence to practise (or eligibility)
