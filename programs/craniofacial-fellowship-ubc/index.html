Overview

A fellowship focused on craniofacial trauma and post-traumatic reconstruction, plus elective adult craniofacial reconstruction including cleft and orthognathic surgery.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Successful completion of a Royal College or American Board certified plastic surgery programme or equivalent
International applicants
TOEFL required if medical school and residency were not in English
Visa support
Institution policy (UBC PGME): foreign trainees must hold an IRCC work permit for the whole training period; the sponsoring department/PGME submits an offer of employment to IRCC and the fellow applies under the LMIA-exempt category
Licence requirement
Institution policy (CPSBC/UBC PGME): postgraduate trainees and fellows at UBC must be licensed with the College of Physicians and Surgeons of BC (educational - postgraduate class; fellow licence max 4 years) before training starts; the department obtains the licence, IMGs need physiciansapply.ca source verification and CPSBC English proficiency

How to apply

Deadline
Apply about 24 months before the desired start
Application method
Direct application about 24 months before the desired start; applicants generally accepted 18-24 months ahead of a January or July start

Funding

Funding
Generous financial support available (offered in conjunction with Stryker Canada)
