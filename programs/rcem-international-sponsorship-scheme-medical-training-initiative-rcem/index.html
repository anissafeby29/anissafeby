Overview

RCEM's MTI sponsorship scheme enabling overseas emergency physicians to train in NHS posts for up to two years with GMC registration without PLAB.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Current trainee in an overseas EM programme, EM consultant, or consultant in another specialty with ≥12 months EM experience; ≥3 years postgraduate training incl. internship and ≥1 year EM training; institutional sponsor in home country; acceptable IELTS/OET
International applicants
Yes – only for doctors based in non-EEA countries; priority to DfID priority and low/lower-middle income countries; must return home after placement
Visa support
Tier 5 (Government Authorised Exchange) visa with Certificate of Sponsorship from the Academy of Medical Royal Colleges
Licence requirement
RCEM sponsorship for full GMC registration without PLAB (not available to those who have failed PLAB)
