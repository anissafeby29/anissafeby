Overview

Competitive, donor-funded 12-month honorary fellowships at Sydney Eye Hospital, through which the Foundation has funded over 150 fellows from 31 countries since 1991.

The programme

Focus
Multiple ophthalmic subspecialties (vitreoretinal, medical retina, cornea, glaucoma, strabismus, oculoplastics, uveitis, neuro-ophthalmology)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Fully qualified ophthalmologist in their country; AHPRA English proficiency (or exemption); internship completed
International applicants
Overseas ophthalmologists welcome
Visa support
Hospital sponsors training visa subclass 407
Licence requirement
AHPRA registration; RANZCO specialist assessment; primary-source verification via ECFMG/EPIC
