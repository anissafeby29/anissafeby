Head and Neck Oncology & Reconstructive Microvascular Surgery Fellowship

Overview

Develops leaders in head and neck oncology and reconstructive surgery, with the fellow performing at least 100 free flaps and meeting criteria as a Cancer Care Ontario head and neck surgical oncologist.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
FRCSC in OTL-HNS or ABO diplomate (or equivalent) preferred; equivalent foreign training acceptable
International applicants
Foreign-trained candidates encouraged to apply
Visa support
Institution policyNon-Canadians need a closed, employer-specific IRCC work permit; Western PGME issues the supporting documentation after CPSO eligibility (allow at least 8 months; processing 4-50+ weeks)
Licence requirement
Ontario licence (educational licence acceptable)

How to apply

Deadline
November, about 1.5 years before start (November 2026 for the 2028 position)
Application method
CV and three letters of reference to the fellowship director

Funding

Funding
Institution policyAll clinical fellowships must be salaried at minimum CAD 65,000/yr from an approved source: sponsoring government (Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, UAE, Yemen), department, or external institution; no personal/family funding; self-funding only for CPSO independent-practice-eligible fellows
