Overview

A three-year programme offering dual ABIM certification in hematology and medical oncology, with 18 months of clinical training at University Hospital and the Ann Arbor VA and 75% protected research time in years two and three.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years
Start
1 July (academic year beginning 1 July)
Positions
7 new positions per year (7 for 2026-2027; 21 fellows in total)
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited
Department
Division of Hematology and Oncology, Department of Internal Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Institution policyinternational graduates need a valid ECFMG certificate and eligibility for Michigan licensure; Michigan medical and controlled substance licences required
International applicants
Institution policyYes - non-US medical graduates must hold a current ECFMG certificate and be eligible for a State of Michigan licence; non-US citizens need ECFMG J-1, permanent residence or qualifying EAD
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 sponsored by ECFMG (or permanent residence / qualifying EAD); other visas only in very limited circumstances, case by case at Program Director discretion with prospective GME Committee approval
Licence requirement
Institution policyState of Michigan medical licence and controlled substance licence (Educational Limited or Permanent) required; permanent-licence holders also need a personal DEA registration

How to apply

Deadline
31 July for the academic year beginning 1 July of the following year (last stated cycle: 31 July 2025 for 2026-2027)
Application method
ERAS only (no paper applications); NRMP match; programme signals considered preferentially

Funding

Funding
Institution policyhouse officers paid on the UM/HOA scale; FY27 (from 1 July 2026) HO I $77,049, HO IV $86,670, HO V $90,137, HO VI $93,742, HO VII $97,492 up to HO IX $105,447, plus annual lump-sum savings payment and benefits
