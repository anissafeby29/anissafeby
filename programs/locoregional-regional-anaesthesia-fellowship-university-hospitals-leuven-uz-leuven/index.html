Overview

UZ Leuven anaesthesia fellowship in locoregional anaesthesia for international doctors, with applications reviewed annually in June.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
Minimum 6 months, maximum 1 year (longer terms exceptionally negotiable)
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Department of Anaesthesiology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
English proficiency minimum B2; official CEFR C1 proof in Dutch, French or German required for the visa to practise medicine in Belgium
International applicants
Yes — EU and non-EU applicants (C1 language level in Dutch, French or German required)
Visa support
For non-EU applicants UZ Leuven assists with the visa to practise medicine in Belgium; fellow then applies for a student visa at the Belgian embassy
Licence requirement
Visa to practise medicine in Belgium and registration with the Order of Physicians (Orde der Artsen); EU applicants also need recognition of medical degree(s)

How to apply

Deadline
Apply 3 years before desired start, by 31 May (e.g. for 2029 start apply by 31 May 2026)
Application method
Email CV, motivation letter (with desired start date and duration), two recommendation letters and English proof (B2) to the department contacts by 31 May; applications discussed in June
