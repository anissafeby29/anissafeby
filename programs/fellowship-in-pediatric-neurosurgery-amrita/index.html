Overview

Fellowship in paediatric neurosurgery and paediatric craniofacial surgery at Amrita, Kochi, with 2-year, 1-year and observership formats.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship (also short-term fellowship and observership)
Duration
2 years preferred; short-term 1-year fellowship and short-term observership also available
Start
1 July (2024 intake)
Positions
Not stated on official page

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
MCh or DNB (Neurosurgery)
International applicants
Yes - official page lists international@amrita.edu / Amrita Center for International Programs (acipkochi@aims.amrita.edu) as contacts for international applicants
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Foreign trainees need National Medical Commission (India) permission (stated on Amrita's international observership page)

How to apply

Deadline
30 May for the 2024 intake; international applicants should apply 4 months before course commencement
Application method
Email detailed CV and 1,000-word letter of interest to Dr Suhas Udayakumaran, copying acipkochi@aims.amrita.edu and international@amrita.edu; interview in June
