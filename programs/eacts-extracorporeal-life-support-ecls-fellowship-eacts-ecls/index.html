EACTS Extracorporeal Life Support (ECLS) Fellowship

Maastricht UMC+, UZ Leuven (Gasthuisberg) and Medical University of Vienna (EACTS Francis Fontan Fund)

Overview

Three one-week visits to Maastricht, Leuven and Vienna giving clinical and simulation exposure to post-cardiotomy mechanical circulatory support.

The programme

Focus

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Last year of training or first four years of independent practice; interest in cardiogenic shock and post-cardiotomy MCS; preferably from a centre with little PC-MCS experience
International applicants
Open to EACTS members; the page sets no nationality or EU-residency restriction (EACTS membership is international). Fluent English required.

Not stated on official page

How to apply

Deadline
Application window 1 March – 1 May 2026 (2026/27 cycle closed)
Application method
Online application to the EACTS Francis Fontan Fund with letter of support, letter of interest, CV with publications and operative experience (documents in English, Word or PDF)

Funding

Funding
Grant of €6,500 (educational grant from Medtronic) plus EACTS Annual Meeting bursary
