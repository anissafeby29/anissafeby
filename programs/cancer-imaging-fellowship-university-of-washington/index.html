Overview

A one-year non-ACGME fellowship providing in-depth experience in oncologic imaging across MRI, CT, ultrasound, PET-CT, PET-MRI and image-guided procedures, with six months each in abdominal imaging and nuclear medicine.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of an ACGME, AOA, ACGME-I Advanced Specialty, RCPSC or CFPC-accredited residency and all USMLE steps; or an international DR residency with home-country DR board certification.
International applicants
International graduates accepted with home-country diagnostic radiology board certification; participates in the department's ABR alternate pathway programme.
Visa support
Committed to supporting international applicants by sponsoring visas.
Licence requirement
Must be eligible to obtain a State of Washington medical license.
