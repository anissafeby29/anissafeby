Overview

A one-year reconstructive microsurgery fellowship in a section performing more than 500 microsurgery cases a year across breast, head and neck, extremity, gender affirmation, burn and lymphedema reconstruction.

Graduates of international-equivalent plastic surgery training are considered
Visa support
