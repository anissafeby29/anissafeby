Overview

Stanford's rheumatology fellowship trains rheumatologists for careers in immunology, clinical rheumatology or clinical epidemiology of rheumatic diseases, combining cutting-edge science with compassionate care.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Three years of postgraduate training in Internal Medicine (or two years under the ABIM Research Pathway); Board Eligible in Internal Medicine at start; USMLE I, II and III passed (waivers considered individually).
International applicants
ECFMG certificate required if applicable; visa status must be submitted by non-US citizens.
Visa support
J-1 visas only
Licence requirement
Institution policyMedical Board of California Postgraduate Training License required within 180 days of starting an ACGME programme (USMLE Steps 1 & 2 CK passed); non-ACGME programmes require a full California licence by the start date

How to apply

Deadline
Applications accepted via ERAS from July 2026 for 2027 positions; interviews start September 2026
Application method
ERAS; NRMP Match

Funding

Funding
Stanford GME offers salaries matched to the Bay Area cost of living; programme page refers to the Stanford GME website for salaries and benefits.
