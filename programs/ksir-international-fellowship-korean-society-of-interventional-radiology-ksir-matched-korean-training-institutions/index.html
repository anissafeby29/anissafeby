Overview

KSIR-funded 1–3 month interventional radiology fellowship that matches Asian radiologists with Korean academic institutions to promote IR across Asia.

The programme

Training type
Visiting Fellowship (clinical training and/or research)
Duration
1–3 months
Start
Within the applicable year
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
KSIR certificate on completion after report
Department
KSIR

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Graduates of medical colleges with sufficient training and experience in radiology; fluent English (TOEFL/TOEIC/IELTS evidence beneficial); must attend the annual IICIR course (IICIR 2027: Seoul, Feb 15–19; free registration for selected fellows)
International applicants
Yes — aims to train Asian radiologists specialising in interventional radiology; participants must return home after training
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Not stated on official page

How to apply

Deadline
May 24 every year (notification the following June)
Application method
Online via KSIR website (intervention.or.kr/ENG): application form, pledge, medical report, cover letter and two recommendation letters

Funding

Funding
KSIR grant: accommodation cost 40,000 KRW/day for 90 days plus economy airfare up to 1,000,000 KRW; fellow arranges own housing
