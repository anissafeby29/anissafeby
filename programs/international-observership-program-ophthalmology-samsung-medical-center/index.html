Overview

Informal shadowing observership in Samsung Medical Center's Department of Ophthalmology covering clinics, rounds, conferences and seminars.

Eligibility and visa

MD degree; Korean or English communication (TOEFL iBT 64 / TOEIC 600 / IELTS 5.5 unless exempt)
International applicants
Yes - international MDs (Ophthalmology listed among accepting departments)

No - SMC does not issue guarantee letters for visa applications for observerships

Not applicable (observation only)
