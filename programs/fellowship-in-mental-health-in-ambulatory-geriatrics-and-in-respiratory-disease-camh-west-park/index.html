Fellowship in Mental Health in Ambulatory Geriatrics and in Respiratory Disease

CAMH and West Park Healthcare Centre / University of Toronto

PsychiatryGeriatric psychiatryClinical Fellowship
Destination

Overview

Assessment and collaborative-care management of mental health conditions in community-dwelling older adults, especially those with respiratory disease.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (renewable)
Start
July 1 preferred (alternative dates negotiable)

Not stated on official page

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Domestic applicants completing/completed general psychiatry residency; international applicants certified as psychiatry specialists
International applicants
Yes — internationally trained specialists eligible if they meet CPSO requirements; English proficiency results may be requested; government-sponsored trainees from Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, UAE and Saudi Arabia apply via STAR
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
CPSO educational (or independent) licence eligibility required
