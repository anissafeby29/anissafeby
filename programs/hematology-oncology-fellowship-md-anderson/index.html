Overview

A three-year integrated programme allowing dual board eligibility in haematology and medical oncology (or medical oncology alone), with 12-18 months of clinical rotations followed by protected research time.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed 3-year ACGME-accredited IM residency (or 4-year RCPSC-accredited IM residency in Canada); ABIM IM board eligible; US citizen, permanent resident or J-1 visa holder
International applicants
Foreign medical graduates accepted; must supply ECFMG certificate and college transcripts; must be US citizen/permanent resident or hold J-1 visa (programme page)
Visa support
J-1 only; the programme does not sponsor any other type of visa (programme page)
Licence requirement
Texas Medical Board physician-in-training permit or Texas medical licence

How to apply
