Breast Reconstruction and Oncoplastic Surgery Clinical Fellowship

Kameda Medical Center (Kameda Medical Hospital)

Plastic SurgeryBreast reconstruction & microsurgeryClinical Fellowship (observerships also accepted)

Overview

Surgical training in breast reconstruction, oncoplastic breast surgery and microsurgery for lymphoedema with patient assessment and conferences.

The programme

Focus
Breast reconstruction and lymphoedema microsurgery

Clinical Fellowship (observerships also accepted)

Not stated on official page

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Medical licence issued by home-country Ministry of Health; three years of clinical experience or training
International applicants
Yes - listed by the Japan Society of Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery (JSPRS) as accepting overseas fellows/observers
Visa support
Certificate of eligibility, certificate of permission of advanced clinical training, and visa
Licence requirement
Hands-on clinical training requires a Certificate of Permission of Advanced Clinical Training (Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare) (clinical training designated hospital for foreign doctors)

How to apply

Deadline
Handled individually
Application method
Contact the Breast Center contact person listed on the JSPRS information sheet

Funding

Funding
No salary; no application fee; accommodation and health insurance available
