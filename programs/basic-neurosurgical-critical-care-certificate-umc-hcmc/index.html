Overview

UMC HCMC Department of Neurosurgery programme in basic neurosurgical intensive care leading to a professional technique certificate.

The programme

Focus
Neurotrauma
Training type
Professional technique certificate programme (Vietnamese Ministry of Health continuing training)
Duration
Per announcement: 29 July 2026 - 29 November 2027 (Course 05)
Start
29 July 2026 (Course 05)
Positions
2-8
Accreditation
Certificate of professional technical training (chứng chỉ đào tạo kỹ thuật chuyên môn) issued by UMC HCMC

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Doctors with a practising certificate or postgraduate degree (MSc, CK1, CK2) in neurology, neurosurgery, emergency/critical care or anaesthesia, or a general licence with at least 1 year in neurology/neurosurgery
International applicants
Not stated on official page
Visa support
Not stated on official page

Vietnamese practising certificate/licence (chứng chỉ/giấy phép hành nghề) required

How to apply

Deadline
Rolling - from announcement until enough applications received
Application method
Registration form and notarised documents to the Center for Health Workforce Training on Social Demand; no postal applications; fees paid on submission until places are filled

Funding
