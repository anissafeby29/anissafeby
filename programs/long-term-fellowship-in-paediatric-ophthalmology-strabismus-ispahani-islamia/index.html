Overview

Long-term paediatric ophthalmology and strabismus fellowship with supervised surgery in a hospital seeing ~1,500 patients daily.

Eligibility
Postgraduate degree in ophthalmology (DO/FCPS/MS or equivalent recognised by BMDC/IIEI&H); binocular single vision; age not over 45
International applicants
Yes - separate 15-month duration for international candidates; Skype interview for foreign candidates
