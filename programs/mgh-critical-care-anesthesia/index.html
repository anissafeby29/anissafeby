Overview

Multidisciplinary critical care training develops ICU management skills through medical, surgical and cardiac intensive care rotations.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Acceptable anesthesiology residency or the specified clinical training in listed surgical and other specialties; consult the program and relevant specialty board.
International applicants
Limited - outstanding international candidates without ACGME training considered individually under MGB GME guidance; contact coordinator first
