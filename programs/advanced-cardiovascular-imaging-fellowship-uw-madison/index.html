Overview

A multidisciplinary one-year fellowship training experts in adult and paediatric cardiovascular imaging, progressing to early-career faculty level responsibility.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Graduate of an ACGME or RCPSC-accredited diagnostic radiology residency; Wisconsin medical license before start; Canadian graduates must be LMCC and hold H-1B.
International applicants
International applicants must have completed radiology residency and USMLE Steps 1–3, be legally able to work in the US and be H-1B eligible.
Visa support
H-1B eligible required.
Licence requirement
Must possess a Wisconsin medical license before beginning fellowship.

How to apply

Deadline
Application review begins 1 September 2026.
Application method
Apply through the UW–Madison Job Site posting (CV, cover letter) plus UW Radiology Fellowship Application, USMLE scores and three letters to the fellowship coordinator; SCARD process.

Funding

Funding
Appointed as Clinical Instructor; moonlighting available.
