Overview

Advanced training in adult liver transplant anesthesiology covering liver transplant recipients, living liver donors, major hepatic resection and complex open vascular surgery, with integration into Stanford's multidisciplinary transplant team (about 160 adult liver transplants per year).

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed residency training in Anesthesiology and at minimum board eligible
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs welcome to apply; must hold a valid ECFMG certificate and be able to obtain Medical Board of California licensure; non-ACGME fellowships need ACGME Non-Standard Training recognition before J-1 sponsorship
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 (ECFMG) is the primary visa; H-1B permitted only in some circumstances with GME approval; O-1 rarely, varies by department
Licence requirement
Must be able to obtain a California medical license

How to apply

Deadline
Rolling; applications accepted starting one year prior to projected start date
Application method
Contact the Program Director (George Zhou, MD) by email
