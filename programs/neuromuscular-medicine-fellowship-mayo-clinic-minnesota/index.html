Neuromuscular Medicine Fellowship

Mayo Clinic — Minnesota

NeurologyNeuromuscular medicineClinical Fellowship
Destination
USA
Rochester, Minnesota, United States
Open official programme page ↗Compare saved →

Overview

A two-year programme of advanced training in the clinical diagnosis, electrodiagnosis, management and pathology of neuromuscular disorders for academic careers.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
2 years
Start
July
Positions
2 per year
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited; eligible for ABPN neuromuscular, ABEM and UCNS neuromuscular pathology examinations
Department
Department of Neurology, Mayo Clinic School of Graduate Medical Education

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of an ACGME- or RCPSC-accredited neurology residency
International applicants
Open to international applicants; graduates of medical schools outside the US/Canada must hold ECFMG certification before appointment
Visa support
Mayo Clinic supports ECFMG J-1 and H-1B sponsorship (J-1 requires an ACGME-accredited/recognised programme; no new H-1B petitions requiring the US$100,000 payment); final visa type at Mayo's discretion
Licence requirement
Fellows must obtain and maintain an appropriate medical licence while enrolled

How to apply

Deadline
AANEM Fellowship Portal opens to institutions March 1; interviews for 2027–2029 held April 2026; no offers before June 1
Application method
AANEM Fellowship Portal

Funding

Funding
Salaried trainee position (stipend and benefits per Mayo Clinic School of GME)
