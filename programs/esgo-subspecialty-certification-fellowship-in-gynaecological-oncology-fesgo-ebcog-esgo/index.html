ESGO Subspecialty Certification Fellowship in Gynaecological Oncology (FESGO-EBCOG)

Overview

Two-to-three-year full-time fellowship at an ESGO-accredited Training Centre leading, with the ESGO Exam, to the FESGO-EBCOG European subspecialty diploma.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship (subspecialty certification)
Duration
2–3 years full-time
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Up to each centre's confirmed number of accredited positions
Accreditation
ESGO-EBCOG Diploma of European Gynaecological Oncologist (FESGO-EBCOG); ESGO Curriculum adopted by UEMS in 2025
Department
Not stated on official page

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
ESGO member; obstetrics and gynaecology specialist certificate; certificate of medical degree; training plan in line with the ESGO Curriculum; must pass the ESGO Exam
International applicants
Available to ESGO members; no nationality restriction stated. Fellows obtain local positions directly from the accredited centre, whose own employment, language and licensing rules apply
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Not stated on official page

How to apply

Deadline
Rolling – apply directly to an accredited training centre
Application method
Contact an ESGO-accredited Training Centre for a local fellowship post, then register via the REDCap online application with CV, degree and specialist certificates and training plan; mandatory eLogbook
