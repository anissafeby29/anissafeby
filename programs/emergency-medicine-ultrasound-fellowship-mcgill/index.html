Overview

An intensive one-year point-of-care ultrasound fellowship at the Jewish General Hospital requiring at least 800 logged scans and a research project.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship

1 year

Institution policyMcGill PGME academic year starts 1 July and ends 30 June

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Institution policyCompleted residency plus Royal College or Board specialty certification; must be eligible for a CMQ training card (European Board certificate not accepted)
International applicants
Yes – McGill PGME accepts international medical graduates (non-sponsored, up to 3 years of training in Quebec) and sponsored applicants via their sponsoring agency; candidates must secure their own funding
Visa support
Institution policyNon-Canadian fellows need an LMIA-exempt employer-specific work permit (code C45), initiated by McGill PGME after the CMQ Letter of Eligibility; no CAQ/study permit
Licence requirement
Collège des médecins du Québec training registration via McGill PGME; French language courses expected

How to apply

Deadline
Institution policy1 September 2026 for the 2027-2028 academic year (McGill PGME online application opens 15 May 2026)
Application method
McGill Postgraduate Medical Education fellowship application

Funding

Funding
Not RAMQ-funded: fellow must secure a scholarship or external funding (government, organisation or university) per McGill PGME rules
