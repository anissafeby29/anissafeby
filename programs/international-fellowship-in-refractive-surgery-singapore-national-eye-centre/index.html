Overview

One-year fellowship in all types of refractive surgery, refractive cataract and phakic IOL surgery, patient selection and complication management.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
SMC general requirements for temporary registration; higher degree/postgraduate diploma in ophthalmology (preferably >=3 years ophthalmic experience); SMC English language requirements; experienced in most aspects of clinical ophthalmology; approval of overseas training from home-country authorities; vaccination/serology report
International applicants
Yes, but as all refractive patients are private, only local fellows may perform surgery under supervision (international fellows observe and assist)
Visa support
Successful application for a Training Employment Pass from Singapore Ministry of Manpower required (MOM fee SGD 360 listed)
Licence requirement
Temporary registration with the Singapore Medical Council (application fee SGD 300, practising certificate SGD 450)
