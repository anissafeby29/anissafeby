Head & Neck Surgical Oncology Fellowship

University of Toronto

Destination

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Overview

An integrated multidisciplinary programme spanning University Health Network, Sunnybrook and Sinai Health, with exposure to ablative, reconstructive and skull-base surgery.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year; 2-year option
Start
Annual intake
Positions
6 clinical positions (4 via AHNS match, 2 outside match) plus 1 research position
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Department of Otolaryngology - Head & Neck Surgery

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Prior ENT, general surgery or plastic surgery residency; licensing requirements apply
International applicants
Yes
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
CPSO (College of Physicians and Surgeons of Ontario) educational licence; must be recognised as a specialist in prior jurisdiction; registration with U of T Post MD Education

How to apply

Deadline
1 May of the application year (decision deadline 15 June); AHNS match timeline for match positions
Application method
American Head and Neck Society (AHNS) Advanced Training Council match for 4 positions; 2 positions offered outside the match to applicants from match-ineligible countries; sponsored applicants via the Post MD Education Office

Funding

Funding
Paid; clinical fellow compensation equal to or greater than PGY3 resident salary
