Overview

A one-year hospital medicine fellowship at Toronto General, Toronto Western and Mount Sinai Hospitals for Family Medicine or Internal Medicine graduates seeking expertise in managing complex medical inpatients.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (possible extension to a 2nd year)
Start
Commonly July 1; may vary with vacancies
Positions
16 per year
Accreditation
University of Toronto clinical fellowship; does not lead to RCPSC certification (per UofT PGME fellowship page)
Department
Department of Medicine, Division of General Internal Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed training in Family Medicine or Internal Medicine
International applicants
Non-Canadian applicants may apply; sponsored applicants from Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia and UAE apply via sponsoring agency to PGME (per UofT PGME fellowship page)
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
CPSO certificate authorizing postgraduate education (educational licence); applicants must be recognized as a specialist/family physician in the jurisdiction where they practised (per UofT PGME fellowship page)

How to apply

Deadline
Ongoing until positions are filled; apply at least 6 months before expected start
Application method
Contact Program Administrator Lorena Manganaan (Lorena.Manganaan@uhn.ca)

Funding

Funding
Not stated on official page
