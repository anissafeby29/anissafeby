Overview

Clinical training in neonatal intensive care in Singapore's largest NICU, enabling fellows to independently manage babies requiring Level 3 neonatal care.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
Institution policyclinical fellowships for foreign doctors are capped at 12 months
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
3 positions at any one time
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Department of Neonatology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
MBBS or equivalent; postgraduate degree or diploma in Paediatrics from home country; at least 1 year prior NICU experience
International applicants
Yes - KKH clinical fellowships are offered to foreign medical doctors; clinical fellows need a supporting letter from their government, regional health authority or home institution stating their position is reserved until return
Visa support
Valid Training Employment Pass issued by the Ministry of Manpower required; processing time at least 12 months
Licence requirement
Temporary Registration with the Singapore Medical Council (SMC) required (SMC fees S$300 application + S$550 practising certificate)

How to apply

Deadline
Institution policyno fixed deadline - apply by email to Education.Office@KKH.com.sg at least 12 months before the intended start (processing time at least 12 months)
Application method
Email Education.Office@KKH.com.sg; KKH application fee S$620 + GST for successful applicants

Funding

Funding
Fellows must be sponsored by their government, regional health authority or an appropriate institution in their home country
