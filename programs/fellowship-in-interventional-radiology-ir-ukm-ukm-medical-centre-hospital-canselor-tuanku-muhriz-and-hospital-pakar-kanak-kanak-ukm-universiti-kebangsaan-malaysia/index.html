Fellowship in Interventional Radiology (IR) (UKM)

UKM Medical Centre (Hospital Canselor Tuanku Muhriz) and Hospital Pakar Kanak-Kanak UKM, Universiti Kebangsaan Malaysia

Overview

UKM interventional radiology fellowship (1–3 years) at the adult and children's specialist hospitals providing graded hands-on diagnostic and therapeutic angiography and intervention to consultant level.
