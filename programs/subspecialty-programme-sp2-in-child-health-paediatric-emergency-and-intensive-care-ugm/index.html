Overview

FK-KMK UGM's child health subspecialist (Sp2) programme offers a 4-semester paediatric emergency and intensive care track for Indonesian paediatricians.

The programme

Training type
Subspecialty training programme
Duration
4 semesters
Start
January 2027 intake (new-student lecture 16 December 2026); FK-KMK UGM runs January and July admission periods

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Indonesian specialist (Sp1) in paediatrics (Sp.A); specialist transcript GPA >= 3.00 (programme accredited A/B); English test AcEPT >= 209 or TOEP PLTI >= 45/450; aptitude test PAPs UGM or TPDA PLTI >= 500; recommendation letter or competence certificate from the relevant collegium; valid STR; certificate of completion of the specialist deployment programme (formerly WKDS) or at least 1 year of clinical service; 2 confidential online references. Clinical experience of at least 1 year at a paediatric specialist education institution (IPDSA), 2 years at an IPDSA network hospital or 3 years at another hospital; recommendation from the IDAI branch chair and from the relevant IDAI working group (UKK). Recommendation from 2 consultants in the field at the UGM Department of Child Health. Maximum age 45 at start (older applicants need a hospital director's recommendation).

How to apply

Deadline
January 2027 intake: online registration 14-27 September 2026 via admissions.ugm.ac.id; documents emailed to the programme 12-23 October 2026; tests 24-30 October 2026; results 4 December 2026
Application method
Online registration at admissions.ugm.ac.id, then email the selection documents (PDF with printed online registration proof) to the programme (sp2anak.fkkmk@ugm.ac.id; 0815-8113-519); academic test, MMPI, health test and programme interview
