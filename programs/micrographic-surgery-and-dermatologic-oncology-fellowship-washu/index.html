Overview

A one-year ACGME fellowship at the WashU Center for Dermatologic and Cosmetic Surgery covering Mohs surgery, reconstruction, laser and cosmetic dermatology.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Board-eligible or board-certified in dermatology before start
International applicants
Institution policyInternational graduates accepted via ECFMG J-1 sponsorship (H-1B at programme discretion); Missouri temporary licence requires a permanent ECFMG certificate
Visa support
Institution policyWashU GME Consortium supports ECFMG J-1 visas (managed by institutional Training Program Liaisons); H-1B may be supported at department/programme discretion; WashU holds ACGME Non-Standard Training recognition so non-accredited fellowships can host J-1 trainees
Licence requirement
Institution policyMissouri Board of Registration for the Healing Arts: residents and fellows in approved Missouri training programmes must hold a Missouri temporary licence (renewed annually; applied for via the hospital) before practising; IMGs need a permanent ECFMG certificate
