Eligibility and visa

At least three years of Advanced Training in Clinical Cardiology and completed FRACP (Part 2) or recognised equivalent; must be registrable in Australia
International applicants
Yes if holding a recognised FRACP-equivalent and registrable in Australia
Visa support
Institution policyEmployer-sponsored Training visa (subclass 407) is Alfred Health's preferred visa for IMGs (granted 13 months); Medical Workforce team supports visa nomination, starting at least 6 months before commencement
