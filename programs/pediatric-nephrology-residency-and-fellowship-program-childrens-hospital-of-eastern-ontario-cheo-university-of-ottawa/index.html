Overview

Royal College-accredited pediatric nephrology training at CHEO for clinical or research careers, including a mandatory one-month transplant rotation at SickKids.

International applicants
Programme consistently has international fellows supported by their governments; foreign applicants see uOttawa PGME website
Visa support
Applicants must hold a valid Canadian visa
Licence requirement
Institution policyCPSO postgraduate education (educational) certificate of registration required, renewed annually; internationally trained clinical fellows must complete a 4-12 week Pre-Entry Assessment Program (PEAP); three-year licensure limit for clinical fellowships
