Overview

AO Trauma short-term fellowship placement in Orthopedics and Traumatology at Centro Hospitalar Universitário São João, Porto, for surgeons focused on fracture care.

The programme

Training type
Short-term International Fellowship (observership with scrub-in where permitted)

6–12 weeks

Placement year after selection
Positions
Not stated on official page

AO Trauma fellowship host centre

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Active AO Trauma member; completed AO Trauma Basic and Advanced Principles courses; orthopaedic trauma is main part of practice; under 45 in year of application; teaching-hospital employment recommended
International applicants
Yes – AO global programme; applications reviewed by the national council of the applicant's country and placed by AO regional fellowship coordinators
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Scrub-in: Yes; additional licence/requirement: Depending on the country. For doctors outside the EU, prior authorization may be required, which is easily granted. Dominant OR language: Portuguese (team English level (4)/5). Documents required: Documentation from the Hospital of origin, referring to the status of the doctor in question A short CV is preferred

How to apply

Deadline
2027 call closed; 2028 call opens spring 2027
Application method
Online application to AO Trauma during the open call (host allocated by regional fellowship coordinator); not directly to the hospital
