Overview

An NIH-funded T32 programme that trains future leaders in academic anesthesia through mentored research in neuroscience/pain, injury/inflammation/immunity, and outcomes research/bioinformatics.

The programme

Training type
Research Fellowship
Duration
Two-year training experiences anticipated for most candidates
Start
Institution policyStanford GME contracts are issued per academic year (1 July - 30 June); standard start late June/1 July
Positions
Four trainees in total, two appointed per year
Accreditation
NIH-funded T32 training grant
Department
Department of Anesthesiology, Perioperative and Pain Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
MD/PhD, MD, PhD or comparable doctoral degree; must be a US citizen, non-citizen national or lawful permanent resident
International applicants
Individuals on temporary or student visas are not eligible
Visa support
Not eligible on temporary visas; post-residency fellowship programme does not sponsor H1B visas and J1 applicants are not eligible
Licence requirement
Post-residency clinical trainees must have a California Medical License before beginning
