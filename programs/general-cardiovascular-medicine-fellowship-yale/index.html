CardiologyGeneral cardiovascular diseaseClinical Fellowship

Overview

An ACGME three-year fellowship with two years of core clinical training and an individualised third year tailored to clinical and research career goals.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years (additional years possible)
Start
July (US academic year; not explicitly stated)
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited
Department
Section of Cardiovascular Medicine, Department of Internal Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed three-year ACGME-accredited internal medicine residency by start; three letters (incl. PD letter), personal statement, USMLE/COMLEX transcript
International applicants
IMGs eligible if they complete an ACGME-accredited IM residency
Visa support
Sponsors J-1 and H-1B visas
Licence requirement
Institution policyHospital obtains a Connecticut Department of Public Health residency training permit on the trainee's behalf (trainee supplies documentation)

How to apply

Deadline
ERAS cycle opens in spring; interview slots assigned in order of application receipt
Application method
ERAS (NRMP Medical Specialties Matching Program)
