Overview

UCSF's three-year Maternal-Fetal Medicine & Reproductive Genetics fellowship provides outstanding clinical and research training, including rotations in the Fetal Treatment Center and Prenatal Diagnostic Center.

International applicants
Institution policyYes - international medical graduates train on ECFMG-sponsored J-1 visas coordinated by the UCSF ISSO
Visa support
Institution policyIMGs in graduate medical training hold J-1 (Alien Physician) visas sponsored by ECFMG, with the UCSF ISSO as Training Program Liaison (request 6 months before start); H-1B for clinical trainees only via exception request
