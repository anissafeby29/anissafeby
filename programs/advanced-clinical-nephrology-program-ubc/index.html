Advanced Clinical Nephrology Program

University of British Columbia

Internal MedicineNephrologyClinical Fellowship
Destination
CAN
Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada
Open official programme page ↗

Overview

A 12-month post-certification program giving advanced exposure to home dialysis, transplantation, intensive care nephrology and renal replacement therapies, with a curriculum tailored to the fellow's interests.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
March 1 or July 1, 2027 (flexible)
Positions
2+ each year
Accreditation
Accredited by the Australian and New Zealand Royal College organizations (per program page); UBC Division of Nephrology certificate on completion
Department
Department of Medicine, Division of Nephrology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed at least 2 years of nephrology in an accredited training program; graduated from medical school within 10 years
International applicants
Highly subscribed by trainees from Australia and New Zealand
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Must be eligible for an educational licence in British Columbia (CPSBC); IELTS Academic minimum 7 in each component if primary medical education/patient care was not in English

How to apply

Deadline
September 1 for a start the following year (2027 intake extended to September 14, 2026)
Application method
CV and three referees to Maeve Lalor, UBC Division & Programs Administrator

Funding

Salary $91,710.67 per annum + benefits
