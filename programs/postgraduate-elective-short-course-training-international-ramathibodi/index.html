Overview

Ramathibodi's short-course postgraduate elective lets international residents, fellows and physicians train in chosen departments for two weeks or more.

The programme

Focus
Multiple specialties (institution-wide observership)
Training type
Observership / short-course training
Duration
Minimum 2 weeks per department/division; rotations start on the first or third Monday of each month (not in April)
Start
Rolling; application windows Nov 1–30 (April–September starts), Feb 1–28 (July–December), May 1–31 (October–March), Aug 1–31 (January–June)
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
International Relations Section, Faculty of Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Resident, fellow or physician at the time of training; communicative level of English (non-native speakers)
International applicants
Yes - programme is for international postgraduate applicants
Visa support
Applicants arrange a Non-Immigrant Education (ED) visa at a Royal Thai Embassy or Consulate
Licence requirement
Not stated on official page

How to apply

Deadline
Apply at least 4 months before the intended start date, within the relevant window
Application method
Online application via the International Relations Section; submit photo, recommendation letter, CV, cover letter, English proficiency score and ID; after acceptance provide health insurance, visa copy, immunisation record and signed NDA

Funding

Funding
Self-funded: programme fee THB 5,000 per week plus academic fee (THB 900 for 1–4 weeks; THB 3,000 for >4 weeks–6 months; THB 6,000 for >6 months–1 year); fees waived under MoU, IFMSA and Asia-Uninet exchanges
