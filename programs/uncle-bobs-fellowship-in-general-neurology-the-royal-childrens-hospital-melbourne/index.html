Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Paediatric neurology trainee at any stage, or general/developmental paediatrician; police and Working with Children checks
International applicants
International medical graduates must meet AHPRA English language requirements (IELTS 7 in all bands or equivalent) for medical registration; IMG enquiries to mwu.enquiries@rch.org.au
Visa support
Institution policyAll RCH employees need Australian citizenship or a visa with work rights; where RCH agrees to sponsor, it engages migration lawyers for a Temporary Skill Shortage (subclass 482) employer-sponsored visa (usually 2-4 years, dependants included); other routes include 417/482 working holiday, 189/190 skilled visas
Licence requirement
AHPRA (Medical Board of Australia) registration required
