Overview

A one-year ACGME paediatric dermatology fellowship with inpatient consults at Children's Healthcare of Atlanta's Arthur M. Blank Hospital and clinical research opportunities.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility

International applicants
SPD match via SF Match is for US and Canadian residents only
Visa support
Institution policyIMGs must be ECFMG-certified and enter training on a J-1 (ECFMG-sponsored) or H-1B visa unless US citizen/green-card holder (Emory GME housestaff policy 5.12)
Licence requirement
Georgia medical licence by fellowship start
