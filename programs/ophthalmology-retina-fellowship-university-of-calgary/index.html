Overview

Busy two-year comprehensive vitreoretinal fellowship at Southern Alberta's only quaternary retinal referral centre performing about 2,000 surgical cases a year.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed (or completing) a Royal College of Canada (or equivalent) accredited ophthalmology residency
International applicants
Equivalent residency accepted; IELTS academic required if medical school outside an English-speaking country
Visa support
Fellows must meet Canadian immigration laws and obtain a work permit
Licence requirement
Eligible for educational or independent licence from the College of Physicians & Surgeons of Alberta
