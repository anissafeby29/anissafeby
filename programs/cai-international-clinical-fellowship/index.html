Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Primary medical degree; structured internship; completed 4-5 year national residency with examinations; at least 1 year post-residency experience in chosen specialty; eligible for Irish Medical Council (Supervised Division) registration; sponsored by national body (no self-sponsored candidates); Academic IELTS 7.0 overall (min 6.5 each domain) or OET grade B in each module; FCAI holders prioritised
International applicants
Yes - designed for doctors from outside Ireland sponsored by their government, hospital or national training body; fellows must return to home country after training
Visa support
Visa and immigration assistance (incl. Irish Embassy liaison) and Medical Council registration support via CAI partner Castel Education
Licence requirement
Eligibility for registration with the Irish Medical Council (Supervised Division)
