ESPGHAN Endoscopy Training Fellowship (Beginner, 3 months)

Hôpital Jeanne de Flandre, CHU de Lille

PaediatricsPaediatric gastroenterologyClinical Training Fellowship (society grant)
Destination

Overview

High-volume paediatric endoscopy training (about 1,000 procedures a year) with a simulator and therapeutic procedures such as PEG and endoscopic gastrojejunostomy.

The programme

Focus
Paediatric gastroenterology (endoscopy)
Training type
Clinical Training Fellowship (society grant)
Duration
3 months
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
ESPGHAN-wide: three 3-month and two 6-month grants
Accreditation
ESPGHAN Endoscopy Training Net – Centre of Endoscopy Training Excellence
Department
Paediatric Gastroenterology / Endoscopy Unit

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
ESPGHAN member in good standing (any type) with at least basic endoscopy experience; beginners and fully trained paediatric endoscopists welcome; support letters from 2 senior paediatric gastroenterologists (own institute and national society)
International applicants
Open to ESPGHAN members (any membership type); no nationality restriction stated, but candidates must meet all medicolegal and country-specific requirements of the host
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Simple agreement for salaried EU delegates; for non-EU and/or unsalaried delegates an agreement and special insurance are needed (host form)

How to apply

Deadline
31 October 2026
Application method
Application form, motivation letter, CV with endoscopy portfolio and two support letters sent to the ESPGHAN office; reviewed anonymously by the Endoscopy SIG core group; academic project expected for presentation at the ESPGHAN Annual Meeting

Funding

Funding
€5,000 grant (€4,000 to fellow, €1,000 to institute)
