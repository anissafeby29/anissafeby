Overview

One-year accredited primary care sports medicine fellowship at Mayo Clinic Square with coverage of professional teams (Timberwolves, Lynx, Twins) and extensive musculoskeletal ultrasound training.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
1 July
Positions
1 per year
Accreditation
Accredited (primary care sports medicine)
Department
Mayo Clinic Sports Medicine (Division of Department of Orthopedic Surgery)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed an ACGME-accredited PM&R residency; all three USMLE/COMLEX steps passed
International applicants
Mayo GME general admissions: graduates of medical schools outside the US/Canada must hold ECFMG certification prior to appointment (or a full unrestricted licence in the programme's state)
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
USMLE Steps 1-3 or COMLEX Levels 1-3 completed per Minnesota guidelines

How to apply

Deadline
15 August of the year preceding the start date; interviews September-November
Application method
ERAS fellowship application plus NRMP Sports Medicine Match

Funding

Funding
Fully institutionally funded
