Overview

A rigorous one-year clinical fellowship (established 2004) in the full range of pediatric CNS malignancies within CHLA's Cancer and Blood Disease Institute.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed ACGME-accredited pediatric hematology/oncology fellowship (or equivalent for FMGs with USMLE Steps 1-3 and ECFMG certificate); eligible for California licence
International applicants
Yes - foreign medical graduates (including J-1 holders) eligible with all three USMLE steps and a valid ECFMG certificate
Visa support
J-1 holders eligible to apply
Licence requirement
Must be eligible to obtain a California medical licence
