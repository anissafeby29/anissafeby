Overview

Cleveland Clinic's REI fellowship offers a rotation of 1-1-1 categorical positions filled through the NRMP match, with applications via ERAS on SREI Common Application dates.

Eligibility and visa

International applicants
International applicants invited to apply through NRMP; U.S. clinical experience preferred but not required; ECFMG requirements apply

Institution policyPermanent or training medical licence/certificate from the state board where training (State Medical Board of Ohio training certificate for Ohio; Florida Board of Medicine for Florida) required before starting; some advanced/non-accredited fellowships require a permanent licence

How to apply

Deadline
In conjunction with SREI Common Application dates
Application method
ERAS and NRMP; three letters of recommendation (one from residency program director); USMLE Step 1-2 and CREOG scores
