Overview

ACGME hand fellowship training in acute hand and upper extremity trauma, microvascular emergencies, reconstructive and congenital conditions, with two-month rotations.

The programme

Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (1 August - 31 July)

Accreditation
ACGME-accredited

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed general, orthopaedic or plastic surgery residency accredited by ACGME, AOA or RCPSC
International applicants
FMGs must hold a valid ECFMG certificate
Visa support
J-1 Clinical House Staff visa (ECFMG-sponsored) for ACGME-accredited fellowship; H-1B not sponsored
Licence requirement
Must qualify for a North Carolina medical (resident training) licence
