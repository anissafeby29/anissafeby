Overview

Duke's endocrinology fellowship offers separate Clinical and Research Tracks preparing fellows for clinical practice, research careers or academic clinician-educator roles.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Institution policyLCME/AOA-accredited MD/DO graduates, or international graduates with a valid ECFMG certificate or full unrestricted US licence; NC full licence or RTL, I-9 employment eligibility, background and NPDB checks
International applicants
Institution policyYes - graduates of medical schools outside the US/Canada are eligible if they hold a valid ECFMG certificate or a full unrestricted US licence
Visa support
Rarely sponsors H-1B or J-1 visas (not supported by T32 grant), though well-qualified candidates needing sponsorship are encouraged to apply.
Licence requirement
Institution policyNorth Carolina full medical licence or Resident Training License (RTL) required before clinical duties

How to apply

Deadline
Applications reviewed in late July; interviews late August-October
Application method
ERAS (Clinical and Research Tracks); NRMP Match; programme signals considered; virtual interviews

Funding

Funding
Institution policyGME trainees paid on the Duke stipend scale; FY27 (from 1 July 2026) PGY-1 $71,052 rising to PGY-4 $79,908, PGY-5 $82,992, PGY-6 $86,292, PGY-7 $90,036 (up to PGY-12 $106,800), plus benefits
