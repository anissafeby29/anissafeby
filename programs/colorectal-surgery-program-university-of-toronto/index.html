Overview

Royal College colorectal surgery training programme based at Mount Sinai Hospital with two trainees per year and a separate international application stream.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Institution policymust be eligible for a CPSO postgraduate education certificate, including recognition as a medical specialist/family physician in the jurisdiction where the applicant practised before the fellowship
International applicants
Accepted via a separate CRS fellowship application form sent to the programme administrator
Visa support
Institution policyinternational clinical fellows need an IRCC work permit; the PGME Office issues an LMIA-exempt offer of employment number and supports the work permit application
Licence requirement
Institution policyclinical fellows must hold a CPSO certificate of registration authorising postgraduate education (educational licence); research fellows do not need CPSO registration

How to apply

Deadline
International applicants: 15 March for the programme year after next; Canadian applicants via CaRMS (January)
Application method
Canadian applicants: CaRMS subspecialty match; international: direct application form

Funding

Funding
Salary per PARO guidelines (Canadian trainees); research year for international trainees only if funding available
