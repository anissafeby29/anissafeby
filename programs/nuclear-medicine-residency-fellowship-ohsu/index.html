Overview

An ACGME-accredited nuclear medicine programme (1–3 years depending on prior training) covering SPECT/CT, PET/CT and PET/MRI with research opportunities.

Focus

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Board certified or board eligible radiologist; eligible for an unrestricted Oregon medical license before start.
International applicants
OHSU has paused recruitment for applicants who would require a new H-1B visa; those with a current H-1B or no visa need (e.g. green card holders) may apply.
Visa support
New H-1B sponsorship paused; current H-1B holders accepted.
Licence requirement
Unrestricted Oregon medical license before start.
