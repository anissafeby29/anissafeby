Overview

Exposure to interventional and diagnostic neuroradiology procedures performed under supervision and current developments in neuroradiology.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Recognised specialist degree in Diagnostic Radiology. General: MBBS or equivalent; national licensing exam passed; 12 months housemanship; registered and in good standing at home; active clinical practice; 3 years post-housemanship experience or postgraduate diploma/degree; sponsored by government, regional health authority or home institution; 2 recommendation letters; SMC English requirements
International applicants
Yes - NNI assists overseas fellows to obtain SMC temporary registration and Training Employment Pass
Visa support
NNI assists with Training Employment Pass (TEP) from the Ministry of Manpower after SMC approval
Licence requirement
Temporary medical registration with the Singapore Medical Council; malpractice indemnity (MPS)

How to apply

Deadline
At least 9 months before targeted commencement date
Application method
Submit NNI application form, CV, 2 recommendation letters and letter of support from employer via the NNI FormSG link

Funding

Funding
Self-funded; NNI processing fee S$545 (incl. GST) after acceptance; all expenses borne by applicant
