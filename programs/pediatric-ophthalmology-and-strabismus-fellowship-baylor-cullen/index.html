Overview

One-year clinical fellowship at Texas Children's Hospital with about 1,500 surgical procedures a year and a required research project.

Focus

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed 36 months of ACGME-accredited US ophthalmology residency (or RCPSC-approved Canadian/equivalent programme) by appointment; Texas medical licence required before the July start
International applicants
Institution policyYes - IMGs welcomed; ECFMG certification required (except Canadian graduates); must secure appropriate visa and TMB permit
Visa support
Institution policyVisa types sponsored vary by department; all IMGs must secure an appropriate visa and Texas Medical Board permit; non-US citizens verify status with BCM International Services
Licence requirement
Institution policyTexas Medical Board Physician-in-Training permit or full Texas medical licence required
