Overview

An intensive 12-month clinical fellowship in body MRI (chest, cardiac, vascular, abdomen, pelvis, prostate and MSK) in a section interpreting over 7,000 body MRIs a year.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Board-certified or board-qualified in diagnostic radiology
International applicants
IMGs may apply for the 4-year Alternate Pathway multi-fellowship plan toward ABR certification
Visa support
Institution policyJohns Hopkins SOM sponsors incoming residents/fellows for J-1 (ECFMG); H-1B only in limited cases (US MD graduates on OPT in ACGME training, or trainees already on H-1B in ACGME-accredited/recognised training with sufficient H-1B time remaining) - GME policy P&F028
Licence requirement
Must be eligible for a Maryland medical licence

How to apply

Deadline
Applications for 2027-2028 opened 1 November 2025; virtual interviews 12 January - 31 March 2026 or until positions filled
Application method
Application form, CV, personal statement and 3 letters (incl. programme director) by email/fax to RadCSFellowship@jhmi.edu

Funding

Funding
Competitive annual salary plus educational funds
