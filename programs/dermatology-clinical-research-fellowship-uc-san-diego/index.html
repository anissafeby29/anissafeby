Overview

Eligibility
Page states fellows must have passed USMLE Steps 1, 2 and 3 and completed PGY1 at an ACGME-accredited program (page refers to a 2021 start; check current cycle)
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs eligible via J-1 (ECFMG) or permanent residence; H-1B only by exception
Visa support
Institution policyNon-US citizens may train holding permanent residence or J-1 (ECFMG); H visas not used except by programme-director exception for applicants already in the US on a valid H visa

Institution policyAppointment contingent on compliance with California medical licensure regulations (Postgraduate Training Licence)
