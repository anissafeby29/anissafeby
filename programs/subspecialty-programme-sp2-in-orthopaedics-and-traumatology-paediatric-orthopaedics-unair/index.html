Overview

FK UNAIR / RSUD Dr. Soetomo orthopaedic subspecialist (Sp2) training in paediatric orthopaedics for Indonesian orthopaedic surgeons.

The programme

Training type
Subspecialty training programme
Duration
Not stated on official page
Start
Two intakes a year: odd (gasal) and even (genap) semester; next intake even semester 2026/2027 (selection results 16 December 2026)
Positions
Orthopaedics Sp2 capacity: 12 (odd semester) and 12 (even semester) across five interests, 2026/2027
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Orthopaedics and Traumatology (Program Studi Subspesialis Orthopaedi dan Traumatologi)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Indonesian specialist in orthopaedics and traumatology (Sp.OT) (domestic or foreign graduate; foreign-trained specialists need an adaptation certificate from a collegium-recognised institution); legalised specialist diploma; valid specialist STR; collegium recommendation; employer consent if employed; signed statement to comply with RSUD Dr. Soetomo/FK UNAIR rules; family consent. Maximum age 45 at programme start (1 January for even-semester, 1 July for odd-semester entry); ELPT/TOEFL > 500; Orthopaedics and Traumatology Collegium recommendation; health certificate; BPJS card; at least 2 years of specialist practice (1 year for university/teaching-hospital educators); RSUD Dr. Soetomo practice permit during training; IDI statement of no malpractice; mental-health test package (psychotest, MMPI, interview) by the RSUD Dr. Soetomo psychiatry department; maximum 2 exam attempts.
International applicants
Indonesian citizens only (Warga Negara Indonesia); Indonesian specialists who trained abroad are eligible with an adaptation certificate
Visa support
Not applicable (Indonesian citizens only)
Licence requirement
Valid Indonesian specialist STR (at least 6 months before expiry); RSUD Dr. Soetomo practice permit (SIP) during training where stated

How to apply

Deadline
Even semester 2026/2027: online registration 1 September - 3 November 2026 (19:00 WIB); TPA and English test 7 November 2026 on Campus C. Odd semester 2026/2027 registration ran 1 April - 22 June 2026
Application method
Online registration via PPMB Universitas Airlangga (ppmb.unair.ac.id), selection fee, TPA and English test, then programme subject exam, health test/MMPI and interview; hard-copy documents for stage-1 passers

Funding

Funding
Self-funded or sponsored; fees 2026/2027: UKT Rp 25,000,000 per semester plus one-off IPI Rp 40,000,000. Ministry of Health study-assignment candidates need a letter confirming MoH/health office covers UKT and IPI
