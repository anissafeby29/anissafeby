Overview

One-year cardiac intensive care fellowship training fellows since 1998, with six months on the cardiac ICU service and tailored rotations depending on cardiology or critical care background.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Board-certified in pediatrics (ABP) and board-certified/eligible in pediatric cardiology or pediatric critical care medicine
International applicants
IMGs welcome; must hold valid ECFMG certificate and documentation of US citizenship, green card or J-1 visa per McGaw requirements
Visa support
J-1 per McGaw requirements; programme does not sponsor H-1B visas
Licence requirement
Must meet eligibility requirements for a temporary Illinois medical license
