Overview

A two-year clinical/research fellowship in pediatric and adolescent gynecology with additional focus on research and minimally invasive surgery.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Graduated from an accredited Canadian ObGyn program (Royal College of Canada) or hold a valid ECFMG medical licence and be allowed to work in Alberta as a Qualified Educational Surgical Assistant
International applicants
Possible for ECFMG-certified applicants allowed to work in Alberta as a Qualified Educational Surgical Assistant
Visa support
Institution policyInternational fellows need an IRCC work permit valid for the whole fellowship; PGME supplies the LMIA-exemption number for the application and fellows cannot start (or enter Canada for training) without it
Licence requirement
Must be allowed to work in Alberta (e.g., as a Qualified Educational Surgical Assistant if not Royal College-trained)

How to apply

Deadline
July 7, 2025 for the 2026 application cycle (check for the current cycle)
Application method
Common application with required materials submitted to the program administrator (louise.gofton@ahs.ca)

Funding

Funding
Institution policyNo PGME funding; clinical fellows must be funded by a PGME-approved source (department/AHS/sponsoring government, hospital or university - no personal funds), with stipend at least equivalent to PGY1 under the Alberta Resident Physician Agreement
