Overview

One- or two-year EM administration fellowship with an optional fully funded Executive MBA/MS in Healthcare Leadership from Cornell's SC Johnson College of Business.

The programme

Focus
Administration & operations
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 or 2 years (2-year track with Executive MBA/MS)
Start
Institution policyNYP graduate staff appointment year is generally 1 July - 30 June
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Non-ACGME
Department
Department of Emergency Medicine, Weill Cornell Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
ABEM board-eligible or board-certified emergency physicians; 2-year track requires admission to the Cornell Executive MBA/MS programme
International applicants
Institution policyYes - foreign nationals accepted if they secure ECFMG J-1 status (or hold work authorisation for the programme duration); IMGs need a valid ECFMG certificate or full NY licence
Visa support
Institution policyNYPH GME generally accepts foreign nationals only on ECFMG-sponsored J-1 status or with independent work authorisation; no H-1B, H-1B1, E-3 or other sponsorship; O-1 supported only in very limited cases at the candidate's expense
Licence requirement
Not stated on official page

How to apply

Deadline
20 August (applications open annually in January)
Application method
Email letter of interest, 2 letters of recommendation and CV to HLMFellowship@med.cornell.edu

Funding

Funding
Annual compensation at current PGY salary; full EMBA/MS tuition sponsored by the department (2-year track)
