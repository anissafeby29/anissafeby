Advanced Heart Failure Fellowship

Libin Cardiovascular Institute, University of Calgary (Foothills Medical Centre)

CardiologyAdvanced heart failure & transplantClinical Fellowship
Destination
CAN
Calgary, Alberta, Canada
Open official programme page ↗

Overview

A one-year fellowship covering advanced HF, temporary/durable MCS (HeartMate 3 implanting centre), transplant follow-up and optional electives at the Mazankowski Heart Institute.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (generally 1 July–30 June; flexible)
Start
Generally 1 July
Positions
1 per academic year
Accreditation
University of Calgary clinical fellowship (non-Royal College unless stated)
Department
Department of Cardiac Sciences, Cumming School of Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed accredited residency programmes in internal medicine and cardiology; in clinical medicine within 12 months of start; central venous access experience expected
International applicants
Yes – PGME issues letter of offer for work permit application; out-of-country applicants must complete ECFMG EICS credential verification and meet CPSA English requirements
Visa support
Letter of offer provided to support work permit application
Licence requirement
Educational licence through the College of Physicians & Surgeons of Alberta (CPSA) arranged via PGME after acceptance
