Overview

Clinical and research stroke fellowships at UHN aligned with the Canadian Stroke Consortium National Stroke Fellowship, including stroke-in-young-adults and CNS vasculitis clinics.

The programme

Focus
Stroke / vascular neurology
Training type
Clinical and Research Fellowship
Duration
1 year (Stroke Expert) or 2 years (Stroke Scholar)
Start
July 1
Positions
2 (dependent on funding)
Accreditation
University of Toronto Post MD Education-registered clinical fellowship (not RCPSC-accredited)
Department
Division of Neurology, University of Toronto

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Neurology or general internal medicine certification
International applicants
Yes — sponsored trainees welcome
Visa support
Foreign national (visa) fellows must be remunerated at a rate commensurate with Canadian fellows; work permit processed via U of T Post MD Education (no specific sponsorship statement on page)
Licence requirement
CPSO independent or educational licence (certificate authorising postgraduate education) required

Funding
Department standard: fellows remunerated at least at PARO PGY-3 level (CA$85,564.01 from July 1, 2025); funding via hospital site or sponsoring agency
