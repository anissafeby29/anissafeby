Overview

A one-year ACGME-accredited fellowship with clinical sites spanning an inpatient hospice unit, a safety-net hospital, an academic medical center, long-term care and home-based hospice care.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed residency in child neurology, family medicine, internal medicine, pediatrics, Med/Peds, PM&R, neurology or radiation oncology; or at least three clinical years of anesthesiology, emergency medicine, OB/GYN, psychiatry, radiology or surgery residency.
