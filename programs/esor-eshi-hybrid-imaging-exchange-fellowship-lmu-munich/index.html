ESOR/ESHI Exchange Programme for Hybrid Imaging Fellowship (Munich)

LMU University Hospital Munich (Klinikum der Universität München)

RadiologyNuclear medicineExchange Fellowship
Destination
DEU
Munich, Bavaria, Germany
Open official programme page ↗

Overview

A three-month structured, mentored exchange giving intense modular training in PET/CT, SPECT/CT and PET/MR, including tracer production and image data processing, at LMU Munich.

The programme

Training type
Exchange Fellowship
Duration
3 months
Start
Second half of the year (to be completed by end of 2026)
Positions
One programme offered in 2026 (centres: Munich or Cambridge)
Accreditation
Certificate from ESR/ESOR and ESHI on receipt of a written report.
Department
Hybrid imaging (PET/CT, SPECT/CT, PET/MR) – host training centre

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Radiologists within the first three to four years after certification wishing to subspecialise; knowledge of current clinical practice in cross-sectional imaging; fluent in English; active ESR and ESHI memberships.
International applicants
Open to ESR/ESHI members; ESOR exchange programmes require training outside the applicant's country of residence/work.

How to apply

Deadline
Not stated on official page
Application method
Via the MyESR portal ('Exchange Programmes for Fellowships'): single PDF with motivation letter, ESOR-format CV, board certification (no more than 4 years old) and at least two recommendation letters; choose preferred training centre.

Funding

Funding
€3,500 grant (ESHI and ESR/ESOR) on completion, towards travel and accommodation.
