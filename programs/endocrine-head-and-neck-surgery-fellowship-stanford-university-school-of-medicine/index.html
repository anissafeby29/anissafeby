Overview

Endocrine-focused head and neck surgery fellowship (HNES) giving advanced training in comprehensive thyroid and parathyroid management, coordinated with Stanford's head and neck oncologic surgery fellowship.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Board eligible for the American Board of Otolaryngology–HNS, American Board of Surgery or American Board of Plastic Surgery (or Canadian equivalent); International Track applicants as per AHNS rules
International applicants
AHNS Endocrine fellowships: International Track requires a non-ACGME/non-RCPSC residency in otolaryngology or general surgery plus ECFMG certification
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 (ECFMG) is the primary visa; H-1B permitted only in some circumstances with GME approval; O-1 rarely, varies by department
Licence requirement
Institution policyMedical Board of California Postgraduate Training License required within 180 days of starting an ACGME programme (USMLE Steps 1 & 2 CK passed); non-ACGME programmes require a full California licence by the start date

How to apply

Deadline
15 March 2027 (AHNS match, July 2028 start)
Application method
AHNS central fellowship match (Advanced Training Council); for July 2028 start: applications 21 Dec 2026 – 15 Mar 2027, interviews Mar–Jun 2027, results 9 Jul 2027; AHNS membership (or pending application) required
