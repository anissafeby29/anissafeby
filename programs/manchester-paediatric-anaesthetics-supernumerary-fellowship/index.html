Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Postgraduate anaesthesia qualification and at least five years of postgraduate anaesthesia experience.
International applicants
Yes - programme designed for international clinicians
Visa support
Via approved sponsorship routes only; conditional offer letter provided to support visa; self-funded visas outside agreed schemes excluded
Licence requirement
GMC registration required (academy can support sponsorship); IELTS 7.5 (7.0 each) or OET B; no previous PLAB failure
