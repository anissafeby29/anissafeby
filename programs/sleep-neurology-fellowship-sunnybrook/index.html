Sleep Neurology Fellowship

Sunnybrook Health Sciences Centre / University of Toronto

NeurologySleep medicineClinical Fellowship
Destination
CAN
Toronto, Ontario, Canada
Open official programme page ↗Compare saved →

Overview

Comprehensive training in adult sleep neurology with broad exposure to neurological and non-neurological sleep disorders at Sunnybrook.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (optional 1–2 research years)
Start
July 1 or September 1
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
University of Toronto Post MD Education-registered clinical fellowship (not RCPSC-accredited)
Department
Division of Neurology, Sunnybrook / University of Toronto

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed accredited training in neurology, internal medicine or psychiatry; eligible for CPSO postgraduate certificate
International applicants
Yes — U of T Department of Medicine fellowships include IMGs who obtain CPSO educational licences
Visa support
Foreign national (visa) fellows must be remunerated at a rate commensurate with Canadian fellows; work permit processed via U of T Post MD Education (no specific sponsorship statement on page)
Licence requirement
CPSO independent or educational licence (certificate authorising postgraduate education) required

How to apply

Deadline
Applications July 1 to October 31
Application method
Send CV and cover letter (interest, clinical and research experience) to the fellowship contact; approved candidates then register via U of T PGME (OATS for non-sponsored fellows)

Funding

Funding
Department standard: fellows remunerated at least at PARO PGY-3 level (CA$85,564.01 from July 1, 2025); funding via hospital site or sponsoring agency
