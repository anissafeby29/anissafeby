Overview

SNEC fellowship listed under overseas fellowships, aimed (per SNEC) at training regional ophthalmologists in general ophthalmology and commonly seen conditions.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (international fellowship is a year-long programme)
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
SNEC certificate of successful completion (assessment submitted to SMC)
Department
SNEC Education - Overseas Fellowship

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
SMC temporary registration requirements; higher degree/postgraduate diploma in ophthalmology, preferably >=3 years ophthalmic experience; SMC English requirements; home-country approval for overseas training
International applicants
Yes - overseas fellowship programme
Visa support
Training Employment Pass from Ministry of Manpower required (SGD 360)
Licence requirement
Singapore Medical Council temporary registration (application SGD 300, practising certificate SGD 450)
