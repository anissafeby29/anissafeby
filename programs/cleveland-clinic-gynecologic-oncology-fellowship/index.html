Overview

Cleveland Clinic's Cleveland programme appears in the Society of Gynecologic Oncology's national participating-program list for advanced multidisciplinary training in gynaecologic cancer care.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years (protected 12-month research year first, then 2 clinical years)
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Rotation of 1-2-1 categorical positions per year (filled through NRMP)
Accreditation
Listed by the Society of Gynecologic Oncology; confirm current ACGME status
Department
Ob/Gyn & Women's Health Institute, Cleveland Clinic

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completing a 4-year ACGME-accredited OB/GYN residency (US) or 5-year RCPSC-accredited OB/GYN residency (Canada); eligible for ABOG written exam; USMLE Steps 1-2; 3rd-year CREOG scores
International applicants
International applicants invited to apply through ERAS/NRMP; US clinical experience preferred but not required; USMLE Steps 1-2, CREOG and ECFMG requirements apply (residency must be US ACGME or Canadian RCPSC)
Visa support
Cleveland Clinic GME supports H-1B and J-1 (ECFMG-sponsored); no permanent-residence sponsorship for trainees
Licence requirement
Not stated on official page

How to apply

Deadline
1 April of the preceding academic year (per SGO common application dates); interviews generally July or August
Application method
ERAS (CV, personal statement, MSPE, transcript, USMLE 1-2, CREOG via ERAS, 3 letters incl. PD SLOE and Gyn Onc SLOE); NRMP match

Funding

Funding
Paid: Cleveland Clinic trainee salary scale effective 16 May 2026 (grad level 5 $80,608; level 6 $84,180; level 7 $89,204)
