Overview

A one-year ACGME dermatopathology fellowship with six months of complementary pathology or clinical dermatology training and a 100% dermatopathology board pass rate.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
2 per year
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited
Department
Dr. Phillip Frost Department of Dermatology and Cutaneous Surgery

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed ACGME-accredited dermatology or pathology residency; USMLE Steps 1–3 before start
International applicants
Non-US citizens/permanent residents train on the ECFMG-sponsored J-1 (alien physician) visa, the standard visa for Jackson residents/fellows
Visa support
ECFMG-sponsored J-1 is the standard visa for non-US-citizen fellows
Licence requirement
Not stated on official page

How to apply

Deadline
15 October of the year before start; interviews January–April
Application method
CAP standardized pathology fellowship application plus three references, mailed to department; no match
