Overview

Grant supporting early-career gynaecological oncologists to complete a 1–2-month fellowship at an ESGO-accredited Training Centre abroad.

The programme

Training type
Travelling Fellowship (grant)
Duration
1–2 months (4–8 weeks)
Start
February – December 2027
Positions
Varies each year depending on ESGO Council budget
Accreditation
ESGO
Department
Not stated on official page

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Invitation from an ESGO-accredited Training Centre; active ESGO membership; junior position at home institution for up to 5 years; speaks the host language or English; not previously funded; commits to return to home country
International applicants
Yes – designed for fellows training outside their country of origin or residence; ESGO members only
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Language of host institution or English

How to apply

Deadline
31 December 2026 (applications opened 1 September 2026)
Application method
Online application with CV, purpose statement, rationale for host centre, letters of support from home and host institutions, and time plan

Funding

Funding
Usually €1,500 for 1 month or €2,000 for 2 months (final €500 paid after report)
