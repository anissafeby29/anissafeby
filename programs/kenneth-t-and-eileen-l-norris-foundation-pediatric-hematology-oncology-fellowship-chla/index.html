Overview

An ACGME-accredited pediatric hematology-oncology fellowship in the largest program of its kind in the western US, with advanced training options in transplantation, coagulation, neuro-oncology, AYA oncology and survivorship.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship

3 years (1 clinical year + 2 research years; optional additional research years)

Institution policyCHLA physician-in-training agreements offer clinical fellow positions beginning 1 July

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed ACGME- or RCPSC-accredited pediatric residency; USMLE Steps 1-3 passed; ECFMG certificate for international graduates; California medical licence
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs may train on CHLA-supported J-1 (ECFMG) visas in ACGME-accredited and ACGME Non-Standard programmes; individual programmes may restrict eligibility to US citizens/permanent residents
