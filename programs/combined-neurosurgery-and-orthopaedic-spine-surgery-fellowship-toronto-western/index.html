Overview

A combined orthopaedic and neurosurgical spine fellowship offering subspecialty clinical training in spinal surgery, with two research projects expected per year.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year clinical (usually July to June); 2-year clinical/research available on request
Start
July
Positions
Up to 5 per year
Accreditation
SNS-CAST accredited
Department
Divisions of Neurosurgery and Orthopaedic Surgery, University of Toronto

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Graduates of accredited orthopaedic or neurosurgical residency programmes
International applicants
Institution policyYes - international applicants who meet CPSO clinical-fellow licensure requirements contact the Fellowship Program Director; applicants from countries with training agreements apply via their sponsoring agency to the PGME Office
Visa support
Institution policyinternational clinical fellows need an IRCC work permit; the PGME Office issues an LMIA-exempt offer of employment number and supports the work permit application
Licence requirement
Institution policyclinical fellows must hold a CPSO certificate of registration authorising postgraduate education (educational licence); research fellows do not need CPSO registration

How to apply

Deadline
Not stated on official page
Application method
Fillable fellowship application form sent to SpineFellowship.Application@uhn.ca

Funding

Funding
Institution policyclinical fellows must be funded (department or sponsoring agency); FEAC recommends pay at least equal to PGY1 on the PARO salary scale, and foreign nationals must be paid at a rate commensurate with a Canadian in the same role
