Overview

Established in 2015, a 12–24-month clinical and research fellowship at Vancouver General, St Paul's and UBC Hospitals training academic head and neck surgical oncologists in ablation and complex microvascular reconstruction (90–130 free flaps per fellow-year) with virtual surgical planning research.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of an accredited otolaryngology–head and neck surgery residency; TOEFL or equivalent if required by CPSBC; eligibility for CPSBC licensure
International applicants
Graduates have gone on to careers in Canada, Europe and the Middle East; non-Canadian applicants must meet CPSBC licensing and English requirements
Visa support
Institution policy (UBC PGME): foreign trainees must hold an IRCC work permit for the whole training period; the sponsoring department/PGME submits an offer of employment to IRCC and the fellow applies under the LMIA-exempt category
Licence requirement
Eligibility for licensure with the College of Physicians and Surgeons of British Columbia
