Overview

A one-year AST/ASN-accredited clinical fellowship at Vancouver General and St Paul's Hospitals covering all aspects of kidney and kidney-pancreas transplant care in a program performing over 300 transplants per year.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year

Accreditation
