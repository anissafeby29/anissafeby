Anesthesiology Critical Care Medicine Fellowship

Destination
USA

Open official programme page ↗

Overview

Fellows gain ICU experience at the main campus and community hospitals, including surgical, cardiovascular, medical and neurological critical care.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year; a separate two-year emergency-medicine track is described
Start
1 August (e.g. appointments beginning 1 August 2027; 1 July if required for military service or J-1 visa holders)
Positions
10 per year (graduates 10 fellows annually)
Accreditation
ACGME
Department
Department of Anesthesiology, Cleveland Clinic

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Standard pathway lists ACGME- or RCPSC-accredited anesthesiology/surgical residency, USMLE steps 1–3 and Ohio licence eligibility; emergency-medicine applicants should review their specific track.
International applicants
Considered if residency completed in an ACGME- or RCPSC-accredited anesthesiology/surgical programme; ECFMG certificate (if applicable) and USMLE Steps 1-3 required
Visa support
J-1 holders accommodated (July 1 start); Cleveland Clinic GME supports H-1B and ECFMG-sponsored J-1
Licence requirement
Eligible for a temporary unrestricted Ohio medical licence.

How to apply

Deadline
Not stated on official page
Application method
SF Match (CV, personal statement, USMLE and in-training scores, ECFMG certificate if applicable, 3 letters incl. Program Director); sequential ACCM+ACTA applicants apply to both programmes via SF Match

Funding

Funding
Paid: Cleveland Clinic trainee salary scale effective 16 May 2026 (e.g. grad level 5 $80,608); ACCM-EM year 2 is clinically funded as part-time attending at MetroHealth
