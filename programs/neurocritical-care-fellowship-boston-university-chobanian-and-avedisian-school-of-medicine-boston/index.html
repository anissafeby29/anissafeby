Overview

BU/Boston Medical Center neurocritical care fellowship at New England's largest safety-net hospital and highest-volume level one trauma centre, training up to four fellows a year in one- and two-year programmes.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 or 2 years, customised to training background (2-year includes 8 months elective; 1-year includes 2 months)
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Up to 4 fellows per year
Accreditation
UCNS-listed Neurocritical Care programme (Boston Medical Center, UCNS Fellowship Directory)
Department
Department of Neurology, Neurocritical Care

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
