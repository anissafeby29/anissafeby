Trauma Anaesthesia Fellowship

Sunnybrook Health Sciences Centre

AnaesthesiologyTrauma & burns anaesthesiaClinical Fellowship
Destination
CAN
Toronto, Ontario, Canada
Open official programme page ↗

Overview

Develops expertise in managing trauma during initial triage, intra- and post-operative care, including training in the Trauma Team Leader role at the largest trauma centre in Canada.

The programme

Focus
Trauma anaesthesia
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
12 months (shorter or longer may be pursued under certain circumstances)
Start
July
Positions
1 per year
Accreditation
University of Toronto PGME-registered fellowship; does not count towards Royal College of Physicians and Surgeons of Canada specialty certification
Department
Department of Anesthesia, Sunnybrook / University of Toronto Department of Anesthesiology and Pain Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Minimum 30 months of clinical anaesthesia training and all requisite certification exams passed in home country; preference for training completed within past seven years; fluency in English mandatory
International applicants
Yes — open to international graduates; applicants whose first language is not English must pass TOEFL/TSE or IELTS
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Temporary CPSO licence on acceptance, then educational licence after a mandated 1–3 month assessment period; fellow must procure malpractice insurance

How to apply

Deadline
Apply at least 15 months in advance; online forms open for July 2027, July 2028 and July 2029 intakes
Application method
Online application form via University of Toronto Department of Anesthesiology and Pain Medicine website (one intake period per application) with letter of intent and signed reference letters
