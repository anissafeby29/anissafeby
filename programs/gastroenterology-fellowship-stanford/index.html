Overview

Broad clinical and procedural training in gastroenterology, hepatology and endoscopy across Stanford, VA Palo Alto and Santa Clara Valley Medical Center.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Medical degree and ACGME-accredited internal medicine residency completed by enrolment; good standing; able to obtain a California medical licence
International applicants
Foreign medical graduates must hold a valid ECFMG certificate; visa policy per Stanford GME
Visa support
Stanford GME permits J-1 (ECFMG; most common) and H-1B (varies by department); IMGs must hold an approved visa and California licensure; check with programme which visas it permits
Licence requirement
Must be able to obtain a California medical licence

How to apply

Deadline
15 July 2026 (ERAS); virtual interviews September-October; NRMP Match
Application method
ERAS plus programme supplemental form; NRMP (single track: Stanford Gastroenterology Research 1820144F2)

Funding

Funding
Paid - Stanford Health Care GME salary by PGY level (2026-27 scale: PGY-4 $113,276.80; PGY-5 $119,558.40; PGY-6 $124,280.00; base includes housing component) plus allowances
