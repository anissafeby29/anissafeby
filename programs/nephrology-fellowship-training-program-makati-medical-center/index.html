Overview

The MakatiMed Section of Nephrology accepts applicants for its fellowship training programme, with interviews and immersion and no entrance examination.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Application, data privacy and consent forms; transcript of records; PRC board examination results; medical degree diploma; certificate of specialty board or good standing; certificate of residency or fellowship training; diplomate/fellow certificate if applicable; recommendation letter (within 6 months); PHP500 processing fee; no entrance examination

How to apply

Deadline
Application deadline 8 March 2026; interviews 9 and 16 March 2026; immersion starts 18 March 2026 (2026 cycle)
Application method
Accomplish the MakatiMed online/PDF application form and email it with requirements to Medical.Education@makatimed.net.ph (subject 'Application form - [fellowship program]'); screening by the department/section training committee (interview and/or written exam) followed by a two-week immersion
