Overview

A 1–12 month research fellowship with the SGH breast pathology team in slide reporting, research meetings and translational breast pathology research, without patient contact.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Basic medical degree; national licensing exam passed where applicable; 12 months' internship; registered and in good standing at home; minimum 3 years' relevant experience; postgraduate pathology diploma/degree preferred
International applicants
Yes — designed for foreign doctors; target audience includes pathologists from regional countries
Visa support
Training Employment Pass (MOM) application required
Licence requirement
No Singapore medical registration needed (no patient contact); Training Employment Pass from Ministry of Manpower
