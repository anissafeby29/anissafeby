Overview

A fellowship with an intensive focus on advanced techniques in deep brain stimulation, plus surgery for epilepsy and pain.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Must be board-eligible by the intended start date and be residents in neurosurgical training programmes in the United States; enfolded fellowships are not possible
International applicants
Non-US citizens may apply if they are residency-trained in the United States

Not stated on official page

How to apply

Deadline
31 March, two years before the starting year (e.g. 31 March 2026 for a 1 July 2028 start)
Application method
