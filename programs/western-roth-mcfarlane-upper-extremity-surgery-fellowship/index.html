Overview

Clinical and research fellowship covering all aspects of upper extremity surgery at Canada's largest upper extremity surgical unit.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
12 months (4 three-month rotations)
Start
1 August
Positions
Maximum 5 per year
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Division of Orthopaedic Surgery

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of approved residency in orthopaedic, plastic or general surgery
International applicants
Institution policyYes - international applicants with completed residency and specialty certification are eligible; sponsored-country citizens apply through their sponsoring agency, others apply directly to the programme
Visa support
Institution policyNon-Canadians need a closed, employer-specific IRCC work permit; Western PGME issues the supporting documentation after CPSO eligibility (allow at least 8 months; processing 4-50+ weeks)
Licence requirement
Institution policyMust be eligible for a CPSO certificate of registration authorizing postgraduate education (IMGs may first complete a Pre-Entry Assessment Program)

How to apply

Deadline
Selections made 15 April, about 14 months before start
Application method
Application form, degree copy, CV, transcript, personal statement, 3 letters; interview and site visit mandatory

Funding

Funding
Institution policyAll clinical fellowships must be salaried at minimum CAD 65,000/yr from an approved source: sponsoring government (Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, UAE, Yemen), department, or external institution; no personal/family funding; self-funding only for CPSO independent-practice-eligible fellows
