Overview

Training spans tertiary trauma care, ambulatory orthopedic surgery and pediatric pain, with additional exposure to addiction psychiatry and chronic pain.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Institution policyGraduate of LCME- or AOA-accredited US school, or IMG with valid ECFMG certificate/full Tennessee licence; prerequisite training must be ACGME, RCPSC/CFPC or ACGME-I accredited
International applicants
Institutional GME requirement: holders of a foreign medical degree need a valid ECFMG certificate; no programme-specific policy stated
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 via ECFMG; H-1B considered case by case by programme with GME; no new O-1 applications supported
Licence requirement
Tennessee medical licence required for moonlighting
