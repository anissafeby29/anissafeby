Overview

The Rady Children's/UC Irvine two-year ACGME-accredited pediatric rehabilitation medicine fellowship trains physiatrists across inpatient rehabilitation, multidisciplinary clinics, neonatal consultation, tone management, electrodiagnostics and motion analysis.
