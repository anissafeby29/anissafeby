Overview

High-volume arthroplasty fellowship across two Stanford services and a VA rotation with exposure to 600+ cases and two required research projects.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
1 August (programme year 1 August - 31 July; next intake 2028-2029)
Positions
3 per year
Accreditation
AAHKS-recognized (not ACGME)
Department
Department of Orthopaedic Surgery (Stanford Hospital and Palo Alto VA)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Board-eligible with completion of an ACGME-accredited orthopaedic residency; must obtain a California medical licence
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs welcome to apply; must hold a valid ECFMG certificate and be able to obtain Medical Board of California licensure; non-ACGME fellowships need ACGME Non-Standard Training recognition before J-1 sponsorship
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 (ECFMG) is the primary visa; H-1B permitted only in some circumstances with GME approval; O-1 rarely, varies by department
Licence requirement
Institution policyMedical Board of California Postgraduate Training License required within 180 days of starting an ACGME programme (USMLE Steps 1 & 2 CK passed); non-ACGME programmes require a full California licence by the start date
