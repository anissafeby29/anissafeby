Overview

PEM fellowship in one of the busiest pediatric EDs in the US (62 beds, over 90,000 visits a year; Level I Trauma Center), with a four-year combined Child Abuse/PEM option.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Graduates of pediatric or emergency medicine residencies (ACGME, RCPSC or CFPC-accredited); board eligible or certified in Pediatrics or Emergency Medicine
International applicants
Foreign medical graduates need ECFMG status report and J-1 visa
Visa support
J-1 visas are the only visas accepted
Licence requirement
Institution policyvalid Ohio Training Certificate and/or Ohio Medical License required before start (licensure fees covered by the programme)
