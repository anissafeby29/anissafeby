Overview

One-year Siriraj neuroanaesthesia fellowship covering neuromonitoring, neuroprotection and anaesthesia for neurosurgery and neurointerventions.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (8 months neurosurgical/hybrid OR plus anaesthesia, radiology, neuro-ICU and neurology rotations and 2 months elective)

Not stated on official page
Positions
