Movement Disorders Fellowship

Toronto Western Hospital (University Health Network) / University of Toronto

Open official programme page ↗Compare saved →

Overview

A well-established programme at Toronto Western's Edmond J. Safra Program providing extensive training in all aspects of clinical movement disorders including surgical therapies.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
2 years (occasionally 1 year)
Start
July 1
Positions
2–3 per year depending on funding
Accreditation
University of Toronto Post MD Education-registered clinical fellowship (not RCPSC-accredited)
Department
Division of Neurology, University of Toronto / Krembil Brain Institute, UHN

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Not stated on official page
International applicants
Yes — U of T Department of Medicine fellowships include IMGs who obtain CPSO educational licences
Visa support
Foreign national (visa) fellows must be remunerated at a rate commensurate with Canadian fellows; work permit processed via U of T Post MD Education (no specific sponsorship statement on page)
Licence requirement
CPSO independent or educational licence (certificate authorising postgraduate education) required

How to apply

Deadline
December 1, two years before start; decisions by spring of the year before start
Application method
Send CV initially to Dr Susan Fox

Funding

Funding
Department standard: fellows remunerated at least at PARO PGY-3 level (CA$85,564.01 from July 1, 2025); funding via hospital site or sponsoring agency
