Overview

ACGME-accredited diagnostic neuroradiology training covering adult and paediatric imaging, spine procedures, head and neck imaging, functional imaging and neuro-endovascular exposure.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year ACGME neuroradiology fellowship; primary pathway adds a second year as junior faculty clinical instructor
Start
2028 appointments for the current match cycle; exact start date not stated
Positions
10 ACGME slots per year
Accreditation
ACGME

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed ACGME-accredited diagnostic radiology residency; licensing requirements apply
International applicants
Limited - foreign medical graduates need an appropriate visa, usually UCSF-sponsored ECFMG J-1; ACGME diagnostic radiology residency required
Visa support
J-1 (usually UCSF-sponsored ECFMG J-1)
Licence requirement
Radiology board certification and California medical licence required prior to appointment

How to apply

Deadline
ERAS/NRMP: application review and virtual interviews begin 2 December 2026; 2027 Neuroradiology Fellowship Match (for 2028 appointments) opens 10 February 2027, match day 5 May 2027
Application method
ERAS and NRMP Neuroradiology Fellowship Match (no external candidates outside the match)
