Overview

Clinical and research training in stem cell transplantation and advanced cellular therapies in a programme established in the early 1990s (57 graduates since 2012).

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
Not stated on official page
Start
1 July
Positions
3-8 per year
Accreditation
Approved by the Texas Medical Board (non-ACGME)
Department
Department of Stem Cell Transplantation and Cellular Therapy

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Board-certified or eligible in medical oncology and/or hematology (exceptions case by case); MD or equivalent; US citizen, permanent resident or J-1 visa
International applicants
IMGs eligible with valid ECFMG certificate and USMLE Step 1 and Step 2 CK (each passed within 3 attempts) (MD Anderson GME eligibility page)
Visa support
J-1 only; MD Anderson does not offer H-1B visas for GME trainees (programme page)
Licence requirement
Texas Medical Board physician-in-training permit or Texas medical licence

How to apply

Deadline
Applications for 2028-2029 open November 2026
Application method
Programme application with CV, personal statement, USMLE/COMLEX transcripts, three letters, credential evaluation and ECFMG certificate for foreign graduates
