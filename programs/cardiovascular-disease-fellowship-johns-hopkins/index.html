Overview

The Johns Hopkins Cardiovascular Disease Fellowship develops leaders and innovators in clinical cardiovascular medicine, discovery and dissemination, with flexible research pathways.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Not stated on official page
International applicants
Institution policyJohns Hopkins SOM accepts international residents/fellows via J-1 ECFMG Alien Physician Exchange Visitor sponsorship (applies to those joining from abroad or already in the US in another status); H-1B only in limited circumstances
Visa support
J-1 (ECFMG) and H-1B visas accepted (programme FAQ); AHFTC page states only J-1 sponsored
Licence requirement
Institution policy (Maryland Board of Physicians): residents and fellows train as registered unlicensed practitioners through their programme; a full Maryland licence requires 1 year (US/Canadian grads) or ECFMG + 2 years (IMGs) of accredited postgraduate training plus licensing exams
