Advanced Airway Management Fellowship

Toronto General Hospital (University Health Network)

AnaesthesiologyAirway managementClinical Fellowship
Destination
CAN
Toronto, Ontario, Canada
Open official programme page ↗Compare saved →

Overview

Focuses on acquiring expertise in airway evaluation and management, including care of patients undergoing complex head and neck surgery, so the fellow can serve as a resource in teaching and managing difficult airways.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
12 months
Start
Normally beginning of February and beginning of August each year
Positions
1 per year
Accreditation
University of Toronto PGME-registered fellowship; does not count towards Royal College of Physicians and Surgeons of Canada specialty certification
Department
Department of Anesthesia and Pain Management, UHN / University of Toronto Department of Anesthesiology and Pain Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed all required anaesthesia examinations, registered with the official regulatory authorities, and at least 5 years of anaesthesia training and experience at an academic centre (1 may be a rotational/internship year); 1–2 years as staff anaesthesiologist strongly advised; current ACLS certification required
International applicants
Yes — applicants trained in countries where English is not the primary language must demonstrate effective communication in English
Visa support
Institution policyinternational clinical fellows need an IRCC work permit; the PGME Office issues an LMIA-exempt offer of employment number and supports the work permit application
Licence requirement
Institution policyclinical fellows must hold a CPSO certificate of registration authorising postgraduate education (educational licence); research fellows do not need CPSO registration

How to apply

Deadline
Applications open 24 months and close 12 months before the intended start date; July 2028 application form currently open
Application method
Online application platform on the University of Toronto Department of Anesthesiology and Pain Medicine website; shortlisted candidates asked for references and interviewed

Funding

Funding
Institution policyclinical fellows must be funded (department or sponsoring agency); FEAC recommends pay at least equal to PGY1 on the PARO salary scale, and foreign nationals must be paid at a rate commensurate with a Canadian in the same role
