Transplantation, Hepatobiliary & Pancreatic Surgery Fellowship

Royal Victoria Hospital (MUHC), McGill University

General SurgeryTransplant surgeryClinical Fellowship
Destination
CAN
Montreal, Quebec, Canada
Open official programme page ↗Compare saved →

Overview

Two-year fellowship: a junior clinical research year (75% research) followed by a senior clinical year in liver, pancreas and kidney transplantation and HPB surgery.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
2 years
Start
Institution policyMcGill PGME academic year starts 1 July and ends 30 June
Positions
2 (1 per year)
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Department of Surgery, Division of General Surgery

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed an accredited general surgery residency and meet Faculty of Medicine fellowship eligibility
International applicants
Institution policyYes - foreign citizens may apply (max 3 years of training in Quebec); must be recognized specialists with proof of non-profit funding
Visa support
Institution policyNon-Canadian fellows need an LMIA-exempt employer-specific work permit (code C45), initiated by McGill PGME after the CMQ Letter of Eligibility; no CAQ/study permit
Licence requirement
Must meet Quebec postgraduate training eligibility (Collège des médecins du Québec training card)

How to apply

Deadline
Institution policyVia the Fellowship Council match (no direct applications to McGill)
Application method
Fellowship Council Match; no direct applications

Funding

Funding
Institution policyUnfunded and self-funded training not permitted; proof of non-profit sponsorship of at least the R-1 salary equivalent (~CAD 60,000/yr) plus tuition/student fees required unless programme offers internal funding
