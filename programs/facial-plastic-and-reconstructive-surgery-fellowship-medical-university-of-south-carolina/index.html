Overview

One-year academic fellowship with a wide breadth of facial plastic and reconstructive surgery across patients of all ages.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed an ACGME-accredited residency in otolaryngology or plastic surgery, or board-certified by ABOto or ABPS
International applicants
AAFPRS directory marks this programme as NOT accepting IMGs or Canadian applicants
Visa support
Institution policyEach MUSC residency/fellowship decides which visas it permits; J-1 (ECFMG) available, and H-1B only in programmes that sponsor it (granted in one-year increments; requires ECFMG certificate, USMLE Steps 1-3 and SC licence)
Licence requirement
Valid South Carolina medical licence

How to apply

Deadline
1 February (AAFPRS application database)
Application method
AAFPRS Foundation Fellowship Database (AAFPRS membership required; $225 fee covers 10 programmes) by 1 February; interviews by 31 May; matched via SF Match in early June
