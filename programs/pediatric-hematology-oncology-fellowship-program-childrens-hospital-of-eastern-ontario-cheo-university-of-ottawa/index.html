Pediatric Hematology/Oncology Fellowship Program

Children's Hospital of Eastern Ontario (CHEO) / University of Ottawa

PaediatricsHematology & oncologyClinical Fellowship
Destination
CAN
Ottawa, Ontario, Canada
Open official programme page ↗

Overview

Comprehensive clinical and academic fellowship in pediatric hematology/oncology at CHEO with a third year for research or focused training such as stem cell transplantation or neuro-oncology.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years (2 clinical years plus a research/focused training year)
Start
Institution policyJuly (following the 1 May-30 September application window)
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Fully accredited by the Royal College of Physicians and Surgeons of Canada
Department
Division of Hematology/Oncology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed three years of core pediatrics or internal medicine at an approved institution
International applicants
Institution policyYes - IMGs eligible for listed fellowship programmes; must be certified (RCPSC, CFPC or recognised international board) or residency-complete, meet English proficiency requirements and complete PEAP
Visa support
Institution policyinternational trainees need an IRCC work permit (LMIA-exempt); the uOttawa PGME/immigration advisor provides the documents needed to apply (CPSO letter of eligibility and LMIA-exemption number)
Licence requirement
Institution policyCPSO postgraduate education (educational) certificate of registration required, renewed annually; internationally trained clinical fellows must complete a 4-12 week Pre-Entry Assessment Program (PEAP); three-year licensure limit for clinical fellowships

How to apply

Deadline
Institution policyDocuments uploaded online between 1 May and 30 September for a start the following July
Application method
Institution policyOnline application via the uOttawa PGME fellowship portal (mail/email/fax not accepted)

Funding

Funding
Institution policyInternational fellows without Canadian status must be funded by an official home-country sponsor (government agency or health-related charity; private funds not accepted) unless fellowship funding is available through an Ottawa hospital
