Overview

A 12-month EUFAC-accredited fellowship training future leaders of emergency ultrasound in clinical care, education, quality assurance and research.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
12 months
Start
Institution policyEmory house staff appointments typically cover a 1 July - 30 June academic year (standard start 1 July)
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
EUFAC-accredited; graduates eligible for ABEM Focused Practice Designation in AEMUS
Department
Department of Emergency Medicine, Emory University School of Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Emergency medicine residency graduates (EM board eligible/certified for FPD eligibility)
International applicants
Institution policyInternational medical graduates eligible if ECFMG-certified and holding J-1 (ECFMG) or H-1B status, or US citizen/permanent resident
Visa support
Institution policyIMGs must be ECFMG-certified and enter training on a J-1 (ECFMG-sponsored) or H-1B visa unless US citizen/green-card holder (Emory GME housestaff policy 5.12)
Licence requirement
Institution policyGeorgia Temporary Postgraduate Training Permit or full Georgia medical licence required before clinical care (training permit max 7 years); USMLE Step 3 required for entry at PGY4+
