Overview

Two-year PCCM fellowship at CHEO, the lead pediatric trauma hospital for the Ottawa region, preparing trainees for the Royal College subspecialty exam and academic careers.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
2 years (possible third year for research/academic pursuits)
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Leads to eligibility for RCPSC subspecialty examination in Pediatric Critical Care
Department
Pediatric Intensive Care Unit, CHEO

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed at least three core years of pediatrics in an accredited Canadian or US programme, or certification in general surgery, emergency medicine, anesthesia or internal medicine with sufficient pediatric experience
International applicants
IMGs enrolled in Canadian pediatric residency programmes eligible; sponsored (foreign government) residents not eligible via CaRMS; other backgrounds considered case by case
Visa support
Institution policyinternational trainees need an IRCC work permit (LMIA-exempt); the uOttawa PGME/immigration advisor provides the documents needed to apply (CPSO letter of eligibility and LMIA-exemption number)
Licence requirement
Institution policyCPSO postgraduate education (educational) certificate of registration required, renewed annually; internationally trained clinical fellows must complete a 4-12 week Pre-Entry Assessment Program (PEAP); three-year licensure limit for clinical fellowships
