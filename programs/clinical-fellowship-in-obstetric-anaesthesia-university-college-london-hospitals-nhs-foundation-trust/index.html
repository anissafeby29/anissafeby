Overview

One of UCLH's 12-month expert-supervised clinical fellowships, with the majority of clinical sessions in obstetric anaesthesia.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
12 months (fixed term)
Start
August (e.g. 5 August 2026 start advertised)
Positions
Typically one post per subspecialty
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Department of Anaesthesia and Perioperative Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
MBBS; Primary FRCA; eligible for full GMC registration with licence to practise at appointment; experience in high-risk anaesthesia and obstetrics; able to perform neuraxial techniques
International applicants
Yes — applicants requiring Skilled Worker visa sponsorship considered
Visa support
Skilled Worker sponsorship applicants welcome and considered alongside all other applications (per NHS Jobs advert)
Licence requirement
Full GMC registration with licence to practise at time of appointment

How to apply
