Overview

An ACGME-accredited fellowship in advanced heart failure, cardiac transplantation and mechanical circulatory support with multidisciplinary inpatient, outpatient and research experience.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
1 July (academic calendar July 1 - June 30)
Positions
3 per year
Accreditation
ACGME (accredited since July 2013); graduates eligible for ABIM Advanced Heart Failure and Transplant Cardiology certification
Department
Division of Cardiovascular Medicine, Stanford School of Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed ACGME-accredited cardiovascular disease fellowship; passed USMLE Steps 1-3; ABIM board certified/eligible in general cardiology; ECFMG certificate for foreign graduates
International applicants
IMGs accepted with valid ECFMG certificate; J-1 visa only
Visa support
J-1 only
Licence requirement
California medical licence must be obtained

How to apply

Deadline
Apply through ERAS; virtual interviews held September-October (dates TBD); no fixed deadline posted
Application method
ERAS (CV, one-page statement, USMLE transcript, medical school transcript, 3 letters incl. one from cardiology PD or chief)

Funding

Funding
Paid GME position on the Stanford Health Care housestaff salary scale (e.g. PGY-7 30,540.80/yr on current posted scale)
