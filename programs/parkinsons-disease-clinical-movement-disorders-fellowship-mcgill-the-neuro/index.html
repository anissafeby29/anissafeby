Overview

Clinical movement disorders training at The Neuro covering Parkinson's disease and other movement disorders.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (2-year clinical and clinical-research versions listed)
Start
Not stated on official page

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
RCPSC (or equivalent) certification in neurology or geriatric medicine
International applicants
Yes — McGill PGME accepts international medical graduates (Canadian, US and foreign citizens) for up to three years of fellowship training in Québec; government-sponsored trainees apply via contract route
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Training card / restricted permit from the Collège des médecins du Québec (CMQ)
