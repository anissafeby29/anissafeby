Uppsala University Hospital – Department of Oto-Rhino-Laryngology (CEORL-HNS Global Outreach)

Overview

Biennial €5,000 CEORL-HNS grant bringing a young ENT specialist from an invited low-resource country to the European ORL-HNS Congress and an observership (2026 host: Uppsala University Hospital).

The programme

Focus
Otorhinolaryngology – head and neck surgery

2–3 weeks including the CEORL-HNS Congress (2026: two-week observership in Uppsala)

Next edition announced at the 9th CEORL-HNS Congress, Berlin 2028
Positions
