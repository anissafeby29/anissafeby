Overview

One- or two-year postgraduate simulation fellowship training EM graduates as educators, scholars and patient safety advocates, with an optional master's in health professions education.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 or 2 years
Start
1 July
Positions
1–2 per year
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Ronald O. Perelman Department of Emergency Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed or completing an ACGME-accredited EM residency; meet NYU Grossman credentialing requirements
International applicants
Institution policyInternational graduates may train on an ECFMG-sponsored J-1 visa only (no H-1B); non-accredited fellowships require a NY licence or limited permit
Visa support
Institution policyNYU Langone does not offer H-1B sponsorship for residents or fellows; J-1 sponsorship available through ECFMG
Licence requirement
Institution policyFor NYU-sponsored ACGME/AOA programmes no NY licence is required by state law (resident exemption, Educ. Law 6526(1)), though individual programmes may require a NY licence or limited permit; trainees in non-accredited programmes MUST hold a NY licence or limited permit before starting; USMLE Step 3 required before final year in ACGME programmes

How to apply

Deadline
Applications accepted 1 July – 15 September of the year before start
Application method
Letter of intent, CV and two letters (one from simulation faculty or PD) to Emsimfellows@NYULangone.org

Funding

Funding
Competitive salary and benefits; academic appointment
