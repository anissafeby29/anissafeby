Overview

A classical (non-malignant) haematology track preparing physicians for clinical and research careers, sharing the first clinical year structure with the haematology/oncology track.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3+ years (1 clinical year followed by 2 or more research years)
Start
1 July
Positions
2 new fellows per year
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited
Department
Dana-Farber Cancer Institute, Brigham and Women's Hospital and Massachusetts General Hospital

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
MD or DO; completed internal medicine residency; passing USMLE Step 1 and 2; ECFMG certificate for IMGs
International applicants
Domestic and international medical graduates are eligible to apply (programme page)
Visa support
