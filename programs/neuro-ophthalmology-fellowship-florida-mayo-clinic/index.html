Eligibility
Completed accredited ophthalmology or neurology residency; ABO or ABPN certification eligibility
International applicants
International applicants must have equivalent training and have passed the ECFMG examination
Visa support
J-1 visas supported only for neurology residency graduates (not ophthalmology graduates at this time)

Active Florida medical licence before matriculation
