Systemic Autoimmune Rheumatic Disease (SARD) Fellowship

University of Toronto

Internal MedicineRheumatologyClinical and Research Fellowship
Destination
CAN
Toronto, Ontario, Canada
Open official programme page ↗

Overview

A 1-2 year fellowship giving advanced training in systemic autoimmune rheumatic diseases through longitudinal scleroderma, lupus and pre-clinical autoimmunity clinics plus scholarly projects.

The programme

Training type
Clinical and Research Fellowship
Duration
1-2 years
Start
January or July
Positions
1-2 depending on funding
Accreditation
University of Toronto clinical fellowship; does not lead to RCPSC certification (per UofT PGME fellowship page)
Department
Department of Medicine, Division of Rheumatology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed rheumatology training
International applicants
Non-Canadian applicants may apply; sponsored applicants from Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia and UAE apply via sponsoring agency to PGME (per UofT PGME fellowship page)
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
CPSO certificate authorizing postgraduate education (educational licence); applicants must be recognized as a specialist/family physician in the jurisdiction where they practised (per UofT PGME fellowship page)

How to apply

Funding

Funding
Division of Rheumatology does not provide fellowship funding; proof of funding required (per division page)
