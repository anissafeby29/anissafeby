Overview

A combined fellowship at the NIH Clinical Center with tracks through NCI (medical oncology, haematology/oncology, cancer prevention) and NHLBI (haematology or combined haematology/transfusion medicine).

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility

International applicants
Fellows may be sponsored on visas if exams/training are complete (programme page)
Visa support
Visa sponsorship possible (H-1B, J-1, O-1 or other); candidates should contact the coordinator early (programme page)

How to apply

Deadline
ERAS from June; deadline 1 August; invitations by mid-August; virtual interviews in September
Application method
ERAS (NIH Clinical Center Program, ACGME ID 1552314155); gold/silver signals preferred
