Overview

A one-year fellowship at RSUP Dr. Kariadi covering adult and paediatric cardiovascular anaesthesia, CPB, perioperative TEE, post-cardiac-surgery ICU care and anaesthesia in the cath lab and ER.

The programme

Focus

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Anaesthesiology and intensive therapy specialist (diploma endorsed by the university rector); STR issued by the Indonesian Medical Council (KKI); practice licence (SIP) as an anaesthesiologist from the local health office; administrative and academic selection
