Overview

UCLA's ACGME-accredited REI fellowship provides 18 months of clinical training in Westwood, Santa Monica and private practice settings plus 18 months of research culminating in a thesis.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years (18 months clinical, 18 months research)
Start
Institution policy (UCLA GME): appointments run 1 July to 30 June
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
ACGME accredited
Department
Department of Obstetrics & Gynecology, David Geffen School of Medicine at UCLA

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Not stated on official page
International applicants
Applicants with a foreign medical degree must include all educational transcripts, exam scores and ECFMG certificate
Visa support
Institution policy (UCLA GME, ACGME programmes): ECFMG-sponsored clinical J-1 is the preferred visa; H-1B considered only for US LCME/AOA graduates moving from F-1 OPT, trainees already in clinical training on H-1B with enough time left, or physicians of national/international renown (department-funded, case by case)
Licence requirement
Institution policy (UCLA GME): all residents/fellows must comply with Medical Board of California (or Osteopathic Board) licensure requirements - a PTL or full California licence - at appointment and reappointment; non-compliance means non-appointment; fellows from outside institutions must have passed all USMLE/COMLEX steps

How to apply

Deadline
Friday, May 1, 2026 (12PM CST)
Application method
ERAS (MSPE, transcripts and USMLE scores uploaded to ERAS); interviews mandatory

Funding

Funding
Institution policy (UCLA ACGME residents/fellows, CIR-SEIU contract): from 1 Sept 2026 PGY4 $108,102; PGY5 $111,894; PGY6 $115,477; PGY7 $118,801; PGY8 $124,242 (PGY1 $98,466); rising from 1 July 2027 (PGY4 $113,508); plus $8,000/yr Resident Enhancement Fund
