Overview

The largest paediatric GHN training programme in Canada, a three-year core fellowship (one clinical year and two research-focused years) training clinicians from around the world for academic careers.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years (1 clinical + 2 research years)
Start
1 July
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
RCPSC-accredited; recognised by the American Board of Pediatrics and the RACP
Department
Division of Gastroenterology, Hepatology and Nutrition

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Institution policymust be eligible for a CPSO postgraduate education certificate, including recognition as a medical specialist/family physician in the jurisdiction where the applicant practised before the fellowship
International applicants
International trainees accepted (SickKids salary support available competitively)
Visa support
Institution policyinternational clinical fellows need an IRCC work permit; the PGME Office issues an LMIA-exempt offer of employment number and supports the work permit application
Licence requirement
Institution policyclinical fellows must hold a CPSO certificate of registration authorising postgraduate education (educational licence); research fellows do not need CPSO registration

How to apply

Deadline
CV pre-screening 2 March - 1 July of the year before the intended start; invited full applications due 15 July
Application method
Institution policyapply to the departmental Fellowship Program Director; sponsored international applicants apply through their sponsoring agency to the PGME Office

Funding

Funding
Salary support on a competitive basis from Ontario MOHLTC (Canadian trainees) or SickKids (international trainees, first two years); third year via competitive sources
