Overview

A two-year medical and surgical retina fellowship for ophthalmology residency graduates, overseen by the McGaw Medical Center.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
2 years
Start
July
Positions
1 surgical (plus 1 medical retina) per year
Accreditation
AUPO FCC-compliant
Department
Department of Ophthalmology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
ABO board-eligible in ophthalmology; US citizen, permanent resident or appropriate visa status
International applicants
Applicants from outside the US must have passed USMLE and be able to secure an Illinois State Medical licence
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 (ECFMG) and H-1B; McGaw sponsors H-1B only for candidates of exceptional academic potential with prior approval before ranking/offer (cost borne by the programme)
Licence requirement
Institution policyIllinois (IDFPR) temporary medical licence required to train (processed by McGaw GME; allow ~60 days)

How to apply

Deadline
1 September for training beginning in July
Application method
Not stated on official page
