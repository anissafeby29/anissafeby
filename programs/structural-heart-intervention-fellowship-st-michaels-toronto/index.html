Structural Heart Intervention Fellowship

St. Michael's Hospital (Unity Health Toronto), University of Toronto

CardiologyInterventional & structural cardiologyClinical Fellowship
Destination
CAN
Toronto, Ontario, Canada
Open official programme page ↗Compare saved →

Overview

A one-year fellowship training fellows as primary operators for TAVI, TEER, LAAC, TMVR and other structural procedures within a multidisciplinary Structural Heart Team.

The programme

Focus
Structural heart disease
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
1 July or by agreement
Positions
1 per year
Accreditation
University of Toronto clinical fellowship (Certificate of Completion from PGME); does not lead to RCPSC certification
Department
Division of Cardiology, Department of Medicine, University of Toronto

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Recognised specialist (eligible for RCPSC/ABMS certification or recognised as specialist in home jurisdiction) with completed cardiology training
International applicants
Yes — international applicants meeting CPSO clinical fellow licensure requirements may apply (recognised specialists)
Visa support
Institution policyinternational clinical fellows need an IRCC work permit; the PGME Office issues an LMIA-exempt offer of employment number and supports the work permit application
Licence requirement
Must be eligible for a CPSO certificate authorizing postgraduate education (educational licence); IMGs complete a Pre-Entry Assessment Program

How to apply

Deadline
Not stated on official page
Application method
Contact the fellowship supervisor

Funding

Funding
Institution policyclinical fellows must be funded (department or sponsoring agency); FEAC recommends pay at least equal to PGY1 on the PARO salary scale, and foreign nationals must be paid at a rate commensurate with a Canadian in the same role
