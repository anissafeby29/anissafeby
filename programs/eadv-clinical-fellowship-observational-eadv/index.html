EADV Clinical Fellowship (Observational)

European Academy of Dermatology and Venereology (EADV) – European host dermatology centres

DermatologyMedical dermatologyObservership (clinical fellowship)
Destination

Overview

EADV-funded two-to-three-month observational fellowship in a high-standard European dermatology centre for residents and recently qualified dermatologists.

The programme

Focus
Dermato-venereology (any subspecialty)
Training type
Observership (clinical fellowship)
Duration
2 or 3 calendar months
Start
During the year after application
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
EADV
Department
Not stated on official page

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
EADV member; recently qualified dermatologist-venereologist or dermatology resident; under 40 on the day of application; signed agreement with a well-known European host centre
International applicants
Open to EADV members of any nationality (2025 awardees included applicants from Uzbekistan and Turkey); host must be a European centre
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Observational; documents in English

How to apply

Deadline
27 February 2026 (2026 call closed)
Application method
MyEADV portal: CV and motivation letter in English, observation programme description, signed host agreement and home head-of-department approval

Funding

Funding
Up to €9,000 (€6,000 for 2 months, €9,000 for 3 months) for travel, accommodation and insurance
