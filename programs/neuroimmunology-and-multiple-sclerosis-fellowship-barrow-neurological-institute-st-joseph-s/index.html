Overview

One-year fully funded Barrow fellowship providing mostly outpatient clinical and research training in neuroimmunology and MS, with no overnight or weekend call.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed an adult or pediatric neurology residency (ACGME-accredited); board certified or eligible
International applicants
Foreign medical graduates with equivalent qualifications eligible if they completed an ACGME-accredited residency and hold ECFMG certification
Visa support
J-1 visa only; the programme does not sponsor H-1B visas
Licence requirement
Current valid unrestricted licence in the US or Canada or its territories
