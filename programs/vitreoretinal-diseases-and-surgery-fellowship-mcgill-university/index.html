Overview

A two-year clinical fellowship in vitreoretinal diseases and surgery at McGill University.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed residency and Royal College or Board certification; recognised specialist; graduate of FAIMER-listed school
International applicants
International specialists accepted with non-profit scholarship funding
Visa support
Institution policyNon-Canadian fellows need an LMIA-exempt employer-specific work permit (code C45), initiated by McGill PGME after the CMQ Letter of Eligibility; no CAQ/study permit
Licence requirement
Must be eligible for a training card from the Collège des Médecins du Québec

How to apply

Deadline
Not stated on official page
Application method
SF Match; matched applicant then applies online to McGill PGME

Funding

Funding
Proof of non-profit funding required (minimum R-1 salary equivalent, ~$60,000/yr, plus tuition); self-funding refused; Pediatrics bursaries may be available
