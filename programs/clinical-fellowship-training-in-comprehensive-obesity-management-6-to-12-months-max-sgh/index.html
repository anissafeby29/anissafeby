Clinical Fellowship Training in Comprehensive Obesity Management (6 to 12 months max)

Singapore General Hospital (SingHealth)

Destination
SGP
Singapore
Open official programme page ↗

Overview

1. Be able to clinically assess a patient with overweight and obesity 2. Be able to prescribe a lifestyle therapeutic program for a patient with overweight and obesity 3. Be able to prescribe adjunctive pharmacotherapy for a patient with overweight and obesity 4.

The programme

Focus
Endocrinology (obesity management)
Training type
Clinical Fellowship (attachment for foreign doctors)
Duration
6 – 12 months
Start
Subject to SMC registration and work pass approval (clinical fellowship processing takes approx. 6-9 months after provisional offer)
Positions
2 Clinical Fellows
Accreditation
Certificate of Training
Department
Division of Medicine / Department of Endocrinology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
To be eligible for training in the subspecialty of obesity, a physician must have completed categorical residency training.; Department requirement: Clinical Fellow must have completed categorical residency training. This training may be in Internal Medicine, Family Medicine or subspecialty training such as endocrinology, gastroenterology, nephrology or cardiology.
International applicants
Yes - SGH fellowship and attachment programmes are offered to foreign doctors for short-term clinical training (more than 1000 international doctors since 2000)
Visa support
Training Employment Pass application with Ministry of Manpower (MOM) upon successful SMC Temporary Registration
Licence requirement
Temporary Registration (for training) with the Singapore Medical Council

How to apply

Deadline
Rolling - applications reviewed monthly; cut-off is the last day of each month (Singapore time)
Application method
Apply through SGH Postgraduate Medical Institute (PGMI) with CV/posting history, case summary and two endorsed recommendation letters (one from Head of Institution/Department); online interview for clinical fellowship; queries pgmi.fellowships@sgh.com.sg

Funding

Funding
Training Fee: S$3,000 (before prevailing GST) per month Programme Funding source: Self-funded
