Neurotology Fellowship

Michigan Ear Institute / Ascension Providence / Wayne State University

ENT / OtolaryngologyOtology & neurotologyClinical Fellowship
Destination

Overview

High-volume private-academic fellowship (over 2,300 otologic and 100 neurotologic/skull base cases a year) including translabyrinthine, retrosigmoid and middle fossa approaches and Gamma Knife.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of an ACGME-accredited otolaryngology–head and neck surgery residency
International applicants
ACGME neurotology fellowship: requires completion of an ACGME-accredited (or RCPSC) otolaryngology–HNS residency; not open to non-US/Canada-trained otolaryngologists
Visa support
Not stated on official page
