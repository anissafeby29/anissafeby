Overview

Twelve-month advanced interventional cardiology programme at UMC HCMC for interventionalists who have completed basic training.

The programme

Focus
Interventional cardiology (complex/CTO PCI)
Training type
Professional technique certificate programme (Vietnamese Ministry of Health continuing training)
Duration
About 12 months (5 October 2026 - 15 October 2027, Course 03)
Start
5 October 2026 (Course 03)
Positions
6-8
Accreditation
Certificate of professional technical training (chứng chỉ đào tạo kỹ thuật chuyên môn) issued by UMC HCMC
Department
Cardiology / Cardiac Catheterisation Laboratory

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Doctors with a basic interventional cardiology certificate of at least 12 months (obtained in Vietnam or abroad) currently performing interventions in a hospital with a cath lab
International applicants
Not stated on official page
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Vietnamese practising certificate/licence (chứng chỉ/giấy phép hành nghề) required

How to apply

Deadline
Rolling - from announcement until enough applications received
Application method
Registration form and notarised documents to the Center for Health Workforce Training on Social Demand; no postal applications; fees paid on submission until places are filled

Funding

Funding
Self-funded - 70,000,000 VND
