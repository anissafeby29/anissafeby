Overview

Two-year AIIMS fellowship in laparoscopic and robotic urology.

The programme

Focus
Endourology and minimally invasive urology
Training type
Clinical Fellowship (AIIMS Fellowship Programme)
Duration
2 years
Start
July session - join 1-15 July (extension possible to 31 July)
Positions
2 (1 open, 1 sponsored) - July 2025 session
Accreditation
AIIMS institutional fellowship; exit examination (40% internal assessment, 60% practical final exam)
Department
Department of Urology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
MCh Urology or equivalent
International applicants
Yes - foreign nationals may apply against the 'Sponsored' seats: registration slip must be sent through the diplomatic channel, a 'No Objection' from the Ministry of Health & Family Welfare (Government of India) is required, and candidates sit the same entrance examination; no seats reserved for foreign nationals except under bilateral agreements
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Selected foreign nationals must be registered with the Medical Council of India / National Medical Commission before joining

How to apply

Deadline
July 2025 session: online registration 9-23 April 2025 (closed); fellowships are advertised by session (January/July) - check aiimsexams.ac.in for the current prospectus
Application method
Online registration on aiimsexams.ac.in; two-stage selection - 60-mark computer-based MCQ test in Delhi/NCR plus 40-mark departmental clinical/practical assessment (50% minimum in each stage)

Funding

Funding
Open candidates paid as Senior Resident (3rd year plus 2 increments, 7th CPC); sponsored candidates and foreign nationals receive no emoluments; admission fee Rs 125 (open) / Rs 1,295 (sponsored)
