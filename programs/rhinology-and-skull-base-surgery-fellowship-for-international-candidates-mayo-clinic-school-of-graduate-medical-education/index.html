Overview

Two-year rhinology and skull base fellowship (est. 2023) designed for international candidates, covering inflammatory sinonasal disease, anterior skull base, orbital, functional nasal surgery, epistaxis and allergy, to prepare graduates for academic careers abroad.

Institution policyArizona trainees must hold a full Arizona medical licence (if eligible: 12 months ACGME training for US/Canadian grads, 36 months for IMGs, USMLE 1-3) or an Arizona postgraduate training permit before day one; Mayo pays/reimburses
