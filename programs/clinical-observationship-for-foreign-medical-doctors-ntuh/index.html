National Taiwan University Hospital (NTUH)

Overview

Observationship allowing foreign doctors to observe general and specialty medical care at NTUH without direct patient care.

The programme

Focus
Multiple specialties (institution-wide observership)
Training type
Observership
Duration
Up to one year
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Certificate issued by the Department of Medical Education on completion
Department
Department of Medical Education

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Foreign nationals with an MD/MB (or DDS/BDS) degree and a licence to practise in their own country
International applicants
Yes - guidelines specifically for foreign medical doctors
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Not required (no direct patient care permitted)

How to apply

Deadline
Submit two months before the planned observationship
Application method
Applicant or affiliated institute submits application form, recommendation letters, passport copy and degree diplomas to the Department of Medical Education; reviewed by the clinical department and hospital committee
