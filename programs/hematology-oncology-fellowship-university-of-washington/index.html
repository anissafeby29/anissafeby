Overview

An ACGME-accredited fellowship using resources across multiple institutions to prepare fellows for academic careers in basic, translational and clinical research in haematology and oncology.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
Typically 3 years (dual boards); single-subspecialty and ABIM Research Pathway also considered
Start
July 2027
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited (ACGME #1555413151)
Department
University of Washington / Fred Hutchinson Cancer Center

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed or enrolled in ACGME, ACGME-I, RCPSC, CFPC or AOA internal medicine residency; passed all USMLE/COMLEX exams; commitment to academic medicine; US citizenship or permanent residence by NRMP rank deadline.
International applicants
US citizenship/permanent residence required in most cases (T32 funding); exception possible for classical haematology track.
Visa support
Generally unable to sponsor J-1 or H-1B; applicants needing sponsorship with classical haematology interest may be eligible via separate funding.
Licence requirement
Not stated on official page

How to apply

Deadline
All materials in ERAS by the date released to programmes (typically third Wednesday in July)
Application method
ERAS with programme signals (primary screen); NRMP Match

Funding

Funding
Partially funded by federal T32 grants; fellows are University of Washington employees
