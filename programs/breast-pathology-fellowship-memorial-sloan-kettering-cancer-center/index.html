Overview

One-year breast pathology fellowship positions focused on diagnosis of neoplastic breast disease and its mimics.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
1 July
Positions
3
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Department of Pathology and Laboratory Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed anatomic pathology residency; certified or eligible for certification by the American Board of Pathology
International applicants
Accepted if eligibility met — IMGs must submit an ECFMG certificate marked 'valid indefinitely'
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
New York State licence or limited permit

How to apply

Deadline
30 September, two years before the planned start date
Application method
Apply online (MSK Embark portal); then send three letters of recommendation, CV, copy of medical school diploma (translated if necessary) and ECFMG certificate for IMGs

Funding

Funding
Stipend commensurate with the applicant's year of postgraduate training
