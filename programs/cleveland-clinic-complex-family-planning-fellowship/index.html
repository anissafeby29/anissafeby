Overview

Cleveland Clinic's Cleveland fellowship is one of the 39 US training sites listed by the Society of Family Planning for advanced clinical care, research and teaching in complex contraception and abortion care.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed Obstetrics and Gynaecology residency; ERAS, NRMP and institution-specific requirements apply
International applicants
Yes - accepts all applications including international candidates via ERAS; Cleveland Clinic GME supports H-1B and ECFMG-sponsored J-1
Visa support
Cleveland Clinic GME supports H-1B and J-1 (ECFMG-sponsored); no permanent-residence sponsorship for trainees

Not stated on official page

How to apply

Deadline
Follows Society of Family Planning application timeline; interview invitations sent via ERAS/Thalamus on SFP dates; no specific date posted
Application method
ERAS (standard fellowship application); positions filled through the NRMP match

Funding

Funding
Paid: Cleveland Clinic trainee salary scale effective 16 May 2026 (e.g. grad level 5 $80,608; level 6 $84,180)
