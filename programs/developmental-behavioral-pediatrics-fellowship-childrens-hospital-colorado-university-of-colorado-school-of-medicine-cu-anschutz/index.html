Children's Hospital Colorado / University of Colorado School of Medicine (CU Anschutz)

Overview

Three-year ACGME-approved DBP fellowship at a tertiary referral centre for the Eastern Rocky Mountain region, with LEND participation and mentored scholarly projects.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Approved by the ACGME Pediatric Residency Review Committee; provides experience for Sub-Board certification in DBP
Department
Section of Developmental Pediatrics

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed three years of core pediatrics training
International applicants
Institution policyGraduates of non-US/Canadian schools eligible with valid ECFMG certificate (or full Colorado licence); must hold or obtain legal work authorization; USMLE Step 3 required before starting a fellowship
Visa support
Institution policyClinical J-1 via ECFMG is the standard route; H-1B for residents/fellows only if CU GME agrees to waive its J-1 requirement
Licence requirement
Institution policyColorado Medical Board physician training licence (or full Colorado licence) required

How to apply

Deadline
Completed applications and letters due by end of September; NRMP Fall Medical Specialties match day 2 December
Application method
NRMP registration and ERAS (opens 1 July 2026)

Funding

Funding
Funded at CU SOM Department of Pediatrics PL4-6 salary levels with full benefits
