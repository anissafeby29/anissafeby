Overview

Provides clinical, translational and basic laboratory training in haematology, medical oncology, cell therapy and bone marrow transplantation with over 100 faculty and the Duke Cancer Institute.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Must have passed USMLE Step 3 (or equivalent) to enter at PGY-3 level or higher (Duke University Hospital policy).
International applicants
NIH-funded years 2-3 restricted to US citizens/permanent residents; other candidates considered if fully funded by their government or medical school.
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 alien physician status sponsored by ECFMG with Duke's assistance; Duke is H-1B cap-exempt and H-1B is possible for clinical trainees who have passed USMLE Steps 1-3 and hold an ECFMG certificate (individual programmes decide which visas they accept)
Licence requirement
Institution policyNorth Carolina full medical licence or Resident Training License (RTL) required before clinical duties
