Overview

Cleveland Clinic's Cleveland programme is included in the American Urogynecologic Society's 2026 national guide to Urogynecology and Reconstructive Pelvic Surgery fellowships.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years (incl. 12 months protected research; 18 months URPS, plus female urology, colorectal, MIGS and PT rotations)
Start
Early August 2027 for the published cycle; confirm with programme
Positions
Rotation of 2-1-2 categorical positions per year (filled through NRMP)
Accreditation
Listed in the American Urogynecologic Society 2026 programme guide; confirm current ACGME status
Department
Center for Urogynecology and Pelvic Reconstructive Surgery, Ob/Gyn & Women's Health Institute, Cleveland Clinic

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed eligible Obstetrics and Gynaecology or Urology residency; ERAS, NRMP and institution-specific requirements apply
International applicants
International applicants invited to apply through ERAS/NRMP; US clinical experience preferred but not required; USMLE Steps 1-2, 3rd-year CREOG, ECFMG requirements
Visa support
Cleveland Clinic GME supports H-1B and J-1 (ECFMG-sponsored); no permanent-residence sponsorship for trainees
Licence requirement
Not stated on official page

How to apply

Deadline
In conjunction with the AUGS Common Application dates; invitations via ERAS on AUGS dates
Application method
ERAS (CV, personal statement, MSPE, transcript, USMLE 1-2, CREOG via ERAS, 3 letters incl. residency PD); NRMP match

Funding

Funding
Paid: Cleveland Clinic trainee salary scale effective 16 May 2026 (grad level 5 $80,608; level 6 $84,180; level 7 $89,204)
