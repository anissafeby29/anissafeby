Overview

One-month CIRSE-funded IR training stay at General University Hospital Attikon (National and Kapodistrian University of Athens) (Athens); expertise: Vascular IR, interventional oncology, MSK interventions, neurointerventions, pain management; training supervisor Dimitrios K. Filippiadis.

How to apply

Deadline
2026 cycle closed; call for the 2027 funding cycle at the end of 2026
Application method
Request centre contact details from CIRSE (up to three centres), make preliminary arrangements, then apply via CIRSE online portal with motivation letter, head-of-department recommendation, host confirmation letter, budget and signed form
