Overview

A one-year clinical spine fellowship providing advanced training in the comprehensive management of spinal disorders, including complex and minimally invasive spinal techniques.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
1 July 2028 (currently accepting applications for July 2028 – June 2029)

Case numbers submitted for CAST reporting purposes
Department
Department of Neurological Surgery

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Successful completion of an ACGME-accredited residency training programme, or an equivalent international programme
International applicants
Graduates of an equivalent international residency programme are eligible to apply
Visa support
Institution policyNewYork-Presbyterian GME accepts foreign nationals only with J-1 (ECFMG) status or their own work authorisation; no H-1B, H-1B1, F-1 or E-3 sponsorship; O-1 supported only in very limited cases (NYP GME Visa Rules, 2027 recruitment packet)
Licence requirement
Must be eligible to obtain a New York State medical licence and DEA registration
