Inflammatory Wound Care and Complex Medical Dermatology Fellowship

University of Toronto (Women's College Hospital / Toronto Western Hospital)

DermatologyComplex medical dermatologyClinical Fellowship
Destination
CAN
Toronto, Ontario, Canada
Open official programme page ↗

Overview

A one-year clinical fellowship in complex inflammatory wounds (vasculitis, pyoderma gangrenosum, vasculopathy) at Women's College Hospital and complex medical dermatology at Toronto Western, including HS surgery.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship

1 year

1–2 per year

Not stated on official page

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
CV, three references, MD and specialty certificates, proof of citizenship
International applicants
TOEFL required if training was not in English or French
Visa support
Institution policyinternational clinical fellows need an IRCC work permit; the PGME Office issues an LMIA-exempt offer of employment number and supports the work permit application
Licence requirement
Institution policyclinical fellows must hold a CPSO certificate of registration authorising postgraduate education (educational licence); research fellows do not need CPSO registration

How to apply

Deadline
Applications open 1 June – 1 September
Application method
Institution policyapply to the departmental Fellowship Program Director; sponsored international applicants apply through their sponsoring agency to the PGME Office

Funding

Funding
Institution policyclinical fellows must be funded (department or sponsoring agency); FEAC recommends pay at least equal to PGY1 on the PARO salary scale, and foreign nationals must be paid at a rate commensurate with a Canadian in the same role
