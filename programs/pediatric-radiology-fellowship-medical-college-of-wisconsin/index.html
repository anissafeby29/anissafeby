Overview

A comprehensive one-year ACGME-accredited paediatric radiology fellowship at Children's Wisconsin with over 120,000 studies a year and three months of electives.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed a DR residency accredited by ACGME or RCPSC (or AOA in ACGME pre-accreditation); ABR eligible; Wisconsin licence.
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs accepted with appropriate visa: J-1 via ECFMG (valid ECFMG certificate) or H-1B where programme agrees
Visa support
Institution policyMCWAH accepts J-1 (ECFMG-sponsored); some programmes pursue H-1B (requires USMLE Step 3 passed) - check with programme; no permanent-residency sponsorship
Licence requirement
Must be eligible for, and obtain before start, a Wisconsin medical license.
