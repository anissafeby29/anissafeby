Overview

University of Toronto Orthopaedic Sports Medicine programme based at Women's College Hospital with rotations across affiliated teaching hospitals.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (3 four-month rotations)
Start
1 August
Positions
8
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Division of Orthopaedic Surgery, Department of Surgery

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed accredited North American orthopaedic residency; foreign fellows with comparable training as determined by CPSO
International applicants
Yes - clinical fellows are surgeons from Canada or international countries who already hold orthopaedic specialist status in their country of origin
Visa support
Foreign fellows require a work visa processed by the University of Toronto (about 12 months lead time)
Licence requirement
Must qualify for licensure with the College of Physicians and Surgeons of Ontario (CPSO); Foreign fellows must show medical training completed in English or TOEFL overall 93 (speaking 24)

How to apply

Deadline
31 October (19 months before the 1 August start)
Application method
CV, 3 reference letters, letter of intent and UTOSM application

Funding

Funding
Canadian fellows self-funded via OHIP billing; international fellows guaranteed PARO PGY3 salary
