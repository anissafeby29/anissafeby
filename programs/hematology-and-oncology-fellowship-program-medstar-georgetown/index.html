Overview

A continuously accredited three-year haematology/oncology fellowship rotating through MedStar Georgetown (phase 1 trials, haematologic malignancy group) and MedStar Washington Hospital Center.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years
Start
1 July

6 per year (combined quota of the two programmes)
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited
Department
Lombardi Comprehensive Cancer Center / MedStar Washington Cancer Institute

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Board eligibility in internal medicine before start (typically 3-year US IM residency); MSPE, 3 letters incl. PD, transcript, USMLE Step 1 and 2

Not stated on official page
