Overview

Specialist training in running early-phase haematology trials, protocol development, regulatory processes and translational research at UCLH's NIHR Clinical Research Facility.

The programme

12 months (fixed term, full time)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Medical degree; MRCP or equivalent; eligible for full GMC registration with licence; completed core medical training or equivalent to ST6; specialist haematology/oncology training and chemotherapy prescribing competency
International applicants
Non-UK trained doctors must be on the GMC register by appointment having completed PLAB 1 and 2 (advert)
Visa support
Applicants requiring Skilled Worker sponsorship are welcome and considered alongside all other applications (NHS Jobs advert)
Licence requirement
Full GMC registration with licence to practise
