Overview

A one-year UHN clinical and research fellowship training emerging leaders in prevention of diabetes complications across cardiac, renal and endocrine care.

The programme

Focus

1 year

Accreditation
University of Toronto clinical fellowship; does not lead to RCPSC certification (per UofT PGME fellowship page)

Department of Medicine, Division of Nephrology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Generally physicians who have completed training in nephrology, endocrinology or cardiology; support from home institution
International applicants
Non-Canadian applicants may apply; sponsored applicants from Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia and UAE apply via sponsoring agency to PGME (per UofT PGME fellowship page)
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
CPSO certificate authorizing postgraduate education (educational licence); applicants must be recognized as a specialist/family physician in the jurisdiction where they practised (per UofT PGME fellowship page)
