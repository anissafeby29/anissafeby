Overview

University of Southern California / LAC+USC Medical Center's Los Angeles programme is included in the American Urogynecologic Society's 2026 national guide to Urogynecology and Reconstructive Pelvic Surgery fellowships.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years (OBGYN-trained); 2 years (Urology-trained)
Start
1 August each academic year
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Listed in the American Urogynecologic Society 2026 programme guide; confirm current ACGME status
Department
Catherine and Joseph Aresty Department of Urology with Obstetrics and Gynecology (USC / LA General)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed residency accredited in the United States or Canada; CV, statement, 3 letters (one from PD/chair), USMLE Steps 1-3, CREOG scores
International applicants
Not stated on official page
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 (ECFMG) processed through the Keck/LAC+USC Training Program Liaison; Keck/USC and LAC+USC do not sponsor H-1B visas; O-1 and TN not accepted for trainees

Not stated on official page
