Overview

A one-year ACGME-accredited fellowship providing advanced regional anesthesia training through extensive clinical work, teaching and research, including dedicated block-fellow supervision days.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
One year
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Two per year
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited
Department
Department of Anesthesiology, Perioperative and Pain Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Graduates of ACGME-, RCPSC- or CFPC-accredited residencies, or exceptionally qualified international graduates with ECFMG certification
International applicants
Exceptionally qualified international graduates with ECFMG certification may be approved
Visa support
Institution policyMount Sinai Health System offers J-1 (ECFMG) and H-1B (categorical candidates in programmes of 2+ years, ECFMG-certified with USMLE Step 3 passed by Match) plus E-3 for Australians; programme director decides which is offered
Licence requirement
Institution policyResidents/fellows in ACGME-accredited programmes do not need a New York State licence (supervised practice exemption); non-ACGME fellows need a valid NY licence or limited permit

Funding
