Overview

UCLA's ACGME-accredited Independent IR Residency provides dedicated interventional radiology training after diagnostic radiology residency, recruited through ERAS and the NRMP.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Not stated on official page
International applicants
IMGs in a US or Canadian residency must have passed USMLE Steps I–III (not required for Medical Council of Canada licensees), have 4 years of ACGME/RCPSC-accredited postgraduate training and qualify for a CA medical licence.
Visa support
Must be US citizen/permanent resident or eligible for an ECFMG-sponsored J-1 visa (reviewed by the GME Dean's office).
Licence requirement
Must qualify for a California medical licence prior to the start of fellowship.

How to apply

Deadline
ERAS December cycle; applications can be submitted from 13 November 2026
Application method
ERAS; participates in the NRMP Match.

Funding

Funding
Institution policy (UCLA ACGME residents/fellows, CIR-SEIU contract): from 1 Sept 2026 PGY4 $108,102; PGY5 $111,894; PGY6 $115,477; PGY7 $118,801; PGY8 $124,242 (PGY1 $98,466); rising from 1 July 2027 (PGY4 $113,508); plus $8,000/yr Resident Enhancement Fund
