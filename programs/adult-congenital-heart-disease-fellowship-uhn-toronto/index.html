Adult Congenital Heart Disease Fellowship

Overview

A two-year fellowship at the Toronto ACHD Program, one of the oldest and largest in the world, which has trained more than 60 ACHD physicians since 1993.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
2 years
Start
July or January
Positions
6–7 per year
Accreditation
University of Toronto clinical fellowship (Certificate of Completion from PGME); does not lead to RCPSC certification
Department
Division of Cardiology, Department of Medicine, University of Toronto

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Recognised specialist (eligible for RCPSC/ABMS certification or recognised as specialist in home jurisdiction) with completed cardiology training
International applicants
Yes — international applicants meeting CPSO clinical fellow licensure requirements may apply (recognised specialists)
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Must be eligible for a CPSO certificate authorizing postgraduate education (educational licence); IMGs complete a Pre-Entry Assessment Program

How to apply

Deadline
July start: apply September–November; January start: apply March–May
Application method
Contact Toronto ACHD programme coordinator
