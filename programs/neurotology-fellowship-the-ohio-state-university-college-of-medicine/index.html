Overview

Two-year neurotology fellowship (est. 1995, ACGME-accredited since 1998) training one fellow at a time at Ohio State Wexner Medical Center and Nationwide Children's Hospital.

International applicants
Institution policyIMGs eligible with ECFMG certification and J-1 eligibility (H-1B only in limited cases); permanent residents always accepted
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 via ECFMG is the recommended option; H-1B only for F-1 graduates of US LCME/AOA schools or current H-1B holders transferring, with GME Office approval

Institution policyState Medical Board of Ohio training certificate or full Ohio licence required before training starts
