Overview

A three-year programme with a clinical first year at Duke University Medical Center and Durham VA followed by research training, with over 80% of graduates in academic ID faculty positions.

The programme

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
IMGs require completion of ACGME-accredited (or equivalent) IM residency and ABIM board-eligibility in IM.
International applicants
Institution policyYes - graduates of medical schools outside the US/Canada are eligible if they hold a valid ECFMG certificate or a full unrestricted US licence
Visa support
Accepts applicants sponsored on ECFMG J-1 clinical visas
Licence requirement
Institution policyNorth Carolina full medical licence or Resident Training License (RTL) required before clinical duties
