Head and Neck Fellowship

Overview

One-year fellowship covering ablative, microvascular (around 150+ free flaps), skull base, robotic, cutaneous malignancy and endocrine head and neck surgery within a multidisciplinary team.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed an otolaryngology residency; passed all three USMLE steps (or California-recognised equivalent); eligible for a California licence and Keck credentialing
International applicants
Possible for IMGs who have passed USMLE Steps 1–3 and are California-licence eligible
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 (ECFMG) processed through the Keck/LAC+USC Training Program Liaison; Keck/USC and LAC+USC do not sponsor H-1B visas; O-1 and TN not accepted for trainees
Licence requirement
California medical licence
