Overview

Fully funded senior fellowship offering advanced training in transthoracic, transesophageal and fetal echocardiography, cardiac MRI and CT.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed or on track to complete a three-year core pediatric cardiology fellowship in an accredited programme
International applicants
Institution policyYes - international graduates must hold ECFMG certification and obtain an appropriate visa and I-9 work eligibility before the start date
Visa support
Foreign medical graduates must hold a J-1 visa (approval by the Designated Institutional Official and programme director required); Nationwide Children's does not sponsor H-1B visas
Licence requirement
Institution policyvalid Ohio Training Certificate and/or Ohio Medical License required before start (licensure fees covered by the programme)
