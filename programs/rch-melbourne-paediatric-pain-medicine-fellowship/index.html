Overview

Interdisciplinary training in acute and chronic pain affecting children and adolescents.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
6 or 12 months full-time
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Accredited by the Faculty of Pain Medicine (ANZCA); counts toward Practice Development Stage
Department
Department of Anaesthesia and Pain Management - Children's Pain Management Service

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
AHPRA registration (or eligibility); Fellowship in a recognised specialty or advanced trainee in an AMC-accredited specialty; FPM trainee registration and Foundation Exam desirable
International applicants
Institution policyInternational candidates welcome; they must obtain AHPRA registration (including English-language requirements) and a visa with work rights (RCH may sponsor a 482 TSS visa) before starting
Visa support
Institution policyAll RCH employees need Australian citizenship or a visa with work rights; where RCH agrees to sponsor, it engages migration lawyers for a Temporary Skill Shortage (subclass 482) employer-sponsored visa (usually 2-4 years, dependants included); other routes include 417/482 working holiday, 189/190 skilled visas
Licence requirement
AHPRA medical registration (or eligibility)
