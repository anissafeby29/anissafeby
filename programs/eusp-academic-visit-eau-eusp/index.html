Overview

Six-to-twelve-week research-focused visit to a European institution or laboratory designed to produce an international peer-reviewed publication.

The programme

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
EAU(N) member for at least one year; urologist, urologist-in-training, researcher, scientist or research nurse; first or co-author of at least 1 peer-reviewed international PubMed publication
International applicants
Open to EAU members (membership for at least one year before applying; 2026 is a transition year); the page sets no nationality restriction, but all visits must take place at a European institution
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Not stated on official page

How to apply

Deadline
Three deadlines per year: 1 January, 1 May and 1 September
Application method
Online application with CV and PubMed list, detailed budget, motivation letter, structured proposal with timeline, senior academic recommendation and host centre confirmation letter

Funding

Funding
Grant of €2,500–€5,000 depending on length and host country
