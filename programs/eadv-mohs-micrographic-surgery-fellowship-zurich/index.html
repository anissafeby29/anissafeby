EADV Mohs Micrographic Surgery Fellowship

University Hospital Zurich and Stadtspital Zürich (Institute of Dermatology, Europaallee)

DermatologyMohs & procedural dermatologyClinical Fellowship (society-funded)
Destination
CHE
Zurich, Switzerland
Open official programme page ↗Compare saved →

Overview

Six-month fellowship across USZ and Stadtspital Zürich with over 200 supervised Mohs cases, more than 50 complex flap/graft reconstructions and over 20 slow Mohs cases.

The programme

Focus
Mohs micrographic surgery / dermatologic surgery
Training type
Clinical Fellowship (society-funded)
Duration
6 months (one consecutive period)
Start
During the year after application; start date agreed with awardee
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
EADV Mohs Fellowship; logbook counts towards ESMS (European Society for Micrographic Surgery) certification (minimum 100 supervised cases)
Department
Department of Dermatology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
EADV member; completed dermatology residency and board-certified in a European country; at least 2 years' dermatosurgery experience; ESMS associate membership; fluent English
International applicants
Restricted to EU/EFTA citizens who are EADV members and board-certified dermatologists in Europe
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
EU/EFTA citizenship and no criminal record required; advance approval by Swiss authorities (recognition of state and specialist exams, MedReg registration, work permit, licence to practise, 6-month residence permit). German B1 needed to treat patients without direct supervision

How to apply

Deadline
27 February 2026 (2026 call closed; fellowship starts the year after application)
Application method
Online application via MyEADV (application form, CV, medical diploma, recommendation letter from head of department); reviewed by EADV Project Proposal and Review Committee

Funding

Funding
Up to €18,000 for travel and accommodation
