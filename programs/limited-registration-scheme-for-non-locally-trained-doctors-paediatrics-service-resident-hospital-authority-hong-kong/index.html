Overview

Hospital Authority route allowing overseas-trained paediatric doctors to work and continue specialist training in Hong Kong public hospitals under limited registration.

The programme

Training type
Clinical employment / training scheme

Renewable contracts up to 3 years

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Full registration with an approved overseas medical authority; qualification comparable to HKAM Intermediate (for Paediatrics, pre-intermediate e.g. MRCPCH theory exams accepted); post-internship clinical experience; enrolled in recognised specialist training
International applicants
For non-locally trained doctors; proficiency in English and Cantonese required for Paediatrics
Visa support
HA arranges working visa after offer
Licence requirement
HA supports application to the Medical Council of Hong Kong for limited registration (no licensing exam required)
