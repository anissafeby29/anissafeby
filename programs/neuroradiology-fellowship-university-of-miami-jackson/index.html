Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
USMLE Steps 1–3 (or COMLEX 1–3) passed; graduated from a four-year ACGME DR residency or RCPSC/CFPC-accredited Canadian DR residency; IMGs must hold valid ECFMG certification.
International applicants
Foreign medical graduates are eligible with ECFMG certification; Jackson Memorial Hospital sponsors J-1 visas (ECFMG) for qualified applicants.
Visa support
J-1 (ECFMG-sponsored) for qualified applicants.
