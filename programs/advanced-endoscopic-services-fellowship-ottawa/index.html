Overview

A one-year advanced therapeutic endoscopy fellowship with high-volume ERCP and EUS (about 800 cases each annually) plus stenting, EMR and deep enteroscopy, with a research component.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Gastroenterology fellowship; preferred North American training experience; English proficiency; preference for applicants who did a GI elective at The Ottawa Hospital
International applicants
Institution policyYes - IMGs eligible for listed fellowship programmes; must be certified (RCPSC, CFPC or recognised international board) or residency-complete, meet English proficiency requirements and complete PEAP
Visa support
Institution policyinternational trainees need an IRCC work permit (LMIA-exempt); the uOttawa PGME/immigration advisor provides the documents needed to apply (CPSO letter of eligibility and LMIA-exemption number)
Licence requirement
Institution policyCPSO postgraduate education (educational) certificate of registration required, renewed annually; internationally trained clinical fellows must complete a 4-12 week Pre-Entry Assessment Program (PEAP); three-year licensure limit for clinical fellowships
