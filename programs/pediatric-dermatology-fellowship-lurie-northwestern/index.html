Overview

Scientific opportunities and broad clinical training in paediatric dermatology at Lurie Children's Hospital, with one to two publications encouraged during the year.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Board eligible or certified in dermatology before starting
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs eligible with a valid ECFMG certificate, valid visa (J-1, H-1B or O-1) or permanent residence, and Illinois IDFPR licence
Visa support
Institution policyMcGaw sponsors J-1 (ECFMG) and supports H-1B only for exceptional candidates approved by the DIO/GMEC before ranking (cost borne by programme)
Licence requirement
Eligible for a temporary Illinois medical licence
