Overview

Three-year ACGME PEM fellowship with 12 months of research time and an optional global health track including experience in Mwanza, Tanzania.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Board-certified or board-eligible in pediatrics and/or emergency medicine at fellowship start; current New York State licence or limited permit required
International applicants
Institution policyYes - foreign nationals accepted if they secure ECFMG J-1 status (or hold work authorisation for the programme duration); IMGs need a valid ECFMG certificate or full NY licence
Visa support
Institution policyNYPH GME generally accepts foreign nationals only on ECFMG-sponsored J-1 status or with independent work authorisation; no H-1B, H-1B1, E-3 or other sponsorship; O-1 supported only in very limited cases at the candidate's expense
Licence requirement
Current New York State medical licence or limited permit required for entry
