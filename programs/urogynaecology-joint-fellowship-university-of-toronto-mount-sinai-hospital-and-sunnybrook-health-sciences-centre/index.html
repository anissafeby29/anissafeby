Overview

A 2-year tertiary urogynaecology fellowship with 12 months at Sunnybrook followed by 12 months at Mount Sinai Hospital.

The programme

Training type

2 years

Institution policyUniversity of Toronto PGME academic year runs 1 July - 30 June (letters of appointment issued per academic year; off-cycle starts possible)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Only Canadian-trained applicants eligible
International applicants
Not accepted – only Canadian-trained applicants
Visa support
Institution policyinternational clinical fellows need an IRCC work permit; the PGME Office issues an LMIA-exempt offer of employment number and supports the work permit application
Licence requirement
Certificate of registration with the College of Physicians and Surgeons of Ontario (CPSO); internationally trained physicians must complete the 4–12-week Pre-Entry Assessment Program (PEAP) before full acceptance (department application guidelines)

How to apply

Deadline
May 1, 2026
Application method
Fellowship Application Form (PDF) submitted to the program director; appointment processed through U of T PGME (OATS). Allow 5–6 months processing for international applicants.

Funding

Funding
Guaranteed annual salary and supported academic travel; protected time and funding for an approved Master's degree
