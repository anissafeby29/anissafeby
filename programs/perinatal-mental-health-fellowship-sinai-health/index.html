Perinatal Mental Health Fellowship

Overview

A one-year academic fellowship in Canada's largest perinatal mental health programme providing advanced treatment of adults in pregnancy and postpartum.

The programme

Focus
Perinatal / reproductive psychiatry
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (clinical and/or research)
Start
July 1 preferred (alternative dates negotiable)
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
University of Toronto PGME-registered fellowship (not RCPSC-accredited subspecialty training)
Department
Not stated on official page

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of an accredited psychiatric residency; strong clinical and academic skills; interest in women's mental health
International applicants
Yes — internationally trained specialists eligible if they meet CPSO requirements; English proficiency results may be requested; government-sponsored trainees from Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, UAE and Saudi Arabia apply via STAR
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
CPSO educational (or independent) licence eligibility required
