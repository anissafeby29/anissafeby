Overview

A two-year ACGME-accredited clinical cardiac electrophysiology fellowship at UAB offering hands-on clinical experience, personalised instruction and research opportunities.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
2 years (PGY-7 and PGY-8)
Start
July
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited
Department
Division of Cardiovascular Disease

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
ERAS application with current CV; must be eligible for Alabama medical licence, controlled substance certificate and DEA registration; valid ECFMG certificate for IMGs; no minimum USMLE score
International applicants
IMGs may apply; valid ECFMG certificate required
Visa support
Division can sponsor J-1 visas; does not sponsor H-1B unless the applicant is already within the US
Licence requirement
Must be eligible for Alabama medical licence and controlled substance certificate

How to apply

Deadline
Applications open and close July through September via ERAS
Application method
ERAS; fellowship Match

Funding

Funding
Salaried fellowship; salaries and benefits by PGY level per UAB GME
