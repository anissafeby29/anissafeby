Overview

Four one-year fully funded paediatric radiology fellowship positions at the Children's Hospital of Eastern Ontario (CHEO).

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
12 months (July 1 – June 30)
Start
July 1
Positions
4 positions
Accreditation
University of Ottawa clinical fellowship (certificate on completion)
Department
Department of Radiology, University of Ottawa — Department of Medical Imaging, Children's Hospital of Eastern Ontario (CHEO)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed accredited radiology training; certified or eligible for certification by RCPSC (Diagnostic Imaging), American Board of Radiology, or equivalent qualifications from another country; proficient in written and spoken English
International applicants
Yes — separate application procedure for foreign fellowship candidates via the uOttawa Faculty of Medicine application website
Visa support
Not stated on official page

CPSO licensure (not detailed on page)
