Overview

One-year ACGME-accredited, Mohs College-approved fellowship in cutaneous oncology, Mohs micrographic, reconstructive and cosmetic surgery at a comprehensive cancer centre.

The programme

Focus
Mohs micrographic surgery
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
Institution policyMD Anderson GME appointments are a minimum of 12 months commencing 1 July or 1 August
Positions
1 per year
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited; American College of Mohs Surgery approved
Department
Department of Dermatology – Mohs & Dermasurgery Unit

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Graduates of an accredited dermatology residency; must pass ABD certification within two years of graduation
International applicants
Institution policyInternational physician trainees accepted; those needing sponsorship must use the ECFMG J-1 visa
Visa support
Institution policyThe ECFMG-sponsored J-1 is the required visa for international physician trainees who need visa sponsorship at MD Anderson; visa-sponsored trainees may not moonlight
Licence requirement
Institution policyA Texas Medical Board Physician-in-Training (PIT) permit (obtained by the GME office for trainees without a full Texas licence) or a full Texas licence is required before starting

How to apply

Deadline
Not stated on official page
Application method
SF Match plus MD Anderson DISCOVER system (both required)

Funding

Funding
Institution policyMD Anderson GME stipend levels AY 2026-27: PGY4 $78,917; PGY5 $81,776; PGY6 $84,650; PGY7 $88,204; PGY8 $91,399 (PGY2 $73,169); trainees may also be appointed without compensation when funded by an outside agency/government
