Overview

The MedStar Georgetown/NRH one-year ACGME-accredited PM&R sports medicine fellowship offers multidisciplinary musculoskeletal training, diagnostic and interventional ultrasound, fluoroscopic spine procedures and the National Capital Sports Medicine Consortium didactics.
