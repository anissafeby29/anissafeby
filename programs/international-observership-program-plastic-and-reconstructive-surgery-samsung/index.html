Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Doctor of Medicine (MD); able to communicate in Korean or English (TOEFL iBT 64, TOEIC 600, IELTS 5.5 or exemption); diploma, employment certificate, passport, physician's statement - originals in English or notarised translations
International applicants
Yes - International Observership Program for foreign physicians
Visa support
SMC does not issue a guarantee letter for visa applications; applicant responsible for visa
Licence requirement
Not required (informal shadowing; observation only)

How to apply

Deadline
At least 4-5 months before the start of training (review up to 2 months); paediatrics at least 6 months
Application method
Combine application/recommendation form, consent and documents in one PDF and email the department contact listed on SMC's Contact Us page
