Overview

Fellowship in Urogynaecology at UKM / Hospital Canselor Tuanku Muhriz: aims to acquire knowledge and skill in managing common pelvic floor dysfunctions.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Postgraduate qualification in Obstetrics & Gynaecology i.e. Doctor of O&G (DrO&G)/ Master in O&G/ Master / MRCOG Part III or equivalent Possess at least 1 year working experience post gazzettement as an Obstetrician & Gynaecologist Independent, Certified and gazetted with interest in Urogynaecology
International applicants
Yes - page sets out the Malaysian Medical Council Temporary Practising Certificate (TPC) route used by non-Malaysian doctors, applied for via UKM
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Malaysian Medical Council Temporary Practising Certificate (TPC); UKM forwards the application to MMC except the Letter of Good Standing

How to apply
