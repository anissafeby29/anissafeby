Overview

An Oxford School of Anaesthesia programme developing audit, quality improvement, leadership and management skills through an international audit project, alongside clinical training.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Trainees and SAS doctors at all levels of training in anaesthesia (junior programme); senior programme for trainees developing an interest in perioperative care or management
International applicants
Institution policyInternational doctors accepted to NDA anaesthetic fellowships via the RCoA Medical Training Initiative or a Skilled Worker visa, subject to GMC registration
Visa support
Institution policy (OUH Nuffield Dept of Anaesthetics fellowships): international fellows enter either via the Medical Training Initiative (RCoA-sponsored MTI, Tier 5 visa with CoS from the Academy of Medical Royal Colleges) or a Skilled Worker (Tier 2) visa with a Certificate of Sponsorship assigned by OUH
Licence requirement
Institution policyGMC registration required to treat patients in the UK; MTI appointees obtain GMC registration sponsored by the RCoA (identity check on arrival in the UK)
