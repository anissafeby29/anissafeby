Overview

A three-year ACGME gastroenterology fellowship at Banner - University Medical Center Tucson and South campuses and the Tucson VA Medical Center.

Eligibility and visa

Certificate of completion (or good standing) in an ACGME-accredited residency; ECFMG certificate for IMGs
International applicants
Welcome to apply with a standard ECFMG certificate; fellows must have U.S. citizenship, permanent residency or valid J-1 status

J-1 only; unable to accept candidates with H-1B visas
