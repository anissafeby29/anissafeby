Neonatology Fellowship

Overview

A high-acuity neonatology fellowship in which fellows serve as unit leaders while preparing for careers in neonatology.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years (optional fourth year for research or Master's degree)
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited (Neonatal-Perinatal Medicine)
Department
Department of Pediatrics, University of Virginia School of Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Currently in an accredited pediatric residency in good standing or completed at least 3 years of general pediatric residency; Virginia licence not required (institutional training permit)
International applicants
ECFMG certificate required if applicable
Visa support
Institution policyUVA institutionally sponsors the J-1 clinical visa; sponsorship of other visa types is decided at department level
Licence requirement
Institution policyAll medical house staff must hold a Virginia Intern and Residency Training License (issued annually; fee paid by the hospital, coordinated by the GME Office) - or a permanent Virginia licence

How to apply

Deadline
Not stated on official page
Application method
ERAS: application, letters from residency director plus two faculty, personal statement, transcript, USMLE/COMLEX, ECFMG certificate if applicable

Funding

Funding
Institution policyUVA house-staff stipend 2026-27: PGY4 $76,245; PGY5 $79,280; PGY6 $82,390; PGY7 $85,775; PGY8 $89,920 (PGY1 $67,930); fellows paid at the PGY level of required prior training; no institutional pay above PGY8
