Neonatal-Perinatal Medicine Fellowship

Children’s Hospital of Philadelphia

Destination
USA
Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, United States

Overview

Comprehensive neonatal-perinatal training across high-acuity clinical environments with protected scholarship, research education and multidisciplinary neonatal care.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years
Start
July (fellowships begin the July following the November match)
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
ACGME-approved paediatric residency prerequisite; fellowship accreditation not stated
Department
Not stated on official page

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Two to three years of ACGME-approved paediatric residency training and board eligibility
International applicants
Yes - graduates of non-US/Canadian/Puerto Rican schools need a valid ECFMG certificate; non-citizens/non-permanent residents must provide a copy of a current appropriate visa
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
USMLE Parts 1, 2 and 3 taken within the 7-year time frame required by the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania (or equivalent)

How to apply

Deadline
ERAS opens early July; complete applications by end of July (programme mailbox closed 11 August in the last dated cycle); interviews September-October; NRMP match results in November
Application method
ERAS and NRMP

Funding

Funding
