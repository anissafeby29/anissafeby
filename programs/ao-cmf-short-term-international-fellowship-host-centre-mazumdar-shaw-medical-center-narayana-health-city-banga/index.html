Overview

AO-funded short-term head and neck / craniomaxillofacial surgery fellowship placement at Mazumdar Shaw Medical Center, Narayana Health City, Bangalore (Bengaluru, India) performing about 700 surgical cases a year.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Graduate of a training programme in a CMF specialty; completed an AO CMF MFT or other hands-on AO CMF course; at least two years' AO CMF membership; younger than 40 in year of application (exceptions possible); 'Interrupting your unconscious biases' module certificate
International applicants
Yes - AO Foundation global programme; fellows from any country are allocated to host centres by AO (membership and course prerequisites apply)
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Scrub-in: Yes; host note on licence/scrub-in: No The visiting Fellow can scrub in as the first or second assistant for ablative or reconstructive surgery as per their interest. Cases are listed daily from Monday to Saturday . They would need to complete the formalities with Narayana Health Acade. Dominant OR language: English (team English written (5) / spoken (5) on a 1-5 scale). How to apply

Deadline
Annual AO CMF call (check AO CMF fellowship page for the next call)
Application method
Online application to the AO Foundation during the open call (host centres allocated by AO); not directly to the hospital

Funding

Funding
Small AO CMF stipend (benchmark USD 550/week in the US, adjusted for local price level; 75-85 fellowships supported yearly); travel and insurance paid by fellow
