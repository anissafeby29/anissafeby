Overview

Short visiting fellowship for dermatologists covering laser and light systems and procedures such as botulinum toxin, fillers, grafts, hair transplantation, peels and photodynamic therapy through case discussion, live demonstration and supervised practice.

The programme

Focus
Cosmetic & laser dermatology
Training type
Visiting Fellowship (short course)
Duration
Programme fee quoted per 1 month (4 weeks); sessions start the first or third week of each month
Start
First or third week of every month except April
Positions
Two applicants accepted at a time per training session
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Division of Dermatology (via International Relations Section)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Dermatologist with at least 10 months of training in general dermatology from an accredited institution; communicative English (TOEFL/IELTS score required); recommendation letter, CV, cover letter, dermatology certificate/diploma
International applicants
Yes - programme run by the International Relations Section for overseas dermatologists
Visa support
Page states international participants require a Non-Immigrant Education (ED) visa, arranged by the applicant at a Royal Thai Embassy/Consulate; copy required after acceptance
Licence requirement
Not stated on official page

How to apply

Deadline
Apply at least three months before the intended start date
Application method
Online application (Google Form) with attached documents; acceptance notified by email from Ramathibodi International Relations

Funding

Funding
Self-funded - tuition 2,000 USD per month, paid by bank transfer within two weeks of acceptance
