Overview

Tailored twelve-month training for international doctors well advanced in specialty training, embedded within GOSH clinical teams to gain specialist paediatric expertise, with anaesthesia among the offered specialties.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
Typically 12 months
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Learning mapped to Royal College curriculum; educational supervisors accredited with the Academy of Medical Educators
Department
GOSH Learning Academy / Department of Anaesthesia

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
GMC-acceptable medical degree; 4+ years of specialty training; passed Part 1 College exam or UK equivalent; postgraduate qualification in specialty (UK Royal College membership/fellowship or home-country equivalent); recent full-time clinical work; IELTS (7.0 each, 7.5 overall) or OET (B each); resident overseas at time of applying
International applicants
Yes — programme designed for international doctors resident overseas who will return home after the visa expires
Visa support
GOSH provides GMC sponsorship; visa applications form part of pre-employment checks
Licence requirement
GMC registration (GOSH-sponsored) required before start

How to apply

Funding

Funding
Self/home-organisation funded: funding agreed with home organisation to cover a placement fee; GOSH does not provide scholarships
