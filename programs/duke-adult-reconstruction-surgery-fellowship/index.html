Adult Reconstruction Surgery Fellowship

Duke University School of Medicine

OrthopaedicsAdult reconstruction / arthroplastyClinical Fellowship
Destination
USA

Open official programme page ↗

Overview

Adult reconstruction fellowship with significant exposure to primary and revision hip and knee arthroplasty, osteotomy and arthroscopy of the hip and knee.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (1 August - 31 July)
Start
1 August
Positions
4
Accreditation
1 ACGME position + 3 non-ACGME positions
Department
Department of Orthopaedic Surgery

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed orthopaedic residency and board-eligible
International applicants
FMGs must hold a valid ECFMG certificate (ACGME position); FMGs eligible only if US permanent residents (green card) not requiring visa sponsorship (non-ACGME positions)
Visa support
J-1 Clinical House Staff visa (ECFMG-sponsored) for ACGME-accredited fellowship; H-1B not sponsored (ACGME position only)
Licence requirement
Must qualify for a North Carolina medical licence; DEA number required
