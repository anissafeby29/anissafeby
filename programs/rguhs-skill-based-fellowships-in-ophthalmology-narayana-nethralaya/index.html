Overview

University-certified 18-month skill-based fellowships run by Narayana Nethralaya under the aegis of RGUHS.

The programme

Focus
Multiple ophthalmic subspecialties
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
18 months
Start
Admissions once a year (July/August) as notified by RGUHS

2 seats in each subspecialty (cornea & refractive, phaco & refractive, vitreoretinal, glaucoma, paediatric & strabismus)
