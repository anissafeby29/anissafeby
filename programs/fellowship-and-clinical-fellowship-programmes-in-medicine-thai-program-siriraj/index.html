Overview

Siriraj Thai Program fellowship and clinical fellowship training in medicine at Thailand's first medical school.

Not stated on official page

Not stated on official page

Department
Department of Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Graduate of the specific field with a valid medical licence from country of origin held for at least 3 years; English proficiency (TOEFL-iBT 64 or IELTS 5.0+, within 2 years); 2 referee recommendation forms; notarised English translations; additional departmental requirements
International applicants
Yes - international applicants accepted but this is a Thai Program; international trainees should study Thai for 3-6 months before the course
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Valid medical licence from country of origin (held at least 3 years)

How to apply

Deadline
Submit at least 6 months before the beginning of the programme academic year (academic year starts 1 July)
Application method
Submit online Forms A and B, recommendation forms and documents (PDF) and email to siint.recruit@gmail.com; review by departmental committee with interview or exam in some programmes

Funding

Funding
Thai programme tuition fee from 350,000 THB per academic year (approx.) plus 4,500 THB processing fee
