Overview

Fully accredited training in gynaecologic oncology with a dedicated research year followed by comprehensive inpatient, outpatient, surgical and systemic-therapy experience.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years
Start
1 August 2027 for the current fellowship cycle
Positions
1 fellow per year
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited
Department
Department of Obstetrics & Gynecology, Division of Gynecologic Oncology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed Obstetrics and Gynaecology residency; ERAS, NRMP and institutional requirements apply
International applicants
Limited
Visa support
Stanford GME permits J-1 (ECFMG-sponsored) and H-1B (in some circumstances, with GME approval); O-1 only in special cases if programme-funded; check with programme which visas it permits
Licence requirement
California medical licence (Medical Board of California) required; IMGs need ECFMG certification

How to apply

Deadline
6 April 2026 for the 1 August 2027 start (interview invitations from 11 May 2026; interviews summer 2026); next cycle dates not yet posted
Application method
ERAS application and NRMP Match; CREOG scores emailed to gynonc@stanford.edu

Funding

Funding
Paid; Stanford GME PGY-based house-staff salary (current table: PGY-4 $113,276.80, PGY-5 $119,558.40, PGY-6 $124,280.00 per year; base includes $12K housing)
