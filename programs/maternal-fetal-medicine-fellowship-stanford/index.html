Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed accredited Obstetrics and Gynaecology residency; ERAS and NRMP requirements apply
International applicants
Limited
Visa support
Stanford GME permits J-1 (ECFMG-sponsored) and H-1B (in some circumstances, with GME approval); O-1 only in special cases if programme-funded; check with programme which visas it permits
Licence requirement
California medical licence (Medical Board of California) required; IMGs need ECFMG certification
