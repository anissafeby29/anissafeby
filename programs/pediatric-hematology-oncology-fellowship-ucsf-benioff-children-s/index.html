Overview

A flexible pediatric hematology/oncology fellowship with San Francisco and Oakland tracks covering hematology, oncology and stem cell transplantation.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years (ABP subspecialty training)
Start
Institution policyMost UCSF residents/fellows are appointed for June-August start dates (off-cycle starts possible)
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
ACGME (ERAS/NRMP programme)
Department
Department of Pediatrics, UCSF School of Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed 2-3 years of accredited residency; board-eligible in pediatrics; 3 letters incl. programme director, MSPE, transcript, CV, personal statement
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs eligible if their school is in the World Directory/FAIMER and they hold a valid ECFMG certificate at application; non-US citizens need permanent residence, an appropriate visa or DACA
Visa support
Institution policyUCSF sponsors J-1 (ECFMG) and H-1B (incl. OPT) visas; H-1B/OPT require pre-approval by UCSF OGME; non-ACGME fellowships can take J-1 trainees only if ACGME Non-Standard Training (NST) recognised
Licence requirement
Institution policyCalifornia Postgraduate Training Licence (PTL) from the Medical Board of California required (full CA licence needed once accredited-training thresholds are reached)

How to apply

Deadline
Not stated on official page
Application method
ERAS (opens July); two tracks - 'Pediatric Hematology/Oncology-San Francisco' and '-Oakland'; apply to both, one interview day

Funding

Funding
Institution policySalaried on UCSF CIR collective-bargaining scale (rates effective 1 Oct 2025, incl. housing allowance): PGY4 $106,127; PGY5 $109,737; PGY6 $113,149; PGY7 $116,315; PGY8 $121,494; 2026-27 scale under union negotiation
