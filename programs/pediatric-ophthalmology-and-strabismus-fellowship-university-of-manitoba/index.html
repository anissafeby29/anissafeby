Overview

One-year pediatric ophthalmology and adult strabismus fellowship at HSC Children's Hospital including ROP, strabismus and pediatric intraocular surgery.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Medical degree from a WHO-recognised university; completed ophthalmology residency; English proficiency; good clinical and communication skills
International applicants
Yes - international applicants must meet University of Manitoba IELTS requirements
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Educational licence from the College of Physicians and Surgeons of Manitoba

How to apply

Deadline
20 September 2026 (for July 2027 start)
Application method
Email CV, three reference letters (<6 months old, on letterhead) and personal statement to ophthalmology@umanitoba.ca; panel interviews for shortlisted applicants
