Overview

Trains clinician-researchers in personalised cancer therapy, early-phase clinical trials and drug development.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 or 2 years
Start
1 July
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
MD Anderson GME programme (non-ACGME)
Department
Department of Investigational Cancer Therapeutics

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed haematology/oncology or medical oncology fellowship (US, Canada or foreign equivalent); eligible for Texas physician-in-training permit or licence; interest in drug development
International applicants
IMGs eligible with valid ECFMG certificate and USMLE Step 1 and Step 2 CK (each passed within 3 attempts) (MD Anderson GME eligibility page)
Visa support
J-1 visa (ECFMG-sponsored) is the required visa for international trainees needing sponsorship; H-1B not offered (MD Anderson GME eligibility page)
Licence requirement
Texas Medical Board physician-in-training permit or Texas medical licence

How to apply

Deadline
Applications open 1 July and close 30 September; interviews October-November
Application method
Online application via programme page
