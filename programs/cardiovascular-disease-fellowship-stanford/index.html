Overview

A three-year ACGME programme fostering academic careers with intensive invasive and noninvasive clinical training and required substantial research.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed ACGME-accredited internal medicine or medicine/pediatrics residency; passed USMLE Steps 1 and 2
International applicants
IMGs eligible with valid ECFMG certificate; J-1 only
Visa support
Only J-1 visas accepted
Licence requirement
Must be able to obtain a California medical licence

How to apply

Deadline
ERAS applications reviewed from July; interviews (virtual) on two dates each in September and October
Application method
ERAS; NRMP Match (physician-scientists may use the Stanford Translational Investigation Pathway)
