Overview

A one-year ACGME pediatric dermatology fellowship with two positions focused on consultative outpatient and inpatient care of infants, children and adolescents.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
12 months
Start
Institution policyMCWAH academic year begins 1 July
Positions
Two
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited
Department
Department of Dermatology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of an ACGME-accredited dermatology residency or equivalent international training
International applicants
Exceptional international candidates eligible for a full unrestricted Wisconsin licence are considered (ECFMG, USMLE Step 3 required), but the page states international applications are not being accepted this cycle
Visa support
Institution policyMCWAH accepts J-1 (ECFMG-sponsored); some programmes pursue H-1B (requires USMLE Step 3 passed) - check with programme; no permanent-residency sponsorship
Licence requirement
Wisconsin medical license before start
