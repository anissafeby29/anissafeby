Overview

Montefiore Einstein's Bronx programme appears in the Society of Gynecologic Oncology's national participating-program list for advanced multidisciplinary training in gynaecologic cancer care.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years
Start
Annual academic intake; confirm exact date with programme
Positions
1 per year
Accreditation
Listed by the Society of Gynecologic Oncology; confirm current ACGME status
Department
Not stated on official page

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed four years of an ACGME-accredited OBGYN residency in the US or Canada before start; USMLE Step 3 passed by January of start year
International applicants
Yes - foreign medical graduates accepted (with US/Canadian ACGME OBGYN residency)
Visa support
J-1 and H-1B sponsored, provided the $100,000 H-1B fee is not applicable
Licence requirement
USMLE Step 3 passed by January of start year to ensure licensure
