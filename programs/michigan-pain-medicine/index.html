Overview

Multidisciplinary pain training integrates medical, interventional, physical and behavioral approaches in university and VA settings.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed ACGME residency in anesthesiology or physical medicine and rehabilitation; other ACGME specialties considered by request. The page also specifies recognized medical-school requirements.
International applicants
Yes - foreign medical school graduates accepted only if the school is recognized by the Medical Board of California
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 sponsored by ECFMG (or permanent residence / qualifying EAD); other visas only in very limited circumstances, case by case at Program Director discretion with prospective GME Committee approval
Licence requirement
Institution policyState of Michigan medical licence and controlled substance licence (Educational Limited or Permanent) required; permanent-licence holders also need a personal DEA registration

How to apply

Deadline
ERAS opens 1 December for the following year; deadline 31 May; rolling review with virtual interviews late April-August
Application method
ERAS

Funding

Funding
Paid at the House Officer level equivalent to training (HOA contract); FY27 scale from 1 July 2026 ranges HO I $77,049 to HO IX $105,447
