Overview

Twelve-month clinical vascular surgery fellowship at UHN, Sunnybrook and St. Michael's for surgeons returning to academic positions, including advanced aortic work.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
12 months
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Unaccredited; does not lead to independent licensure in Ontario
Department
Department of Surgery, Division of Vascular Surgery

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed vascular surgery training in country of origin; fluent spoken and written English
International applicants
Designed for candidates who completed vascular training in their country of origin
Visa support
Institution policyinternational clinical fellows need an IRCC work permit; the PGME Office issues an LMIA-exempt offer of employment number and supports the work permit application
Licence requirement
Institution policyclinical fellows must hold a CPSO certificate of registration authorising postgraduate education (educational licence); research fellows do not need CPSO registration

How to apply

Deadline
Not stated on official page
Application method
Direct application (CV, case log, 3 references, letter of intent) to the Program Director's office

Funding

Funding
Institution policyclinical fellows must be funded (department or sponsoring agency); FEAC recommends pay at least equal to PGY1 on the PARO salary scale, and foreign nationals must be paid at a rate commensurate with a Canadian in the same role
