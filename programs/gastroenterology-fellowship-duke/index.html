Overview

A three-year general gastroenterology and hepatology programme with clinical and T32-supported research tracks across tertiary, VA and community hospitals.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
IMGs must have completed an ACGME-accredited Internal Medicine residency and be ABIM board-eligible in Internal Medicine.
International applicants
Institution policyYes - graduates of medical schools outside the US/Canada are eligible if they hold a valid ECFMG certificate or a full unrestricted US licence
Visa support
J-1 supported on clinical track; H-1B not supported; visa holders not eligible for the T32-funded Research Track.
Licence requirement
Institution policyNorth Carolina full medical licence or Resident Training License (RTL) required before clinical duties

How to apply

Deadline
Applications from July; interviews September-early October
Application method
ERAS; NRMP Match; three letters incl. one from residency Chair/Programme Director; USMLE Steps 1-3

Funding

Funding
Research track supported by T32 grant
