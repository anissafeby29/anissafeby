Overview

A one-year clinical–research fellowship (75% research / 25% clinical) in regional anaesthesia.

The programme

Training type
Research Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
2027–2028 academic year intake
Positions

Accreditation
McGill Postgraduate Medical Education (PGME) fellowship
Department
Department of Anesthesia, McGill University (McGill University Health Centre and affiliated hospitals)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Graduate of a medical school listed in the FAIMER directory; completed an anaesthesia programme of three or more years and obtained a specialty certificate in anaesthesia
International applicants
Yes — Regular Track positions open to Canadian and international applicants; French language training mandatory for fellows with no or limited conversational French
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Not stated on official page

How to apply

Deadline
2027–2028 applications opened 15 May 2026; deadline for most fellowships 1 September 2026 (see PGME Application Timelines)
Application method
Online application through the McGill Postgraduate Medical Education office; interviews held on Zoom

Funding

Funding
Candidates must secure funding in order to apply; self-funding not accepted; Quebec-trained residents may apply for RAMQ-funded fellowships
