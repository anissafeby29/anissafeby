Overview

Clinical fellowships across the full range of adult cardiac surgery at a centre performing 1,800 open-heart cases a year and Canada's leading valve, aortic arch, VAD and heart transplant centre.

The programme

Focus
Adult cardiac / aortic / transplant and MCS
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
12 months (may be extended)
Start
Institution policyUniversity of Toronto PGME academic year runs 1 July - 30 June (letters of appointment issued per academic year; off-cycle starts possible)
Positions
5–6 fellows per year
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Division of Cardiac Surgery

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Certified cardiac surgeon in Canada or country of origin, or passed MCCQE
International applicants
Open to Canadian and international surgeons; IMG fellows must complete a 4–12 week Pre-Entry Assessment Period (PEAP); TOEFL/TSE or TOEFL-iBT (93, speaking 24) if medical school instruction was not in English
Visa support
Institution policyinternational clinical fellows need an IRCC work permit; the PGME Office issues an LMIA-exempt offer of employment number and supports the work permit application
Licence requirement
Institution policyclinical fellows must hold a CPSO certificate of registration authorising postgraduate education (educational licence); research fellows do not need CPSO registration

Varies: some fellows self-funded, some sponsored by their country or external agency, some funded by the hospital division (set out in the job-offer letter)
