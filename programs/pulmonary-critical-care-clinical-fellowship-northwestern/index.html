Overview

A three-year combined PCCM fellowship meeting ABIM requirements in both specialties, with continuity clinics at Northwestern and Jesse Brown VA and research opportunities.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Enrolled in or completed ACGME-accredited internal medicine residency.
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs eligible with a valid ECFMG certificate, valid visa (J-1, H-1B or O-1) or permanent residence, and Illinois IDFPR licence
Visa support
ECFMG-sponsored J-1 (McGaw sponsoring institution); J-1 fees paid by applicant
Licence requirement
Institution policyIllinois (IDFPR) temporary medical licence required to train (processed by McGaw GME; allow ~60 days)
