Overview

A one-year ACGME-accredited addiction psychiatry fellowship (accredited since 1998) at the University of Hawaii with one position per year.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (PGY-5)
Start
Not stated on official page

1 PGY-5 position per year
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited (ACGME programme ID 4011421033, per AAMC ERAS participating-programmes list)
Department
Department of Psychiatry (Hawaii Residency Programs)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Successful completion of 4 years of psychiatry residency; USMLE Steps 1-3 or COMLEX Levels 1-3 passed
International applicants
IMGs accepted with standard ECFMG certification; non-US citizens must hold a J-1 visa
Visa support
J-1 only (non-US citizens must hold a J-1 visa)
