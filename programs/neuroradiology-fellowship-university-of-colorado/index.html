Overview

An ACGME-approved one-year neuroradiology fellowship at University of Colorado Hospital with rotations at Children's Hospital Colorado and Denver Health.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
Institution policyCU School of Medicine GME training agreements run 1 July - 30 June (sample 2025-26 agreement: 7/1/2025 - 6/30/2026)
Positions
5 per year
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited
Department
Department of Radiology, University of Colorado School of Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed radiology residency; ABR certified or eligible.
International applicants
Institution policyGraduates of non-US/Canadian schools eligible with valid ECFMG certificate (or full Colorado licence); must hold or obtain legal work authorization; USMLE Step 3 required before starting a fellowship
Visa support
Institution policyClinical J-1 via ECFMG is the standard route; H-1B for residents/fellows only if CU GME agrees to waive its J-1 requirement
Licence requirement
Colorado medical license required before start (stated for neuroradiology).

How to apply

Deadline
Not stated on official page

ERAS; NRMP neuroradiology match.

Funding
Institution policySalaried on CU GME base stipend scale 2026-27: PGY4 $90,960.59; PGY5 $94,589.52; PGY6 $97,824.22; PGY7 $102,995.34
