Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
International physicians; medical hands-on practice requires full participation in the SIF-Prime Course and permission from the Korean Ministry of Health and Welfare
International applicants
Yes - Severance International Fellowship (SIF) for international physicians; developing-country physicians (OECD DAC list) given preference for discounts/waivers
Visa support
Visa support included in administration fee; visa extension fees waived
Licence requirement
Observership requires no Korean licence; hands-on practice requires Korean MOHW permission via the SIF-Prime Course

How to apply

Deadline
Fees payable 2 weeks before training start
Application method
Apply via the Yonsei Institute for Global Health (YIGH) Severance International Fellowship application

Funding

Funding
Training fee: observership US$2,000/month, hands-on US$3,000/month (discounts/waivers decided by department; MOU institutions, YUHS recommendations, Ministry of Health letters eligible); admin fee US$100 (observation) or US$200 (hands-on)
