Paediatric General Radiology Fellowship

The Hospital for Sick Children (SickKids)

RadiologyPaediatric radiologyClinical Fellowship
Destination

Toronto, Ontario, Canada
Open official programme page ↗Compare saved →

Overview

A clinical fellowship in one of the world's busiest paediatric radiology departments (~130,000 examinations a year) covering radiographs, fluoroscopy, ultrasound, CT and MRI in children.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (some flexibility for 1- or 2-year programmes)
Start
July (sometimes also January)
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Accredited by the Royal College of Physicians and Surgeons of Canada as a one-year Paediatric Radiology Residency Program (Competence by Design since July 2024)
Department
Department of Diagnostic and Interventional Radiology, The Hospital for Sick Children

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed a diagnostic radiology residency programme, board-certified as a radiologist in country of origin, and meet minimum eligibility requirements for CPSO licensure; advanced English proficiency (assessed at interview).
International applicants
Open to both Canadian and international applicants (fellows from Canada, Chile, Argentina, Brazil, Switzerland, Saudi Arabia, Oman, Iran, India, Thailand, Indonesia and Australia as of May 2026).
Visa support
Institution policyinternational clinical fellows need an IRCC work permit; the PGME Office issues an LMIA-exempt offer of employment number and supports the work permit application
Licence requirement
Must meet minimum eligibility requirements for licensure with the College of Physicians and Surgeons of Ontario (CPSO).

How to apply

Deadline
Applications open 1 September 2026; all documents due 16 October 2026
Application method
Apply directly to SickKids: completed application form, CV and letter of intent via the online link; three reference letters dated 2026 addressed to the Paediatric Radiology Program Director; remote interviews.

Funding

Funding
Funded and sponsored positions available; 20 working days paid vacation; some conference travel funding.
