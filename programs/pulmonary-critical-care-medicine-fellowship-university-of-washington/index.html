Overview

Trains academic leaders, scientists and scholars in pulmonary and critical care medicine through physician-scientist, clinician-educator and clinical tracks.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
Not stated on official page
Start
Institution policyUW GME academic year runs 1 July - 30 June (standard start 1 July)
Positions
2026 season: 3 physician-scientist, 1 clinician-educator, 2 clinical track
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Division of Pulmonary, Critical Care & Sleep Medicine, Department of Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed ACGME- or RCPSC-accredited Internal Medicine residency; must be eligible for an NIH T32 stipend.
International applicants
Institution policyYes - international medical graduates must hold valid ECFMG certification as a condition of eligibility for fellowship application; non-US citizens need permanent residence, a valid EAD, or eligibility for J-1, F-1 OPT or H-1B
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 sponsored by ECFMG (ACGME programmes and non-ACGME programmes approved as ACGME non-standard training); H-1B only with prior GME Office (and ISO) approval before ranking/offer, for limited eligibility categories and requires USMLE Step 3; F-1 OPT accepted; no O-1 or permanent-residence sponsorship
Licence requirement
Institution policyactive Washington State provider licence required throughout training (limited/resident licence or full physician licence as specified by the programme)

How to apply

Deadline
1 August (all materials)
Application method
ERAS (one track only); signals encouraged; NRMP Match (Physician-scientist 1918156F0, Clinician-educator 1918156F1, Clinical 1918156F2)

Funding

Funding
Primarily supported by NIH (T32 stipend eligibility required)
