Overview

An independent IR residency accepting two residents a year (ESIR graduates may finish in one year) at UC Medical Center, leading to IR/DR dual certification.

The programme

Training type
Independent Residency

1–2 years (1 year with ESIR)

Not stated on official page

ACGME-accredited

Department of Radiology, University of Cincinnati (UC Medical Center)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of an ACGME-accredited DR residency in the US or abroad, and ABR board certification/eligibility at the start of training.
International applicants
ECFMG certificate requested if applicable; ABR eligibility required.
