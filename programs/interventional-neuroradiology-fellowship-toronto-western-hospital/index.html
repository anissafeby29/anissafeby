Overview

A high-volume interventional neuroradiology fellowship founded in 1984 at Toronto Western Hospital (~1,800 INR procedures a year), training fellows from neuroradiology, neurosurgery and neurology backgrounds in the full spectrum of neurovascular disease.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1–2 years depending on background
Start
Staggered starts (one fellow 1 June, others 1 July and 1 August)
Positions
3, sometimes 4, positions annually
Accreditation
Held in accordance with UEMS recommendations and WFITN training guidelines; University of Toronto INR fellowship certificate
Department
Joint Department of Medical Imaging (UHN, Sinai Health, Women's College Hospital) – Toronto Western Hospital

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Proficient in written and spoken English; completed an accredited radiology training programme; certified by the Royal College of Physicians and Surgeons of Canada in Diagnostic Imaging, American Board of Radiology certified/eligible, or equivalent qualifications (CPSO uses the GMC directory to recognise specialty certification).
International applicants
Over a hundred fellows from all over the world have graduated; trainees are generally selected two years in advance.
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Trainees must be PGME approved and CPSO licensed; paperwork can take up to 9 months.

How to apply

Deadline
2028–2029 cycle: online applications opened 6 April 2026 and closed 9 August 2026; reference letters due 23 August 2026; interviews September 2026 – January 2027.
Application method
Online Fellowship Application System (University of Toronto Department of Medical Imaging): CV, candidate letter, proof of certification and three reference letters (one from the residency programme director), all in PDF; up to 2 programmes on one form.

Funding

Funding
Applicants must secure CAD $85,564.01/annum as training salary; positions are Funded (supervisor, hospital, grant, endowment or international agency) or Sponsored (agencies from Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, UAE only); self-funding from personal funds is not acceptable.
