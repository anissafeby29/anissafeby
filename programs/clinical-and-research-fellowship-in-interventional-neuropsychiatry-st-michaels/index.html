Clinical and Research Fellowship in Interventional Neuropsychiatry

St. Michael's Hospital (Unity Health Toronto) / University of Toronto

PsychiatryNeuropsychiatryClinical and Research Fellowship
Destination
CAN
Toronto, Ontario, Canada
Open official programme page ↗

Overview

Training in IV ketamine and psychedelic-assisted therapies, rTMS, ECT, emerging neurostimulation and digital therapeutics for treatment-resistant neuropsychiatric conditions.

The programme

Training type
Clinical and Research Fellowship
Duration
Minimum 1 year (longer negotiable)
Start
July 1 preferred (alternative dates negotiable)
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
University of Toronto PGME-registered fellowship (not RCPSC-accredited subspecialty training)
Department
Not stated on official page

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Medical degree from a recognised school and specialist designation in psychiatry; must meet CPSO minimum licensure eligibility
International applicants
Yes — internationally trained specialists eligible if they meet CPSO requirements; English proficiency results may be requested; government-sponsored trainees from Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, UAE and Saudi Arabia apply via STAR
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
CPSO educational (or independent) licence eligibility required

How to apply

Deadline
November 30 (later applications case-by-case)
Application method
University of Toronto Psychiatry Fellowship Program online application (documents.med.utoronto.ca) with CV, MD and specialty certificates and 3 references (one from residency programme director)

Funding

Funding
Funded position; minimum stipend CA$65,000 per year
