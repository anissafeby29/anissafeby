Overview

Paediatric orthopaedic fellowship at BC Children's Hospital, which sees about 20,000 outpatients a year, with an optional two-year Master's track.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 or 2 years (2-year option includes a Master's degree)
Start
July or August
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Department of Orthopaedics, Faculty of Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Must have completed all requirements to be registered as a specialist in home country (approved residency)
International applicants
Yes - open to specialists registered in their home country
Visa support
Institution policy (UBC PGME): foreign trainees must hold an IRCC work permit for the whole training period; the sponsoring department/PGME submits an offer of employment to IRCC and the fellow applies under the LMIA-exempt category
Licence requirement
Must qualify for licensure with the College of Physicians and Surgeons of BC

How to apply

Deadline
31 October 2026 (for 2028/29 intake; window opens 15 April two years before start)
Application method
Online UBC Orthopaedics fellowship application

Funding
