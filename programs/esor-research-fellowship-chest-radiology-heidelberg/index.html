Overview

A 12-month research fellowship at the Heidelberg TLRC imaging platform working on airway disease, lung cancer, inflammation/infection and pulmonary vascular disease projects using CT, MRI and PET hybrid imaging.

The programme

Training type
Research Fellowship
Duration
12 months (1 October – 1 October)
Start
1 October (2025/26 cycle: 1 October 2025 – 1 October 2026)
Positions
1 place
Accreditation
Certificate from ESR/ESOR and Heidelberg University Medical Center on completion; 'Dr. med.' or 'Dr. sc. hum.' obtainable if academic requirements are met.
Department
Diagnostic and Interventional Radiology – Translational Lung Research Center (TLRC) Imaging Platform

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Recently board-certified radiologist (within three years, under age 36) residing in Europe and a full ESR member, pursuing chest radiology subspecialisation; advanced English and basic German required.
International applicants
Restricted to radiologists residing in Europe; candidates should apply to institutions outside their country of residence/work.
Visa support
The fellow is responsible for his/her own visa, work permit and insurance; the training centre helps with organisational details.
Licence requirement
Not stated on official page

How to apply

Deadline
Applications currently closed (per ESOR page).
Application method
Email application to ESOR as a single PDF (motivation letter, ESOR CV, recommendation letters, certifications, language certificates, publications, ESR membership proof, passport); Skype interview.

Funding

Funding
Grant of €23,000 from the training centre, paid monthly via ESOR.
