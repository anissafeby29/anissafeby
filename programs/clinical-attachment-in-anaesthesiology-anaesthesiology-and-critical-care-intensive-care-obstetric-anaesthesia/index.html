Overview

Clinical attachment modules in anaesthesiology and critical care, intensive care, obstetric anaesthesia and paediatric anaesthesia at UMMC.

The programme

Focus
Anaesthesia, intensive care and obstetric anaesthesia
Training type
Clinical Attachment (observership or limited hands-on)
Duration
Observation: up to 90 days; hands-on: 1-12 months
Start
Institution policyNo fixed intake; training start date is arranged per applicant (applications due at least 6 months before the start date for non-Malaysians in fellowship/subspecialty training, 2 months for clinical attachments)
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Department of Anaesthesiology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Limited clinical training or clinical observation only; maximum 12 months. General: Master's degree/clinical master or equivalent recognised by the Malaysian Medical Council (MMC); registration with MMC; Professional Visit Pass (PLIK) approval; supported by Head of Clinical Department; approved by UMMC Credentialing and Privileging Committee
International applicants
Yes - UMMC offers its Clinical Fellowship Programme to both local and overseas candidates
Visa support
Professional Visit Pass (PLIK) approved by the Immigration Department of Malaysia
Licence requirement
Registration with the Malaysian Medical Council

How to apply

Deadline
Non-Malaysians: apply at least 6 months before training start date; accept offer within 1 month; observation attachments: at least 2 months before start
Application method
Apply online via the UMMC Fellowship/Training Attachment Programme e-services portal (register and log in)

Funding

Funding
Non-Malaysian: observation RM2,500 (1 day-2 weeks) or RM5,000 (15 days-4 weeks); hands-on RM36,000 per year prorated; plus RM100 management fee
