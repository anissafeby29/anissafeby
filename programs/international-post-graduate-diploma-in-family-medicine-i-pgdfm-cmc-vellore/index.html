Overview

Two-year blended-learning family medicine diploma equipping general practitioners in the developing world with continuing, comprehensive primary care skills ('refer less, resolve more').

The programme

Training type
Postgraduate Diploma (blended distance learning)
Duration
2 years (blended: online modules, monthly Zoom sessions, onsite contact programmes of about 2 weeks)
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Up to 50 seats per year
Accreditation
Accredited distance education initiative by ICMDA, CMC Vellore Distance Education Unit and Loma Linda University; not equivalent to MD/DNB and not recognised by the Medical Council of India
Department
Distance Education Unit

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Valid MBBS or equivalent medical degree and a valid medical licence to practise
International applicants
Yes — SAARC, NRI and foreign nationals can apply; aimed at doctors in the developing world
