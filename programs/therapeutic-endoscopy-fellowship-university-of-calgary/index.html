Overview

An academically oriented ERCP/EUS and luminal therapeutics fellowship with about 1,600 ERCPs and 1,300 EUS a year and mandatory research.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1-2 years (two-year candidates with concurrent graduate degree prioritised)
Start
1 July
Positions
1-2 per year
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Division of Gastroenterology and Hepatology (Peter Lougheed Centre)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Training in basic endoscopy (gastroscopy and colonoscopy) required; typically trained gastroenterologists, general surgeons also welcomed.

How to apply

Deadline
31 March the year prior to fellowship start; interviews late May/early June
Application method
Application documents plus 3 reference letters sent directly by referees to the GI programme administrator

Funding

Funding
Fully funded by the Pentax Advanced Therapeutic Endoscopy Fellowship Award, up to CAD 75,000/year
