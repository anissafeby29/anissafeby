Overview

65-week full-time PHCC family medicine certificate programme enhancing clinical and academic competencies for doctors pursuing primary care careers in Qatar.

The programme

Training type
Certificate Training Programme
Duration
65 weeks (1.5 years), full time
Start
1 September (first cohort started 1 September 2025)
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
PHCC certificate; supports licensing in Qatar's government and private sectors

PHCC Family Medicine training

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
MBBS or equivalent with at least one year of internship; must meet Qatar residency application criteria (IFOM 72 or USMLE pass plus English exam); not enrolled in or accepted to a Qatar residency
International applicants
Not explicitly stated; required documents include passport copy and Qatar ID (QID) copy
