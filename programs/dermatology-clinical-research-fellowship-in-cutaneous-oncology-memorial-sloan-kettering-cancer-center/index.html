Overview

Highly selective 12–24-month clinical research fellowship (since 2003) in melanoma, cutaneous T-cell lymphoma and skin cancer, emphasising epidemiology, imaging technologies and AI-supported research.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of an accredited dermatology residency
International applicants
Institution policyInternational trainees accepted with J-1 (ECFMG) sponsorship, or H-1B only if already in the US in a transferable status; eligibility must be confirmed with the programme coordinator
Visa support
Institution policyMSK sponsors GME trainees as J-1 (ECFMG Alien Physician) or H-1B; H-1B only for applicants already in the US on H-1B in GME or in a status allowing in-country change/extension - MSK will not file H-1B petitions for trainees residing outside the US; O-1 rarely, needs GME review 6 months ahead
Licence requirement
Institution policyMSK does not require licensure for trainees in ACGME-accredited programmes unless the programme director specifies; trainees in non-ACGME programmes must hold a NY licence or limited permit; work outside the training programme requires a full NY licence
