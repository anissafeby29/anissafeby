Overview

One-year oncologic imaging fellowship at the Montpellier Cancer Institute focusing on gynaecological malignancy, peritoneal carcinomatosis, pancreatic, sarcoma and rectal cancer imaging plus funded tumour-response research.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
European radiologists within first three years after certification wishing to subspecialise in oncologic imaging; research track record in image analysis; advanced English (French advantageous); valid ESR membership; online interview
International applicants
Limited to European radiologists (per ESOR page)
Visa support
Fellow responsible for work permit/visa, insurance and accommodation; training centre helps with administrative tasks
Licence requirement
Not stated on official page
