Overview

A one-year emergency-trauma radiology fellowship engaging fellows in real-time trauma imaging at Ryder Trauma Center (about 4,000 trauma patients a year).

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed a DR residency; passed USMLE Steps 1, 2CS, 2CK and 3 (eligible); ECFMG for foreign graduates.
International applicants
Foreign medical graduates are eligible with ECFMG certification; Jackson Memorial Hospital sponsors J-1 visas (ECFMG) for qualified applicants.
Visa support
J-1 (ECFMG-sponsored) for qualified applicants.
