Overview

An AFC diploma programme creating PTM physician specialists through ED, online medical consultation and HEMS experiences, academic sessions and a scholarly project.

The programme

Focus
Emergency medical services
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
12 months (staff physicians)
Start
1 July
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
First RCPSC-accredited Prehospital and Transport Medicine AFC Diploma programme in Canada
Department
Department of Emergency Medicine, University of Ottawa

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Institution policymedical degree from a WDOMS-listed school recognised in Canada; completed specialty training; RCPSC/CFPC or recognised international certification (or exam-eligible); English proficiency (e.g. IELTS 7 each band); fellowship must be in the same discipline as specialist recognition
International applicants
Yes — listed among uOttawa fellowships accepting International Medical Graduates
Visa support
Institution policyinternational trainees need an IRCC work permit (LMIA-exempt); the uOttawa PGME/immigration advisor provides the documents needed to apply (CPSO letter of eligibility and LMIA-exemption number)
Licence requirement
Eligible for CPSO licensure in Ontario and CMPA liability coverage; international clinical fellows complete a 4–12 week Pre-Entry Assessment Program

How to apply

Deadline
15 August for July start
Application method
Letter of interest, CV and 3 references via uOttawa fellowship application system

Funding

Funding
Institution policyinternational fellows without Canadian status must be funded by an official home-country sponsor (government agency or health-related charity; private funds not accepted) unless fellowship funding is available through an Ottawa hospital
