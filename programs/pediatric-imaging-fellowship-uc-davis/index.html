Overview

A one-year paediatric imaging fellowship at UC Davis Children's Hospital with ten months of core paediatric imaging plus nuclear, neuro and cardiac paediatric time.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of an ACGME-accredited PGY-1 in the US or Canada and DR residency; California licence eligibility.
International applicants
Requires residency completed in the US or Canada.
Visa support
Institution policyClinical J-1 (ECFMG) or EAD only; UC Davis Health does not sponsor H-1B visas for residents and fellows
Licence requirement
Eligible for a California medical license.
