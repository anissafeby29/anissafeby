Overview

Cardiac, thoracic and major vascular anesthesia training offers individualized experience in echocardiography, research and simulation.

How to apply

Deadline
31 March of the year preceding programme start
Application method
SF Match (SCA Adult Cardiothoracic Anesthesiology Fellowship Match) with ITE scores and ~3 letters

Funding

Funding
Paid; Mayo Clinic GME stipend by graduate level (effective 11 March 2026: e.g. Graduate Level 5 $89,036/yr); benefits include 20 working days' vacation
