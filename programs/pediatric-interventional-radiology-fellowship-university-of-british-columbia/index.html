Overview

Paediatric IR fellowship at BC Children's Hospital for those who have completed adult IR or paediatric diagnostic radiology fellowship.

The programme

Focus
Paediatric interventional radiology
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (a second year possible with Fellowship Director approval; third year only under extenuating circumstances, subject to CPSBC approval)
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
1 fully funded position
Accreditation
UBC clinical fellowship under CPSBC policy (not an RCPSC-accredited residency)
Department
UBC Department of Radiology, Faculty of Medicine — BC Children's Hospital

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of adult interventional radiology or paediatric diagnostic radiology fellowship
International applicants
Yes — all qualified applicants encouraged to apply; Canadian citizens and permanent residents given priority
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Eligibility for licensure by the College of Physicians and Surgeons of British Columbia

How to apply

Deadline
Application period for the 2028–2029 cycle has closed
Application method
UBC Radiology online Fellowship Application Form (Step-by-Step Application Guide); referees email letters to ubcrad.fellowship@ubc.ca; maximum 2 subspecialties per academic year (IR applicants may apply to IR only)

Funding

Funding
Funded and/or sponsored/self-funded positions as listed: 1 fully funded position
