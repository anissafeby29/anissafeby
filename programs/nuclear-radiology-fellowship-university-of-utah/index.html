Overview

A one-year ACGME nuclear radiology fellowship training diagnostic radiologists in nuclear medicine, molecular imaging and radiopharmaceutical therapies, including PET/CT and SPECT/CT in oncology, cardiology and neurology.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Must meet Utah medical licensure requirements and be a graduate of an LCME-accredited US/Canadian school, an AOA-accredited US osteopathic college, or a non-US school with a valid ECFMG certificate before appointment.
International applicants
IMGs with a valid ECFMG certificate are eligible to apply; visa policy not stated on this page.
