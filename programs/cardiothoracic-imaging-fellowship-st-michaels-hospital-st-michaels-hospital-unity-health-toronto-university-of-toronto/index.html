Overview

Cardiothoracic imaging fellowship at St. Michael's Hospital, home to a large Heart and Vascular Program and structural heart (TAVI) programme.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (July 1 – June 30)
Start
Institution policyUniversity of Toronto PGME academic year runs 1 July - 30 June (letters of appointment issued per academic year; off-cycle starts possible)
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
University of Toronto PGME clinical fellowship (non-accredited by RCPSC)
Department
Department of Medical Imaging, Temerty Faculty of Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Proficient in written and spoken English; completed accredited radiology training; RCPSC Diagnostic Imaging certification, ABR certified/eligible, or equivalent qualification (CPSO uses GMC CCT/specialty recognition directory)
International applicants
Yes — international applicants accepted if funded or sponsored; self-funded/personal-fund arrangements not acceptable under PGME guidelines
Visa support
Sponsored trainees from Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, UAE register via STAR with a prospective funding letter; visa process handled through U of T PGME (details not stated on page)
Licence requirement
CPSO postgraduate education licence

How to apply

Deadline
2028–2029 cycle closed (online application April 6 – August 9, 2026; references by August 23, 2026); interviews September 2026 – January 2027
Application method
U of T Department of Medical Imaging Online Fellowship Application System (up to 2 programmes on one form; CV, letter to Program Director, proof of certification, 3 reference letters)

Funding

Funding
Fellows must secure CAD 85,564.01/annum training salary; some positions funded, others require external funding (host, hospital, grant, or international sponsoring agency)
