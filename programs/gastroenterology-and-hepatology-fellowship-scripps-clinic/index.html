Overview

San Diego's oldest GI fellowship (since 1959), a three-year ACGME programme at Scripps Clinic and Scripps Green Hospital preparing fellows for board certification in gastroenterology and transplant hepatology.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions

Accreditation
ACGME-accredited

Scripps Graduate Medical Education - Division of Gastroenterology and Hepatology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed or expected completion of an ACGME-accredited residency; meet licensure requirements including a California Physician's and Surgeon's License; ECFMG certificate if applicable
International applicants
IMGs may apply with ECFMG certificate but the programme does not sponsor J-1 or H-1 visas
Visa support
No - Scripps Clinic does not sponsor J-1 or H-1 visas
Licence requirement
California Physician's and Surgeon's License
