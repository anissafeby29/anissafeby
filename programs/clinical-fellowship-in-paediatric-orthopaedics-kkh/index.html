Overview

Six-month fellowship in elective and emergency paediatric orthopaedic management.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
6 months
Start
January, April, July or October
Positions
2 Fellows per 6-month block; intakes January, April, July and October
Accreditation
Certificate of fellowship
Department
Department of Orthopaedic Surgery

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
General orthopaedic surgeon intending to specialise in paediatric orthopaedics; from a government-supported institution and/or academic medical centre affiliated with a medical school (not wholly for-profit private institutions); financially self-sufficient
International applicants
Yes - overseas fellows from eligible institutions
Visa support
Not stated on official page
