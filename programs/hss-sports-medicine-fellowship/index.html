Overview

ACGME, AOSSM and AANA accredited sports medicine fellowship at the HSS Sports Medicine Institute offering clinical experience in the recognition and management of athletic injuries.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (optional two-year research and clinical track)
Start
1 August (next positions 2027-2028)
Positions
7 ACGME-accredited positions plus 1 research position per year
Accreditation
ACGME, AOSSM and AANA accredited
Department
Department of Orthopaedic Surgery
