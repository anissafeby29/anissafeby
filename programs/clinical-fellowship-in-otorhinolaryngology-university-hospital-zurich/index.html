Overview

One-year clinical fellowship in neurotology, phoniatrics or audiology for trained ENT specialists at senior physician level, integrated into the USZ ENT team including night and weekend duties.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
Ideally 1 March or 1 September
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Not part of the FMH/SIWF official training programme; USZ departmental certificate awarded
Department
Department of Otorhinolaryngology – Head and Neck Surgery

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
ENT specialist training completed in Switzerland or in a country whose certificate is recognised as equivalent by MEBEKO (and SIWF if applicable); senior-physician level; German at least C1
International applicants
Open to foreign-trained ENT specialists with MEBEKO-recognised equivalent certification and C1 German
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Recognition of specialist title by MEBEKO
