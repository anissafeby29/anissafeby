Overview

Individualised global ophthalmology fellowship with 6-12 months in the field at Moran Global Outreach partner institutions.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Prior US fellowship training in cornea, glaucoma, retina or uveitis preferred but not required
International applicants
Department page: residencies must have been completed in the US and, because training occurs at the federally funded VA, only US citizens can be hired for paid positions; observational-only fellowships available for foreign graduates via Moran Global Outreach
Visa support
No - US citizens only for paid positions
Licence requirement
Utah medical licence, Utah controlled substance licence and DEA licence required
