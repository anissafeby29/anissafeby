Overview

A PEM fellowship at a Level I Pediatric Trauma Center with an integrated Division of Transport Medicine, preparing future leaders in clinical care, teaching, advocacy, administration and research.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completing or completed a pediatrics or emergency medicine residency; 3-4 letters of recommendation; fellows provide backup call
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs accepted via ECFMG J-1 (USMLE Step 1 and Step 2 CK, ECFMG certification, Ministry of Health Statement of Need); H-1B case by case
Visa support
Institution policyChildren's National processes ECFMG J-1 (Alien Physician) visas and accepts F-1 OPT EAD; H-1B considered case by case depending on current federal requirements
Licence requirement
Institution policyFellows may not start training at Children's National until they hold a full DC licence or the applicable DC training licence
