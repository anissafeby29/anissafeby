Neonatal Hemodynamics and Targeted Neonatal Echocardiography (NHTNE) Subspecialty Fellowship

BC Women's Hospital & Health Centre / University of British Columbia

Overview

One-year fully funded fellowship at BC Women's Hospital NICU (about 7,200 births a year) training neonatologists in neonatal hemodynamics and targeted neonatal echocardiography.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (3 months echocardiography lab, 9 months NICU)
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Designed per Royal College of Physicians and Surgeons of Canada Area of Focused Competence (AFC) in NHTNE training requirements
Department
Division of Neonatology (with Division of Pediatric Cardiology)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of a clinical fellowship in neonatal-perinatal medicine from an accredited Canadian programme or equivalent; eligibility for CPSBC licensure; preference for foundational neonatal cardiac ultrasound training
International applicants
Equivalent (non-Canadian) neonatal-perinatal training accepted per call for applications
Visa support
Institution policy (UBC PGME): foreign trainees must hold an IRCC work permit for the whole training period; the sponsoring department/PGME submits an offer of employment to IRCC and the fellow applies under the LMIA-exempt category
Licence requirement
Eligibility for licensure with CPSBC

How to apply

Deadline
31 August 2025 for the 2026-2027 intake (annual call)
Application method
Letter of intent, abbreviated CV and letter of support emailed as a single PDF to the programme

Funding

Funding
Fully funded at the clinical fellow level
