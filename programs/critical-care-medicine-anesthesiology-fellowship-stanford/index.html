Overview

Multidisciplinary intensive-care training with one- and two-year options tailored to prior training and academic goals.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Anaesthesiology or internal medicine residency; duration depends on board requirements and research interests
International applicants
Yes - Stanford Health Care welcomes IMGs to its sponsored training programmes (approved visa and Medical Board of California licensure required)
Visa support
Stanford GME permits J-1 (ECFMG; most common) and H-1B (varies by department); IMGs must hold an approved visa and California licensure; check with programme which visas it permits
Licence requirement
Institution policyMedical Board of California Postgraduate Training License required within 180 days of starting an ACGME programme (USMLE Steps 1 & 2 CK passed); non-ACGME programmes require a full California licence by the start date
