Cardiothoracic and Vascular Anaesthesia Fellowship

University Hospitals Leuven

AnaesthesiologyCardiothoracic anaesthesiaClinical Fellowship
Destination
BEL
Leuven, Flemish Brabant, Belgium
Open official programme page ↗

Overview

Supervised cardiothoracic and vascular anaesthesia training, including transplantation and perioperative echocardiography.

The programme

Focus
Adult cardiothoracic anaesthesia
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
12 consecutive months
Start
1 October (programme runs 1 October - 30 September)
Positions
2 per year
Accreditation
EACTA fellowship programme; completion acknowledged by department with EACTA (not recognised by national regulatory authority)
Department
Department of Anesthesiology, University Hospitals Leuven

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Board certification by commencement, Belgian registration and Dutch or B2 English; non-EU appointments are limited.
International applicants
Not stated on official page
Visa support
Not sponsored - any work visa must be obtained by the fellow at own expense
Licence requirement
Belgian national medical registration, obtained by the fellow at own expense before patient care

How to apply

Deadline
Not stated on official page
Application method
Submit a cover letter, CV, degrees and three references to the programme directors.

Funding

Funding
Paid; employment contract with host centre, approx. EUR 2,500 net monthly salary; no accommodation or travel provided
