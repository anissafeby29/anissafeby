Overview

A four-year programme for international medical graduates combining year-long OHSU fellowships (body, cardiothoracic, neuro, MSK, nuclear, emergency, paediatric) toward ABR certification eligibility.

The programme

Focus
Diagnostic and interventional radiology (subspecialty rotations)
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
4 years (year-to-year renewal)
Start
July (recruiting for July 2027)
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
ABR Alternate Pathway
Department
Department of Diagnostic Radiology, OHSU School of Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
International medical graduates pursuing the ABR Alternate Pathway; applicants seeking or holding J-1 visas are not eligible
International applicants
Designed for IMGs; recruiting for July 2027 start (first year in cardiothoracic or MSK imaging).
Visa support
Recruitment paused for applicants needing new H-1B visas; current H-1B holders and non-visa applicants accepted; J-1 seekers addressed separately on page.
Licence requirement
Oregon medical license (generally MD Postgraduate License) required.
