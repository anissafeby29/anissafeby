Overview

One-month glaucoma observership at listed European centres (e.g. Vienna, Leuven, Rotterdam, Madrid, Nottingham), with an EUR 1,500 EGS grant for European residents.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility

International applicants
Partly - EGS grant limited to Europe-resident applicants; self-funded observerships at EGS-accredited centres open irrespective of country of origin (arranged directly with host, no EGS certificate)
