Overview

A one-year PET/molecular imaging fellowship at UW–Madison with access to internationally renowned imaging research programmes.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Graduate of an ACGME or RCPSC-accredited diagnostic radiology residency; Wisconsin medical license before start; Canadian graduates must be LMCC and hold H-1B.
International applicants
Unable to accept or sponsor J-1 physician status; candidates must be H-1B (or E-3) eligible if sponsorship is needed.
Visa support
Unable to accept or sponsor J-1 physician status; candidates must be H-1B (or E-3) eligible if sponsorship is needed.
Licence requirement
Must possess a Wisconsin medical license before beginning fellowship.

How to apply

Deadline
Full consideration deadline 6 December 2025 (prior cycle); applications considered until filled.
Application method
Apply through the UW–Madison Job Site posting (CV, cover letter) plus UW Radiology Fellowship Application, USMLE scores and three letters to the fellowship coordinator; SCARD process.

Funding

Funding
Appointed as Clinical Instructor; moonlighting available.
