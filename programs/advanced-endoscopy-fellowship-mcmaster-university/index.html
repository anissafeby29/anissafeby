Overview

A one-year ERCP/EUS fellowship for future therapeutic endoscopists in biliary and pancreatic disorders, targeting at least 800 procedures (600 ERCP and/or 200 EUS).

Eligibility
Graduates of a gastroenterology or general surgery residency in Canada, the US or equivalent abroad, competent in core endoscopy and colonoscopy.

Equivalent graduates from abroad eligible; must comply with McMaster PGME requirements.
