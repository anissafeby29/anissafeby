Neurotology Fellowship

University of Cincinnati / Cincinnati Children's Hospital

ENT / OtolaryngologyOtology & neurotologyClinical Fellowship
Destination
USA
Cincinnati, Ohio, United States
Open official programme page ↗

Overview

Comprehensive neurotology training spanning multidisciplinary skull base, complex otology, vestibular and paediatric neurotology, with about one research day a week.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
2 years
Start
1 July
Positions
One fellow every other year
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited
Department
Department of Otolaryngology–Head and Neck Surgery

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of an ACGME-accredited otolaryngology–head and neck surgery residency
International applicants
ACGME neurotology fellowship: requires completion of an ACGME-accredited (or RCPSC) otolaryngology–HNS residency; not open to non-US/Canada-trained otolaryngologists
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
USMLE Steps 1–3

How to apply

Deadline
1 June

Funding

Funding
Per PGY level; health insurance, 3 weeks vacation, educational funds
