Overview

Siriraj's international fellowship pathway in ophthalmology subspecialties, coordinated by the Faculty's international postgraduate training office.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed specialty training with a valid medical licence from country of origin held for at least 3 years; English proficiency meeting Mahidol minimum; 2 referees
International applicants
Yes - Siriraj International Residency/Fellowship Training accepts foreign doctors; international trainees advised to study Thai for 3-6 months before the course
Visa support
Yes - International Relations Office sends acceptance letter with tuition invoice and visa support document
Licence requirement
Not stated on official page

How to apply

Deadline
Apply at least 6 months before start (some programmes year-round)
Application method
Download department application forms (Form A, Form B, recommendation forms) and email to siint.recruit@gmail.com; online interview may be required

Funding

Funding
Self-funded: tuition generally THB 350,000 per academic year plus processing fee; no monthly stipend; dormitory rental waived
