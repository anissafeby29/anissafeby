Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Successful completion of a US residency programme; all three steps of USMLE or COMLEX; IMGs welcome (J-1 sponsorship only)
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs eligible with ECFMG certification and qualifying visa (J-1, or H-1B case by case)
Visa support
Institution policyUF-COM sponsors J-1 via ECFMG; new H-1B only in limited case-by-case circumstances for select specialties (requires department approval, USMLE Steps 1-3 and Florida licence)
Licence requirement
Institution policyMust meet Florida Board of Medicine licensure (training registration) requirements

How to apply

Deadline
Applications accepted July through September via ERAS (for positions beginning the following July)
Application method
ERAS and NRMP
