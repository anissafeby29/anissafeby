Overview

A one-year ACGME AHFTC fellowship at one of the largest US heart transplant centres (around 100 heart transplants and 100 LVAD implants per year).

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
At least three years in an ACGME-approved cardiology fellowship program
International applicants
Accepted with ECFMG certification and J-1 sponsorship
Visa support
J-1 visas sponsored via ECFMG; other visa types not sponsored
Licence requirement
Institution policyNorth Carolina full medical licence or Resident Training License (RTL) required before clinical duties

How to apply

Deadline
Accepting applications for the 2027–2028 academic year; interviews and offers may be made on a rolling basis
Application method
ERAS application with CV, personal statement (max 250 words), USMLE transcripts, medical school transcript, 3 letters of recommendation and ECFMG certificate (if applicable)

Funding

Funding
Institution policyGME trainees paid on the Duke stipend scale; FY27 (from 1 July 2026) PGY-1 $71,052 rising to PGY-4 $79,908, PGY-5 $82,992, PGY-6 $86,292, PGY-7 $90,036 (up to PGY-12 $106,800), plus benefits
