Overview

A small three-year combined pulmonary/critical care fellowship with two fellows per year, training at Johnson City, Bristol Regional and Holston Valley medical centres.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
2 per year (6 fellows total)
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited (combined pulmonary/critical care since 1995)
Department
Division of Pulmonary, Critical Care and Sleep Disorders Medicine, Department of Internal Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Department application requirements: completion of an ACGME-accredited internal medicine residency; USMLE Step 3/COMLEX Level 3 pass
International applicants
Department of Internal Medicine requirements: US citizenship, green card, or eligibility for a J-1 visa (or equivalent)
Visa support
J-1 (or equivalent) eligibility accepted; H-1B not stated
Licence requirement
Tennessee medical licence, training licence or licence exemption
