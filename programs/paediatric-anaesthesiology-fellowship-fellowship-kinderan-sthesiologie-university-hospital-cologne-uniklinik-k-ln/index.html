Overview

A competency-based paediatric anaesthesia fellowship using entrustable professional activities under close supervision, following the national Fellowship Kinderanästhesiologie curriculum.

The programme

Training type

Minimum 12 months full-time equivalent (up to 3 months paediatric/neonatal ICU may be credited)
