Overview

A three-year ACGME-accredited pediatric cardiology fellowship at the Herma Heart Institute, with an optional fourth-year non-invasive imaging fellowship.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years (4th-year non-invasive imaging fellowship available)
Start
1 July each year
Positions
3 fellows per year
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited
Department
Herma Heart Institute, Children's Wisconsin
