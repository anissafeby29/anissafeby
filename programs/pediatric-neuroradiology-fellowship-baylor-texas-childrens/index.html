Overview

An advanced paediatric neuroradiology fellowship at Texas Children's Hospital with the full complement of paediatric medical and surgical subspecialties and one weekend call shift a month.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed ACGME-accredited (or equivalent) radiology residency and a prior fellowship in general pediatric radiology or adult neuroradiology
International applicants
Institution policyYes - IMGs welcomed; ECFMG certification required (except Canadian graduates); must secure appropriate visa and TMB permit
Visa support
Institution policyVisa types sponsored vary by department; all IMGs must secure an appropriate visa and Texas Medical Board permit; non-US citizens verify status with BCM International Services
Licence requirement
Texas medical licensure (training or full) required.
