EACTS Transcatheter Aortic Valve Implantation (TAVI) Fellowship

Ospedale Casa Sollievo della Sofferenza (EACTS Francis Fontan Fund)

Cardiothoracic SurgeryAdult cardiac surgeryTravelling Fellowship (society-funded clinical placement)
Destination

Overview

Three-month fellowship for cardiac surgery trainees learning percutaneous treatment of valve disease, including CT/TEE planning and device selection.

Deadline
Application window 1 March – 1 May 2026 (2026/27 cycle closed)
