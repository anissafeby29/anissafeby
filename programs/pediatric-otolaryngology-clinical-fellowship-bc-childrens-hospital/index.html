Pediatric Otolaryngology Clinical Fellowship

Overview

One-year clinical fellowship running the paediatric otolaryngology consult service, NICU/PICU airway input and specialty clinics (cleft, craniofacial, cochlear implant, vascular anomalies, aerodigestive).
