ERUS Robotic Curriculum Fellowship (Robot-Assisted Radical Prostatectomy)

EAU Robotic Urology Section (ERUS) – ERUS Robotic Certified Host Centres

UrologyUrologic oncology & roboticsClinical Fellowship (structured robotic curriculum)
Destination
EUR
ERUS certified host centre, Multiple, Europe (multi-country)
Open official programme page ↗Compare saved →

Overview

Structured ERUS fellowship taking fellows from simulation and dry/wet labs to independently performing a robot-assisted radical prostatectomy, assessed by blinded experts.

The programme

Focus
Robotic urology
Training type
Clinical Fellowship (structured robotic curriculum)
Duration
Modular curriculum with skills assessments on days 1, 28, 35 and 180, plus mandatory 5-day ORSI Academy skills course
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
One fellow per host centre per year
Accreditation
ERUS Robotic Curriculum endorsed by EAU Executive, ESU, EUSP and ERUS; successful fellows certified as ERUS Robotic Fellows
Department
Not stated on official page

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
EAU membership for at least 6 months; at least 1 PubMed article; last 2 years of residency or young fellow up to 7 years after residency; support letter from an ERUS host-trainer
International applicants
Open to EAU members meeting the criteria; no nationality restriction stated
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Mentored console training at host centre (local licensing rules apply)

How to apply
