Regional Anaesthesia Fellowship Program

Overview

Twelve-month fellowship giving hands-on experience in basic to advanced ultrasound-guided regional anaesthesia, including perineural catheters, with over 3,000 peripheral nerve blocks performed annually.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Basic medical degree plus a postgraduate qualification registrable with the Singapore Medical Council, or in a Singapore-recognised anaesthesiology programme having passed FANZCA or FRCA finals; must meet SMC Conditional Medical Registration criteria and English requirement
International applicants
Yes - CGH's Office of Medical Post-Graduate Education runs fellowships for local and overseas medical professionals; foreign medical specialists may apply
