Overview

ACGME-accredited fellowship with eight attendings covering otology, laryngology, airway reconstruction and endoscopic sinus surgery, with CREST research training.

ACGME pediatric otolaryngology fellowship: residency must be ACGME-, AOA-, ACGME-I (Advanced Specialty) or RCPSC-accredited (ASPO application guide); other international graduates not eligible
