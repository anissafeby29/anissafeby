Total Burn Care Clinical Fellowship

Sunnybrook Health Sciences Centre – Ross Tilley Burn Centre (University of Toronto)

Overview

A clinical fellowship providing training in total acute and rehabilitative care of burn patients, including critical care and burn surgery, under five burn surgeon-intensivists.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed residency in general or plastic surgery and eligible to write the board examination in that specialty
International applicants
Institution policyYes - international applicants who meet CPSO clinical-fellow licensure requirements contact the Fellowship Program Director; applicants from countries with training agreements apply via their sponsoring agency to the PGME Office
Visa support
Institution policyinternational clinical fellows need an IRCC work permit; the PGME Office issues an LMIA-exempt offer of employment number and supports the work permit application
Licence requirement
Must be eligible for a CPSO postgraduate education certificate of registration

How to apply

Deadline
15 December 2026 for the 2028–2029 fellowship year
Application method
Letter of interest, CV and three reference letters to the Education Lead, Ross Tilley Burn Centre

Funding

Funding
Institution policyclinical fellows must be funded (department or sponsoring agency); FEAC recommends pay at least equal to PGY1 on the PARO salary scale, and foreign nationals must be paid at a rate commensurate with a Canadian in the same role
