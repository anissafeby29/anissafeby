Overview

A two-year fellowship mirroring the Royal College PGY4-5 rheumatology training, open to internationally trained internists, leading to eligibility for the RCPSC SEAP rheumatology examinations.

The programme

Training type
Clinical and Research Fellowship
Duration
2 years
Start
July 1
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
RCPSC Subspecialty Examination Affiliate Program (SEAP) eligible
Department
Department of Medicine, Division of Rheumatology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed an approved internal medicine training program outside Canada
International applicants
Non-Canadian applicants may apply; sponsored applicants from Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia and UAE apply via sponsoring agency to PGME (per UofT PGME fellowship page)
Visa support
Work permit and CPSO licensure process often takes 6-8 months for candidates outside Canada
Licence requirement
CPSO certificate authorizing postgraduate education (educational licence); applicants must be recognized as a specialist/family physician in the jurisdiction where they practised (per UofT PGME fellowship page)

How to apply

Deadline
Not stated on official page
Application method
Per UofT PGME process; contact fellowships.medicine@utoronto.ca

Funding

Funding
Division does not provide funding and does not accept self-funded applicants; proof of funding equivalent to PGY-3 PARO salary required
