Overview

Cross-departmental training prepares fellows to assess perioperative risk, manage comorbidity and develop a perioperative consultation service.

The programme

Training type

Not stated on official page

Non-ACGME, as listed in the departmental fellowship index.
Department
Department of Anesthesiology, Vanderbilt University Medical Center

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Complete an ACGME-accredited anesthesiology or general-surgery residency before the fellowship.
International applicants
Institutional GME requirement: holders of a foreign medical degree need a valid ECFMG certificate; no programme-specific policy stated
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 via ECFMG; H-1B considered case by case by programme with GME; no new O-1 applications supported
Licence requirement
Institution policyHouse staff train under a Tennessee Board of Medical Examiners licence exemption obtained by GME; full Tennessee licence needed for moonlighting and for non-ACGME clinical fellows with a secondary faculty appointment
