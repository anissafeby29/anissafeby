General Paediatrics and Adolescent Medicine Fellowship

Overview

Fellowship providing broad experience in general paediatric medical conditions with compulsory subspecialty, neonatology and children's emergency postings.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1-, 2- or 3-year postings (duration may vary according to service and candidate needs)
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
1-2 fellowship training positions for a given duration
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Department of Paediatrics, Division of Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Medical graduates from recognised medical schools as determined by the Singapore Medical Council
International applicants
Yes - KKH clinical fellowships are offered to foreign medical doctors; clinical fellows need a supporting letter from their government, regional health authority or home institution stating their position is reserved until return
Visa support
Valid Training Employment Pass issued by the Ministry of Manpower required; processing time at least 12 months
Licence requirement
Temporary Registration with the Singapore Medical Council (SMC) required (SMC fees S$300 application + S$550 practising certificate)

How to apply

Deadline
Institution policyno fixed deadline - apply by email to Education.Office@KKH.com.sg at least 12 months before the intended start (processing time at least 12 months)
Application method
Email Education.Office@KKH.com.sg; KKH application fee S$620 + GST for successful applicants

Funding

Funding
Candidates should be self-funded or have funding from external sources/institutions
