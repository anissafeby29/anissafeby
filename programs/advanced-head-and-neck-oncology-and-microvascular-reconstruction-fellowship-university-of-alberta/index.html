Advanced Head and Neck Oncology and Microvascular Reconstruction Fellowship

Overview

A one-year fellowship accredited by the AHNS and CAHNSO, with a minimum of 150 microvascular reconstructions and 30-40 TORS procedures.

The programme

Training type

1 year

Institution policyPreferred start 1 July; otherwise must start on the first of a month

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of OTL-HNS, General Surgery or General Plastic Surgery residency; interview with director
International applicants
Open to international candidates with fellowship/board certification (or national equivalent); foreign graduates must obtain a Canadian training visa and an Alberta educational licence
Visa support
Institution policyInternational fellows require a Canadian work permit before starting; PGME issues the offer/FVCA number, documents due to PGME at least 6 months before start
Licence requirement
Institution policyCollege of Physicians & Surgeons of Alberta (CPSA) registration required before clinical fellowship begins, incl. CPSA English language proficiency where applicable

Funding

Funding
Institution policyFellowships without salary/funding not permitted; from 1 Jan 2026 salary must be at least the PARA R1 level; PGME provides no salary
