Cornea Fellowship

The Royal Victorian Eye and Ear Hospital

Destination

Overview

Comprehensive training in cornea, eye banking and refractive surgery, with substantial exposure to corneal infections, ocular surface disease, corneal transplantation and excimer laser surgery.
