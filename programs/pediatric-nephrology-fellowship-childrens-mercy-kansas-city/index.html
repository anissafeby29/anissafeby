Overview

Three-year pediatric nephrology fellowship continually ACGME-accredited since 1991, with 15–20 kidney transplants and over 100 kidney biopsies a year.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years
Start
July 2027 (NRMP Medicine and Pediatric Specialties Match July 2027 appointments)
Positions
1 per year (3 total)
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited (since 1991)
Department
Not stated on official page

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Institution policyLCME/AOA graduate, Fifth Pathway, or international graduate with valid ECFMG certificate; above PGY-1 must have passed USMLE/COMLEX Step 3 and completed prior PGY levels
International applicants
Institution policyYes - graduates of non-US/Canadian schools need a current ECFMG certificate; non-US citizens generally need ECFMG J-1 sponsorship
Visa support
Institution policyprimarily J-1 sponsored by ECFMG; H-1B sponsored in some cases; F-1 OPT accepted; Canadian graduates of LCME schools train on TN status
Licence requirement
Institution policymust be licensed to practise medicine in Missouri and/or Kansas as required by Children's Mercy; trainees at PGY-3 and above must pass USMLE/COMLEX Step 3 before starting

How to apply

Deadline
NRMP Medicine and Pediatric Specialties Match (July 2027 appointments): rank order list certification deadline 18 November 2026; Match Day 2 December 2026
Application method
ERAS and Pediatric Nephrology Match; direct applications accepted outside the match cycle

Funding

Funding
Includes tuition allowance of $5,250 per year
