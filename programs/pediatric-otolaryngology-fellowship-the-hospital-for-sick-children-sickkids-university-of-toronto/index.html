Overview

Established in 1985-86, a world-renowned fellowship at SickKids, RCPSC-accredited and recognised by ASPO as ACGME-equivalent, with one clinical year and an optional second year.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year clinical (optional second year)
Start
1 July
Positions
SickKids page: two fellows accepted per year; U of T page: one per year (check with programme)
Accreditation
RCPSC-accredited; recognised by ASPO as ACGME-equivalent
Department
Department of Otolaryngology – Head & Neck Surgery

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed Otolaryngology-Head & Neck Surgery residency
International applicants
Foreign medical graduates accepted
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Educational licence from the College of Physicians and Surgeons of Ontario (CPSO); candidate must be recognised as a medical specialist in the jurisdiction of prior practice
