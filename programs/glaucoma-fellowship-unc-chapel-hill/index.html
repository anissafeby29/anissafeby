Overview

One-year glaucoma fellowship with outpatient care at the UNC Kittner Eye Center and surgery at UNC Hospitals Hillsborough.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Must obtain an unrestricted North Carolina medical licence before beginning the fellowship (all 3 parts of USMLE must be passed)
International applicants
Institution policyForeign graduates must be ECFMG-certified and eligible for J-1 visa (H-1B not sponsored)
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 (ECFMG) only; H-1B visas are not sponsored at UNC Hospitals
Licence requirement
Unrestricted North Carolina medical licence required before starting; all three USMLE steps must be passed

How to apply

Deadline
1 October of the year before the 1 July start (SF Match)

SF Match CAS glaucoma fellowship application
