Overview

Pan-African specialist and subspecialist paediatric training at Red Cross War Memorial Children's Hospital for African doctors who return to strengthen child health services at home.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
African citizen; medical doctor or allied health professional; programme not available in home country; nominated by home institution leadership; affiliated with an African university/government/public hospital; leave of absence with guaranteed employment on return; English proficiency at UCT level
International applicants
Only for citizens of African countries (not open to Asian applicants)
