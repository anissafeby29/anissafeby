Overview

EUR 60,000 award for a young European anterior-segment ophthalmologist to gain one year of clinical experience at a centre of excellence anywhere in the world.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Full ESCRS members working in Europe who finished ophthalmology training and are in second year of cataract, cornea or refractive subspecialty experience (trainees excluded)
International applicants
Limited - applicants must be ophthalmologists working in Europe; host centre may be anywhere in the world
