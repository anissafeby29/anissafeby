Overview

One-year sports medicine fellowship based in the Department of Family Medicine covering athletes and active individuals, with high-school, college and event coverage and 1:1 MSK ultrasound teaching.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
July
Positions
2 per year from 2027 (one family medicine-trained, one PM&R-trained)
Accreditation
CAQ sports medicine pathway (100% CAQ pass rate stated)
Department
Department of Family Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Board-eligible graduate of an accredited family medicine or PM&R residency
International applicants
Mayo GME general admissions: graduates of medical schools outside the US/Canada must hold ECFMG certification prior to appointment (or a full unrestricted licence in the programme's state)
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Must be able to obtain a full Florida medical licence

How to apply

Deadline
10 August of the year preceding the start date
Application method
ERAS fellowship application plus NRMP Primary Care Sports Medicine Match; three letters of recommendation including PD letter
