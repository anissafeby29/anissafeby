Overview

One-year subspecialty fellowships in cardiac MRI, echocardiography, fetal echocardiography, electrophysiology, inherited arrhythmias, interventional catheterisation, and heart function and transplantation.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed (or completing by start date) training in paediatric cardiology
International applicants
Accepts applicants from Canada and internationally
Visa support
Institution policyinternational clinical fellows need an IRCC work permit; the PGME Office issues an LMIA-exempt offer of employment number and supports the work permit application
Licence requirement
Institution policyclinical fellows must hold a CPSO certificate of registration authorising postgraduate education (educational licence); research fellows do not need CPSO registration

How to apply

Deadline
Applications accepted 1 September 2026 to 31 March 2027 for the year starting 1 July 2028 (firm deadline)
Application method
Online Fellowship Application Form plus application package (CV, letter of intent, degree and specialty certificates, 3 reference letters)

Funding

Funding
SickKids payroll (fully funded) or externally funded positions with minimum salary requirement; cardiac MRI accepts only self/externally funded for 2027-28
