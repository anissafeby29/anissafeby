Overview

One-year paediatric anaesthesia fellowship in Thailand funded by WFSA and the Society for Pediatric Anesthesia (USA) for an anaesthesiologist from Asia.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship (WFSA-funded scholarship)
Duration
1 year
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
WFSA scholarship(s) - 1 scholarship stated for the neuro and paediatric streams in the last published call
Accreditation
World Federation of Societies of Anaesthesiologists (WFSA) Fellowship Programme; BARTC supported by WFSA and the Royal College of Anesthesiologists of Thailand
Department
Bangkok Anesthesia Regional Training Centre - Pediatrics

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
WFSA criteria: qualified physician fully trained in anaesthesia per home-country regulations; supported by own National Anaesthesiology Society and current workplace; must return to a recognised post in country of origin; early-career anaesthesiologists preferred, preferably working in a teaching hospital; valid passport
International applicants
Yes - fellowship for anaesthesiologists from Asian low- and middle-income countries (BARTC page: 'anesthesiologists from Asia')

Not stated on official page

Not stated on official page
