Overview

Two-year structured paediatric cardiac intensive care fellowship split between Amrita (Kochi) and Institut Jantung Negara (Kuala Lumpur).

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Postgraduate degree in anaesthesiology or general paediatrics with some intensive care experience
International applicants
Yes - joint Children's HeartLink programme designed for trainees from low- and middle-income countries; selected applicants complete each country's formalities for local permissions and practice privileges
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Selected applicants must complete each country's formalities for local permissions and practice privileges (India and Malaysia)
