Overview

ISN Fellowships give physicians from disadvantaged parts of the world the opportunity to pursue essential nephrology training abroad to advance kidney care in their home region.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
ISN members from lower, lower-middle and upper-middle income countries (World Bank classification).
International applicants
Designed for physicians from low-resource countries to train abroad and return home; also supports glomerular disease (Otsuka) and AKI/CRRT (Ravi Mehta) training tracks.
