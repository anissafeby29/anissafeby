Overview

A three-year clinical GI fellowship sponsored by Loma Linda University Medical Center, the Loma Linda VA and Riverside University Health, with close involvement in a large liver transplant programme.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Medical degree and completion of an ACGME-accredited internal medicine residency by enrolment; able to obtain a California Medical License; ECFMG certificate if foreign medical degree
International applicants
Accepted (ECFMG-certified graduates of international medical schools)
Visa support
J-1 (ECFMG-sponsored) eligibility, or an existing H-1B from another US GME programme, or F-1 needing H-1B to continue education
Licence requirement
Must be able to obtain a California Medical License
