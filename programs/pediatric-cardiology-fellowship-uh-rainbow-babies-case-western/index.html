Overview

A three-year ACGME-accredited pediatric cardiology fellowship at one of the nation's top-ranked pediatric cardiology and heart surgery programmes.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of at least three years of training in an accredited general pediatrics (or other qualifying) residency
International applicants
Institution policyGraduates of non-US schools need a currently valid ECFMG certificate (ACGME programmes) and must be eligible for an Ohio training certificate or licence
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 via ECFMG; H-1B at UHCMC's sole discretion under certain conditions (ECFMG certificate and USMLE/COMLEX Step 3 passed at application); J-1 holders cannot enter non-ACGME programmes
Licence requirement
Institution policyMust be eligible for an Ohio training certificate and/or unrestricted Ohio licence; fellowship candidates must have passed USMLE/COMLEX Step 3 before starting
