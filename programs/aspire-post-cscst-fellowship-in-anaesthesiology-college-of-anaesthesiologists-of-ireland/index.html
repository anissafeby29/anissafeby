Overview

A 12-month HSE NDTP-funded post-CSCST programme giving exceptional Irish-trained anaesthesiologists high-quality subspecialty training and advanced clinical skills with a personal supervisor and SpR salary.

The programme

Focus
Combined training pathways

Training Department (with HSE National Doctors Training and Planning)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
CAI CSCST holder within three years of completing Specialist Anaesthesiology Training, or entered the specialist division of the MCI register within two years
International applicants
No — restricted to holders of a CSCST from the College of Anaesthesiologists of Ireland
Visa support
Not applicable (Irish CSCST holders)
Licence requirement
Irish Medical Council registration (specialist division referenced for Aspire eligibility)
