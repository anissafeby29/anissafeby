Overview

Clinical and research spine fellowships at a regional AOSpine centre performing over 1,000 spinal operations a year, with six-month consultant rotations across deformity, degenerative, trauma and tumour surgery.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
12 or 24 months
Start
February and August each year
Positions
6 full-time posts per year
Accreditation
RCS-approved; Spine Society of Europe accredited fellowship site
Department
Centre for Spinal Studies and Surgery

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed orthopaedic or neurosurgical residency; expected to perform at junior consultant level; IELTS for non-native English speakers
International applicants
CSSS holds GMC sponsorship status and may sponsor registration of select highly qualified non-EU graduates
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Full GMC registration required

How to apply

Deadline
Applications accepted in October and April each year (up to two years in advance); interviews first Friday of January and July
Application method
Contact the CSSS fellowship administrator during the application windows

Funding

Funding
Standard tariff salary with spinal on-call commitment
