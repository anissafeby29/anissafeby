Overview

UMMC fellowship/subspecialty programme in medical retina and uveitis with expert clinical, research, teaching and leadership training.

The programme

Focus
Medical retina & uveitis
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years full programme (24 months in-house + 12 months at another recognised institution)
Start
Institution policyNo fixed intake; training start date is arranged per applicant (applications due at least 6 months before the start date for non-Malaysians in fellowship/subspecialty training, 2 months for clinical attachments)
Positions
1 local per year; up to 2 if there is an international candidate
Accreditation
UMMC fellowship; can count toward Malaysian National Specialist Register subspecialty training
Department
Department of Ophthalmology, Faculty of Medicine, Universiti Malaya

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Recognised postgraduate ophthalmology qualification; CV, 2 referees, employer support letters; interview (online or in person); valid medical indemnity
International applicants
Yes - page states the programme can take an international fellow
Visa support
Applicants must be approved for a Professional Visit Pass (PLIK) by the Immigration Department of Malaysia (no sponsorship stated)
Licence requirement
Registration with the Malaysian Medical Council; approval by UMMC Credentialing & Privileging Committee

How to apply

Deadline
At least 6 months before intended start (non-Malaysian hands-on/fellowship)
Application method
Online via the UMMC Fellowship/Training Attachment Programme e-services portal; non-Malaysians apply at least 6 months before start

Funding

Funding
Non-Malaysian training fee RM36,000 per year (+RM100 management fee); 15%/30% discount for lower-middle/low-income countries
