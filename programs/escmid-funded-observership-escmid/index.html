Overview

ESCMID-funded observership of five days to one month at an ESCMID Collaborative Centre in clinical microbiology or infectious diseases.

The programme

Focus
Infectious diseases / clinical microbiology
Training type
Observership (society-funded)
Duration
5 days to 1 month
Start
Visits December 2026 – December 2027 (autumn 2026 call)
Positions
About 100 observerships per year
Accreditation
ESCMID Collaborative Centre (over 100 ECCs)
Department
Not stated on official page

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Active ESCMID Young Scientist member or ESCMID LMIC member; enrolled in or holding a postgraduate degree in ID, CM or related field; one ECC per cycle; no ESCMID observership in previous 12 months
International applicants
Yes – observers from outside Europe may apply but only to a European ECC; European observers may go to any ECC except in their home country
Visa support
Applicant responsible for visa; dates cannot be guaranteed if visas are delayed
Licence requirement
Observational

How to apply

Deadline
6 October 2026, 12:00 CET (applications from 8 September 2026); two calls per year
Application method
Agree purpose and dates with an ECC and obtain its endorsement letter, then apply online via ESCMID member login

Funding

Funding
Up to €2,000 (reimbursed after approval and post-visit requirements)
