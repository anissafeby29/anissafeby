ESPN Training Grant (Paediatric Nephrology Clinical Attachment)

Overview

ESPN grant enabling young paediatric nephrologists to spend up to six months training at an established European paediatric nephrology centre.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Attachment / Training Grant
Duration
Up to 6 months
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Up to 3 grants twice yearly; a centre may host an ESPN trainee once every 2 years
Accreditation
ESPN
Department
Not stated on official page

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
ESPN member; young paediatric nephrologist (or paediatrician working with children with kidney disease) within Europe; preference for applicants combining a research project with the clinical attachment
International applicants
Restricted to young paediatric nephrologists within Europe who are ESPN members; training normally in a different country
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Not stated on official page

How to apply

Deadline
1 February and 1 September each year
Application method
Send application form (signed by head of department), CV, host acceptance letter, supervisor reference and traineeship details to the ESPN President and YPNN coordinator

Funding

Funding
Up to €3,000 (€500 per month)
