Overview

A four-year structured training pathway for international medical graduates fulfilling ABR Alternate Pathway requirements, with ABR Core and oral exam preparation alongside DR residents.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
IMGs who completed medical training and radiology residency outside the US/Canada, eligible to practise independently or board certified in their country; must hold an Ohio medical license or training certificate and not be in the NRMP.
International applicants
Designed specifically for international medical graduates.
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 via ECFMG is the recommended option; H-1B only for F-1 graduates of US LCME/AOA schools or current H-1B holders transferring, with GME Office approval
Licence requirement
Ohio medical license or Ohio training certificate required.
