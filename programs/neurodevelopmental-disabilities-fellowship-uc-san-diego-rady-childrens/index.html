Overview

One-year ACGME-accredited fellowship for child neurologists in the diagnosis and lifespan management of neurodevelopmental disabilities, based at Rady Children's Hospital-San Diego.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
Not stated on official page

1 per year

ACGME-accredited; graduates eligible for ABPN NDD certification
Department
Division of Child Neurology, Department of Neurosciences

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed residency in Child Neurology or Neurodevelopmental Disabilities; ABPN board certified/eligible in Neurology with Special Qualification in Child Neurology
International applicants
U.S. citizens, permanent residents and J-1 visa applicants are eligible to apply
Visa support
J-1 visa applicants eligible
Licence requirement
Current, valid, unrestricted license to practice medicine in the United States
