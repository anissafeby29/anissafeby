Overview

A clinical research fellowship in general oncology or a chosen disease site in which fellows run their own longitudinal Fellows Clinic and teach residents and students.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Research Fellowship
Duration
1-2 years
Start
1 July or 1 January
Positions
Varies based on funding
Accreditation
University of Ottawa PGME fellowship (not RCPSC-certifying)
Department
Department of Medicine, Division of Medical Oncology (The Ottawa Hospital Cancer Centre)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Medical degree and internal medicine residency (approx. 30% clinical, 70% research workload)
International applicants
International graduates without Canadian legal status apply through uOttawa PGME's international trainee process (see uOttawa PGME IMG page)
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
CPSO postgraduate education certificate (per uOttawa PGME)
