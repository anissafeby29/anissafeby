Overview

RCPCH sponsorship route to GMC registration for international paediatricians who have secured an NHS training-focused post.

The programme

Training type
Sponsorship scheme (GMC registration)
Duration
Two-year time restriction
Start
Flexible (proposed start at least four months ahead)
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
International Office

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Job offer for an NHS post with a strong training/educational component (usually ST3–ST8 equivalent); GMC-accepted PMQ (ECFMG verified); 3-year postgraduate paediatric qualification accepted by RCPCH; 36 of last 60 and 10 of last 12 months clinical practice; no failed PLAB in last 3 years
International applicants
After the NHS England pause of MTI, IPSS is now open to all doctors outside the EEA (page updated 28 August 2026); IELTS Academic 7.0 each/7.5 overall or OET Medicine B
Visa support
IPSS cannot help with visas; the employer usually provides a Skilled Worker visa
Licence requirement
RCPCH sponsorship route to GMC registration

How to apply

Deadline
Rolling (requires a job offer first)
Application method
Screening by RCPCH (job description, post submission form, CV, English scores) then sponsorship application; contact ipss@rcpch.ac.uk

Funding

Funding
No application fee; must enrol as RCPCH Associate Member
