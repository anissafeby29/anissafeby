Overview

ZOC's standing advanced-training programme letting overseas ophthalmologists undertake clinical observation or research in its departments for up to a year.

The programme

Training type
Visiting training / observership
Duration
2 weeks to 1 year (long-term >3 months; short-term <3 months)
Start
Rolling
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Foreign Affairs Office / clinical departments

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Bachelor's or higher in a medicine-related field; licence and preferably >=2 years experience for clinical training; Chinese or English; generally age <=50; two recommendation letters
International applicants
Yes - for foreign nationals and Hong Kong, Macao, Taiwan residents
Visa support
Admission Notice issued for applying for a Chinese visa at the embassy/consulate
Licence requirement
Valid medical licence in home country for clinical training (training is primarily clinical observation or research)

How to apply

Deadline
At least 3 months before intended start
Application method
Email application form and notarised/authenticated documents to international@gzzoc.com at least 3 months before start

Funding

Funding
Self-funded: application RMB 800; long-term tuition RMB 5,000/month; short-term RMB 400/workday; insurance ~RMB 150/month
