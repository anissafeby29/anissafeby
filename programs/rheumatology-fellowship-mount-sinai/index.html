Overview

A two-year programme across the Mount Sinai Health System focusing on clinical proficiency in year one and clinical or basic research in year two, with an optional third research year.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
2 years (optional third research year)
Start
1 July

Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Division of Rheumatology, Department of Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Usually 2–3 years of internal medicine completed; board eligible by end of first fellowship year
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs accepted with valid ECFMG certificate; may not begin training until an appropriate visa is obtained
Visa support
Institution policyMount Sinai Health System offers J-1 (ECFMG) and H-1B (categorical candidates in programmes of 2+ years, ECFMG-certified with USMLE Step 3 passed by Match) plus E-3 for Australians; programme director decides which is offered
Licence requirement
Institution policyResidents/fellows in ACGME-accredited programmes do not need a New York State licence (supervised practice exemption); non-ACGME fellows need a valid NY licence or limited permit

How to apply

Deadline
Completed applications reviewed through 15 September
Application method
ERAS and NRMP Specialties Matching Service

Funding

Funding
Institution policyACGME fellows paid by PGY level (Mount Sinai Hospital stipend effective January 2026: PGY4 $106,313, PGY5 $110,565); non-ACGME fellow pay set by programme funds
