Overview

CCINR-recognised interventional neuroradiology fellowship based at Sir Charles Gairdner Hospital with over 800 neurointerventional operations a year across Western Australia's state-wide service.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 or 2 years (by mutual agreement, based on prior experience)
Start
Next available start date: February 2028 (positions run January to January)
Positions
1 per calendar year
Accreditation
Fulfils the requirements of a Training Service of the Conjoint Committee for Recognition of Training in Interventional Neuroradiology (CCINR)
Department
NIISwa (state-wide neuroradiology service)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
FRANZCR eligibility or equivalent board certification is a requirement
International applicants
Yes — 'We welcome both local and international applicants, however FRANZCR eligibility or equivalent board certification is a requirement'
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Medical registration with AHPRA required for clinical employment (not detailed on page)

How to apply

Deadline
Not stated on official page
Application method
Applications handled by the NIISwa fellowship email; send up-to-date CV, reference letters and contact details of three referees with a covering letter (prior experience, training preference, 1 or 2 years, external funding, career goals, plans to sit RANZCR exams)

Funding

Funding
Funded and unfunded positions exist (ANZSNR list: 4 or 5 funded DNR, 2 unfunded DNR, 1 INR); applicants asked whether they possess external funding
