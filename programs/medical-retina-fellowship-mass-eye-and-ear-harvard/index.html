Overview

One-year medical retina fellowship providing broad clinical and research training for academic or practice careers in medical retina.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Board eligible in ophthalmology; ACGME-accredited ophthalmology residency or equivalent; USMLE Steps I-III passed; full medical licence
International applicants
Institution policyYes - IMGs need a standard ECFMG certificate for J-1 clinical sponsorship, completed base residency training and a Statement of Need from their home country
Visa support
Institution policyclinical J-1 (Alien Physician) sponsored by ECFMG, coordinated by the MGB Office for Global Professionals and Scholars (Training Program Liaison for MGH/BWH); GPS also processes H-1B, TN, O-1 and other visas - availability is programme-specific
Licence requirement
Must be eligible for a full medical licence from the Massachusetts Board of Registration in Medicine
