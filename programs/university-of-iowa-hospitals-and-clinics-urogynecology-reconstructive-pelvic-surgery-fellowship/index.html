Overview

University of Iowa Hospitals and Clinics's Iowa City programme is included in the American Urogynecologic Society's 2026 national guide to Urogynecology and Reconstructive Pelvic Surgery fellowships.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years (OBGYN track); 2 years (Urology track)
Start
July 2028 for the current recruitment cycle (July 2027 for the 2026 cycle)
Positions
2 positions starting July 2028 (1 OBGYN-trained, 1 urology-trained); 1 position in the 2026 cycle
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited (both tracks)
Department
Department of Obstetrics and Gynecology, Carver College of Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed medical school and an Obstetrics and Gynecology residency (OBGYN track) or Urology residency (Urology track) at an accredited institution; 3 letters incl. 2 SLOEs; CREOG scores
International applicants
Accepted via ERAS; international residents directed to apply through ECFMG/ERAS
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Not stated on official page

How to apply

Deadline
2026 cycle deadline was 26 January 2026; next cycle follows AUGS fellowship key dates (not yet posted on programme page)
Application method
ERAS and NRMP Match (NRMP programme no. 1203221F0)

Funding

Funding
Paid GME position; competitive stipends and health coverage per UI Health Care GME
