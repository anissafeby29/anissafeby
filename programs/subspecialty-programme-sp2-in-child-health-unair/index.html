Overview

FK UNAIR's paediatric subspecialist (Sp2) programme trains Indonesian paediatricians as consultants in ten interests from neonatology to nutrition and metabolic diseases.

The programme

Focus
Multiple paediatric subspecialties (allergy-immunology, gastrohepatology, haemato-oncology, infection and tropical diseases, cardiology, neonatology, growth and development, endocrinology, nephrology, nutrition and metabolic diseases)
Training type
Subspecialty training programme
Duration
Not stated on official page
Start
Two intakes a year: odd (gasal) and even (genap) semester; next intake even semester 2026/2027 (selection results 16 December 2026)
Positions
12 (odd semester) and 12 (even semester), 2026/2027, across all interests
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Child Health (Program Studi Subspesialis Ilmu Kesehatan Anak)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Indonesian specialist in paediatrics (Sp.A) (domestic or foreign graduate; foreign-trained specialists need an adaptation certificate from a collegium-recognised institution); legalised specialist diploma; valid specialist STR; collegium recommendation; employer consent if employed; signed statement to comply with RSUD Dr. Soetomo/FK UNAIR rules; family consent. Specialist GPA >= 3.00; ELPT/TOEFL >= 500; 1 year continuous work in the chosen subspecialty at a paediatric specialist education institution (IPDSA) or 3 years outside an IPDSA; symposium/workshop evidence in the field; hospital recommendation with statement to return; lifelong specialist STR; Sp.A competence certificate; IDAI UKK recommendation and 2 peer references.
International applicants
Indonesian citizens only (Warga Negara Indonesia); Indonesian specialists who trained abroad are eligible with an adaptation certificate
Visa support
Not applicable (Indonesian citizens only)
Licence requirement
Valid Indonesian specialist STR (at least 6 months before expiry); RSUD Dr. Soetomo practice permit (SIP) during training where stated

How to apply

Deadline
Even semester 2026/2027: online registration 1 September - 3 November 2026 (19:00 WIB); TPA and English test 7 November 2026 on Campus C. Odd semester 2026/2027 registration ran 1 April - 22 June 2026
Application method
Online registration via PPMB Universitas Airlangga (ppmb.unair.ac.id), selection fee, TPA and English test, then programme subject exam, health test/MMPI and interview; hard-copy documents for stage-1 passers

Funding

Funding
Self-funded or sponsored; fees 2026/2027: UKT Rp 25,000,000 per semester plus one-off IPI Rp 40,000,000. Ministry of Health study-assignment candidates need a letter confirming MoH/health office covers UKT and IPI
