Advanced Cardiothoracic Imaging Fellowship

UC Davis Health

Destination
USA

Overview

A one-year fellowship with six months each in adult and paediatric cardiovascular imaging (including 4D flow and mapping) and adult thoracic imaging.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility

International applicants
Requires residency completed in the US or Canada.
Visa support
Institution policyClinical J-1 (ECFMG) or EAD only; UC Davis Health does not sponsor H-1B visas for residents and fellows
