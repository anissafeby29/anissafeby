Siriraj International Fellowship Training in Clinical Pain Management

Faculty of Medicine Siriraj Hospital, Mahidol University

Physical Medicine & RehabilitationPain medicineClinical Fellowship
Destination
THA
Bangkok, Thailand
Open official programme page ↗

Overview

One-year multidisciplinary hands-on pain fellowship sponsored by IASP for clinicians from low-resource settings, covering assessment, interventional procedures and comprehensive pain management.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
1 August 2026 (runs to 31 July 2027)
Positions
2 for 2026 (1 IASP-sponsored scholarship, 1 self-funded)
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Siriraj Pain Management Clinical Training Center, Department of Anesthesiology (open to rehabilitation medicine specialists)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Physician in anaesthesiology, rehabilitation medicine, neurology, psychiatry or related field with at least 1 year clinical experience, board certification in relevant specialty and clinical-level English
International applicants
Yes — specifically for physicians currently practising in Southeast Asia or other developing countries
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Not stated on official page

How to apply

Deadline
31 January 2026 for 2026 intake (annual call; results March)
Application method
Email application form, CV, medical certificate, board certification and two letters (one confirming institutional support) to siiro@mahidol.ac.th; choose scholarship, self-funded or both

Funding

Funding
IASP scholarship: tuition waiver, shared dormitory, airfare, health insurance, monthly stipend and educational allowance; self-funded track fee US$12,500
