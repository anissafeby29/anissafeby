Oculoplastic and Orbital Surgery Fellowship (DES en oculoplastie)

Université de Montréal Department of Ophthalmology (CHUM / CUO-Hôpital Maisonneuve-Rosemont / CHU Sainte-Justine)

OphthalmologyOculoplasticsClinical Fellowship
Destination
CAN
Montreal, Quebec, Canada
Open official programme page ↗

Overview

Two-year adult and paediatric oculoplastic, lacrimal and orbital surgery fellowship with access to related specialties in the second year.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
2 years
Start
1 July
Positions
1 new fellow in odd-numbered years
Accreditation
Université de Montréal postdoctoral clinical fellowship (moniteur clinique)
Department
Hôpital Maisonneuve-Rosemont or CHUM

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Ophthalmologists who have completed residency; functional French required (French exam on admission)
International applicants
Not stated on official page
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Quebec training registration via Collège des médecins du Québec (implied by university postdoctoral enrolment)

How to apply

Deadline
1 May of the preceding year
Application method
Email application (diplomas, CV, two recommendation letters, motivation letter) to the programme contact listed on the page

Funding

Funding
Only bursaries from a university, government body, recognised granting agency or MSSS-eligible foundation are accepted
