Medical Training Initiative (Paediatrics) - MTI(p)

Royal College of Paediatrics and Child Health (RCPCH)

Overview

RCPCH-quality-assured scheme giving paediatricians from outside the UK/EEA up to two years of postgraduate training in the NHS, with College sponsorship for GMC registration and visa.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Training Scheme
Duration
Up to 2 years
Start
Flexible (proposed start at least six months from application)
Positions
Posts usually at middle-grade level (induction up to 6 months at ST3, then ST4-5 level)
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
RCPCH Medical Recruitment / International Office

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Paediatricians currently in training outside the UK/EEA, overseas at time of applying with no UK/EEA residency rights; practising clinically in an AoMRC priority country; IELTS Academic minimum 7.0 in each band and 7.5 overall (or OET equivalent); GMC-accepted primary medical qualification verified via ECFMG; minimum 3-year postgraduate paediatric qualification accepted by RCPCH or full MRCPCH; 36 of last 60 months and 10 of last 12 months in direct clinical practice; intention to return home
International applicants
Yes - designed specifically for international medical graduates from outside the UK/EEA (priority to World Bank Low Income and Lower Middle Income countries)
Visa support
RCPCH provides GMC registration sponsorship and recommendation for a Temporary Work - Government Authorised Exchange visa Certificate of Sponsorship (issued by AoMRC)
Licence requirement
GMC registration sponsored by RCPCH
