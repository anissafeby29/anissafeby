Overview

The Independent IR residency replaces the IR fellowship as the route to subspecialised IR training after diagnostic radiology residency, with rotations across three UCSF campuses, vascular surgery and IR clinic.

The programme

Training type
Independent Residency (post-DR)
Duration
1 year (ESIR graduates) or 2 years (non-ESIR)
Start
After completion of PGY-5 DR year
Positions
Up to 4 per year (fellowships page lists 3 positions)
