Overview

A two-year programme with two weekly continuity clinics (UAB and VA) and a division research base of over $20 million in grants.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Eligible for Alabama medical licence and controlled substance certificate (all USMLE steps completed at application) and federal DEA registration; no minimum USMLE score; must meet VA eligibility checklist.
International applicants
IMGs accepted with a valid ECFMG certificate.
Visa support
The division can sponsor J-1 visas; does not sponsor H-1B unless the applicant is currently within the US.
Licence requirement
Must be eligible for an Alabama medical licence

How to apply

Deadline
ERAS applications June through May; participates in ERAS signalling
Application method
ERAS; NRMP (code 1007150F0); holistic review

Funding

Funding
UAB fellow salaries and benefits by PGY level; T32 research support for eligible US citizens/permanent residents
