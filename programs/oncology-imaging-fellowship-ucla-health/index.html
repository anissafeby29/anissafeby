Overview

A new one-year clinical fellowship concentrated in multi-modality cancer imaging (CT, MRI, PET/CT, PET/MR) with optional exposure to image-guided oncology procedures.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Not stated on official page
International applicants
IMGs in a US or Canadian residency must qualify for a CA medical licence, have completed a radiology residency (or 4 years postgraduate radiology training) and passed USMLE Steps I–III.
Visa support
Institution policy (UCLA GME, ACGME programmes): ECFMG-sponsored clinical J-1 is the preferred visa; H-1B considered only for US LCME/AOA graduates moving from F-1 OPT, trainees already in clinical training on H-1B with enough time left, or physicians of national/international renown (department-funded, case by case)
Licence requirement
Must qualify for a California medical licence prior to the start of fellowship.

How to apply

Deadline
Applications accepted from 1 September 2026; interviews 13 January – 31 March 2027; offers from 25 January 2027
Application method
UCLA Recruit online application.

Funding

Funding
Institution policy (UCLA ACGME residents/fellows, CIR-SEIU contract): from 1 Sept 2026 PGY4 $108,102; PGY5 $111,894; PGY6 $115,477; PGY7 $118,801; PGY8 $124,242 (PGY1 $98,466); rising from 1 July 2027 (PGY4 $113,508); plus $8,000/yr Resident Enhancement Fund
