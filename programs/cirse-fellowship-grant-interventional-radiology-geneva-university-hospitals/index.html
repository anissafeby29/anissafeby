Overview

A CIRSE-recommended IR training centre within a major university hospital radiology department with a 24/7 interventional service, two angio suites and expertise in vascular IR, interventional oncology and paediatric IR.

How to apply

Deadline
2026 funding cycle closed; call for the 2027 cycle to be made at the end of 2026.
Application method
Request centre contact details from CIRSE (up to three centres), arrange with the centre, then apply via the CIRSE online application portal; reviewed by an independent board (one application per funding cycle).

Funding

Funding
CIRSE grant of €3,000 for a one-month stay.
