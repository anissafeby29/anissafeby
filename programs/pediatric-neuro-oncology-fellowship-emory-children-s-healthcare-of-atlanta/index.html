Overview

A one-year pediatric neuro-oncology fellowship combining clinical rotations and clinical research in one of the largest pediatric neuro-oncology programmes in the US.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (60% clinical, 40% research); possible additional research year
Start
Institution policyEmory house staff appointments typically cover a 1 July - 30 June academic year (standard start 1 July)

Not stated on official page

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed pediatrics residency plus ACGME pediatric hematology/oncology fellowship or child neurology training; foreign applicants with equivalent qualifications eligible
International applicants
Foreign applicants with equivalent qualifications are eligible to apply
Visa support
Institution policyIMGs must be ECFMG-certified and enter training on a J-1 (ECFMG-sponsored) or H-1B visa unless US citizen/green-card holder (Emory GME housestaff policy 5.12)
Licence requirement
Institution policyGeorgia Temporary Postgraduate Training Permit or full Georgia medical licence required before clinical care (training permit max 7 years); USMLE Step 3 required for entry at PGY4+
