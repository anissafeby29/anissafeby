Overview

A PEM fellowship at Dayton Children's with individualised training, research seminars and Wright State epidemiology and biostatistics courses.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years for pediatrics graduates; 2 years for emergency medicine graduates
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
2 per year
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited (programme code 3243832050)
Department
Department of Pediatrics, Wright State University Boonshoft School of Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of a general pediatric or emergency medicine residency; ABP or ABEM board eligible/certified; able to obtain an Ohio medical licence
International applicants
Permanent US residence, US citizenship or green card holder required (per official page)
Visa support
No - permanent residency or citizenship required
Licence requirement
Must be able to obtain a State of Ohio medical licence
