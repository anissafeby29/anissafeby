Overview

A Trust fellowship combining paediatric cardiac anaesthesia with cardiac intensive care.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
18 months: one-year anaesthesia post with an extra 6 months of cardiac ICU
Start
Posts start at different times of year on the usual rotation dates of February, May, August and November
Positions
1 cardiac anaesthesia post
Accreditation
Advanced paediatric anaesthesia training per the Royal College of Anaesthetists CCT curriculum
Department
Department of Anaesthesia, Great Ormond Street Hospital for Children NHS Foundation Trust

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Trainees near the end of specialty training in anaesthesia (years 5-7) or recently CCT; completed basic and intermediate paediatric anaesthesia blocks; demonstrated commitment to paediatric anaesthesia
International applicants
Yes - doctors trained overseas must register with the GMC before working in the UK
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
GMC registration required (overseas-trained doctors must register before starting)

How to apply

Deadline
Posts advertised 6-12 months before start date (BMJ Careers, NHS Jobs, GOSH and London Deanery websites); no fixed deadline
Application method
Apply to advertised posts via BMJ Careers / NHS Jobs / GOSH / London Deanery adverts (open competition); enquiries to departmental contacts
