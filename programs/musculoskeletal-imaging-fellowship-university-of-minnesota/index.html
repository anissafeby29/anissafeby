Overview

A one-year clinical MSK fellowship broadening knowledge and independent interpretive and technical skills, with strong orthopaedic and MSK oncology collaboration and 8 weeks of electives.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Successful completion of a diagnostic radiology residency from an ACGME or equivalent programme in the USA or Canada; non-US radiology graduates considered case by case if completed training is at least five years including one clinical year.
International applicants
Graduates of non-US radiology programmes considered case by case (at least five years of training); at least one letter must be from a US radiologist or US institution; J-1 visas not supported.
Visa support
J-1 not supported (H-1B preferred per ABR pathway page).
