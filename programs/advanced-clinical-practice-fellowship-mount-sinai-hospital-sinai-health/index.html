Advanced Clinical Practice Fellowship

Mount Sinai Hospital (Sinai Health)

AnaesthesiologyAdvanced clinical anaesthesiaClinical Fellowship
Destination
CAN
Toronto, Ontario, Canada
Open official programme page ↗

Overview

Broad clinical exposure across diverse surgical services, including substantial major oncological surgery and high-risk obstetric care, building skills for safe independent practice.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
Minimum 12 months
Start
Usually February and August
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
University of Toronto PGME-registered fellowship; does not count towards Royal College of Physicians and Surgeons of Canada specialty certification
Department
Department of Anesthesia and Pain Management, Mount Sinai Hospital / University of Toronto

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Meet University of Toronto clinical fellowship requirements; completed anaesthesia certification examinations in home jurisdiction
International applicants
Yes — page notes it may benefit internationally trained anaesthesiologists seeking experience within the Canadian health care system
Visa support
Institution policyForeign nationals need an LMIA-exempt work permit; U of T PGME issues the IRCC offer-of-employment number and CPSO letter of eligibility about 5 months before start
Licence requirement
Eligibility for registration with University of Toronto PGME and a postgraduate education certificate of registration from the College of Physicians and Surgeons of Ontario

How to apply

Deadline
February 2027 closed; October 2027 intake closes 1 December 2026; February 2028 intake closes 1 April 2027
Application method
Online application platform on the University of Toronto Department of Anesthesiology and Pain Medicine website

Funding

Funding
Institution policyFellowships funded by department, sponsoring agency or external source; PGME guidelines recommend at least PARO PGY1-equivalent remuneration and pay commensurate with a Canadian in the same role for visa trainees
