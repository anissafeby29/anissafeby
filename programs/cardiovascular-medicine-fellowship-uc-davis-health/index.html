Overview

UC Davis's three-year cardiovascular disease fellowship provides 24 months of clinical training at UC Davis Health and VA Northern California with flexible academic and research training.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years
Start
July 15
Positions
5 first-year fellows per year
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited Cardiovascular Disease programme (ACGME ID 1410521111, listed in AAMC ERAS 2027 participating programmes)
Department
Division of Cardiovascular Medicine, Department of Internal Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of an ACGME-accredited internal medicine residency; USMLE Step 1 and 2 passed; three letters (one from current PD)
International applicants
Foreign medical graduates need a valid ECFMG certificate
Visa support
J-1 visas accepted
Licence requirement
Not stated on official page

How to apply

Deadline
July 15 (submission of application and supporting documents for full consideration)
Application method
ERAS; programme signals prioritized

Funding

Funding
Salaried; $1,000 education stipend, meal stipend and four weeks paid vacation
