Overview

Advanced thoracic anesthesia training focuses on high-acuity chest surgery in adults and children, including lung transplantation and complex oncological procedures.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed anesthesiology residency (recommendation from residency PD required); unrestricted California medical licence before start
International applicants
IMGs may apply; Medical Board of California Applicant Evaluation Status Letter required
Visa support
Institution policy (UCLA GME, ACGME programmes): ECFMG-sponsored clinical J-1 is the preferred visa; H-1B considered only for US LCME/AOA graduates moving from F-1 OPT, trainees already in clinical training on H-1B with enough time left, or physicians of national/international renown (department-funded, case by case)
Licence requirement
Unrestricted California medical licence before start; postgraduate training licence (PTL) not sufficient

How to apply

Deadline
No deadline (rolling review once file complete)
Application method
Email completed fellowship application PDF and supporting documents to the fellowship director (Dr Nir Hoftman)

Funding

Funding
Institution policy (UCLA ACGME residents/fellows, CIR-SEIU contract): from 1 Sept 2026 PGY4 $108,102; PGY5 $111,894; PGY6 $115,477; PGY7 $118,801; PGY8 $124,242 (PGY1 $98,466); rising from 1 July 2027 (PGY4 $113,508); plus $8,000/yr Resident Enhancement Fund
