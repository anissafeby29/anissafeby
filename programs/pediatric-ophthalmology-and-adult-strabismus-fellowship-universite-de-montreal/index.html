Overview

Fellowship at CHU Sainte-Justine in strabismus and motility disorders, paediatric ophthalmology and ophthalmic genetics, with optional adult strabismus.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 to 2 years
Start
1 July
Positions
1 per year
Accreditation
Université de Montréal postdoctoral clinical fellowship (moniteur clinique)
Department
CHU Sainte-Justine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Ophthalmologists who have completed residency; functional French required (French exam on admission)
International applicants
Yes - page states a foreign candidate may be granted a departmental training bursary; French proficiency required
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Quebec training registration via Collège des médecins du Québec (implied by university postdoctoral enrolment)

How to apply

Deadline
1 May of the preceding year
Application method
Email application (diplomas, CV, two recommendation letters, motivation letter) to the programme contact listed on the page

Funding

Funding
Some bursaries reserved for Quebec residents and for Canadian/US trainees; foreign candidates may be granted one of the department's training bursaries
