Overview

A broad MSK imaging and intervention fellowship with over 2,000 image-guided MSK procedures and 2,000 MSK ultrasounds a year.

1 year

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
ABR board certified or eligible in Diagnostic Radiology; eligible for Alabama medical licensure.
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs eligible with a valid ECFMG certificate (or full unrestricted US licence); immigration status agreed with UAB ISSS before offer
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 (ECFMG) is the recommended route; H-1B (requires USMLE Step 3) decided case by case with UAB ISSS and currently not viable for candidates outside the US because of the H-1B fee uncertainty
Licence requirement
Eligible for (unrestricted) Alabama medical licence.

Funding

Funding
PGY-6 salary plus $40,000 for independent MSK radiograph reading; internal moonlighting $250–300/hour.
