Clinical Neurophysiology (EEG) Fellowship

Mayo Clinic — Minnesota

NeurologyClinical neurophysiologyClinical Fellowship
Destination
USA
Rochester, Minnesota, United States
Open official programme page ↗

Overview

A one-year ACGME EEG fellowship covering EEG and prolonged recordings, surgical monitoring and cortical stimulation mapping with formal neurophysiology coursework.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
July 1
Positions
Up to 3 per year
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited; eligible for ABPN clinical neurophysiology and ABCN certification
Department
Department of Neurology, Mayo Clinic School of Graduate Medical Education

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Enrolled in or completed an accredited US neurology residency
International applicants
Open to international applicants; graduates of medical schools outside the US/Canada must hold ECFMG certification before appointment
Visa support
Mayo Clinic supports ECFMG J-1 and H-1B sponsorship (J-1 requires an ACGME-accredited/recognised programme; no new H-1B petitions requiring the US$100,000 payment); final visa type at Mayo's discretion
Licence requirement
Fellows must obtain and maintain an appropriate medical licence while enrolled

How to apply

Deadline
Early January of the year preceding the start date
Application method
ERAS application with three letters of recommendation; NRMP Match

Funding

Funding
Salaried trainee position (stipend and benefits per Mayo Clinic School of GME)
