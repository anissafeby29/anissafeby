Glaucoma Fellowship (Formation complémentaire en glaucome)

Université Laval / CHU de Québec - Centre universitaire d'ophtalmologie (Hôpital du Saint-Sacrement)

Overview

Twelve-month AUPO-recognised glaucoma fellowship at Quebec City's tertiary CUO covering filtering surgery, drainage implants, revisions and MIGS.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
12 months (1 July-30 June), optional extra clinical or research year
Start
1 July
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Recognised by AUPO and the American and Canadian glaucoma societies; attestation of complementary training
Department
Centre universitaire d'ophtalmologie (CUO)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed ophthalmology residency; oral and written French (TCF TP >= 450 for out-of-Quebec graduates)
International applicants
Yes - process for physicians trained outside Quebec/Canada (invitation letter, MSSS confirmation, immigration steps); French mandatory
Visa support
Faculty issues invitation letter (8-10 months ahead) enabling immigration process
Licence requirement
Collège des médecins du Québec registration as clinical fellow (moniteur clinique)

How to apply

Deadline
1 September (application to programme director)
Application method
Email dossier (incl. French motivation letter) to programme director before 1 September; interview

Funding

Funding
Self-funded clinical fellow: must secure funding of at least CAD 45,000 per year (home-country scholarship, institution or recognised non-Canadian public body)
