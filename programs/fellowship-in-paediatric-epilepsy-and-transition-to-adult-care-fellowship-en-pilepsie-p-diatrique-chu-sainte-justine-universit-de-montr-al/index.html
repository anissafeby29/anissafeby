Fellowship in Paediatric Epilepsy and Transition to Adult Care (Fellowship en épilepsie pédiatrique)

CHU Sainte-Justine / Université de Montréal

Overview

Two-year Université de Montréal-accredited DESS in paediatric epilepsy with EEG training, presurgical evaluation, ketogenic diet and transition clinics.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
2 years (1 year minimum)
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Accredited DESS of Université de Montréal
Department
Service de neurologie

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed accredited training in neurology or paediatric neurology including at least 4 months of electrophysiology; sufficient French
International applicants
Foreign physicians admitted as 'moniteurs cliniques' via Université de Montréal Postdoctoral Medical Studies (start process at least 12 months ahead); sufficient French is mandatory (TEF or equivalent if not exempt)
Visa support
Institution policyFellows (moniteurs) from outside Canada need a temporary work permit; Université de Montréal provides the job-offer letter submitted to IRCC
Licence requirement
Letter of eligibility from the Collège des médecins du Québec required

How to apply

Deadline
Not stated on official page
Application method
Letter of motivation and CV to Dr Aris Hadjinicolaou

Funding

Funding
Fellows are not paid under the FMRQ agreement; CHU Sainte-Justine runs a competitive matching-funds bursary (CAD 40,000 for 12 months) for foreign candidates
