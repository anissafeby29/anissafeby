Overview

One of the larger child abuse pediatrics training programmes in the US, preparing fellows for academic and leadership roles in child abuse pediatrics.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Funding for at least one fellow each year (3-4 fellows at a time)
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited
Department
Division of Child Abuse Pediatrics

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed ACGME-accredited pediatrics residency and board-eligible in pediatrics
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs eligible with a valid ECFMG certificate, valid visa (J-1, H-1B or O-1) or permanent residence, and Illinois IDFPR licence
Visa support
Institution policyMcGaw sponsors J-1 (ECFMG) and supports H-1B only for exceptional candidates approved by the DIO/GMEC before ranking (cost borne by programme)
Licence requirement
Must meet eligibility requirements for a temporary Illinois medical license
