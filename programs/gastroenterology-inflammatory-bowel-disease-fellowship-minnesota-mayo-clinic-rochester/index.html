Overview

A one-year fellowship for gastroenterologists planning academic careers focused on IBD clinical practice and research, within a 10-gastroenterologist IBD Interest Group.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Three years of post-MD internal medicine training and prior GI subspecialty training (unfilled positions may be offered to US/Canadian ACGME IM graduates in December).
International applicants
Open to international applicants; graduates of non-US/Canadian schools must hold a valid ECFMG certificate before appointment.
Visa support
Mayo Clinic supports ECFMG J-1 and H-1B sponsorship; new H-1B petitions requiring the USD 100,000 payment will not be pursued (only conversions/extensions not subject to the payment).
