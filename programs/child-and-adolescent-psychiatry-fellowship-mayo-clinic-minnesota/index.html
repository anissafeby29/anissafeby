Overview

A two-year ACGME child and adolescent psychiatry fellowship in Rochester with continuity outpatient care across both years, grounded in clinical practice, education and research.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
2 years
Start
July
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited; eligible for ABPN child and adolescent psychiatry certification
Department
Department of Psychiatry and Psychology, Mayo Clinic School of Graduate Medical Education

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
No USMLE score cut-off (holistic review); Minnesota licensure requires each USMLE step passed within three attempts
International applicants
Open to international applicants; graduates of medical schools outside the US/Canada must hold ECFMG certification before appointment
Visa support
Mayo Clinic supports ECFMG J-1 and H-1B sponsorship (J-1 requires an ACGME-accredited/recognised programme; no new H-1B petitions requiring the US$100,000 payment); final visa type at Mayo's discretion
Licence requirement
All residents must be licensed by end of PGY-2; Minnesota Board of Medical Practice requires each USMLE step passed within three attempts and Step 3 timing rules

How to apply

Deadline
Apply via ERAS as soon as ERAS opens (recommended)
Application method
ERAS

Funding

Funding
Salaried trainee position (stipend and benefits per Mayo Clinic School of GME)
