Overview

Harborview-based training spans perioperative trauma care, resuscitation and trauma analgesia, with 60% fellowship time and 40% faculty work.

The programme

Focus
Trauma anaesthesia
Training type
Clinical Instructorship
Duration
12 months; two additional orientation months are recommended for fellows new to US practice
Start
Any point in the academic year; most fellows start between July and September
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Non-ACGME
Department
Department of Anesthesiology & Pain Medicine, University of Washington (Harborview Medical Center)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Foreign graduates must document an accepted medical school, anesthesiology residency and unrestricted licence in their country of residence. Application includes USMLE steps 1–3 and ECFMG documentation when applicable.
International applicants
Accepted: foreign graduates need a WHO-listed medical school, completed anesthesiology residency and unrestricted home-country licence; USMLE 1-3 and ECFMG documentation required
Visa support
H-1B sponsorship may be offered in limited, exceptional cases for highly qualified candidates.
Licence requirement
Current medical licence required with application
