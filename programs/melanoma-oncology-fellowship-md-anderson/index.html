Overview

Established in 2019, a one-year clinical melanoma oncology fellowship with an optional second year of clinical, translational or basic research.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year clinical, optional 2nd research year
Start
1 July
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Approved by MD Anderson GMEC and the Texas Medical Board (non-ACGME)
Department
Department of Melanoma Medical Oncology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
MD/DO; completed ACGME-accredited IM residency or foreign equivalent; valid ECFMG certificate; USMLE Step 1 and 2 CK passed within 3 attempts; US citizen, permanent resident or eligible for J-1
International applicants
Foreign medical graduates must supply ECFMG certificate, proof of foreign licensure and undergraduate transcript (programme page)
Visa support
J-1 (ECFMG-sponsored); MD Anderson does not offer or sponsor H-1B visas for GME trainees (programme page)
Licence requirement
Texas Medical Board physician-in-training permit or Texas medical licence
