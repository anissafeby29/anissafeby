Advanced Medical Dermatology Fellowship

University of Toronto

DermatologyMedical dermatologyClinical Fellowship
Destination
CAN
Toronto, Ontario, Canada
Open official programme page ↗

Overview

Training in the management of severe systemic dermatological disease such as CTCL and pemphigus variants, with preference for future academics and those pursuing a graduate degree.

The programme

Focus
Complex medical dermatology
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1–2 years
Start
1 July of the following year
Positions
0–4 per year
Accreditation
University of Toronto clinical fellowship (Certificate of Completion); does not lead to RCPSC certification
Department
Division of Dermatology, Department of Medicine, University of Toronto

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Fully trained and certified dermatologists
International applicants
Institution policyYes - international applicants who meet CPSO clinical-fellow licensure requirements contact the Fellowship Program Director; applicants from countries with training agreements apply via their sponsoring agency to the PGME Office
Visa support
Institution policyinternational clinical fellows need an IRCC work permit; the PGME Office issues an LMIA-exempt offer of employment number and supports the work permit application
Licence requirement
Must be eligible for a CPSO certificate authorizing postgraduate education (educational licence); IMGs complete a Pre-Entry Assessment Program

How to apply

Deadline
Open anytime
Application method
Institution policyapply to the departmental Fellowship Program Director; sponsored international applicants apply through their sponsoring agency to the PGME Office

Funding

Funding
Institution policyclinical fellows must be funded (department or sponsoring agency); FEAC recommends pay at least equal to PGY1 on the PARO salary scale, and foreign nationals must be paid at a rate commensurate with a Canadian in the same role
