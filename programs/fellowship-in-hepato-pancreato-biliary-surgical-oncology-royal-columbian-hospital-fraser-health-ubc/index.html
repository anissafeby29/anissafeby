Overview

One- to two-year SLS fellowship in open and minimally invasive HPB surgical oncology at Royal Columbian Hospital.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed certified general surgery residency; RCPSC, ABS, or non-North American general surgery specialty certificate
International applicants
Holders of a general surgery certificate from outside Canada/US eligible
Visa support
Institution policy (UBC PGME): foreign trainees must hold an IRCC work permit for the whole training period; the sponsoring department/PGME submits an offer of employment to IRCC and the fellow applies under the LMIA-exempt category
Licence requirement
Must be eligible for a BC (CPSBC) independent, restricted or educational licence

How to apply

Deadline
1 December 2026 for 2027-2028 (cycle opens 1 Oct 2026; interviews 1 Nov-15 Dec 2026; offers January 2027)
Application method
SLS fellowship application (CV and letter of reference); successful applicants then file CPSBC licensing and UBC Postgraduate Dean's Office applications by 1 January 2027

Funding

Funding
Institution policy (UBC PGME): fellows are not covered by the Resident Doctors of BC agreement; a written Training Agreement with the sponsoring Health Authority/University sets pay; $500 PGME administrative fee plus annual CPSBC licence fee; self-funded or unfunded postgraduate trainees are not eligible
