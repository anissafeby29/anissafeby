Overview

General and cardiac paediatric intensive care training in a PICU accredited for two years of core CICM training, with fellows recruited from Australia and overseas.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
6 to 24 months
Start
February and August intakes
Positions

Accreditation

Department
Paediatric Intensive Care Unit (PICU)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Not stated on official page
International applicants
Fellowships offered to applicants from all over the world; visa and registration take at least 6 months
Visa support
Institution policyAll RCH employees need Australian citizenship or a visa with work rights; where RCH agrees to sponsor, it engages migration lawyers for a Temporary Skill Shortage (subclass 482) employer-sponsored visa (usually 2-4 years, dependants included); other routes include 417/482 working holiday, 189/190 skilled visas
Licence requirement
IELTS minimum 7 in each band if English is not first language
