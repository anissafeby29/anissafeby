Overview

Two-year PEM fellowship at BC Children's Hospital, a tertiary paediatric facility and regional trauma centre whose emergency department sees about 43,000 visits annually.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Residents in Canadian Royal College training programmes in Pediatrics or Emergency Medicine, and international applicants who have completed their specialty training; senior EM residents seeking an area of competence also welcome
International applicants
Yes - international applicants who have completed their specialty training are accepted
Visa support
Institution policy (UBC PGME): foreign trainees must hold an IRCC work permit for the whole training period; the sponsoring department/PGME submits an offer of employment to IRCC and the fellow applies under the LMIA-exempt category
Licence requirement
Licensure with the College of Physicians and Surgeons of British Columbia (CPSBC) required (postgraduate/educational licence for fellows; CPSBC English-language proficiency requirements apply)

Funding

Funding
Institution policy (UBC PGME): fellows are not covered by the Resident Doctors of BC agreement; a written Training Agreement with the sponsoring Health Authority/University sets pay; $500 PGME administrative fee plus annual CPSBC licence fee; self-funded or unfunded postgraduate trainees are not eligible
