Overview

Intensive one-year fellowship in periarticular, pelvic and acetabular fracture reconstruction and foot and ankle trauma, intended for future academic tertiary-care surgeons.

Eligibility
Institution policyCompleted residency with specialist certification (RCPSC/CFPC, ABMS or recognised specialist in home jurisdiction) and eligible for CPSO postgraduate education registration
International applicants
Open to graduates of Canadian programmes and outside institutions meeting training criteria
Visa support
Institution policyNon-Canadians need a closed, employer-specific IRCC work permit; Western PGME issues the supporting documentation after CPSO eligibility (allow at least 8 months; processing 4-50+ weeks)
Licence requirement
Institution policyMust be eligible for a CPSO certificate of registration authorizing postgraduate education (IMGs may first complete a Pre-Entry Assessment Program)
