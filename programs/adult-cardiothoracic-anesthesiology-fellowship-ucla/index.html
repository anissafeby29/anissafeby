Adult Cardiothoracic Anesthesiology Fellowship

Overview

Cardiothoracic training combining operating-room care, advanced echocardiography and cardiothoracic intensive care, including transplant and mechanical-support cases.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
1 August (expected start date)
Positions
4 per year
Accreditation
ACGME
Department
Department of Anesthesiology and Perioperative Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Anaesthesiology residency graduate; SF Match application with USMLE Steps 1-3 (or COMLEX), ABA Basic Exam result, ITE scores, 3 letters (one from residency PD); valid California medical licence before starting
International applicants
IMGs may apply; valid ECFMG certificate and notarized English translations of diploma/transcripts required
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Valid California medical licence required prior to starting

How to apply

Deadline
Application submission period November through January via SF Match (2027-2028 intake opened November 2025); interviews in March
Application method
SF Match (sfmatch.org) plus supplemental documents emailed to the programme
