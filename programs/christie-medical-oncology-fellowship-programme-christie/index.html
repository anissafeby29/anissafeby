Overview

Clinical and research medical oncology fellowships at Europe's largest single-site cancer hospital across sub-specialties including breast, GI, lung, melanoma, early-phase trials and cell therapy, with a structured fellowship education programme.

How to apply

Deadline
Rolling; posts advertised as they become available on NHS Jobs / Christie vacancy portal
Application method
Apply to individual posts on NHS Jobs or The Christie vacancy portal; enquiries the-christie.fellowships@nhs.net

Funding

Funding
Trust-funded posts; academic fellowships (MD/PhD) via MCRC/CRUK Manchester; externally funded fellowships (e.g. ESMO) also hosted
