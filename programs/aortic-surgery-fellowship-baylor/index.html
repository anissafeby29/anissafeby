Aortic Surgery Fellowship

Baylor College of Medicine

Cardiothoracic SurgeryAortic surgeryClinical Fellowship
Destination
USA

Overview

A one-year aortic-focused fellowship covering open and endovascular repair of the aortic root, arch, descending and thoracoabdominal aorta at Baylor St. Luke's Medical Center and the Texas Heart Institute.

The programme

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed cardiothoracic surgery training (or equivalent), or in the final year of training in home country; English fluency required
International applicants
International graduates accepted; must contact ECFMG and meet Texas Medical Board Institutional Permit requirements
Visa support
J-1 visa documentation required for IMGs
Licence requirement
Institution policyTexas Medical Board Physician-in-Training permit or full Texas medical licence required before starting
