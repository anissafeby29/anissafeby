Overview

Comprehensive one-year sports medicine and arthroscopy fellowship using a mentorship model, leading to eligibility for the ABOS sports medicine subspecialty certificate.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
Programme page: the academic year begins in August
Positions
1 per year
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited (Review Committee for Orthopaedic Surgery)
Department
Department of Orthopedic Surgery

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Passed USMLE Steps 1-3; able to obtain Arizona licence; completed approved orthopaedic surgery residency
International applicants
Institution policyGraduates of non-US medical schools eligible if ECFMG-certified before appointment (applicants still obtaining certification may interview but cannot be ranked or appointed) or holding a full unrestricted licence in the programme state; Canadian graduates after 1 July 2025 also need ECFMG certification
Visa support
Institution policyMayo Clinic School of Graduate Medical Education supports ECFMG J-1 and H-1B visas (Mayo selects visa type). Since the Sept 2025 $100,000 H-1B fee, Mayo will not file new H-1B petitions that trigger the fee - H-1B only for change/extension of status (USMLE Steps 1-3 required); otherwise ECFMG J-1 (Steps 1-2, ACGME-accredited/recognised programme). F-1 OPT accepted for first year for US-school graduates
Licence requirement
Institution policyArizona trainees must hold a full Arizona medical licence (if eligible: 12 months ACGME training for US/Canadian grads, 36 months for IMGs, USMLE 1-3) or an Arizona postgraduate training permit before day one; Mayo pays/reimburses
