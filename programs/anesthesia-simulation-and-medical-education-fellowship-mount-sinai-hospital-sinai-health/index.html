Anesthesia Simulation and Medical Education Fellowship

Mount Sinai Hospital (Sinai Health)

Overview

A one- or two-year salaried fellowship equipping physicians to conduct independent simulation and medical education research alongside clinical anaesthesia practice.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 or 2 years
Start
February or August
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
University of Toronto PGME-registered fellowship; does not count towards Royal College of Physicians and Surgeons of Canada specialty certification
Department
Department of Anesthesia and Pain Management, Mount Sinai Hospital / University of Toronto

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Minimum 30 months of clinical anaesthesia training and all requisite anaesthesia certification exams passed in home country (University of Toronto departmental requirements); preference for candidates within seven years of completing training
International applicants
Yes — University of Toronto fellowships accept international graduates; English proficiency required where training was not in English
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Eligibility for registration with University of Toronto PGME and a postgraduate education certificate of registration from the College of Physicians and Surgeons of Ontario
