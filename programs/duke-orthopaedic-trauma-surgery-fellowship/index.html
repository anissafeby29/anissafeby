Orthopaedic Trauma Surgery Fellowship

Duke University School of Medicine

Overview

Trauma fellowship covering acute evaluation and management of orthopaedic injuries and treatment of post-traumatic deformities and nonunions.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (1 August - 31 July)
Start
1 August
Positions
1
Accreditation
Non-ACGME; OTA accredited
Department
Department of Orthopaedic Surgery

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Institution policyLCME/AOA-accredited MD/DO graduates, or international graduates with a valid ECFMG certificate or full unrestricted US licence; NC full licence or RTL, I-9 employment eligibility, background and NPDB checks
International applicants
FMGs eligible only if US permanent residents (green card) not requiring visa sponsorship
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 alien physician status sponsored by ECFMG with Duke's assistance; Duke is H-1B cap-exempt and H-1B is possible for clinical trainees who have passed USMLE Steps 1-3 and hold an ECFMG certificate (individual programmes decide which visas they accept)
Licence requirement
Must qualify for a North Carolina medical licence; DEA number required

How to apply

Funding

Funding
Institution policyGME trainees paid on the Duke stipend scale; FY27 (from 1 July 2026) PGY-1 $71,052 rising to PGY-4 $79,908, PGY-5 $82,992, PGY-6 $86,292, PGY-7 $90,036 (up to PGY-12 $106,800), plus benefits
