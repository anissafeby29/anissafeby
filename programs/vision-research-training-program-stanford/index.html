Overview

Full-time, NEI-funded postdoctoral training for future vision-research leaders, pairing laboratory mentorship with clinical ophthalmology exposure and structured scientific development.

The programme

Focus
Clinical research
Training type
Postdoctoral Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
By arrangement
Positions
3 scholars funded, each for 1 year
Accreditation
National Eye Institute T32
Department
Department of Ophthalmology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed PhD, MD or equivalent doctorate (domestic or foreign institution) by start; US citizen or permanent resident; Stanford mentor engaged in vision research; full-time research in mentor's laboratory
International applicants
Not eligible: US citizens or permanent residents only (NIH NRSA requirement); doctorate may be from a domestic or foreign institution
Visa support
None: US citizenship or permanent residence required
Licence requirement
Clinical duties, if any, require applicable eligibility
