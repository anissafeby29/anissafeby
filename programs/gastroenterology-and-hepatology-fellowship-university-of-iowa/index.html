Overview

An academic GI and hepatology fellowship supported by an NIH training grant, training competent specialists, educators and scientists at the James A. Clifton Digestive Health Center.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of internal medicine training (occasionally candidates with a doctoral degree in an associated biological science).
International applicants
Accepted; ECFMG certificate must accompany the ERAS application.
Visa support
J-1 sponsorship accepted; H-1B not accepted at this time.
Licence requirement
Not stated on official page

How to apply

Deadline
ERAS applications around 19 July; application deadline 15 August
Application method
ERAS only; NRMP registration required

Funding

Funding
Stipend on UI Health Care scale; may be funded by NIH training grant/NRSA (with NIH payback agreement), Department of Internal Medicine or VA
