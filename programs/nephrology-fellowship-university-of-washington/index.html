Overview

A highly competitive ACGME-accredited nephrology fellowship with clinical training at Harborview, UW Medical Center and Puget Sound VA plus outpatient dialysis at Northwest Kidney Centers.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
2 years clinical (research track at least 2 additional years)
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
5-6 fellows per year (up to 2 research track)
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited since 1983
Department
Division of Nephrology, Department of Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
US board-certified or board-eligible in Internal Medicine (or ABIM 'exceptionally qualified'); US clinical experience recommended.
International applicants
Institution policyYes - international medical graduates must hold valid ECFMG certification as a condition of eligibility for fellowship application; non-US citizens need permanent residence, a valid EAD, or eligibility for J-1, F-1 OPT or H-1B
Visa support
US citizen, permanent resident or J-1 only; H-1B applicants not considered
Licence requirement
Eligible for Washington State medical licensure
