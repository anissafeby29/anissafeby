Fellowship Training in Clinical Cardiology

National Heart Centre Singapore (NHCS, SingHealth)

CardiologyGeneral cardiologyClinical Fellowship (hands-on)
Destination
SGP
Singapore
Open official programme page ↗Compare saved →

Overview

Provides the subspecialty fellow with theoretical and practical aspects of clinical cardiology, preventive cardiology and cardiovascular rehabilitation.

The programme

Focus
General / clinical cardiology
Training type
Clinical Fellowship (hands-on)
Duration
12 months
Start
Approx. 6-9 months after provisional offer (processing with relevant authorities)
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Completion proves experience gained; does not imply recognition for registration with any medical council
Department
Department of Cardiology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
MMed or equivalent; applicants who have completed 3 years of internal medicine training may also be considered
International applicants
Yes - NHCS offers fellowship positions each year to cardiologists, cardiothoracic surgeons and cardiac anaesthetists, including overseas fellows (estimated costs listed for foreign trainees)
Visa support
Training Employment Pass (Ministry of Manpower) required - fee SGD 360
Licence requirement
Temporary Medical Registration with the Singapore Medical Council

How to apply

Deadline
Reviewed twice a year: applications received 1 Feb-31 Jul reviewed in Oct/Nov; applications received 1 Aug-31 Jan reviewed in Apr/May
Application method
Email completed NHCS Fellowship Application Form and CV to the NHCS Fellowship Administrator, Office of Training & Education (nhcs.ote@nhcs.com.sg)

Funding

Funding
Self-funded; NHCS does not offer stipends or accommodation. Estimated costs: NHCS administrative fee SGD 500, SMC temporary registration SGD 300 + practising certificate SGD 550, Training Employment Pass SGD 360, malpractice insurance SGD 455-910
