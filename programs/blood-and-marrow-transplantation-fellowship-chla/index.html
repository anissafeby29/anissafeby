Overview

A pediatric BMT fellowship providing the clinical training defined by FACT for competency in blood and marrow transplantation.

The programme

Training type
Advanced Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1-2 years
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
1-2 per year
Accreditation
Non-ACGME; conforms to FACT requirements for BMT training
Department
Children's Hospital Los Angeles / Keck School of Medicine of USC

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed ACGME-accredited pediatric hematology/oncology (or allergy/immunology) fellowship; eligible for California licence
International applicants
Yes - U.S. citizenship not required; ECFMG certificate required if applicable
Visa support
Institution policyCHLA sponsors J-1 visas (ECFMG); application/visa fees and support available to trainees in ACGME-accredited or ACGME non-standard programmes
Licence requirement
Must be eligible to obtain a California medical licence
