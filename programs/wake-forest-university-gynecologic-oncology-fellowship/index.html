Overview

Wake Forest University's Winston-Salem programme appears in the Society of Gynecologic Oncology's national participating-program list for advanced multidisciplinary training in gynaecologic cancer care.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years (24 months clinical, 12 consecutive months protected research)
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
ACGME
Department
Division of Gynecologic Oncology, Department of Obstetrics and Gynecology, Wake Forest University School of Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed Obstetrics and Gynaecology residency; ERAS, NRMP and institution-specific requirements apply
International applicants
Institution policyYes - IMGs with a valid ECFMG certificate and J-1 visa
Visa support
Institution policyinternational medical graduates must hold a valid ECFMG certificate and obtain a J-1 visa
Licence requirement
Institution policyNorth Carolina Resident Training License (RTL) or full NC licence required before starting (full licence needed for moonlighting)

How to apply

Deadline
ERAS application deadline 1 April each year; interview invitations late April on the SGO common notification date; two interview dates over the summer
Application method
ERAS and NRMP Match

Funding

Funding
Competitive salary based on completed residency PGY level; 2026-27 stipends PGY-5 $83,137, PGY-6 $86,264, PGY-7 $89,390; health/dental/vision insurance and CME budget
