Overview

Observership programmes for foreign doctors keen to gain exposure in NNI neuroscience departments.

The programme

Focus
Neurosciences (neurosurgery, neurology, neuroradiology)
Training type
Observership
Duration
Clinical observership max 3 months; hands-off observership max 28 days
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
NNI Education Office

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
MBBS or equivalent; 12 months housemanship; registered in home country; department criteria; 2 recommendation letters
International applicants
Yes - observership programmes for foreign doctors
Visa support
Clinical observership: SMC/MOM required; hands-off: no SMC/MOM approval needed (visa from home country if required for entry)
Licence requirement
Clinical observership requires SMC registration; hands-off observership does not

How to apply

Deadline
At least 6-9 months before (clinical observership); 2-3 months (hands-off)
Application method
Email NNI application form, CV, 2 recommendation letters and current medical licence to nnieducationoffice@nni.com.sg

Funding

Funding
Processing fee S$545 (incl. GST) for each successful applicant
