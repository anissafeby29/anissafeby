Overview

Since 1934, an intensive three-year ACGME-accredited combined haematology and medical oncology programme that has trained more than 200 graduates.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years
Start
1 July
Positions
8 per year
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited
Department
Cleveland Clinic Cancer Institute (Taussig Cancer Center)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Board eligible/board certified in internal medicine.
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs eligible with a current valid ECFMG certificate; Cleveland Clinic holds ACGME Non-Standard Training recognition so approved non-accredited fellowships can take ECFMG J-1 trainees
Visa support
Institution policyH-1B (requires USMLE Steps 1-3 passed; Step 3 results by 30 April for NRMP programmes) and J-1 (ECFMG); O-1 for fellows case by case; no permanent-residence sponsorship for trainees
Licence requirement
Institution policyPermanent or training medical licence/certificate from the state board where training (State Medical Board of Ohio training certificate for Ohio; Florida Board of Medicine for Florida) required before starting; some advanced/non-accredited fellowships require a permanent licence

How to apply

Deadline
Applications reviewed in July; submit by 31 July for interview consideration
Application method
ERAS; NRMP Main Match only (no positions outside the Match); interviews September-November

Funding

Funding
Cleveland Clinic GME benefits listed on programme page (e.g., $1,000 annual educational allowance; GME-funded meeting trip up to $2,500).
