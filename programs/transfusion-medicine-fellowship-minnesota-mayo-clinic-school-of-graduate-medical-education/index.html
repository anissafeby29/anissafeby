Overview

A one-year ACGME blood banking/transfusion medicine fellowship at Mayo Rochester meeting ABPath subspecialty board requirements.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
1 July
Positions
2
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited (Mayo Clinic School of Graduate Medical Education)
Department
Department of Laboratory Medicine and Pathology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Physician with US citizenship or a valid US visa who meets ABPath blood banking/transfusion medicine certification requirements
International applicants
Yes if holding a valid US visa and meeting ABPath subspecialty board requirements
Visa support
Mayo Clinic supports ECFMG J-1 visa sponsorship and H-1B visas in special circumstances (Mayo GME policy)
Licence requirement
Not stated on official page

How to apply

Deadline
1 January of the previous year (18 months before programme start); later submissions considered if positions remain unfilled
Application method
Online via Mayo Clinic CRM 'Recruit' application system (no printed applications)
