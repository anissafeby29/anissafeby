University of Texas MD Anderson Cancer Center

Overview

ACGME-accredited CGSO fellowship with seven new positions a year: six in the two-year clinical track and one in a three-year NIH T32-funded research track.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
2 years (Track 1) or 3 years (Track 2 with research)
Start
Institution policyappointments run 12 months from 1 July (medical trainees) or 1 August (surgical trainees); surgical trainee orientation 1 August
Positions
7 per year
Accreditation
ACGME; SSO
Department
Department of Surgical Oncology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Institution policyMD/DO or foreign equivalent with a valid ECFMG certificate (for international graduates); Texas Medical Board requirements; tobacco-free pre-employment screen
International applicants
Institution policyYes - international graduates need a valid ECFMG certificate and J-1 sponsorship through ECFMG
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 (ECFMG-sponsored) is the required visa for international physician trainees needing sponsorship
Licence requirement
Institution policyTexas Medical Board Physician-in-Training (PIT) permit (obtained via the GME office) or full Texas medical licence before starting
