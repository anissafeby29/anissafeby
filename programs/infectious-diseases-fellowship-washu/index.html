Overview

A fellow-centred ACGME-accredited programme combining comprehensive clinical ID training with protected scholarly time to develop the next generation of ID leaders.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
2 years (integrated programme for most fellows)
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
5 per year
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited
Department
Division of Infectious Diseases, Department of Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
MD, MD/PhD, DO or equivalent (e.g., MBBS) and completed accredited internal medicine residency.
International applicants
IMGs with US residency welcome; IMGs with non-US IM residency eligible with valid ECFMG certification and USMLE Step 3 passed at time of application.
Visa support
Visa sponsorship available
Licence requirement
Institution policy (Missouri Board of Registration for the Healing Arts): residents and fellows in approved Missouri training programmes must hold a Missouri temporary licence (renewed annually; applied for via the hospital) before practising; IMGs need a permanent ECFMG certificate
