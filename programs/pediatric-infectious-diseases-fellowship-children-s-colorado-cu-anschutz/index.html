Overview

A well-rounded three-year pediatric infectious diseases fellowship run by the Section of Pediatric Infectious Diseases and Epidemiology at Children's Hospital Colorado.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years
Start
8 July (one year after the match)
Positions
1-2 fellows per year
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited (since 1998)
Department
Department of Pediatrics, University of Colorado School of Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Not stated on official page
International applicants
Institution policyGraduates of non-US/Canadian schools eligible with valid ECFMG certificate (or full Colorado licence); must hold or obtain legal work authorization; USMLE Step 3 required before starting a fellowship
Visa support
Institution policyClinical J-1 via ECFMG is the standard route; H-1B for residents/fellows only if CU GME agrees to waive its J-1 requirement
Licence requirement
Institution policyColorado Medical Board physician training licence (or full Colorado licence) required

How to apply

Deadline
ERAS July cycle: applications may be submitted from 1 July 2026; NRMP Pediatric Specialties Fall Match rank order list certification deadline 2 Dec 2026
Application method
ERAS; NRMP match

Funding

Funding
Institution policySalaried on CU GME base stipend scale 2026-27: PGY4 $90,960.59; PGY5 $94,589.52; PGY6 $97,824.22; PGY7 $102,995.34
