Neonatal-Perinatal Medicine Fellowship

Overview

A three-year ACGME-accredited neonatal-perinatal fellowship preparing physicians for academic neonatology, practice, research, education and leadership.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years
Start
Generally July (start dates may be flexible)
Positions
5 per year (15 total)
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited
Department
Department of Pediatrics, UT Southwestern Medical Center

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed/enrolled in 3-year general pediatrics residency or ABP alternate pathway; US citizen, permanent resident or J-1; valid ECFMG certification; USMLE Step 1 and Step 2 CK passed before application review
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs must be ECFMG-certified and hold an ECFMG-sponsored J-1 visa for clinical training at UTSW
Visa support
Institution policyClinical residents/fellows (ACGME and non-ACGME) on J-1 sponsored by ECFMG; UTSW does not usually sponsor H-1B for clinical training
Licence requirement
State requirement: Texas Medical Board Physician-in-Training (PIT) permit for participation in a Board-approved GME/fellowship programme (supervised practice only)

How to apply

Deadline
NRMP Medicine and Pediatric Specialties Match (July 2027 appointments): rank order list certification deadline 18 November 2026; Match Day 2 December 2026
Application method
ERAS application; NRMP Neonatal-Perinatal Medicine match

Funding

Funding
Institution policyPaid house-staff stipend (employer varies: UTSW, Parkland, Children's Health or Scottish Rite); 2026-27 scale PGY4 $82,225, PGY5 $86,343, PGY6 $89,505, PGY7 $95,050
