Overview

ACGME-accredited sports medicine and shoulder fellowship with three two-month rotations completed twice and team coverage including the San Francisco Giants.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (1 August - 31 July)
Start
1 August
Positions
3
Accreditation
ACGME, AOSSM and AANA accredited
Department
Department of Orthopaedic Surgery

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed orthopaedic surgery residency
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs eligible if their school is in the World Directory/FAIMER and they hold a valid ECFMG certificate at application; non-US citizens need permanent residence, an appropriate visa or DACA
Visa support
Institution policyUCSF sponsors J-1 (ECFMG) and H-1B (incl. OPT) visas; H-1B/OPT require pre-approval by UCSF OGME; non-ACGME fellowships can take J-1 trainees only if ACGME Non-Standard Training (NST) recognised
Licence requirement
California state medical licence
