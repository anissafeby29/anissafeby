Overview

A one-year non-accredited breast imaging fellowship based at the Stefanie Spielman Comprehensive Breast Center (James Cancer Hospital), with about 3,500 interventional procedures annually.

The programme

Focus
Breast imaging
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
1 July
Positions
2 per year (2028-29)
Accreditation
Non-accredited
Department
Department of Radiology, Ohio State University College of Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed a diagnostic radiology residency; able to obtain a full Ohio license.
International applicants
Department supports the ABR International Medical Graduate Alternate Pathway; page does not otherwise address IMG eligibility.
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Must be able to obtain a full and unrestricted Ohio medical license before the start date.

How to apply

Deadline
Applications accepted from 1 October 2026 for 2028-29; interviews 11 January–31 March 2027; SBI/NRMP match.
Application method
SBI fellowship match application, CV, personal statement, USMLE/COMLEX scores, transcript and three letters emailed to programme manager; NRMP Breast Imaging match.

Funding

Funding
PGY-6 Clinical Instructor stipend $80,478 (2025-26) plus $1,500 education fund and benefits.
