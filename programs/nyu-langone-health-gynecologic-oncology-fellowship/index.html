Overview

NYU Langone Health's New York programme appears in the Society of Gynecologic Oncology's national participating-program list for advanced multidisciplinary training in gynaecologic cancer care.

Clinical Fellowship

July (page describes July start following the application cycle)

ACGME
Department
Department of Obstetrics and Gynecology, Division of Gynecologic Oncology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed, or on track to complete, an accredited Obstetrics and Gynecology residency in the United States or Canada
International applicants
International medical school graduates may apply (US/Canadian OB/GYN residency required); ECFMG certificate and college transcript required
Visa support
Institution policyNYU Langone does not offer H-1B sponsorship for residents or fellows; J-1 sponsorship available through ECFMG
Licence requirement
Institution policyFor NYU-sponsored ACGME/AOA programmes no NY licence is required by state law (resident exemption, Educ. Law 6526(1)), though individual programmes may require a NY licence or limited permit; trainees in non-accredited programmes MUST hold a NY licence or limited permit before starting; USMLE Step 3 required before final year in ACGME programmes
