Fellowship in Mechanical Circulatory Support (MCS)

Deutsches Herzzentrum der Charité (DHZC)

Destination

Overview

Two-year structured fellowship in short- and long-term mechanical circulatory support (over 350 MCS patients a year) with focused TTE/TOE training and research.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Specialist in internal medicine/cardiology, anaesthesiology or cardiac surgery; additional qualification in intensive care medicine or international equivalent; 6 months full-time cardiac/cardiac-surgical ICU or cardioanaesthesia experience
International applicants
Not restricted by nationality on page ('or international equivalent' accepted for ICU qualification); German C1 required
