Overview

A breast imaging fellowship with weekly multidisciplinary breast cancer conferences, an established research group and sponsored meeting attendance for accepted presentations.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Must meet Utah medical licensure requirements and be a graduate of an LCME-accredited US/Canadian school, an AOA-accredited US osteopathic college, or a non-US school with a valid ECFMG certificate before appointment.
International applicants
IMGs with a valid ECFMG certificate are eligible, but due to regulations on non-ACGME fellowships the department is unable to accommodate J-1 and H-1B visa applicants.
Visa support
Unable to accommodate J-1 or H-1B visa applicants (non-ACGME programmes).
Licence requirement
Must meet Utah state medical licensure requirements.

How to apply

Deadline
Preferred deadline 10 November (applications accepted from 1 October)
Application method
NRMP Radiology Match (SBI-sponsored, All-in policy)

Funding

Funding
Appointed as Visiting Instructor (typical); salary, conference/vacation time and benefits competitive.
