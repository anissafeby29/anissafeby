Overview

A one-year programme established in 2026 offering advanced training in voice, airway and swallowing disorders, with about 250 operative cases and 400 in-office procedures.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Otolaryngology-Head & Neck Surgery residency
International applicants
Foreign medical graduates accepted
Visa support
Institution policyinternational clinical fellows need an IRCC work permit; the PGME Office issues an LMIA-exempt offer of employment number and supports the work permit application
Licence requirement
Educational licence from the College of Physicians and Surgeons of Ontario (CPSO); candidate must be recognised as a medical specialist in the jurisdiction of prior practice

How to apply
