Pediatric Dermatology Fellowship

University of California, San Francisco

DermatologyPaediatric dermatologyClinical Fellowship
Destination

San Francisco, California, United States

Overview

Two funded ACGME positions with outpatient and inpatient training, OR-based laser and procedural surgery and multidisciplinary clinics at Mt. Zion and UCSF Benioff Children's Hospital.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
Institution policyMost UCSF residents/fellows are appointed for June-August start dates (off-cycle starts possible)
Positions
2 funded positions per year
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited
Department
Department of Dermatology, UCSF School of Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
ABD certified/eligible; completed US ACGME or Canadian RCPSC-accredited dermatology residency by start
International applicants
Institution policyYes - international medical graduates train on ECFMG-sponsored J-1 visas coordinated by the UCSF ISSO
Visa support
Institution policyIMGs in graduate medical training hold J-1 (Alien Physician) visas sponsored by ECFMG, with the UCSF ISSO as Training Program Liaison (request 6 months before start); H-1B for clinical trainees only via exception request
Licence requirement
Eligible for and obtain a California medical licence
