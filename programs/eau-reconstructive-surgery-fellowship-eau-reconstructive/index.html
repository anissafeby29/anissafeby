Overview

One-year clinical fellowship in prosthetic, urethral, transgender and penile reconstructive surgery at Frankfurt and/or UCLH London with an EAU section tutor.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship (hands-on or observer status)
Duration
1 year at one centre or 6 months at each of 2 centres; observer status may be shorter with pro-rata funding
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
European Urological Scholarship Programme (EUSP), European Association of Urology; Certified EUSP Host Centres (EAU/EBU certification) recommended or required

Not stated on official page

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
EAU member; preferably 3+ PubMed publications (first author on one); logbook or proof of reconstructive surgical experience; fluent English or German
International applicants
Open to EAU members (membership for at least one year before applying; 2026 is a transition year); the page sets no nationality restriction, but all visits must take place at a European institution
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Hands-on experience requires an application for a licence to practise in the UK and Germany; otherwise observational status
