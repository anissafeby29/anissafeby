Cardiac Pacing Fellowship (job advert)

Overview

Training in all aspects of CRM device therapy (CRT, ICD, S-ICD, leadless and conduction system pacing) at a high-volume implanting centre, aiming for CCDS/EHRA/HRUK accreditation.

Eligibility and visa

At least three years of Advanced Training in Clinical Cardiology and completed FRACP (Part 2) or recognised equivalent; Ahpra registration
International applicants
Yes if holding a recognised FRACP-equivalent and Ahpra-registrable
Visa support
Institution policyEmployer-sponsored Training visa (subclass 407) is Alfred Health's preferred visa for IMGs (granted 13 months); Medical Workforce team supports visa nomination, starting at least 6 months before commencement
