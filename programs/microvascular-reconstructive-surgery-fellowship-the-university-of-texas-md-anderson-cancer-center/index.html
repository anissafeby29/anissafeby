Overview

Established in 1988, one of the largest reconstructive microsurgery training programmes in the world, with approximately 1,400 microsurgical cases a year.

International applicants
Institution policyInternational physician trainees accepted; those needing sponsorship must use the ECFMG J-1 visa
Visa support
Institution policyThe ECFMG-sponsored J-1 is the required visa for international physician trainees who need visa sponsorship at MD Anderson; visa-sponsored trainees may not moonlight
Licence requirement
Applicants eligible for a Texas medical licence may join the Clinical Specialist programme
