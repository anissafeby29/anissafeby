Pediatric Emergency Medicine Fellowship

Medical College of Wisconsin / Children's Wisconsin

Destination

Overview

A three-year ACGME-accredited PEM fellowship training specialists in clinical care, supervision and research, with optional master's training in clinical and translational science.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years
Start
1 July
Positions
2-3 fellows per year
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited (over 35 years)
Department
Department of Pediatrics, Medical College of Wisconsin

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Must have completed a US-accredited residency programme
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs accepted with appropriate visa: J-1 via ECFMG (valid ECFMG certificate) or H-1B where programme agrees
Visa support
Institution policyMCWAH accepts J-1 (ECFMG-sponsored); some programmes pursue H-1B (requires USMLE Step 3 passed) - check with programme; no permanent-residency sponsorship
Licence requirement
Institution policyMust maintain proper Wisconsin medical licensure per MCWAH licensure policies

How to apply

Deadline
Applications accepted July through October via ERAS
Application method
ERAS (applications accepted July-October); NRMP match

Funding

Funding
Institution policySalaried on MCWAH stipend scale from 1 Jul 2026: PGY4 $81,689; PGY5 $85,490; PGY6 $88,570; PGY7 $93,524; PGY8 $96,982
