Overview

One-year ACGME-accredited Mohs fellowship run jointly with the Department of Dermatology at NewYork-Presbyterian Hospital and Weill Cornell Medicine.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Accredited dermatology residency required
International applicants
Institution policyYes - foreign nationals accepted if they secure ECFMG J-1 status (or hold work authorisation for the programme duration); IMGs need a valid ECFMG certificate or full NY licence
Visa support
Institution policyNYPH GME generally accepts foreign nationals only on ECFMG-sponsored J-1 status or with independent work authorisation; no H-1B, H-1B1, E-3 or other sponsorship; O-1 supported only in very limited cases at the candidate's expense
Licence requirement
Institution policyNo licence required for trainees in ACGME-accredited programmes unless the Program Director specifies; non-ACGME programmes require a NYS licence or limited permit; work outside the programme requires a full NYS licence
