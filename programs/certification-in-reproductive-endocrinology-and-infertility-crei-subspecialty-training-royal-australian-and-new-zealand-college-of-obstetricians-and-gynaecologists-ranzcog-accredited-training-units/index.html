Overview

A RANZCOG subspecialty program training FRANZCOG specialists to manage reproductive endocrine disorders and infertility and to lead assisted conception units.

The programme

Training type
Subspecialty Training Programme
Duration
3 years (Years 1–3 clinical training, plus post-Year 3 requirements)
Start
Training year begins early February (Semester 1)
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
RANZCOG subspecialty certification
Department
RANZCOG Subspecialty Training Program (CREI)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Specialists in obstetrics and gynaecology holding (or completing) FRANZCOG; competitive subspecialty selection – see RANZCOG selection information for applicants

Licence requirement
