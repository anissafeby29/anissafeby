Overview

A tailored, funded fellowship offering exposure to all aspects of general thoracic surgery, with an optional year of lung transplantation.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Typically for candidates who have completed a thoracic surgery residency
International applicants
Funded positions available to both Canadian and international surgeons
Visa support
Institution policyinternational clinical fellows need an IRCC work permit; the PGME Office issues an LMIA-exempt offer of employment number and supports the work permit application
Licence requirement
Institution policyclinical fellows must hold a CPSO certificate of registration authorising postgraduate education (educational licence); research fellows do not need CPSO registration

How to apply

Deadline
Applications accepted any time; decisions made in April for the following year
Application method
UHN online Fellowship Application; reference letters to the Fellowship Director
