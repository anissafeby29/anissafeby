Overview

ASOPRS-recognised fellowship training across ophthalmic plastic and reconstructive surgery, orbit, skull base, trauma and cosmetic oculofacial care.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
Not stated on official page
Start
July, in odd calendar years
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
ASOPRS-recognised fellowship (formal ASOPRS accreditation 2022)
Department
Wilmer Eye Institute, Oculoplastic Surgery Division

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed ACGME or Royal College ophthalmology residency; ASOPRS match requirements apply
International applicants
Only graduates of ACGME or Royal College of Physicians and Surgeons of Canada ophthalmology residencies
Visa support
Institution policyJohns Hopkins SOM sponsors incoming residents/fellows for J-1 (ECFMG); H-1B only in limited cases (US MD graduates on OPT in ACGME training, or trainees already on H-1B in ACGME-accredited/recognised training with sufficient H-1B time remaining) - GME policy P&F028
Licence requirement
Institution policy (Maryland Board of Physicians): residents and fellows train as registered unlicensed practitioners through their programme; a full Maryland licence requires 1 year (US/Canadian grads) or ECFMG + 2 years (IMGs) of accredited postgraduate training plus licensing exams

How to apply

Deadline
ASOPRS match via SF Match, held in winter roughly 1.5 years before the July start; see ASOPRS for dates
Application method
SF Match (ASOPRS match) Central Application Service
