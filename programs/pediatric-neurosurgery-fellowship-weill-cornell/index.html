Overview

An ACPNF-accredited fellowship based at the Weill Cornell and Columbia campuses of the Children's Hospital of New York providing comprehensive training in paediatric neurosurgical disorders.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Residents or graduates of ACGME-approved US neurosurgery residencies or RCPSC-approved Canadian neurosurgery residencies
International applicants
Graduates of foreign neurosurgical residency programmes may apply for a match number, but completion will not confer eligibility for American Board of Pediatric Neurological Surgery certification
Visa support
Institution policyNewYork-Presbyterian GME accepts foreign nationals only with J-1 (ECFMG) status or their own work authorisation; no H-1B, H-1B1, F-1 or E-3 sponsorship; O-1 supported only in very limited cases (NYP GME Visa Rules, 2027 recruitment packet)
Licence requirement
Institution policy (NY State Education Dept): trainees in ACGME-accredited programmes do not need a NY physician licence or limited permit; other (non-ACGME) clinical fellowship posts require a NY licence or limited permit (IMGs need ECFMG certification). NYP reimburses initial licence fee ($735) and renewals
