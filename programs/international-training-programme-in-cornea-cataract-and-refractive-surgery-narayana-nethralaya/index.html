Overview

Intensive programme for international ophthalmologists in cataract, keratoconus, dry eye and anterior segment management and refractive surgery, with a monthly logbook.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Postgraduate qualification in ophthalmology (MS or equivalent; DO/DOMS may apply), registered to practise ophthalmology independently in own country; microscope experience desirable
International applicants
Yes - training programme designed for international ophthalmologists
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Temporary registration from the National Medical Commission of India (Section 34, NMC Act) required; process may take 2-3 months
