Institution policyMust have completed all time and examination requirements to be registered as a specialist in home country (or RCPSC specialist requirements), with letter from home specialty college

Institution policyYes - international specialists may train as Postgraduate Fellows (CPSBC educational licence generally granted to IMGs who will return home)

Institution policyForeign fellows need a work permit; sponsoring department obtains an offer-of-employment ID (LMIA-exempt route) and fellow completes the immigration application
