Overview

A RANZCOG subspecialty program equipping FRANZCOG specialists to diagnose and treat complicated pregnancies and ensure continuity of care from the fetal to newborn periods.

The programme

Training type
Subspecialty Training Programme
Duration
3 years (Years 1–3 clinical training, plus post-Year 3 requirements)
Start
Training year begins early February (Semester 1)
