Overview

Full-time training in eyelid, lacrimal and orbital disorders including thyroid eye disease, oncology and trauma with multidisciplinary collaboration.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
12 months
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
1 per year for the NUH programme and 1 per year for the Cluster programme (rotating NUH, Ng Teng Fong General Hospital, Alexandra Hospital)
Accreditation
NUH fellowship certificate on completion of the full programme
Department
Department of Ophthalmology, National University Health System (NUHS)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Recognised medical degree for SMC temporary registration; MMed (Ophthalmology), FRCS or equivalent with 2 years advanced general ophthalmology rotations; 3 years additional experience/advanced training preferable; teaching/research record; English (IELTS 7 each band, TOEFL iBT 100, or OET B if degree not taught in English); 2 referees; certificate of good standing; sponsorship by government, health authority or employer in home country
International applicants
Yes - programme accepts overseas doctors; NUH Fellowship Desk assists with registration and permits
Visa support
Yes - NUH Fellowship Desk assists in obtaining a Training Employment Pass (TEP) from the Ministry of Manpower after SMC approval
Licence requirement
Temporary medical registration with the Singapore Medical Council (SMC); BCLS certification on boarding; malpractice and health insurance
