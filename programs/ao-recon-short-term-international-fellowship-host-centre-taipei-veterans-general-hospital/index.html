Overview

AO-funded short-term adult reconstruction / arthroplasty fellowship placement at Taipei Veterans General Hospital (Taipei, Taiwan) performing about 10000 surgical cases a year.

The programme

Training type
Short-term International Fellowship (observership with scrub-in where permitted)
Duration
6-12 weeks
Start
Placement year after selection
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
AO Foundation fellowship host centre
Department
Division of Joint Reconstruction, Department of Orthopaedics and Traumatology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Arthroplasty is the main focus of practice; working knowledge of English; younger than 45 in year of application; AO Recon course attendance beneficial; teaching-hospital employment recommended
International applicants
Yes - AO Foundation global programme; fellows from any country are allocated to host centres by AO (membership and course prerequisites apply)
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Scrub-in: Yes. Dominant OR language: Chinese (team English written (4) / spoken (4) on a 1-5 scale)

How to apply

Deadline
Annual AO Recon call (2027 call announced on AO Recon page)
Application method
Online application to the AO Foundation during the open call (host centres allocated by AO); not directly to the hospital

Funding

Funding
Small AO Recon stipend (benchmark USD 550/week in the US, adjusted for local price level); travel and insurance paid by fellow
