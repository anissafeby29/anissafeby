Overview

A one-year fellowship in inner-city community psychiatry serving marginalised urban populations through St. Michael's Hospital.

The programme

Focus
Public / community psychiatry
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
July 1 preferred (alternative dates negotiable)
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
University of Toronto PGME-registered fellowship (not RCPSC-accredited subspecialty training)
Department
Not stated on official page

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Medical degree from a recognised school and specialist designation in psychiatry; must meet CPSO minimum licensure eligibility
International applicants
Yes — internationally trained specialists eligible if they meet CPSO requirements; English proficiency results may be requested; government-sponsored trainees from Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, UAE and Saudi Arabia apply via STAR
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
CPSO educational (or independent) licence eligibility required

How to apply

Deadline
Rolling (apply well in advance)
Application method
One-page letter of intent and CV to the fellowship co-leads, plus University of Toronto fellowship application

Funding

Funding
Self-sourced stipend (minimum CA$78,190.61/year) via scholarships, return-of-service, home-country sponsorship or OHIP billing
