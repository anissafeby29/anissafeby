Advanced Head and Neck Oncologic and Microvascular Reconstructive Surgery Fellowship

Overview

Prepares fellows for careers as comprehensive head and neck surgical oncologists, with a one-year clinical track or a two-year research track; fellows are appointed clinical instructors.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Board certified or eligible in Otolaryngology, Plastic Surgery or General Surgery
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs from non-LCME schools must hold ECFMG certification before entry; non-US citizens need employer-sponsored visa (J-1/H-1B per programme)
Visa support
Institution policyUPMC ME programmes may (not required to) offer J-1 via ECFMG and/or H-1B; programme can offer either or both; H-1B requires USMLE Steps 1-3 passed by match date
Licence requirement
Must be eligible for Pennsylvania medical licensure
