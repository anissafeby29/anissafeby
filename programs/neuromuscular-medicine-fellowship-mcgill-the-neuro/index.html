Overview

Clinical neuromuscular and EMG training at the Montreal Neurological and General Hospitals, with optional CNSF EMG certification and research.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed residency and Royal College or Board (or equivalent) certification
International applicants
Yes — McGill PGME accepts international medical graduates (Canadian, US and foreign citizens) for up to three years of fellowship training in Québec; government-sponsored trainees apply via contract route
