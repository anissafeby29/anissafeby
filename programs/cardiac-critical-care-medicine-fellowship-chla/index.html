Overview

A one-year non-ACGME post-fellowship programme in pediatric cardiac critical care centred on CHLA's Cardiothoracic Intensive Care Unit.

The programme

Training type
Advanced Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
July
Positions
1-2 fellows annually
Accreditation
Non-ACGME
Department
Division of Critical Care Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed Pediatric Critical Care or Pediatric Cardiology fellowship
International applicants
Yes - U.S. citizenship not required
Visa support
Institution policyCHLA sponsors J-1 visas (ECFMG); application/visa fees and support available to trainees in ACGME-accredited or ACGME non-standard programmes
Licence requirement
Eligible for California medical licence

How to apply

Deadline
1 September for July start
Application method
Email PCICS common application form, personal statement, CV, three letters and medical diploma to CardiacCCMFellowship@chla.usc.edu

Funding

Funding
Salary minimums PGY-4 $84,073.60, PGY-5 $87,401.60, PGY-6 $90,667.20, PGY-7 $93,828.80, PGY-8 $96,948.80 plus housing and meal stipends
