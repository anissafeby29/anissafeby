Overview

One-year glaucoma fellowship at the University of Iowa Department of Ophthalmology & Visual Sciences.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
1 July (orientation); runs 1 July-30 June
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Department of Ophthalmology & Visual Sciences

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of an accredited ophthalmology residency by 1 July start
International applicants
Institution policyGraduates of non-US/Canadian schools eligible with a valid ECFMG certificate (or full unrestricted US licence, or Fifth Pathway); visa trainees accepted at programme discretion
Visa support
Institution policyAcceptable visas: J-1 (ECFMG), J-2 with EAD, H-1B, H-4 with EAD, E-3 (Australian citizens), F-1 OPT (first residency year only); no department is required to accept visa trainees; J-1 requires USMLE Steps 1-2 passed, H-1B requires Steps 1-3 passed before offer
Licence requirement
Must be licensed in Iowa by 30 June before start

How to apply

Deadline
Normally accepted until 1 October
Application method
SF Match Central Application Service only

Funding
