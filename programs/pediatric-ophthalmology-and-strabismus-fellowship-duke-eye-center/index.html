Overview

One-year fellowship covering all aspects of pediatric ophthalmology and strabismus in a division seeing more than 20,000 children annually.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Institution policyLCME/AOA-accredited MD/DO graduates, or international graduates with a valid ECFMG certificate or full unrestricted US licence; NC full licence or RTL, I-9 employment eligibility, background and NPDB checks
International applicants
IMGs who do not qualify for a full North Carolina medical licence cannot be offered positions
Visa support
No - Duke does not provide immigration sponsorship for fellows / clinical associates
Licence requirement
Full North Carolina medical licensure required (training licence not accepted)

How to apply

Deadline
Fall of the year before training (about two months before the match deadline)
Application method
SF Match; two letters of reference

Funding

Funding
Institution policyGME trainees paid on the Duke stipend scale; FY27 (from 1 July 2026) PGY-1 $71,052 rising to PGY-4 $79,908, PGY-5 $82,992, PGY-6 $86,292, PGY-7 $90,036 (up to PGY-12 $106,800), plus benefits
