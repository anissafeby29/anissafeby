Overview

Baylor's three-year REI Fellowship trains competent REI clinicians who function as independent investigators, with access to the Cell Biology department's research faculty.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Graduates of U.S. accredited or osteopathic medical schools who completed a four-year ACGME-accredited OB/GYN residency
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs welcomed and encouraged to apply; ECFMG certification required (except Canadian graduates); must secure an appropriate visa and Texas Medical Board permit

Institution policyVisa types sponsored vary by department (check with the programme); IMGs must secure an appropriate visa
