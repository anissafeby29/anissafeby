International Training Program in Sleep Medicine

Faculty of Medicine, Chulalongkorn University / King Chulalongkorn Memorial Hospital

Destination
THA

Overview

International training programme giving trainees comprehensive knowledge of the diagnosis and treatment of adult and paediatric sleep disorders, including polysomnography interpretation and a clinical research project.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship (international training programme)
Duration
6 months
Start
1 January (runs 1 January to 30 June)
Positions
Up to 3 attendees per year
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
