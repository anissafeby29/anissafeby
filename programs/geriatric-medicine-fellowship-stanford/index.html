Overview

A one-year ACGME-accredited joint Stanford University and VA Palo Alto Health Care System fellowship, administered by the Palo Alto VA GRECC, that prepares fellows to become skilled practitioners and leaders in Geriatric Medicine.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (optional additional 2-year advanced research training)
Start
July 1
Positions
Up to 3 fellows per year
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited
Department
Section of Geriatric Medicine, Department of Medicine; VA Palo Alto Health Care System GRECC

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of an ACGME or AOA accredited residency in Family Medicine or Internal Medicine by fellowship start; certified or actively pursuing board certification in primary specialty; USMLE/COMLEX Steps 1-3.
International applicants
IMGs accepted with ECFMG certification; applicants must be US citizens, permanent residents or hold a J-1 visa.
Visa support
J-1 only; Stanford does not sponsor H-1B visas for international medical graduates.
Licence requirement
Must be license-eligible in California

How to apply

Deadline
Applications open in July via ERAS; interviews August-October; NRMP rank list deadline in November (no fixed closing date stated)
Application method
ERAS; NRMP Match
