Overview

Clinical and research training in kidney disease across Stanford Hospital, the VA Palo Alto system and Santa Clara Valley Medical Center, with multiple scholarly pathways.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of an ACGME-accredited residency in the United States (internal medicine); three letters incl. PD, MSPE, USMLE Steps 1 and 2
International applicants
Limited - must have completed a US ACGME-accredited residency; J-1 sponsorship only
Visa support
J-1 only; H-1B not sponsored
Licence requirement
Institution policyMedical Board of California Postgraduate Training License required within 180 days of starting an ACGME programme (USMLE Steps 1 & 2 CK passed); non-ACGME programmes require a full California licence by the start date
