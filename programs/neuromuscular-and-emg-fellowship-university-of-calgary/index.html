Neuromuscular and EMG Fellowship

University of Calgary (Cumming School of Medicine)

NeurologyNeuromuscular medicineClinical Fellowship
Destination
CAN
Calgary, Alberta, Canada
Open official programme page ↗Compare saved →

Overview

Training for sub-specialisation in neuromuscular neurology and EMG, with an optional clinical research fellowship emphasising trials and academic development.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year clinical (1–2 years with research focus); 2–3 year clinical research track
Start
July 1
Positions
2–3 per year
Accreditation
University of Calgary PGME clinical fellowship (not RCPSC-accredited)
Department
Department of Clinical Neurosciences, University of Calgary

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Not stated on official page
International applicants
Yes — international applicants must have confirmed external salary funding
Visa support
IMGs who are not Canadian citizens/PRs need a Canadian work permit (processing at least 10 months for international applicants)
Licence requirement
CPSA educational licence (Provisional Register – Postgraduate Trainee) via UCalgary PGME

How to apply

Deadline
April 15, 2027 for July 2028 start (applications open January 1, 2027; virtual interviews May 1–15)
Application method
Email personal statement, CV and two letters (sent directly by referees) to the fellowship administrator

Funding

Funding
Fellows must apply for competitive awards (e.g. NMD4C); international applicants need external salary funding
