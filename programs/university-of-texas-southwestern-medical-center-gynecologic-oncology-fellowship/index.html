Overview

University of Texas Southwestern Medical Center's Dallas programme appears in the Society of Gynecologic Oncology's national participating-program list for advanced multidisciplinary training in gynaecologic cancer care.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years
Start
1 July
Positions
Approved for 4 fellows in a rotating 2-1-1 format
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited
Department
Department of Obstetrics and Gynecology, Division of Gynecologic Oncology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed ACGME- or RCPSC-accredited OBGYN residency; passed ABOG written exam; eligible for Texas medical licence; 3 letters incl. Gyn-Onc SLOE and PD SLOE
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs must be ECFMG-certified and hold an ECFMG-sponsored J-1 visa for clinical training at UTSW
Visa support
Institution policyClinical residents/fellows (ACGME and non-ACGME) on J-1 sponsored by ECFMG; UTSW does not usually sponsor H-1B for clinical training
Licence requirement
Texas medical licence required before fellowship start (1 July)

How to apply

Deadline
Follows SGO common application and interview-invitation dates
Application method
ERAS (programme no. 2254822002) and NRMP Match

Funding

Funding
Institution policyPaid house-staff stipend (employer varies: UTSW, Parkland, Children's Health or Scottish Rite); 2026-27 scale PGY4 $82,225, PGY5 $86,343, PGY6 $89,505, PGY7 $95,050
