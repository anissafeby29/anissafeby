International Surgical Fellowship Programme – Otolaryngology Head and Neck Surgery

Royal College of Surgeons in Ireland (RCSI) / HSE

Destination

Overview

Two years of structured postgraduate surgical training at RCSI training sites in Ireland for sponsored international surgical trainees, available in otolaryngology–head and neck surgery among other specialties.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Suitably qualified surgical trainees nominated by their employer or another sponsoring agency; international medical graduates from outside the EU/EEA/Switzerland
International applicants
Yes — designed for IMGs from outside the EU, EEA and Switzerland
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Regulatory requirement: registration with the Medical Council of Ireland in the appropriate division (e.g. General, Supervised or Trainee Specialist) is mandatory to practise
