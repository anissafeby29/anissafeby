Overview

Non-ACGME one-year (extendable to two) fellowship focused on clinical/translational research with additional clinical education, for trainees pursuing academic paediatric otolaryngology.

The programme

Training type
Research Fellowship
Duration
1 year (option to extend to 2)
Start
1 July – 30 June
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Non-ACGME-accredited
Department
Division of Pediatric Otolaryngology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed an OHNS residency; eligible for temporary or permanent Illinois medical licence; USMLE Steps 1–3; ABOto-eligible candidates preferred
International applicants
Open to international candidates who are ECFMG-certified and have passed USMLE Steps 1–3
Visa support
J-1 and H-1B visas will be considered
Licence requirement
Temporary or permanent Illinois medical licence
