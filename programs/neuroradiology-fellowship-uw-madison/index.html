Overview

An ACGME-accredited one-year neuroradiology fellowship, with an optional 12-month clinical instructorship second year for further subspecialty training.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
1 July
Positions
5 per year
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited
Department
Department of Radiology, UW–Madison

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Graduate of an ACGME or RCPSC-accredited diagnostic radiology residency; Wisconsin medical license before start; Canadian graduates must be LMCC and hold H-1B.
International applicants
Not stated on official page
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Must possess a Wisconsin medical license before beginning fellowship.

How to apply

Deadline
For 2028-29: ERAS submission from 1 September 2026; interviews 11 January–31 March 2027.
Application method
ERAS (December cycle); NRMP neuroradiology match; SCARD policies.

Funding

Funding
Appointed as Clinical Instructor; moonlighting available.
