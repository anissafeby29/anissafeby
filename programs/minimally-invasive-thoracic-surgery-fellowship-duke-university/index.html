Overview

Advanced training in minimally invasive and robotic lung and oesophageal resection, thoracic and foregut oncology, and benign foregut surgery.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed an ACGME-accredited (or international equivalent) integrated CT residency, 4/3 CT residency or traditional 2–3 year CT fellowship; board-eligible/certified by ABTS or equivalent international board
International applicants
Foreign medical graduates who do not plan to practise in the US are also eligible
Visa support
Sponsorship of J-1 visas only
Licence requirement
Eligible for a full or resident training licence in North Carolina
