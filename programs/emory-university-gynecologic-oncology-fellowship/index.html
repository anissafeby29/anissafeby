Overview

Emory University's Atlanta programme appears in the Society of Gynecologic Oncology's national participating-program list for advanced multidisciplinary training in gynaecologic cancer care.

The programme

Clinical Fellowship

3 years (first year entirely research)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed Obstetrics and Gynaecology residency; ERAS, NRMP and institution-specific requirements apply
International applicants
Not explicitly stated; application requires ECFMG certificate if applicable
Visa support
Institution policyIMGs must be ECFMG-certified and enter training on a J-1 (ECFMG-sponsored) or H-1B visa unless US citizen/green-card holder (Emory GME housestaff policy 5.12)
Licence requirement
Institution policyGeorgia Temporary Postgraduate Training Permit or full Georgia medical licence required before clinical care (training permit max 7 years); USMLE Step 3 required for entry at PGY4+

How to apply

Deadline
Follows the SGO Program Directors Network application timeline (no date on programme page)
Application method
ERAS and NRMP Match (3 letters incl. residency PD and a gyn onc faculty member; CREOG scores and ECFMG certificate if applicable)
