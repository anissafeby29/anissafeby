Overview

Funded fourth-year advanced fellowships in interventional catheterisation, cardiac ICU, echo/CT/MRI, transplant/heart failure, pulmonary hypertension, electrophysiology and clinical effectiveness.

The programme

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Graduates of, or senior fellows in, ACGME-accredited pediatric cardiology programmes; candidates from equivalent overseas programmes considered
International applicants
Candidates from equivalent programmes overseas will be considered; foreign graduates should check Medical Board of California requirements
Visa support
J-1 visas accepted
Licence requirement
California medical licence required; all applicants must be eligible for California licensure

How to apply

Deadline
Uniform applications accepted 15 June - 30 August
Application method
Uniform application, CV and 3 letters to Fellowship Coordinator and Subspecialty Program Director (no match); CVICU applicants use PCICS application form
