Overview

Honorary clinical fellowship in pulmonology and critical care at basic and advanced levels.

The programme

Focus
Respiratory and critical care medicine
Training type
Honorary Clinical Fellowship
Duration
Maximum 12 months (honorary clinical fellowship)
Start
Estimated 6-8 months after required documents reach SMC; plus about 2 weeks after arrival for SMC registration
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Department of Medicine (Respiratory & Critical Care)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
NUH general requirements: basic medical degree; national licensing exam where applicable; 12 months housemanship; registered and in active practice in home country for the preceding 3 years; at least 3 years as Medical Officer after housemanship; good standing; 2 referees; sponsored by government, regional health authority or home institution (full-time employed); English test (IELTS 7 each / TOEFL iBT 100 / OET B) if basic degree not taught in English; plus department-specific requirements
International applicants
Yes - the Honorary Clinical Fellowship scheme offers foreign doctors attachments to NUH specialties
Visa support
Training Employment Pass (TEP) from Ministry of Manpower required; dependants' passes cannot be applied for under the TEP
Licence requirement
Temporary Medical Registration with the Singapore Medical Council

How to apply

Deadline
Not stated on official page
Application method
Download the NUH application form and email the completed form with documents to fellowship_application@nuhs.edu.sg (honorary positions only)

Funding

Funding
Honorary (unpaid) - no salary, stipend, allowance or accommodation. Fees after acceptance: NUH processing S$560, SMC application S$300, registration/practising certificate S$550, malpractice insurance S$966/12 months, BCLS S$150, TEP S$360, plus departmental training fee if applicable
