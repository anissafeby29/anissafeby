Overview

University of Chicago's Chicago programme appears in the Society of Gynecologic Oncology's national participating-program list for advanced multidisciplinary training in gynaecologic cancer care.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed Obstetrics and Gynaecology residency; ERAS, NRMP and institution-specific requirements apply
International applicants
Institution policyForeign nationals accepted with visa sponsorship, primarily J-1 (ECFMG); H-1B case by case
Visa support
Institution policyPrimarily J-1 via ECFMG (preferred); H-1B case by case only with programme and GME Office approval (programme bears H-1B costs)
Licence requirement
Institution policyIllinois temporary (training) or permanent medical licence required before any patient care; cost borne by trainee

How to apply

Deadline
Application opened December 2025; deadline 5 April 2026 (closed); interview invitations 11 May 2026
Application method
ERAS Fellowships (CAF, test transcripts) plus diploma and CREOG scores sent to programme; NRMP

Funding

Funding
Institution policyUniform UCMC GME stipend scale FY26: PGY4 $86,036; PGY5 $89,776; PGY6 $91,647; PGY7 $95,154; PGY8 $96,824 (PGY9+ by approved letter)
