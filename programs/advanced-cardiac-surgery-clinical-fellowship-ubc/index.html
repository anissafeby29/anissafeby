Advanced Cardiac Surgery Clinical Fellowship

University of British Columbia (St. Paul's Hospital, Vancouver General Hospital, BC Children's Hospital)

Cardiothoracic SurgeryCongenital cardiac surgeryClinical Fellowship
Destination

Overview

Advanced clinical fellowships at three UBC teaching hospitals, including a St. Paul's track with 25–35 transplants, 15–20 assist devices and 20–30 ECMOs a year.

The programme

Focus
Adult cardiac / heart transplant / congenital
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (maximum 2 years)
Start
1 July
Positions
1 at each hospital
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Division of Cardiac Surgery

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Successfully completed a cardiac surgical residency recognised by the Royal College of Physicians and Surgeons of Canada
International applicants
Open to Canadian and international applicants
Visa support
Institution policy (UBC PGME): foreign trainees must hold an IRCC work permit for the whole training period; the sponsoring department/PGME submits an offer of employment to IRCC and the fellow applies under the LMIA-exempt category
Licence requirement
Institution policy (CPSBC/UBC PGME): postgraduate trainees and fellows at UBC must be licensed with the College of Physicians and Surgeons of BC (educational - postgraduate class; fellow licence max 4 years) before training starts; the department obtains the licence, IMGs need physiciansapply.ca source verification and CPSBC English proficiency

How to apply

Deadline
31 March of the year prior to the start date
Application method
Direct application to the UBC Division of Cardiac Surgery; applications reviewed each April for a 1 July start the following year

Funding

Funding
Own funding through sponsorship preferred, but funding can be provided by the Division of Cardiac Surgery
