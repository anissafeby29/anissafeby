Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
US/Canadian LCME/AOA graduate or foreign graduate with valid ECFMG certificate, full US licence or Fifth Pathway; completed accredited 3-year IM residency (or 2 years for ABIM Research Pathway)
International applicants
Graduates of schools outside North America eligible with valid ECFMG certificate (programme page)
Visa support
Sponsors applicants on J-1 or H-1B visas (programme page)
