Fellowship in Paediatric Ophthalmology and Strabismus

University Malaya Medical Centre (UMMC)

OphthalmologyPaediatric ophthalmology & strabismusClinical Fellowship
Destination
MYS
Kuala Lumpur, Federal Territory of Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia
Open official programme page ↗

Overview

Clinically oriented fellowship in paediatric eye disease and adult strabismus including adjustable sutures, paediatric cataract exposure and ROP screening/treatment.

The programme

Focus
Paediatric ophthalmology
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1-year or 3-year programme
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
UMMC fellowship; can count toward Malaysian National Specialist Register subspecialty training
Department
Department of Ophthalmology, Faculty of Medicine, Universiti Malaya

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Postgraduate qualification recognised by the Malaysian National Specialist Registry; teaching/research record; good English
International applicants
Not explicitly stated for this track; UMMC portal sets non-Malaysian fees and requirements
Visa support
Applicants must be approved for a Professional Visit Pass (PLIK) by the Immigration Department of Malaysia (no sponsorship stated)
Licence requirement
Registration with the Malaysian Medical Council; approval by UMMC Credentialing & Privileging Committee

How to apply

Deadline
At least 6 months before intended start (non-Malaysian hands-on/fellowship)
Application method
Online via the UMMC Fellowship/Training Attachment Programme e-services portal; non-Malaysians apply at least 6 months before start

Funding

Funding
Non-Malaysian training fee RM36,000 per year (+RM100 management fee); 15%/30% discount for lower-middle/low-income countries
