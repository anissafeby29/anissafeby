Overview

Asan Medical Center's international visiting scholar programme training overseas physicians in AMC's clinical departments for up to a year.

The programme

Focus
Multiple medical specialties (host department approval required)
Training type
Visiting scholar / observership-type clinical training
Duration
Up to 1 year (extension only with department approval)
Start
Year-round
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Host clinical department (approval required; departments not listed on page)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Physician with medical degree and medical licence, working in an overseas hospital; proficient in Korean or English (TOEIC/IELTS score or telephone interview may be required)
International applicants
Yes - ASAN International Visiting Scholars (IViS) Program for overseas physicians
Visa support
Visa assistance provided for training periods exceeding 90 days
Licence requirement
Medical licence from home country required; Korean licensure not stated; travel/injury insurance required

How to apply

Deadline
At least 3 months before the start of training
Application method
Online application to Asan Medical International (AMI) with application form, recommendation letter, CV, employment certificate, medical licence, diploma, passport, photo and immunisation form; reviewed by AMI and approved by the host department (and AMC Education Committee if over 1 month)
