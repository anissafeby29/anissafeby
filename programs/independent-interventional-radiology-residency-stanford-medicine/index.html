Overview

An ACGME-accredited program offering 1 or 2 years of dedicated IR training after diagnostic radiology residency, leading to eligibility for dual IR/DR certification from the ABR.

The programme

Training type
Independent Residency (post-DR)
Duration
1 or 2 years (ESIR graduates may enter in the second/final year)
Start
July 2027 (2026–2027 NRMP cycle; Match Day 5 May 2027)
Positions
4
Accreditation
ACGME accredited
Department
Department of Radiology, Stanford University School of Medicine (Division of Vascular and Interventional Radiology)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of a diagnostic radiology residency; both ESIR and non-ESIR applicants accepted.
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs welcome to apply; must hold a valid ECFMG certificate and be able to obtain Medical Board of California licensure; non-ACGME fellowships need ACGME Non-Standard Training recognition before J-1 sponsorship
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 (ECFMG) is the primary visa; H-1B permitted only in some circumstances with GME approval; O-1 rarely, varies by department
Licence requirement
Institution policyMedical Board of California Postgraduate Training License required within 180 days of starting an ACGME programme (USMLE Steps 1 & 2 CK passed); non-ACGME programmes require a full California licence by the start date

How to apply

Deadline
All materials must be received by 2 December 2026
Application method
ERAS (ACGME/RCPSC/UCNS Fellowship – December cycle) and NRMP Radiology Fellowship Match; personal statement, CV, 3 letters, USMLE Steps 1–3, photograph; ESIR verification for ESIR applicants.

Funding

Funding
Institution policyPaid on Stanford Health Care housestaff scale 2026-27: PGY4 $113,276.80, PGY5 $119,558.40, PGY6 $124,280.00, PGY7 $130,540.80 (base includes housing allowance)
