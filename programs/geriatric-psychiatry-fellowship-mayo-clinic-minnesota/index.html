Overview

A one-year clinical fellowship in the prevention, diagnosis and management of mental disorders in older adults across medical, surgical and neurological settings.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
July
Positions
1 per year
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited
Department
Department of Psychiatry and Psychology, Mayo Clinic School of Graduate Medical Education

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of a general psychiatry residency in an ACGME-accredited, AOA-approved, ACGME-I Advanced Specialty-accredited, RCPSC- or CFPC-accredited programme; final-year residents welcome; qualified for ABPN psychiatry certification
International applicants
Open to international applicants; graduates of medical schools outside the US/Canada must hold ECFMG certification before appointment
Visa support
Mayo Clinic supports ECFMG J-1 and H-1B sponsorship (J-1 requires an ACGME-accredited/recognised programme; no new H-1B petitions requiring the US$100,000 payment); final visa type at Mayo's discretion
Licence requirement
Fellows must obtain and maintain an appropriate medical licence while enrolled

How to apply

Deadline
September 1 of the previous year or until filled (rolling interviews)
Application method
Online application via Mayo's Recruit system; no matching program; letters dated within six months

Funding

Funding
Salaried trainee position (stipend and benefits per Mayo Clinic School of GME)
