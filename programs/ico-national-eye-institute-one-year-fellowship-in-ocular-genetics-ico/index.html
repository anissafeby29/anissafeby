Overview

Twelve-month ocular genetics training served at the NEI/NIH in Bethesda for eligible ophthalmologists from ICO-eligible countries.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed residency; passed specialist ophthalmology exam; permanently based and working in an ICO-eligible country; reasonable fluency in English or host language; must return home after training; no prior ICO award
International applicants
Yes - for ICO-eligible candidates
