Adult Interventional Cardiology Fellowship (AFC)

Libin Cardiovascular Institute, University of Calgary (Foothills Medical Centre)

CardiologyInterventional & structural cardiologyClinical Fellowship
Destination
CAN
Calgary, Alberta, Canada
Open official programme page ↗

Overview

A two-year Royal College AFC interventional programme in a 7,800-procedure, 2,400-PCI per year service covering STEMI, complex/CTO PCI, structural intervention and mechanical circulatory support.

The programme

Focus
Interventional cardiology
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
2 years
Start
1 July (next intake 1 July 2028; July 2027 positions filled)
Positions
2–4 trainees at a time
Accreditation
Royal College of Physicians and Surgeons of Canada – Area of Focused Competence (AFC); AFC Diploma eligible
Department
Department of Cardiac Sciences, Cumming School of Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Royal College certified or eligible for Royal College cardiology exams (or equivalent); international applicants must show training equivalency (≥8 months clinical cardiology incl. ≥4 months CCU, ≥3 months cath, ≥4 months echo, ≥1 month EP, ≥1 month nuclear/stress)
International applicants
Yes – positions available for international applicants; must demonstrate training equivalency and meet CPSA English proficiency requirements
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Educational licence through the College of Physicians & Surgeons of Alberta (CPSA) arranged via PGME after acceptance

How to apply

Deadline
Domestic: 31 December 2026; International: 31 March 2027 (for July 2028 start)
Application method
Letter of intent, CV, three physician references (institutional email), one allied-health reference, proof of degrees/certification and English proficiency to the Training Committee

Funding

Funding
Travel funding for courses and conferences mentioned; salary not stated
