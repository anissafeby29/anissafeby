Mayo Clinic School of Graduate Medical Education

Overview

A first-of-its-kind two-year US fellowship training future oncology leaders from low- and middle-income countries, with 12 months of clinical radiation oncology plus medical oncology, palliative care, radiology, dosimetry and research.

The programme

Focus

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Graduate of a recognised US or international medical school who completed oncology residency (e.g. radiation or clinical oncology) in a low- or middle-income country and intends to return to practise there
International applicants
Yes — designed specifically for oncologists from low- and middle-income countries
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
ECFMG certification and USMLE Step 3 required before start

How to apply

Deadline
31 March of the year preceding the start; interviews April–May (rolling possible)
Application method
Online via Mayo Clinic 'Recruit' application service (no printed applications)

Funding

Funding
Endowment-supported (Davenport family gift); research support and funding available
