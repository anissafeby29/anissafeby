Overview

One of the largest pediatric endocrinology training programmes in the United States, affiliated with Texas Children's Hospital.

The programme

Training type

July (entry in July following the December Fall Subspecialty Match)

4 per year

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of an ACGME-accredited US pediatric residency prior to matriculation
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs welcomed and encouraged to apply; ECFMG certification required (except Canadian graduates); must secure an appropriate visa and Texas Medical Board permit
Visa support
Institution policyVisa types sponsored vary by department (check with the programme); IMGs must secure an appropriate visa
Licence requirement
Institution policyTexas Medical Board Physician-in-Training permit or full Texas medical licence required before starting
