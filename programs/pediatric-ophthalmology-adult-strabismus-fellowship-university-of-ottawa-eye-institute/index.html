Pediatric Ophthalmology & Adult Strabismus Fellowship

Overview

One-year fellowship centred at CHEO with adult strabismus at iCare, building competence across medical and surgical paediatric ophthalmology and adult strabismus.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
1 July
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Institution policyUniversity of Ottawa clinical fellowship - does not lead to any Canadian (RCPSC/CFPC) certification
Department
Department of Ophthalmology, University of Ottawa

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
MD from approved school; completed ophthalmology residency or equivalent; documented paediatric/adult exam and primary horizontal strabismus surgery experience
International applicants
Priority to Canadian residency graduates; special consideration for international candidates who will practise in regions lacking paediatric ophthalmology resources; foreign graduates contact coordinator first
Visa support
Institution policyinternational trainees need an IRCC work permit (LMIA-exempt); the uOttawa PGME/immigration advisor provides the documents needed to apply (CPSO letter of eligibility and LMIA-exemption number)
Licence requirement
Educational licence from the College of Physicians and Surgeons of Ontario (stated for uveitis; applies to trainees generally)

How to apply

Deadline
30 September of the year preceding the fellowship (notification by 1 December)
Application method
Personal statement, CV, transcripts and three reference letters to the Departmental Education Program Coordinator

Funding

Funding
$60,000/year for an applicant without external funding
