Overview

An intensive one-year fellowship preparing physicians for academic careers in BMT and immune effector cell therapies, including CAR-T and NK cells.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
Institution policyMost UCSF residents/fellows are appointed for June-August start dates (off-cycle starts possible)
Positions
2
Accreditation
Non-ACGME
Department
Division of Hematology and Oncology, Department of Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Board certified in internal medicine and BE/BC graduate of an accredited US hematology and/or medical oncology fellowship
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs eligible if their school is in the World Directory/FAIMER and they hold a valid ECFMG certificate at application; non-US citizens need permanent residence, an appropriate visa or DACA
Visa support
US citizens, permanent residents or J-1 visa holders only (H-1B not accepted)
Licence requirement
Must be eligible for a California medical licence
