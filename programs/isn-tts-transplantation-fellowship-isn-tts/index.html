ISN-TTS Transplantation Fellowship

International Society of Nephrology (ISN) / The Transplantation Society (TTS)

Overview

A joint ISN-TTS fellowship giving young physicians from low-resource areas hands-on training in transplantation at advanced host institutions to raise transplant standards at home.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Within 5 years of last doctoral degree; from a lower, lower-middle or upper-middle income country; training in tissue typing, transplant nephrology, transplant coordination, transplant surgery or related renal transplantation fields.
International applicants
For young physicians from low-resource areas training at advanced host institutions.
