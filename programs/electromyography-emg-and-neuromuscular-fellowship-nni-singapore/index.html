Overview

Training in planning, performing and interpreting EMG studies for neuromuscular conditions.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed or enrolled in an accredited neurology/rehabilitation medicine or equivalent programme (plus NNI general criteria)
International applicants
Yes — programmes are designed for overseas physicians (trained fellows from 15+ countries); must be sponsored by home government/health authority/employer
Visa support
NNI assists with Singapore Medical Council temporary registration and Ministry of Manpower Training Employment Pass (TEP)
Licence requirement
Singapore Medical Council temporary medical registration; MPS malpractice indemnity cover

How to apply

Deadline
At least 9 months before the intended start date
Application method
Online FormSG application with NNI form, CV, 2 recommendation letters and employer support letter, at least 9 months before start

Funding

Funding
Self-funded/sponsored — all training and personal expenses borne by the applicant; S$545 processing fee after acceptance
