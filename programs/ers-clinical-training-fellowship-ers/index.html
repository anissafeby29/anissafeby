ERS Clinical Training Fellowship

European Respiratory Society (ERS) – European host institutions

Overview

ERS-funded 1–6-month clinical fellowship to learn a respiratory skill or procedure not available at home, at an institution in another European country.

The programme

Focus
Respiratory medicine / pulmonology
Training type
Clinical Training Fellowship (society-funded)
Duration
1–6 months, full-time
Start
From 1 March 2027 (October 2026 call)
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
ERS (also ERS/ERN-LUNG joint fellowship for rare respiratory disease)
Department
Not stated on official page

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Active ERS member; qualified clinician or healthcare professional working in respiratory medicine, mainly early career; skill/procedure not available at home institution; mandatory return plan to home institution
International applicants
Yes – all nationalities; European and non-European based applicants may apply (Standard programme: Europe-to-Europe or overseas-to-Europe; separate programme for scientifically developing countries). Applicants must be eligible for a visa for the host country
Visa support
Fellow responsible; must be eligible for a visa for the fellowship period
Licence requirement
Must communicate in the language spoken at the host institution

How to apply

Deadline
1 October 2026 (call opened 1 August 2026; decisions mid-February 2027)
Application method
Online application with host supervisor acceptance form and home supervisor support form; host chosen by applicant (Web Host Registry can help)

Funding

Funding
ERS-funded (amount per application guidance)
