Overview

A one-year fellowship at Mayo Clinic Arizona's Comprehensive Stroke Center training neurologists in the management of stroke and all cerebrovascular disorders.

The programme

Focus
Stroke / vascular neurology
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
July
Positions
1 per year
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Department of Neurology, Mayo Clinic School of Graduate Medical Education

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of an accredited neurology or physical medicine residency in the US or Canada
International applicants
Open to international applicants; graduates of medical schools outside the US/Canada must hold ECFMG certification before appointment
Visa support
Page notes IMGs on J-1 visas may complete only one year of fellowship; Mayo sponsors ECFMG J-1 and (limited) H-1B
Licence requirement
Fellows must obtain and maintain an appropriate medical licence while enrolled

How to apply

Deadline
November 15 of the year preceding appointment
Application method
ERAS; Vascular Neurology Match (NRMP Specialties Matching Service); interviews February–March

Funding

Funding
Salaried trainee position (stipend and benefits per Mayo Clinic School of GME)
