Overview

A three-year, intensive academic GI and hepatology programme with particular strengths in GI oncology, transplant hepatology, therapeutic endoscopy and IBD, preparing fellows as leaders and physician scientists.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years
Start
July
Positions
5 per year (4 clinical track, 1 research track)
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited (continually accredited since 1987)
Department
Division of Gastroenterology and Hepatology, Department of Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Graduation from an accredited medical school (top two-thirds of class); continuous clinical practice with lapses not exceeding three years; completion of PGY-3 internal medicine in an ACGME-accredited programme.
International applicants
Must be a U.S. citizen, lawful permanent resident, refugee, asylee, or possess appropriate documentation to legally train at the University of Colorado.
