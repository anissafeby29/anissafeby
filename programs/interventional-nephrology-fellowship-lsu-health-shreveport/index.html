Overview

One of the earliest US interventional nephrology programmes, offering a one-year hands-on fellowship in dialysis access care with simulation training and ASDIN-aligned didactics.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed nephrology fellowship and board certified in internal medicine; certificates of IM residency and nephrology training (or nephrology board pass).
International applicants
Open to US citizens, green card holders and FMGs on J-1 visas with prior US training; J-1 holders with no prior US medical training are not candidates.
Visa support
J-1 only; H-1B holders not supported
Licence requirement
Must meet LSBME requirements for a fellowship training permit
