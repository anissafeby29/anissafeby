Inflammatory Bowel Disease Fellowship

University of Calgary (Cumming School of Medicine)

Overview

A 12-month funded programme developing clinical and research expertise in IBD through 4-5 dedicated IBD clinics a week, IBD endoscopy and a major research project.

The programme

Training type

Duration
12 months (at least 3-4 months dedicated research)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of an accredited gastroenterology training programme with demonstrated interest in IBD.
International applicants
Applications considered from applicants of any nationality provided they are eligible for licensure as trainees in Canada.
