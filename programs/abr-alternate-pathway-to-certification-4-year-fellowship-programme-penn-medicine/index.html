Overview

A four-year programme for international medical graduates completing year-long fellowships across Penn divisions to fulfil ABR Alternate Pathway requirements for ABR certification eligibility.

The programme

Focus
Diagnostic and interventional radiology (subspecialty rotations)
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
4 years (year-long fellowships; annual contract renewal)
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Limited
Accreditation
Operates with the American Board of Radiology Alternate Pathway
Department
Department of Radiology, Hospital of the University of Pennsylvania

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
International medical graduates seeking ABR certification via the Alternate Pathway; must meet ABR and Pennsylvania licensing requirements.
International applicants
Designed for international medical graduates; department provides sponsorship to IMGs fulfilling ABR eligibility requirements.
Visa support
Visa sponsorship supported per institutional policy, determined case by case; applicants must have legal ability to work in the US or independently meet requirements.
Licence requirement
Must meet Pennsylvania licensing requirements (fellows licensed at PGY-6 or above).

How to apply

Deadline
Open year-round, reviewed quarterly; applicants encouraged to apply before 1 October of the year preceding desired enrolment
Application method
Application to the department; virtual interviews with participating divisions.
