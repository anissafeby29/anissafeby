Pediatric Critical Care Medicine Fellowship

OHSU Doernbecher Children's Hospital (Oregon Health & Science University)

PaediatricsCritical careClinical Fellowship
Destination

Overview

A small three-year PCCM fellowship (one fellow per year) in a 20-bed PICU serving Oregon and neighbouring states, with ECMO, trauma and transport experience.
