Overview

One-year clinically focused palliative medicine fellowship administered by the Department of Family Medicine, preparing clinician-educators for leadership in palliative care.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed an ACGME, AOA, ACGME-International Advanced Specialty, RCPSC or CFPC accredited residency; board-certified/eligible in a co-sponsoring primary specialty
International applicants
Graduates of ACGME-I accredited residencies are eligible on paper, but trainees must have legal status to train
Visa support
Generally no — programme does not accept applicants on visa status other than Canadian citizens; Trainees must be US citizens, lawful permanent residents, refugees, asylees, or hold documentation allowing them to legally train at CU School of Medicine
