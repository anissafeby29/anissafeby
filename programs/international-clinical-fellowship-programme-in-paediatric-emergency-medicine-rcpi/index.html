Overview

An RCPI-certified international fellowship of up to 3 years in paediatric emergency medicine for government-sponsored overseas doctors.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Primary medical degree in English; internship; ≥1 year post-residency experience; completed national residency (e.g. Saudi or Arab Board); IELTS 7.0 (6.5 each) or OET B
International applicants
Yes – exclusively for overseas doctors sponsored by their government (self-sponsored candidates not accepted); citizens of eligible countries only; must return home after training
Visa support
RCPI assists with visa application and Medical Council registration
Licence requirement
Eligible for Irish Medical Council registration (Supervised Division)
