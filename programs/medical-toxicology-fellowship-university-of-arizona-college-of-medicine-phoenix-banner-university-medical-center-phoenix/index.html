Overview

A 24-month ACGME-accredited medical toxicology fellowship established in 1983, based at Banner – University Medical Center Phoenix and Phoenix Children's Hospital with a high-volume bedside toxicology service.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration

Start
1 July
Positions
2-3 per year (alternating between two and three positions)
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited Medical Toxicology programme (ACGME ID 1180321001; listed in AAMC ERAS 2027 participating programmes, Medical Toxicology (Emergency Medicine))
Department
Department of Emergency Medicine

Eligibility and visa
