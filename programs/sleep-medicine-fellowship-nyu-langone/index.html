Overview

Prepares fellows for academic or clinical sleep medicine careers with training at Bellevue, VA NY Harbor, NYU Langone ambulatory clinics and Tisch Hospital.

Eligibility

International applicants
Institution policyInternational graduates may train on an ECFMG-sponsored J-1 visa only (no H-1B); non-accredited fellowships require a NY licence or limited permit
Visa support
NYU Langone does not offer H-1B sponsorship for house staff, residents or fellows; J-1 sponsorship available through ECFMG.
Licence requirement
Institution policyFor NYU-sponsored ACGME/AOA programmes no NY licence is required by state law (resident exemption, Educ. Law 6526(1)), though individual programmes may require a NY licence or limited permit; trainees in non-accredited programmes MUST hold a NY licence or limited permit before starting; USMLE Step 3 required before final year in ACGME programmes
