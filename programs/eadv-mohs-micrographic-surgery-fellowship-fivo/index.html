EADV Mohs Micrographic Surgery Fellowship

Overview

Six-month Mohs and dermatologic oncology fellowship at a monographic skin cancer unit performing about 600 Mohs procedures a year.

The programme

Focus
Mohs micrographic surgery / dermatologic surgery
Training type
Clinical Fellowship (society-funded)
Duration
6 months (one consecutive period)
Start
During the year after application; start date agreed with awardee
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
EADV Mohs Fellowship; logbook counts towards ESMS (European Society for Micrographic Surgery) certification (minimum 100 supervised cases)
Department
Department of Dermatology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
EADV member; completed dermatology residency and board-certified in a European country; sufficient surgical skills
International applicants
Restricted to EADV members who are board-certified dermatologists in a European country (residents eligible if training ends by December of the application year); medical degree and dermatology certification must be from a listed European country
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Membership of the local Colegio Oficial de Médicos required: homologation of the medical degree if not Spanish and B2 Spanish certificate for non-native speakers; fellow employed by the hospital

How to apply

Deadline
27 February 2026 (2026 call closed; fellowship starts the year after application)
Application method
Online application via MyEADV (application form, CV, medical diploma, recommendation letter from head of department); reviewed by EADV Project Proposal and Review Committee

Funding

Funding
Up to €18,000 for travel and accommodation
