Overview

The combined MGH–Brigham ACGME fellowship training across BWH, MGH, Boston Children's and West Roxbury VA with two clinical years, a scholarly third year and optional fourth research year.

The programme

Focus
General cardiovascular disease
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years (optional 4th research year; 4-year short-track science pathway)
Start
1 July (no off-cycle applications)
Positions
16 per year
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited (combined BWH–MGH programme; accelerated 2+2 EP and ACHD pathways available)
Department
Mass General Brigham Heart and Vascular Institute

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Three years of ACGME-accredited internal medicine residency (2 years for short-track science pathway); CV, 3 letters incl. PD, personal statement, USMLE report, ECFMG certificate if IMG
International applicants
Yes – applications reviewed without US clinical training, but preference given to applicants with US clinical experience
Visa support
Sponsors ECFMG J-1 visas or continuity of H-1B visas
Licence requirement
Not stated on official page
