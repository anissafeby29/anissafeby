Overview

Fee-paying international fellowship in SNUH Radiology across abdominal, chest, cardiovascular, breast and neuro imaging, with supervised preliminary reporting, conferences and research participation.

The programme

Focus
Diagnostic and interventional radiology (subspecialty fellowships)
Training type
International Fellowship (observership / research)
Duration
1 week to 1 year (department recommends 3–6 months; ≥6 months for animal research); SNUH programme: 1 month to 1 year
Start
All year round
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
SNUH certificate of completion
Department
Department of Radiology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Medical doctors; subspecialty tracks in abdominal/GU, chest, cardiovascular, breast, neuro/head-and-neck imaging and research
International applicants
Yes — programme is for foreign doctors; fellows attend reading sessions and write preliminary reports, but real clinical patient care is limited because of medical licence restrictions
Visa support
Visa assistance provided by SNUH (plus gown, ID card and completion certificate)
Licence requirement
Home medical licence (English version) required; no Korean licence, hence no direct patient care

How to apply

Deadline
Applications all year round; submit at least 3 months before training begins
Application method
Submit application via SNUH Global; reviewed and approved by department; documents incl. health certificate, medical-information pledge, photo, English medical licence, passport, employment certificate and diploma (latter two not needed for <90 days); travel insurance once confirmed

Funding

Funding
Self-funded — education fee 6,000 USD/month per person; no financial support from SNUH
