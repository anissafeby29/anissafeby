Overview

A 12-month senior clinical fellow post in breast medical oncology at UCLH working with consultant oncologists alongside specialist trainees, with an active trials portfolio and teaching programme.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Medical degree; MRCP or equivalent; full GMC registration; completed core medical training or equivalent; ST-level medical oncology experience (ST3-ST6 equivalent)
International applicants
Non-UK trained doctors must be on the GMC register by appointment having completed PLAB 1 and 2 (advert)
Visa support
Applicants requiring Skilled Worker sponsorship are welcome and considered alongside all other applications (NHS Jobs advert)
Licence requirement
Full GMC registration with licence to practise
