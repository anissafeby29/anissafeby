Overview

A clinical epilepsy fellowship at Toronto Western with a first year in epilepsy clinics and EEG and an optional second year focused on epilepsy surgery and scalp/intracranial EMU monitoring.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1–2 years (second year subject to funding)
Start
July 1
Positions
1–2 per year depending on funding
Accreditation
University of Toronto Post MD Education-registered clinical fellowship (not RCPSC-accredited)
Department
Division of Neurology, University of Toronto / Krembil Brain Institute, UHN
