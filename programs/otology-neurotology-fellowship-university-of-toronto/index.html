Overview

The largest otology/neurotology programme in Canada at Sunnybrook, providing advanced training in otology, neurotology and skull base surgery including about 180 cochlear implants.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (possible extension)
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
1-2 per year
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Department of Otolaryngology – Head & Neck Surgery (Sunnybrook Health Sciences Centre)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Institution policymust be eligible for a CPSO postgraduate education certificate, including recognition as a medical specialist/family physician in the jurisdiction where the applicant practised before the fellowship
International applicants
Foreign medical graduates accepted
Visa support
Institution policyinternational clinical fellows need an IRCC work permit; the PGME Office issues an LMIA-exempt offer of employment number and supports the work permit application
Licence requirement
Educational licence from the College of Physicians and Surgeons of Ontario (CPSO); candidate must be recognised as a medical specialist in the jurisdiction of prior practice

How to apply

Deadline
December of the preceding year; offers made 18 months in advance
Application method
CV and three letters of reference

Funding

Funding
Canadian fellows: guaranteed base salary; international fellows: department stipend for year one (research), otherwise own institutional/grant support
