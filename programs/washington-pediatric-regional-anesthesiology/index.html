Overview

Training at Seattle Children's combines advanced pediatric regional anesthesia with an Acting Instructor appointment and supervised academic development.

Training type

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed an ACGME pediatric-anesthesia fellowship or equivalent training; international medical graduates must pass all three USMLE steps.
International applicants
International medical graduates accepted if all three USMLE steps passed; ECFMG and citizenship-status documentation required where applicable
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 sponsored by ECFMG (ACGME programmes and non-ACGME programmes approved as ACGME non-standard training); H-1B only with prior GME Office (and ISO) approval before ranking/offer, for limited eligibility categories and requires USMLE Step 3; F-1 OPT accepted; no O-1 or permanent-residence sponsorship
Licence requirement
Current medical licence required with application

How to apply

Funding

Funding
