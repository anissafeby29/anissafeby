Overview

AO-funded short-term spine surgery fellowship placement at Sint Maartenskliniek (Ubbergen (near Nijmegen)), a department performing about 770 surgical cases a year.

The programme

Training type

Placement year after selection

Not stated on official page

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Spine physician or orthopaedic, neuro- or trauma surgeon with strong interest in spine surgery; at least one prior AO course/event; at least two years' AO membership; no AO Spine fellowship in past two years (except self-funded); up to 45 years old
International applicants
Yes – AO global programme; fellows from any country are placed by AO regional fellowship coordinators (membership and course prerequisites apply)
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Scrub-in: Yes; additional licence/requirement to scrub in: No special/additional requirements. Dominant OR language: Dutch (team English level (5)/5). Documents required: The fellow needs to hold a medical degree in orthopedic surgery or neurosurgery. Letter stating his/her interest in spine surgery as well as letters of recommendation (2). The fellow needs to be fully vaccinated against Hepatitis and have an MRSA cle
