Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed ACGME-accredited orthopaedic residency or AOA-accredited osteopathic surgery residency
International applicants
Foreign graduates are non-qualified unless they completed an ACGME- or AOA-accredited US residency
Visa support
Institution policy (UTHSC GME Policy #140, rev. 7/2025): non-US-permanent-resident IMGs in ACGME programmes must train on an ECFMG J-1 visa; J-1 holders are not permitted in non-ACGME or non-standard training programmes; H-1B only with Department Chair and Associate Dean for GME approval (department pays fees)
Licence requirement
Eligible for DEA certificate and medical licences in Tennessee and Mississippi
