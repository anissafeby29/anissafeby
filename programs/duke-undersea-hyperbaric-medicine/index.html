Overview

A clinical fellowship in hyperbaric oxygen treatment, diving medicine and the care of emergency and non-emergency hyperbaric patients.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
Institution policyDuke GME appointments run 1 July - 30 June, with a few exceptions
Positions
Up to 4 annually, depending on funding
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Center for Hyperbaric Medicine and Environmental Physiology, Department of Anesthesiology, Duke University School of Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed an ACGME-certified residency and board eligible in own specialty; eligible for North Carolina trainee licensure
International applicants
Institution policyYes - graduates of medical schools outside the US/Canada are eligible if they hold a valid ECFMG certificate or a full unrestricted US licence
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 alien physician status sponsored by ECFMG with Duke's assistance; Duke is H-1B cap-exempt and H-1B is possible for clinical trainees who have passed USMLE Steps 1-3 and hold an ECFMG certificate (individual programmes decide which visas they accept)
Licence requirement
Eligibility for North Carolina trainee medical licensure
