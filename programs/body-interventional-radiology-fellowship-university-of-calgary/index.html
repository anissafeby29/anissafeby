Overview

A one-year comprehensive interventional radiology fellowship at Foothills Medical Centre offering a wide cross-section of vascular and non-vascular procedures, with only one fellow per year.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Not stated on official page
International applicants
Welcomes domestic and international fellows; sponsored fellows must apply via sponsoring agencies with an active PGME educational contract; English proficiency evidence (IELTS Academic 7.0 in each band or OET Medicine grade B) required where medical school was not primarily in English.
Visa support
After acceptance, the PGME office provides instructions for applying for a work permit.
Licence requirement
Educational licensure with the College of Physicians and Surgeons of Alberta (CPSA), arranged through PGME after acceptance.

How to apply

Deadline
Rolling offers for 1 July starts: Canadian graduates should apply at least 10–12 months before 1 July; international medical graduates at least 18–24 months before 1 July.
Application method
Online application form with CV, personal letter and three reference letters emailed by referees to radiology.fellowship@ucalgary.ca; sponsored trainees apply via their sponsoring agency through the PGME office.

Funding

Funding
Programme receives ongoing funding support from EFW Radiology; sponsored positions accepted via PGME.
