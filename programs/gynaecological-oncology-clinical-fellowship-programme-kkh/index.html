Overview

Equips trainees with the knowledge and skills to function as a gynaecologic oncologist at a centre seeing over 450 new gynaecological cancers a year.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
At least 2 years after MRCOG, MRANZCOG, M.Med (O&G) or equivalent, preferably after 1 year in a gynaecological oncology unit; surgical dexterity for advanced pelvic surgery; conversant in English; accreditation approved by MOH, SMC and KKH
International applicants
Yes - fellows must sign agreement to return to their home country after completing the programme

Not stated on official page
