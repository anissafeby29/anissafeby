Overview

Hands-on interventional radiology clinical fellowship in UMMC's Department of Biomedical Imaging, offered through the UMMC Fellowship/Training Attachment Programme.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship (hands-on)
Duration
1–3 years
Start
Institution policyNo fixed intake; training start date is arranged per applicant (applications due at least 6 months before the start date for non-Malaysians in fellowship/subspecialty training, 2 months for clinical attachments)
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
UMMC fellowship training (hands-on), approved by UMMC C&P Committee
Department
Department of Biomedical Imaging

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Master's degree / clinical master's or equivalent recognised by the Malaysian Medical Council (Second Schedule, Medical Act 1971); MMC registration; Professional Visit Pass (PLIK) approval; support of Head of Department; approval by UMMC Credentialing and Privileging Committee
International applicants
Yes — non-Malaysian applicants accepted; apply at least 6 months before start (fellowship/hands-on) and obtain PLIK visa
Visa support
Professional Visit Pass (PLIK) approval by the Immigration Department of Malaysia required (applicant obtains)
Licence requirement
Registration with the Malaysian Medical Council required

How to apply

Deadline
Institution policyRolling; non-Malaysians must apply at least 6 months before the training start date (Malaysians at least 2 months)
Application method
Register and apply online on the UMMC Fellowship/Training Attachment Programme e-services portal; non-Malaysians ≥6 months before start; accept offer within 1 month

Funding

Funding
Fee-paying (see UMMC Fees Schedule on portal)
