Overview

Three-year pediatric ID fellowship jointly run by St. Jude and UTHSC at Le Bonheur, with at least 54 weeks of clinical rotations and national leadership in immunocompromised and HIV care.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Passed USMLE Steps 1-3, COMLEX 1-3 or MCCQE I-II; completed pediatrics or med-peds residency accredited by ACGME, ACGME-I or RCPSC (exceptionally qualified applicant exception per ACGME); IMGs need ECFMG certification and J-1 eligibility
International applicants
Yes - IMGs with ECFMG certification who are J-1 eligible
Visa support
International medical graduates must hold ECFMG certification and be eligible for a J-1 visa
