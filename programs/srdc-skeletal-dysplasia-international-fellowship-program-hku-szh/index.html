SRDC-Skeletal Dysplasia International Fellowship Program

The University of Hong Kong-Shenzhen Hospital / HKU Department of Orthopaedics and Traumatology (Skeletal Dysplasia Research and Diagnosis Centre)

Destination

Open official programme page ↗Compare saved →

Overview

International fellowship in the diagnosis and treatment of skeletal dysplasias across HKU-linked training centres.

The programme

Focus
Paediatric orthopaedics / skeletal dysplasia
Training type
Clinical Fellowship (international)
Duration
Full fellows 3-6 months; Hong Kong fellows 3 months; visiting fellows 1-2 weeks
Start
Phase I 1 July-31 Dec 2025; Phase II 1 Jan-30 Jun 2026
Positions
5 full fellows, 2 Hong Kong fellows, 3 visiting fellows
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Paediatric Orthopaedics

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Qualified medical practitioner who has completed specialist orthopaedic training; priority for patient-organisation recommendations, 2+ years' experience (10+ cases) and age under 45; visiting fellows: senior paediatric orthopaedic clinicians with 5+ years' experience
International applicants
Yes - international programme; travel subsidy (max RMB 5,000) for developing-country applicants; Hong Kong doctors cannot apply for the regular fellowship
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Medical Council of Hong Kong registration required only for Hong Kong fellows

How to apply

Deadline
Phase I 31 May 2025; Phase II 30 September 2025 (check for the next cycle)
Application method
SRDC application form, HKU-SZH training form, two recommendation letters, fitness certificate, licence, employment certificate, ID and qualifications - email paediortho@hku-szh.org

Funding

Funding
RMB 10,000/month (mainland) or RMB 12,000/month (Hong Kong); accommodation self-arranged
