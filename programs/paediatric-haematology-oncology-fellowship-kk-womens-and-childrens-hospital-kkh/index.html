Overview

Comprehensive course in paediatric haematology/oncology at a centre seeing about 100 new childhood cancer cases a year, with exposure to BMT and cellular therapies.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
6 to 12 months
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
1-2 fellows per year
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Paediatric Haematology/Oncology Service

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Qualified paediatricians with postgraduate paediatric training; minimum 36 months paediatrics post-housemanship; must fulfil SMC criteria for temporary registration; prior haematology/oncology experience preferred
International applicants
Yes - KKH clinical fellowships are offered to foreign medical doctors; clinical fellows need a supporting letter from their government, regional health authority or home institution stating their position is reserved until return
Visa support
Valid Training Employment Pass issued by the Ministry of Manpower required; processing time at least 12 months
Licence requirement
Temporary Registration with the Singapore Medical Council (SMC) required (SMC fees S$300 application + S$550 practising certificate)

How to apply

Deadline
Institution policyno fixed deadline - apply by email to Education.Office@KKH.com.sg at least 12 months before the intended start (processing time at least 12 months)
Application method
Email Education.Office@KKH.com.sg; KKH application fee S$620 + GST for successful applicants

Funding

Funding
Institution policyself-funded - KKH application fee, training fee, SMC temporary registration, Training Employment Pass, malpractice insurance and all personal expenses are borne by the applicant (home-country support letter required)
