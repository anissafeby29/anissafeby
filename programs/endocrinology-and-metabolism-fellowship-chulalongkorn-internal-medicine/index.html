Overview

Chulalongkorn subspecialty residency in endocrinology and metabolism selecting four internists for academic year 2027 through a transparent document and interview process.

Academic year 2027

4 selected plus 2 reserves (AY2027)

Division of Endocrinology and Metabolism, Department of Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
MD (or equivalent recognised by the Medical Council of Thailand) with Thai medical licence, plus Thai Diploma/Board in Internal Medicine, or final-year internal medicine resident, or eligible to sit the internal medicine board that year; application form, licence, 3 reference letters; interview
