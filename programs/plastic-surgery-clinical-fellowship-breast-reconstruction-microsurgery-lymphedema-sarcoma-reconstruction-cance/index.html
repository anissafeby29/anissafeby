Plastic Surgery Clinical Fellowship (Breast Reconstruction, Microsurgery, Lymphedema, Sarcoma Reconstruction)

Cancer Institute Hospital (CIH), Japanese Foundation for Cancer Research

Overview

Clinical training starting as surgical assistant with opportunity to perform surgery in breast reconstruction, (super)microsurgery, lymphedema and sarcoma reconstruction, plus conferences and research.
