Geriatric Oncology Fellowship

University of Toronto (University Health Network / Mount Sinai Hospital)

Destination

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Overview

Trains geriatricians or medical oncologists to provide enhanced care of older adults with cancer, with an optional combined geriatric oncology/palliative care track.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (possible 1-year extension)
Start
1 July (flexible)
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
University of Toronto Department of Medicine clinical fellowship registered with PGME; does not lead to RCPSC certification (UofT PGME page)
Department
Division of General Internal Medicine and Geriatrics with Department of Supportive Care, UHN/MSH

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Subspecialty training in geriatric medicine and/or medical oncology (internal medicine, radiation or surgical oncology also considered); fluent in English; eligible for CPSO educational licence
International applicants
Outstanding qualified applicants encouraged to apply regardless of current country of residence (programme page)
Visa support
PGME Office provides successful sponsored applicants with documentation to apply for a work permit (UofT PGME page); otherwise not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Must be eligible for a CPSO certificate authorizing postgraduate education (educational licence); applicant must be recognized as a specialist in the jurisdiction where they practised (UofT PGME page)
