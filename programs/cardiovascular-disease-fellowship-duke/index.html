Overview

Duke's three-year ACGME general cardiovascular disease fellowship combining clinical rotations, simulation, didactics, procedures and mentored research to train future leaders in cardiovascular health.

The programme

Focus
General cardiovascular disease
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years
Start
Institution policyDuke GME appointments run 1 July - 30 June, with a few exceptions
Positions
10 first-year positions
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited
Department
Division of Cardiology, Department of Medicine, Duke University School of Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of an ACGME-accredited internal medicine residency and ABIM board eligibility in internal medicine (required for ABIM cardiology certification)
International applicants
IMGs accepted; must have completed an ACGME-accredited internal medicine residency
Visa support
J-1 clinical visas sponsored by ECFMG; H-1B and other visa types not sponsored
Licence requirement
Institution policyNorth Carolina full medical licence or Resident Training License (RTL) required before clinical duties

How to apply

Deadline
ERAS deadline (stated on page as July of the application year)
Application method
ERAS application; participates in the NRMP Match

Funding

Funding
Institution policyGME trainees paid on the Duke stipend scale; FY27 (from 1 July 2026) PGY-1 $71,052 rising to PGY-4 $79,908, PGY-5 $82,992, PGY-6 $86,292, PGY-7 $90,036 (up to PGY-12 $106,800), plus benefits
