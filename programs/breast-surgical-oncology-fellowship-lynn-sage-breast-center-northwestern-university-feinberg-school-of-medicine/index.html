Overview

Breast fellowship with eight months of surgical rotations at the Lynn Sage Breast Center and an optional second research year.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed general surgery residency
International applicants
Applicants from outside the US must have passed USMLE (or equivalent), be ECFMG-certified and able to secure a permanent Illinois licence
Visa support
Institution policyMcGaw sponsors J-1 (ECFMG) and supports H-1B only for exceptional candidates approved by the DIO/GMEC before ranking (cost borne by programme)
Licence requirement
Permanent Illinois medical licence, Illinois controlled substance licence and DEA certificate
