Neurosurgical Anesthesiology Fellowship

Northwestern University Feinberg School of Medicine (McGaw Medical Center)

Overview

Described as the world’s first ICPNT-accredited fellowship, providing advanced training in anesthesia for complex intracranial, spinal and endovascular neurosurgical procedures.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (optional second year in clinical outcomes research)

Not stated on official page

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Not stated on official page
International applicants
Because the programme is unaccredited (by ACGME), both American and international medical graduates may apply
Visa support
Institution policyMcGaw sponsors J-1 (ECFMG) and supports H-1B only for exceptional candidates approved by the DIO/GMEC before ranking (cost borne by programme)
Licence requirement
Institution policyIllinois (IDFPR) temporary medical licence required to train (processed by McGaw GME; allow ~60 days)
