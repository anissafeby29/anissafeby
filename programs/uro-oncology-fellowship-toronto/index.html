Overview

An SUO-accredited uro-oncology fellowship aiming to develop the next generation of innovators and thought leaders in genitourinary cancer.

The programme

Focus
Urologic oncology
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
Not stated on official page
Start
1 August
Positions
3 per year
Accreditation
Society of Urologic Oncology (SUO)
Department
Division of Urology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Institution policymust be eligible for a CPSO postgraduate education certificate, including recognition as a medical specialist/family physician in the jurisdiction where the applicant practised before the fellowship
International applicants
TOEFL (93, speaking 24) or IELTS academic (6.5, no band below 6) required if training was not in English
Visa support
Institution policyinternational clinical fellows need an IRCC work permit; the PGME Office issues an LMIA-exempt offer of employment number and supports the work permit application
Licence requirement
Must be licensed by the College of Physicians and Surgeons of Ontario (CPSO) with CMPA coverage

How to apply

Deadline
28 February, 17 months before the 1 August start
Application method
SUO match plus application form and CV emailed to the programme office; in-person or Zoom interview

Funding

Funding
Institution policyclinical fellows must be funded (department or sponsoring agency); FEAC recommends pay at least equal to PGY1 on the PARO salary scale, and foreign nationals must be paid at a rate commensurate with a Canadian in the same role
