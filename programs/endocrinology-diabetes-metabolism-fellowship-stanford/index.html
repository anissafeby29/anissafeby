Overview

Advanced clinical and research training in endocrinology, diabetes and metabolism across Stanford Health Care, VA Palo Alto and Santa Clara Valley Medical Center.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
2-3 years (clinical and research tracks)
Start
July 2027 (2026 interview cycle)
Positions
3 for July 2027 entry: 1 Research Track, 2 Clinical Track
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Division of Endocrinology, Stanford Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed internal medicine residency; ERAS, NRMP and Stanford GME requirements apply
International applicants
J-1 visa holders eligible for the Clinical Track only; Research Track limited to US citizens and permanent residents (NIH funding restrictions)
Visa support
J-1 (ECFMG-sponsored) visa; Clinical Track only
Licence requirement
Medical Board of California licensure rules apply (36 months ACGME/RCPSC/CFPC postgraduate training incl. 24 consecutive months in one programme)

How to apply

Deadline
ERAS applications reviewed from July/August 2026; virtual interviews September-October 2026; NRMP Match
Application method
ERAS and NRMP Match (ECFMG status report required for foreign graduates)

Funding

Funding
Paid - Stanford Health Care GME salary by PGY level (2026-27 scale: PGY-4 $113,276.80; PGY-5 $119,558.40; PGY-6 $124,280.00; base includes housing component) plus allowances
