Overview

A well-established one-year ACGME pediatric dermatology clinical fellowship (13,000 patient visits a year) combining 60% outpatient clinic, 20% inpatient consultation and 20% research, with extensive laser and procedural training.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of dermatology residency; application by email with all required documents
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs welcome to apply; must hold a valid ECFMG certificate and be able to obtain Medical Board of California licensure; non-ACGME fellowships need ACGME Non-Standard Training recognition before J-1 sponsorship
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 (ECFMG) is the primary visa; H-1B permitted only in some circumstances with GME approval; O-1 rarely, varies by department
Licence requirement
California medical license required within 180 days of starting; DEA registration also required (fees reimbursed by Stanford GME)

How to apply
