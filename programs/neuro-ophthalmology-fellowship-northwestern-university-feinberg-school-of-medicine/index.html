Overview

One funded AUPO-compliant fellowship position each year for an ophthalmology or neurology graduate of an ACGME-accredited residency.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
ACGME-accredited residency in ophthalmology or neurology
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs eligible with a valid ECFMG certificate, valid visa (J-1, H-1B or O-1) or permanent residence, and Illinois IDFPR licence
Visa support
Institution policyMcGaw sponsors J-1 (ECFMG) and supports H-1B only for exceptional candidates approved by the DIO/GMEC before ranking (cost borne by programme)
Licence requirement
Institution policyIllinois (IDFPR) temporary medical licence required to train (processed by McGaw GME; allow ~60 days)
