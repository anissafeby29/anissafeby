Overview

One-year clinical vascular neurology fellowship with the UF Health Comprehensive Stroke Program, offering three ACGME positions annually and customisable electives.

Eligibility and visa

International applicants
Considered; department sponsors J-1 visas for eligible applicants (no longer able to sponsor H-1B)
