Overview

ACGME-accredited nonoperative sports medicine fellowship for PM&R graduates with rotations in sports, musculoskeletal and regenerative medicine clinics and team coverage.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed an ACGME-accredited PM&R residency; USMLE or COMLEX all three steps passed
International applicants
Mayo GME general admissions: graduates of medical schools outside the US/Canada must hold ECFMG certification prior to appointment (or a full unrestricted licence in the programme's state)
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
USMLE/COMLEX Steps 1-3 completed per Minnesota guidelines

How to apply

Deadline
15 August of the year preceding the start date; interviews September-November
Application method
ERAS fellowship application plus NRMP Primary Care Sports Medicine Match
