Overview

One-month CIRSE-funded IR training stay at Fondazione Policlinico Universitario Agostino Gemelli IRCCS (Rome); expertise: Vascular IR, interventional oncology; training supervisor Alessandro Posa.

Eligibility and visa

How to apply

Deadline
2026 cycle closed; call for the 2027 funding cycle at the end of 2026
Application method
Request centre contact details from CIRSE (up to three centres), make preliminary arrangements, then apply via CIRSE online portal with motivation letter, head-of-department recommendation, host confirmation letter, budget and signed form

Funding

Funding
€3,000 CIRSE grant for travel, housing and indirect costs
