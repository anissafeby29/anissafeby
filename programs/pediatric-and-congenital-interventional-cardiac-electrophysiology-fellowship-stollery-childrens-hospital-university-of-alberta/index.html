Pediatric and Congenital Interventional Cardiac Electrophysiology Fellowship

Stollery Children's Hospital / University of Alberta

PaediatricsCardiologyClinical Fellowship
Destination
CAN

Overview

One-year fellowship in advanced clinical and interventional electrophysiology at a Western Canadian referral site performing 80-100 EP and 60-80 device procedures a year.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Physicians with completed training in pediatric cardiology
International applicants
Department of Pediatrics fellowship page: sponsored international trainees must apply via their sponsoring agency to PGME (pgspon@ualberta.ca); applications sent directly by trainees are not accepted for the sponsored route
Visa support
Institution policyInternational fellows require a Canadian work permit before starting; PGME issues the offer/FVCA number, documents due to PGME at least 6 months before start
Licence requirement
Institution policyCollege of Physicians & Surgeons of Alberta (CPSA) registration required before clinical fellowship begins, incl. CPSA English language proficiency where applicable

Funding
Institution policyFellowships without salary/funding not permitted; from 1 Jan 2026 salary must be at least the PARA R1 level; PGME provides no salary
