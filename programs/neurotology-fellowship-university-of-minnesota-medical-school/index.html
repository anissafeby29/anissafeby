Overview

ACGME neurotology and otology fellowship with up to one research day a week, involving University of Minnesota and HealthPartners neurotologists.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
2 years
Start
1 July
Positions
One fellow every other year
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited
Department
Department of Otolaryngology–Head and Neck Surgery

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of an ACGME-accredited otolaryngology–head and neck surgery residency
International applicants
ACGME neurotology fellowship: requires completion of an ACGME-accredited (or RCPSC) otolaryngology–HNS residency; not open to non-US/Canada-trained otolaryngologists
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Residency permit or medical licence from the Minnesota Board of Medical Practice

How to apply

Deadline
30 June
Application method
SF Match (Neurotology Match) – CV, photograph and letters of recommendation via sfmatch.org

Funding

Funding
Base stipend 2019–20: Level 1 $66,194; Level 2 $68,311
