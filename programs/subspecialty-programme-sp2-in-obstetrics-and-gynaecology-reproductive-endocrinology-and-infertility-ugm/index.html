Overview

UGM's O&G subspecialist programme trains obstetrician-gynaecologists in reproductive endocrinology and infertility, one of four tracks (FER, fetomaternal, social O&G, gynaecologic oncology) with a departmental focus on fertility preservation.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Indonesian specialist (Sp1) in obstetrics and gynaecology (Sp.OG); specialist transcript GPA >= 3.00 (programme accredited A/B); English test AcEPT >= 209 or TOEP PLTI >= 45/450; aptitude test PAPs UGM or TPDA PLTI >= 500; recommendation letter or competence certificate from the relevant collegium; valid STR; certificate of completion of the specialist deployment programme (formerly WKDS) or at least 1 year of clinical service; 2 confidential online references. Police clearance (SKCK) and statement of no ongoing legal case; must pass the O&G Collegium selection exam and present the Collegium recommendation before university selection. Maximum age 45 at start.
