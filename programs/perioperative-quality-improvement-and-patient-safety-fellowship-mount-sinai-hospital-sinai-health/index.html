Overview

A one-year fellowship providing anaesthesiologists with the knowledge and skills to plan, execute and lead perioperative quality improvement and patient safety initiatives, aligned with a year-long QI methodology curriculum.

Department
Department of Anesthesia and Pain Management, Mount Sinai Hospital / University of Toronto

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Minimum 30 months of clinical anaesthesia training and all requisite anaesthesia certification exams passed in home country (University of Toronto departmental requirements); preference for applicants within 7 years of training completion and with at least a year of independent practice or prior fellowship
International applicants
Yes — University of Toronto fellowships accept international graduates; English proficiency required where training was not in English
Visa support
Institution policyForeign nationals need an LMIA-exempt work permit; U of T PGME issues the IRCC offer-of-employment number and CPSO letter of eligibility about 5 months before start
Licence requirement
Eligibility for registration with University of Toronto PGME and a postgraduate education certificate of registration from the College of Physicians and Surgeons of Ontario
