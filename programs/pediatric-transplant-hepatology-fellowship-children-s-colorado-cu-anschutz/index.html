Overview

A one-year fourth-year fellowship training future leaders in pediatric transplant hepatology at Children's Hospital Colorado.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (fourth year)
Start
Institution policyCU School of Medicine GME training agreements run 1 July - 30 June (sample 2025-26 agreement: 7/1/2025 - 6/30/2026)
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited (one-year)
Department
Department of Pediatrics, University of Colorado School of Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Board eligible/certified in general pediatrics and completed an ACGME-accredited 3-year pediatric gastroenterology fellowship
International applicants
Institution policyGraduates of non-US/Canadian schools eligible with valid ECFMG certificate (or full Colorado licence); must hold or obtain legal work authorization; USMLE Step 3 required before starting a fellowship
Visa support
Institution policyClinical J-1 via ECFMG is the standard route; H-1B for residents/fellows only if CU GME agrees to waive its J-1 requirement
Licence requirement
Institution policyColorado Medical Board physician training licence (or full Colorado licence) required
