Overview

An integrated physician-scientist pathway offering substantial protected research during anaesthesiology residency followed by one or two years of research fellowship training.

The programme

Training type
Combined Fellowship
Duration
Up to 9 months research during CA1-3 residency years plus at least one additional research fellowship year
Start
With residency intake
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Not a stand-alone subspecialty accreditation
Department
Department of Anesthesiology, Perioperative and Pain Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Applicants pursuing Stanford anaesthesiology residency and a physician-scientist career
International applicants
Limited
Visa support
Stanford GME permits J-1 (ECFMG; most common) and H-1B (varies by department); IMGs must hold an approved visa and California licensure; check with programme which visas it permits
Licence requirement
Stanford residency and California training requirements apply

How to apply

Deadline
1 October of the application year (email CV and one-page research statement to the FARM directors)
Application method
Apply to Stanford anesthesiology residency and email CV plus one-page research/career statement to FARM directors; FARM interviews added to residency interview; no separate FARM match spot

Funding

Funding
Resident salary plus an additional $15,000 department research fund annually; research year often on departmental T32 grants; NIH Loan Repayment eligible
