Overview

ACGME fellowship with nine paediatric otolaryngology faculty and about 6,000 procedures a year including laryngotracheal reconstruction, vascular anomaly and cochlear implant surgery.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed an ACGME- or RCPSC-accredited otolaryngology–HNS residency; ABOto board eligible/certified
International applicants
ACGME pediatric otolaryngology fellowship: residency must be ACGME-, AOA-, ACGME-I (Advanced Specialty) or RCPSC-accredited (ASPO application guide); other international graduates not eligible
