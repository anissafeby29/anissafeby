Overview

Tailored visiting programme across Siriraj rehabilitation OPD/IPD, electrodiagnosis and subspecialty clinics (neuro, cardiac, paediatric, SCI, spasticity, swallowing, prosthetics) and interventions such as FEES and chemoneurolysis.
