Overview

Three-month subspecialty training at internationally recognised centres in cataract, cornea, glaucoma, retina, neuro, oncology, oculoplastics, paediatrics, uveitis and more (no refractive or aesthetic surgery).

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
From a low-resource or underserved country and permanently living/working there; under 40; completed residency and specialist ophthalmology degree; support from previous and present chair; commitment to return
International applicants
Yes - specifically for ophthalmologists from low-resource or underserved countries; female ophthalmologists strongly encouraged
