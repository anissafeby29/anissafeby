Overview

A one-year UCLA-VA geriatric medicine fellowship with training across UCLA Health, the Greater Los Angeles VA and Harbor-UCLA, plus community nursing homes and a PACE centre, with optional inpatient, SNF and home-based pathways.

International applicants
International medical graduates welcome.
