Facial Plastic Surgery Fellowship (Sydney – Marcells / Meller programmes)

Australasian Academy of Facial Plastic Surgery (AAFPS) – Sydney

Overview

Full-time 12-month AAFPS fellowships in reconstructive and cosmetic facial plastic and nasal surgery, requiring a research project, a meeting presentation and a peer-reviewed publication.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Trained in ENT, head and neck or plastic surgery; FRACS or equivalent; registered or eligible to register with the Medical Board of Australia; AAFPS membership
International applicants
Open to overseas surgeons; AAFPS advises allowing up to 6 months for visa processing
Visa support
Applicants must arrange visa (up to 6 months)
Licence requirement
Medical Board of Australia registration (or eligibility)

How to apply

Deadline
30 March (directors select candidates in June); Meller programme full until June 2028
Application method
Application form, personal statement, CV and three reference letters emailed to AAFPS
