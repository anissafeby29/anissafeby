Overview

Three-month ESCP fellowship in functional colorectal disorders at a European host centre such as Meander Medical Center.

The programme

Focus
Colorectal surgery (functional disorders / pelvic floor)
Training type
Clinical Fellowship (society-funded)
Duration
3 months
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
2 three-month fellowships (2025 cycle, across host centres)
Accreditation
European Society of Coloproctology (ESCP)
Department
Not stated on official page

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
ESCP member for at least 12 months; at least 5 years' general surgery training; senior trainee or early-career consultant; manifested interest in coloproctology
International applicants
Aimed at European surgeons training in coloproctology visiting a centre outside their home country in Europe
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Licence to practise colorectal surgery in the host centre required (ESCP provides guidance); host-language ability helpful, fluent English accepted by most hosts

How to apply

Deadline
2025 applications closed (deadline was 2 May)
Application method
Online application to ESCP; possible interview
