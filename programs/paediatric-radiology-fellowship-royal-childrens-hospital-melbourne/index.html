Overview

A full-time fellowship furthering learning in all areas of paediatric diagnostic radiology, with clinical reporting and multidisciplinary team involvement under the guidance of paediatric radiology consultants.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Not stated on official page
International applicants
International Medical Graduate applicants must meet AHPRA English language proficiency requirements.
Visa support
Institution policyAll RCH employees need Australian citizenship or a visa with work rights; where RCH agrees to sponsor, it engages migration lawyers for a Temporary Skill Shortage (subclass 482) employer-sponsored visa (usually 2-4 years, dependants included); other routes include 417/482 working holiday, 189/190 skilled visas
Licence requirement
AHPRA registration (IMGs must meet AHPRA English language standards); National Criminal Record Check and Working with Children Check required.
