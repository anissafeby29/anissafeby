Overview

A comprehensive three-year general paediatric cardiology programme accredited by the Royal College and the American Board of Pediatrics, having trained fellows from over 40 countries.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Licensed paediatrician or eligible for licensing in home jurisdiction; North American and international applicants accepted
International applicants
International applicants accepted
Visa support
Institution policyinternational clinical fellows need an IRCC work permit; the PGME Office issues an LMIA-exempt offer of employment number and supports the work permit application
Licence requirement
Institution policyclinical fellows must hold a CPSO certificate of registration authorising postgraduate education (educational licence); research fellows do not need CPSO registration

How to apply

Deadline
Last posted window: applications 1 September 2025 to 1 January 2026 for the year starting 1 July 2027 (deadlines firm)
Application method
Online Fellowship Application Form with uploaded package (CV, letter of intent, degree and paediatrics certificate with translations) plus three reference letters emailed to cardiology.meded@sickkids.ca

Funding

Funding
Funding available for a limited number of positions each academic year; on-call stipend
