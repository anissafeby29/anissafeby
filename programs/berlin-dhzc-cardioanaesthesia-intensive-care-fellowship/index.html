Overview

A full-time clinical fellowship combining cardiovascular anaesthesia, intensive care and structured echocardiography training.

The programme

Focus
Adult cardiothoracic anaesthesia
Training type
Combined Fellowship
Duration
24 months full-time (intensive care module also available separately as a 1- or 2-year fellowship; purely anaesthesiological 2-year advanced cardioanaesthesiology track offered)
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Training under Berlin Medical Association regulations (clinic director holds 36 months anaesthesiology and 24 months intensive care training authorisation); prepares for EACTAIC/EACVI, DGAI and EDEC echo certification and DGAI cardioanaesthesia criteria
Department
Department of Cardiac Anesthesiology and Intensive Care Medicine (Klinik für Kardioanästhesiologie und Intensivmedizin), DHZC

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Anaesthesia specialist qualification obtained within three years, continuing-education evidence, completed/ongoing doctorate, German C1 and English B2.
International applicants
Not explicitly addressed; applicants need German at C1 and English at B2 level
