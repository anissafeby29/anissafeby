Paediatric Nephrology Fellowship

The Hospital for Sick Children (SickKids), University of Toronto

Overview

A Royal College-accredited subspecialty programme at SickKids offering exposure to the full spectrum of renal disease in children, including dialysis and transplantation, with optional one-year subspecialty programmes.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
2 years (core); one-year subspecialty programmes in transplantation, dialysis & apheresis, nephrogenetics, complement-mediated diseases
Start
1 July
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Royal College of Physicians and Surgeons of Canada
Department
Division of Nephrology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
TOEFL 615 / IELTS 7.0 minimum recommended
International applicants
Institution policyYes - international applicants who meet CPSO clinical-fellow licensure requirements contact the Fellowship Program Director; applicants from countries with training agreements apply via their sponsoring agency to the PGME Office
Visa support
Institution policyinternational clinical fellows need an IRCC work permit; the PGME Office issues an LMIA-exempt offer of employment number and supports the work permit application
Licence requirement
Institution policyclinical fellows must hold a CPSO certificate of registration authorising postgraduate education (educational licence); research fellows do not need CPSO registration

How to apply

Deadline
Application portal opens early November; deadline mid-January of the year preceding the start
Application method
Email nephrology.trainingprogram@sickkids.ca to join distribution list; online portal

Funding

Funding
Some subspecialty programmes funded applicants only; transplant and complement programmes have funding competitions
