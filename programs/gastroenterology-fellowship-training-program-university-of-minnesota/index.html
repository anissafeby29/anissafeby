Overview

A three-year ACGME fellowship with two-month rotations across University of Minnesota-M Health Fairview, Minneapolis VA, Hennepin and Regions hospitals and about six months of research.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed or anticipated completion of an ACGME (U.S.) or ACGME-I internal medicine or combined medicine residency; passed USMLE Steps 1, 2 and 3.
International applicants
U.S. citizens, permanent residents and J-1 visa holders considered; ACGME-I residency graduates eligible.
Visa support
J-1 considered; H-1B not sponsored.
