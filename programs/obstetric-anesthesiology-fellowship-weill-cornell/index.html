Overview

A 12-month ACGME-accredited programme based in the Alexandra Cohen Hospital for Women and Newborns at NewYork-Presbyterian/Weill Cornell, providing advanced clinical, academic and leadership training in obstetric anesthesiology.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of an ACGME-accredited anesthesiology residency; board eligible or certified
International applicants
Institution policyForeign nationals accepted only if they secure ECFMG J-1 status or hold work authorization for the programme duration; IMGs need ECFMG certification
Visa support
Institution policyNYPH generally accepts foreign nationals only on ECFMG-sponsored J-1 (or with own work authorization); no H-1B, E-3 or other sponsorship; O-1 supported only in very limited cases at the trainee's expense; no visas funded by foreign governments
