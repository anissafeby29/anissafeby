Overview

Faculty-wide observational short-term training scholarship that can be taken in the Department of Ophthalmology, also used to assess prospective fellows.

The programme

Training type
Observership (scholarship-funded)
Duration
1-3 months
Start
Per department availability
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Department of Ophthalmology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
MD/MBBS (or health science bachelor's) holders who are citizens of eligible ASEAN CLMV countries or World Bank low/lower-middle-income countries (country list per application year)
International applicants
Yes - restricted to citizens of eligible countries (check annual list)
Visa support
Acceptance and visa letters issued 3-4 weeks after department evaluation
Licence requirement
Not applicable (strictly observational)

How to apply

Deadline
Four rounds: 31 January, 30 April, 31 July, 31 October
Application method
Online application per Siriraj International Relations instructions

Funding

Funding
Monthly stipend THB 15,000
