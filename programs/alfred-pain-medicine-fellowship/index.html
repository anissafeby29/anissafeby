Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Advanced trainee nearing completion of, or fellow in, a relevant specialty (anaesthesia, rehabilitation medicine, surgery, psychiatry, internal medicine, general practice) recognised by the AMC; AHPRA registration; registered FPM trainee; Foundation FPM exam passed before commencement
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs accepted with end-to-end recruitment support (AHPRA registration, college, visa); IMG fellows typically use the competent authority or short-term specialist training pathway
Visa support
Institution policyEmployer-sponsored Training visa (subclass 407) is Alfred Health's preferred visa for IMGs (granted 13 months); Medical Workforce team supports visa nomination, starting at least 6 months before commencement
Licence requirement
Registration as a medical practitioner with AHPRA
