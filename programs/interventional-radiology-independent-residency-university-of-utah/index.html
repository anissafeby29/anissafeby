Overview

A one- or two-year ACGME-accredited independent IR residency following ESIR or a four-year ACGME DR residency, with IR admitting privileges at the University of Utah.

The programme

Training type
Independent Residency
Duration
1 or 2 years
Start
Institution policyUniversity of Utah GME academic year begins 1 July
Positions
2 approved positions
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited
Department
Department of Radiology and Imaging Sciences, University of Utah School of Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Must meet Utah medical licensure requirements and be a graduate of an LCME-accredited US/Canadian school, an AOA-accredited US osteopathic college, or a non-US school with a valid ECFMG certificate before appointment.
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs may apply on the same basis as US/Canadian graduates but need ECFMG certification; non-US citizens/non-permanent residents need an institutionally sponsored visa
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 clinical visa (ECFMG-sponsored) for non-US-citizen trainees providing patient care; H-1 visas not accepted for residencies or fellowships (GME IMG eligibility policy)
Licence requirement
Must meet Utah state medical licensure requirements.

How to apply

Deadline
Preferred application deadline 15 December.
Application method
ERAS only; NRMP match. ERAS form, CV, personal statement, transcript, MSPE, USMLE/COMLEX scores and three letters.
