Allergy and Immunology Fellowship

UC Davis Health / UC Davis Children's Hospital

PaediatricsAllergy & immunologyClinical Fellowship
Destination
USA
Sacramento, California, United States
Open official programme page ↗Compare saved →

Overview

A two-year ACGME-accredited fellowship caring for adult and pediatric patients with allergic and immunologic disorders, with an optional research year.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
2 years (optional funded 3rd research year with MAS in Clinical Research)
Start
Institution policyUC Davis Health GME one-year appointments run 1 July - 30 June (GME sample appointment agreement)
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited
Department
Department of Pediatrics, UC Davis School of Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed residency in internal medicine, pediatrics or med-peds; board eligible/certified per ABP/ACGME; IMGs must meet California PTL/licence requirements and hold a clinical J-1 (ECFMG-sponsored)
International applicants
Institution policyNon-US citizens need ECFMG-sponsored clinical J-1 (USMLE Steps 1-2) or an EAD; Canadian graduates also need clinical J-1
Visa support
Institution policyClinical J-1 (ECFMG) or EAD only; UC Davis Health does not sponsor H-1B visas for residents and fellows
Licence requirement
Not stated on official page

How to apply
