Overview

A one- or two-year programme preparing individuals for academic cranial neurosurgery through high-volume transcranial, endoscopic endonasal, transorbital and radiosurgical treatment of brain and skull base lesions.

Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 (ECFMG) is the primary visa; H-1B permitted only in some circumstances with GME approval; O-1 rarely, varies by department
Licence requirement
Institution policyMedical Board of California Postgraduate Training License required within 180 days of starting an ACGME programme (USMLE Steps 1 & 2 CK passed); non-ACGME programmes require a full California licence by the start date
