Overview

A training programme designed to produce academic urologic oncologists capable of establishing comprehensive urologic oncology programmes at other teaching institutions.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Institution policyMD/DO or foreign equivalent with a valid ECFMG certificate (for international graduates); Texas Medical Board requirements; tobacco-free pre-employment screen
International applicants
Institution policyInternational physician trainees accepted; those needing sponsorship must use the ECFMG J-1 visa
Visa support
Institution policyThe ECFMG-sponsored J-1 is the required visa for international physician trainees who need visa sponsorship at MD Anderson; visa-sponsored trainees may not moonlight
Licence requirement
Institution policyA Texas Medical Board Physician-in-Training (PIT) permit (obtained by the GME office for trainees without a full Texas licence) or a full Texas licence is required before starting
