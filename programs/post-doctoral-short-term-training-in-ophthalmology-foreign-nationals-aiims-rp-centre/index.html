Overview

AIIMS short-term (up to 6 months) non-degree training arranged with the Dr R P Centre for Ophthalmic Sciences for sponsored ophthalmologists, including foreign nationals.

The programme

Training type
Short-term training / observership
Duration
Up to 6 months (period agreed with the department)
Start
Mutually agreed with the department
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
No degree/diploma awarded
Department
Dr. Rajendra Prasad Centre for Ophthalmic Sciences

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Individuals sponsored by their institutions/hospitals/universities, private practitioners or postgraduate students; training does not lead to a degree/diploma
International applicants
Yes - AIIMS short-term training guidelines set fees for foreign nationals; RP Centre states ophthalmologists from India and abroad have trained under its post-doctoral short and long term trainee programme
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Not stated on official page

How to apply

Deadline
Rolling
Application method
Apply to AIIMS Academic Section with bio-data (4 copies), attested degree copies and prescribed form, routed through employer for in-service applicants; specify discipline and dates

Funding

Funding
Self-funded: fee in INR equivalent of USD 200 for the first month and USD 100 per subsequent month for foreign nationals; no hostel accommodation
