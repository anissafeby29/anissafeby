Transplant Hepatology Fellowship

University of Toronto / University Health Network (Toronto General Hospital)

Overview

Fully funded transplant hepatology fellowship at a centre performing about 200 adult liver transplants a year (25-30% living donor), rotating through inpatient, outpatient and research weeks.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1-2 years
Start
1 July
Positions
4 fully funded positions per year
Accreditation
University of Toronto Department of Medicine clinical fellowship (PGME-registered; not RCPSC-certifying)
Department
Multi-Organ Transplant Program and Division of Gastroenterology & Hepatology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Not stated on official page
International applicants
International applicants who meet CPSO requirements for licensure as a clinical fellow may contact the fellowship programme director; trainees from countries with University of Toronto postgraduate training agreements apply via their sponsoring agency.
Visa support
PGME provides successful international applicants with documentation to apply for the appropriate Canadian work permit.
Licence requirement
Must be eligible for a CPSO certificate authorizing postgraduate education (educational licence); applicants must be recognised as specialists in the jurisdiction where they practised before the fellowship.
