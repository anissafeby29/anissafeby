Overview

A one-year body imaging fellowship for board-certified or eligible radiologists who have completed an accredited radiology residency.

The programme

Focus
Abdominal/Body imaging
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
Institution policyUCMC GME contract year runs 1 July - 30 June (2026-27 compensation year 1 July 2026 - 30 June 2027)
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Not ACGME accredited
Department
Department of Radiology, University of Cincinnati (UC Medical Center)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of an ACGME-accredited DR residency in the US or abroad, and ABR board certification/eligibility at the start of training.
International applicants
ECFMG certificate requested if applicable; ABR eligibility required.
Visa support
Institution policyUC Health requires incoming trainees entering the US for the first time to be J-1 (ECFMG) eligible; no new H-1B sponsorship since October 2025, but H-1B transfers from other institutions accepted
Licence requirement
Institution policyOhio permanent medical licence or State Medical Board of Ohio training certificate required; USMLE Step 3 required for entry at PGY-4 or above

How to apply

Deadline
Not stated on official page
Application method
CV, personal statement, three letters (under six months old, on letterhead), USMLE scores and ECFMG certificate if applicable, emailed to the fellowship coordinator.

Funding

Funding
Institution policyUCMC stipends effective 21 June 2026: PGY4 $76,934; PGY5 $80,126; PGY6 $83,103; PGY7 $86,831; PGY8 $88,181 (PGY1 $70,185)
