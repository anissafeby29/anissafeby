Overview

One-year paediatric oncology fellowship for ASEAN fellows-in-training at Singapore's university children's hospital.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Fellows-in-training from the ASEAN region (experience in the field preferred) with support of a host institution in the home country
International applicants
Tailored for ASEAN-region trainees
Visa support
Institution policyforeign clinical fellows need SMC temporary registration and a Ministry of Manpower training pass, arranged with the NUH Fellowship Desk after departmental recommendation; home-country sponsorship (government, health authority or employer) required
Licence requirement
Qualifications must be registrable with the Singapore Medical Council
