Overview

Self-funded visiting fellowship in which KSR helps foreign radiologists find a suitable Korean institution and tutor, with no restriction on duration or timing.

The programme

Focus
Diagnostic and interventional radiology (subspecialty fellowships)
Training type
Visiting Fellowship (self-funded)
Duration
No limitation — based on applicant's wish
Start
No limitation of timing (must start in applicable year)
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
KSR certificate of completion
Department
KSR International Liaison Committee

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
No limitation for applicants (per KSR); licensed medical doctor and radiologist; must become international member of KSR
International applicants
Yes — for foreign radiologists; recipients must return home after training
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Copies of licences as medical doctor and radiologist required

How to apply

Deadline
August 31 of the previous year
Application method
Send application form, recommendation from national radiological society president, CV, passport, medical report, pledge, licences and employment certificate to KSR International Liaison Committee (office@radiology.kr)

Funding

Funding
No financial support from KSR; tuition fee may be charged for tutoring service
