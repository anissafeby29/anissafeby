Overview

Comprehensive clinical fellowship with large cochlear and auditory brainstem implant volumes and over 100 acoustic neuromas a year at NYU Langone, Bellevue and the Manhattan VA.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
2 years
Start
1 July
Positions
One fellow every other year
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited
Department
Department of Otolaryngology–Head and Neck Surgery

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of an ACGME-accredited otolaryngology–head and neck surgery residency
International applicants
ACGME neurotology fellowship: requires completion of an ACGME-accredited (or RCPSC) otolaryngology–HNS residency; not open to non-US/Canada-trained otolaryngologists
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
New York medical licence

How to apply

Funding

Funding
