Overview

A two-year fellowship mirroring the University of Toronto Geriatric Medicine subspecialty residency (Competence by Design), after which graduates can apply to write the Royal College Geriatric Medicine exam.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
2 years
Start
July 1
Positions
1 per year
Accreditation
RCPSC Subspecialty Examination Affiliate Program (SEAP) pathway; eligible to write Royal College Geriatric Medicine exam
Department
Department of Medicine, Division of Geriatric Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Institution policymust be eligible for a CPSO postgraduate education certificate, including recognition as a medical specialist/family physician in the jurisdiction where the applicant practised before the fellowship
International applicants
Non-Canadian applicants may apply; sponsored applicants from Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia and UAE apply via sponsoring agency to PGME (per UofT PGME fellowship page)
Visa support
Institution policyinternational clinical fellows need an IRCC work permit; the PGME Office issues an LMIA-exempt offer of employment number and supports the work permit application
Licence requirement
CPSO certificate authorizing postgraduate education (educational licence); applicants must be recognized as a specialist/family physician in the jurisdiction where they practised (per UofT PGME fellowship page)

How to apply

Deadline
Application opens beginning of July, closes end of August (mirrors CaRMS)
Application method
Contact program (supervisor Dov Gandell)

Funding

Funding
Institution policyclinical fellows must be funded (department or sponsoring agency); FEAC recommends pay at least equal to PGY1 on the PARO salary scale, and foreign nationals must be paid at a rate commensurate with a Canadian in the same role
