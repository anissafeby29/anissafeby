Overview

One-year EAU Endourology Section fellowship at Fundació Puigvert combining hands-on minimally invasive surgery, research and personal mentorship from the Section board.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
EAU member; urologist or urologist-in-training aged 45 or younger (exceptions possible); preferably 3+ PubMed publications with at least one as first author
International applicants
Open to EAU members (membership for at least one year before applying; 2026 is a transition year); the page sets no nationality restriction, but all visits must take place at a European institution
