Overview

One of only a few U.S. trauma anesthesiology fellowships, with seven months on the Trauma Anesthesia Service plus ICU and elective rotations, averaging 16 shifts a month.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed an anesthesiology residency (U.S. or abroad) and passed USMLE Steps
International applicants
Residency abroad accepted with USMLE passed
Visa support
Accepts a variety of visas but cannot sponsor new H-1 visa applications at this time; applicants already on H-1B may be considered
Licence requirement
Institution policy (Missouri Board of Registration for the Healing Arts): residents and fellows in approved Missouri training programmes must hold a Missouri temporary licence (renewed annually; applied for via the hospital) before practising; IMGs need a permanent ECFMG certificate
