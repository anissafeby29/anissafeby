University Malaya Medical Centre (UMMC)

Open official programme page ↗

Overview

Clinical attachment (observation or limited clinical training, max 12 months) in UMMC Biomedical Imaging units including interventional, neuro, clinical, breast, musculoskeletal radiology and nuclear medicine.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Attachment / Observership

Up to 12 months
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
UMMC fellowship training (hands-on), approved by UMMC C&P Committee
Department
Department of Biomedical Imaging

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Master's degree / clinical master's or equivalent recognised by the Malaysian Medical Council (Second Schedule, Medical Act 1971); MMC registration; Professional Visit Pass (PLIK) approval; support of Head of Department; approval by UMMC Credentialing and Privileging Committee; attachment limited to clinical observation or limited clinical training
International applicants
Yes — non-Malaysian applicants accepted; apply at least 6 months before start (fellowship/hands-on) and obtain PLIK visa
Visa support
Professional Visit Pass (PLIK) approval by the Immigration Department of Malaysia required (applicant obtains)
Licence requirement
Registration with the Malaysian Medical Council required
