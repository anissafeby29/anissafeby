Overview

Observership giving general paediatric surgery exposure to junior paediatric surgeons from resource-challenged settings.

The programme

Training type
Observership

3-6 months

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Recommended by HOD or two referees; conversant in English; letter of good standing from home licensing authority
International applicants
Yes - for junior paediatric surgeons, particularly from resource-challenged settings
