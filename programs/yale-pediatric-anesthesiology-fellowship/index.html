Overview

A 12-month ACGME-approved programme at Yale New Haven Hospital preparing graduates to become leaders in pediatric anesthesiology, with an option to extend for research training.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
12 months clinical (can be extended for additional research training)
Start
Institution policyYale-New Haven GME resident/fellow agreements run for one year beginning 1 July and ending 30 June (YNHH sample contract)
Positions
2
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited (approved by ABA and ACGME)
Department
Department of Anesthesiology, Yale School of Medicine (Yale New Haven Children's Hospital)
