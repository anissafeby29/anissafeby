Overview

A 12-month fellowship focused on breast, melanoma and trauma reconstruction combining Sunnybrook's trauma centre and cancer centre exposure.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Certified plastic surgeon in Canada or country of origin (or passed MCCQE)
International applicants
International graduates accepted; all IMG fellows must complete a 4–12 week Pre-Entry Assessment Period (PEAP); TOEFL required if medical school instruction was not in English
Visa support
University of Toronto PGME Office assists non-Canadian fellows in obtaining a work permit
Licence requirement
Must be licensed by the College of Physicians and Surgeons of Ontario (CPSO), with CMPA coverage

How to apply

Deadline
1 May 2026 for the 2027–2028 academic year
Application method
Letter of interest with start date, two letters of recommendation, two additional referees, CV and TOEFL proof if applicable, sent to the Sunnybrook office manager

Funding

Funding
Institution policyclinical fellows must be funded (department or sponsoring agency); FEAC recommends pay at least equal to PGY1 on the PARO salary scale, and foreign nationals must be paid at a rate commensurate with a Canadian in the same role
