Overview

Training combines pediatric operating rooms, pain services and intensive care with regular exposure to congenital cardiac cases.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
2027-28 fellowship year for the latest completed match
Positions
1–2 per year
Accreditation
ACGME
Department
Department of Anesthesiology, Duke University School of Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed ACGME anesthesiology residency, eligibility for ABA certification, PALS certification and North Carolina medical licensure.
International applicants
Institution policyYes - graduates of medical schools outside the US/Canada are eligible if they hold a valid ECFMG certificate or a full unrestricted US licence
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 alien physician status sponsored by ECFMG with Duke's assistance; Duke is H-1B cap-exempt and H-1B is possible for clinical trainees who have passed USMLE Steps 1-3 and hold an ECFMG certificate (individual programmes decide which visas they accept)
Licence requirement
Must meet North Carolina Medical Board requirements

How to apply

Deadline
2027-28 cycle: application deadline 15 May 2026 (closed); Match Day 23 June 2026; next cycle not yet posted
Application method
SF Match, with additional ABA Basic Exam and in-training exam reports

Funding

Funding
Institution policyGME trainees paid on the Duke stipend scale; FY27 (from 1 July 2026) PGY-1 $71,052 rising to PGY-4 $79,908, PGY-5 $82,992, PGY-6 $86,292, PGY-7 $90,036 (up to PGY-12 $106,800), plus benefits
