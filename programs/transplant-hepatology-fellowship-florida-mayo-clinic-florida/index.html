Transplant Hepatology Fellowship (Florida)

Mayo Clinic — Florida

Internal MedicineHepatologyClinical Fellowship
Destination
USA
Jacksonville, Florida, United States
Open official programme page ↗Compare saved →

Overview

A one-year fellowship providing hands-on training in clinical hepatology and the care of patients before, during and after liver transplantation, fulfilling UNOS criteria.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
July
Positions
2 per year
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited (Mayo Clinic School of Graduate Medical Education)
Department
Mayo Clinic School of Graduate Medical Education

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed accredited internal medicine residency and gastroenterology fellowship before starting
International applicants
Institution policyGraduates of non-US medical schools eligible if ECFMG-certified before appointment (applicants still obtaining certification may interview but cannot be ranked or appointed) or holding a full unrestricted licence in the programme state; Canadian graduates after 1 July 2025 also need ECFMG certification
Visa support
Institution policyMayo Clinic School of Graduate Medical Education supports ECFMG J-1 and H-1B visas (Mayo selects visa type). Since the Sept 2025 $100,000 H-1B fee, Mayo will not file new H-1B petitions that trigger the fee - H-1B only for change/extension of status (USMLE Steps 1-3 required); otherwise ECFMG J-1 (Steps 1-2, ACGME-accredited/recognised programme). F-1 OPT accepted for first year for US-school graduates
Licence requirement
Institution policyFlorida fellows must hold a full Florida Board of Medicine (or Osteopathic) licence before starting (3-6 months to obtain); residents need Florida registration as an unlicensed physician; Mayo reimburses fees

How to apply

Deadline
Apply during the second year of gastroenterology fellowship; positions filled at least 18 months before start
Application method
AASLD third-party application service; positions filled at least 18 months before start
