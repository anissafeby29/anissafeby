Overview

University of Colorado Anschutz Medical Campus's Aurora programme is included in the American Urogynecologic Society's 2026 national guide to Urogynecology and Reconstructive Pelvic Surgery fellowships.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Graduated from a US OB/GYN or Urology residency programme by the start date
International applicants
Requires completion of a US OB/GYN or Urology residency
Visa support
Institution policyClinical J-1 via ECFMG is the standard route; H-1B for residents/fellows only if CU GME agrees to waive its J-1 requirement
Licence requirement
Institution policyColorado Medical Board physician training licence (or full Colorado licence) required
