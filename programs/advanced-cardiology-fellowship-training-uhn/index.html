Overview

Subspecialty fellowship pathways at the Peter Munk Cardiac Centre include interventional cardiology, electrophysiology, advanced heart failure, imaging and adult congenital heart disease.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
Varies by pathway (e.g. ACHD 2 years; EP 2-3 years; HCM 2 years; cardiotoxicity 2 years)
Start
Varies
Positions
Varies by pathway (e.g. ACHD 6-7; cardiovascular surgery 6; interventional cardiology 4)
Accreditation
Unaccredited; does not lead to independent licensure in Ontario
Department
Peter Munk Cardiac Centre, University Health Network

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Prior cardiology training; pathway-specific requirements apply
International applicants
Yes - several pathways state they recruit from national and international applicants (e.g. ACHD, cardiovascular surgery)
Visa support
Institution policyInternational clinical fellows need an IRCC work permit; the PGME Office issues an LMIA-exempt offer of employment number and supports the work permit application
Licence requirement
Institution policyClinical fellows must hold a CPSO certificate of registration authorising postgraduate education (educational licence); research fellows do not need CPSO registration
