Overview

A two-year Scripps Clinic programme with an ACGME-accredited coronary intervention year followed by a year of structural heart and peripheral vascular intervention training, accepting three fellows per year.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
2 years (ACGME-accredited coronary year followed by a structural heart and peripheral vascular year)
Start
July
Positions
3 fellows per year
Accreditation
First year ACGME-accredited
Department
Scripps Clinic Graduate Medical Education, Division of Cardiovascular Diseases

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed, or expect to complete, an ACGME-accredited residency and cardiology training; meet all licensure and certification requirements; ECFMG certificate if applicable
International applicants
IMGs with ECFMG certificate may apply but Scripps Clinic does not sponsor J-1 or H-1 visas
Visa support
Scripps Clinic does not sponsor J-1 or H-1 visas
Licence requirement
Must meet California licensure requirements
