Overview

University of Texas, MD Anderson Cancer Center's Houston programme appears in the Society of Gynecologic Oncology's national participating-program list for advanced multidisciplinary training in gynaecologic cancer care.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
4 years (24 months research + 24 months clinical)
Start
1 July
Positions
3 new fellows per year (12 in programme)
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited (#2254822001)
Department
Department of Gynecologic Oncology and Reproductive Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed ACGME-accredited 4-year OBGYN residency (or RCPSC 5-year) and eligible for ABOG written exam; US citizen or permanent resident
International applicants
Not eligible unless US citizen or permanent resident at time of application
Visa support
None (US citizenship or permanent residency required)
Licence requirement
Not stated on official page

How to apply

Deadline
2026 cycle: 6 April 2026 for position beginning 1 July 2027; in-person interviews in August
Application method
ERAS and NRMP Match

Funding

Funding
Competitive salary and fringe benefits; research years funded on NIH T32 training grant
