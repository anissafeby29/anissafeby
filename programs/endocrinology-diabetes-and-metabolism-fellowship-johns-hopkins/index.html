Overview

An ACGME-accredited fellowship (since 1987) at The Johns Hopkins Hospital and Bayview with Clinical Scholars and Research Scholars tracks; research scholars may obtain T32 support for further research years.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
2 years (ACGME); research scholars track supported for at least 2 additional research years

Not stated on official page
Positions
3 per year
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Not stated on official page
International applicants
IMGs welcome but must have completed an ACGME-accredited US internal medicine residency
Visa support
J-1 and existing H-1B holders considered primarily for the clinical scholars track
Licence requirement
Institution policy (Maryland Board of Physicians): residents and fellows train as registered unlicensed practitioners through their programme; a full Maryland licence requires 1 year (US/Canadian grads) or ECFMG + 2 years (IMGs) of accredited postgraduate training plus licensing exams
