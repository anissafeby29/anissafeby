Overview

Twelve-month EAPCI grant for clinical training in complex PCI, structural or other interventional cardiology at a hosting centre of excellence in another ESC country.

The programme

Focus
Interventional cardiology
Training type
Clinical Fellowship (society grant)
Duration
12 months (shorter periods not accepted)
Start
After full application and host confirmation (2027 cycle)
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
EAPCI (ESC) Hosting Centre
Department
Not stated on official page

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Qualified cardiologist or in final year of cardiology training; under 40; not yet in a permanent, senior staff or consultant post; EAPCI/ESC requirements per rules
International applicants
Restricted to citizens or permanent residents of ESC member countries (ESC National Cardiac Societies, which include some non-EU countries; see the rules' appendix). Training must be in an ESC member country other than the applicant's own. Final validation subject to visa approval where needed
Visa support
Grant validation subject to visa approval; fellow arranges visa and paperwork
Licence requirement
Must meet host requirements (language level, medical licence, authority approval, health insurance) to act as first operator; non-EU applicants may get extra time for visa and translations

How to apply

Deadline
Next application period 19 February – 31 March 2027
Application method
Online via My ESC (Applications > New Grant Application) with CV, supervisor recommendation, host pre-agreement letter (and Part B if centre not listed) and language certificate

Funding

Funding
€26,865 for living expenses, paid to the recipient in three instalments
