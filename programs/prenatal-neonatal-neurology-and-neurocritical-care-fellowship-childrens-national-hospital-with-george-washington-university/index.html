Prenatal-Neonatal Neurology and Neurocritical Care Fellowship

Children's National Hospital (with George Washington University)

PaediatricsNeurologyClinical Fellowship
Destination
USA
Washington, District of Columbia, United States
Open official programme page ↗

Overview

Two-year fellowship in fetal and neonatal neurology and neonatal neurocritical care, with an 80% clinical track preparing fellows for UCNS certification and an NIH T32 research track.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
2 years
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
2 positions each year (clinical track); separate NIH T32 clinical research track
Accreditation
Accredited by the United Council for Neurologic Subspecialties (UCNS) for Neonatal Neurocritical Care
Department
Developing Brain Institute / Division of Neurology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed residency in child neurology or neurodevelopmental disabilities, or fellowship in neonatology, with demonstrated interest in the immature brain
International applicants
Not stated on official page
Visa support
Children's National processes the J-1 (Alien Physician) visa sponsored by ECFMG (requires USMLE Step 1 and Step 2 CK, ECFMG certification and Ministry of Health Statement of Need); valid EAD from F-1 OPT also accepted (GME candidates page)
Licence requirement
Full licensure to practise medicine in the United States required
