Overview

Siriraj one-year fellowship in orthopaedic sports medicine and arthroscopy, including a rotation at the sport science centre.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (10 months orthopaedics, 1 month sport science centre, 1 month elective)
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
1 per year
Accreditation
Siriraj institutional certificate (Faculty of Medicine Siriraj Hospital)
Department
Department of Orthopaedic Surgery

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Graduate of an accredited medical school; medical licence accredited by the Thai Medical Council; Thai board in orthopaedic surgery (or general surgery) or foreign training accredited by the Thai Medical Council
International applicants
Programme document lists 'Medical insurance (for the foreigner)' among fees and refers enquiries to the Siriraj International Recruitment Team, indicating foreign applicants are accepted; candidates need a licence accredited by the Thai Medical Council
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Medical licence accredited by the Thai Medical Council required

How to apply

Deadline
Not stated on official page
Application method
Apply through the Siriraj Postgraduate Education Division (international recruitment: siint.recruit@gmail.com); assessment ends with oral, written and psychomotor tests plus thesis defence

Funding

Funding
Self-funded: registration fee 350,000 THB/year, medical insurance for foreigners 50,000 THB, research expenses 50,000 THB
