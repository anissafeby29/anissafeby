Overview

International training in gynaecologic endoscopic surgery at Siriraj's TG-MET centre, progressing from diagnostic laparoscopy/hysteroscopy (3 months) to advanced operative endoscopy (1 year) with English didactics and lab training.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
At least one of: licentiate doctor of medicine from a qualified institute; qualified for Board of Obstetrics and Gynecology; three years' experience in gynaecologic surgery
International applicants
Yes - international programme for foreign gynaecologists, taught in English with dry/wet lab training
