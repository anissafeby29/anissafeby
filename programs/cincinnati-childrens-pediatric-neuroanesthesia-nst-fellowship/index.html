Overview

An NST fellowship providing advanced pediatric anesthesia training with special emphasis on neurosurgical procedures, designed to develop foreign-trained anesthesiologists into academically oriented pediatric neuroanesthesiologists.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Fully trained anesthesiologists from abroad with a strong interest in pediatric anesthesiology; approval by CCHMC GMEC Committee on Exceptionally Qualified Candidates
International applicants
Designed for foreign-trained anesthesiologists

NST participants usually receive J-1 visas sponsored by ECFMG
