Overview

Comprehensive training in advanced head and neck oncologic surgery, pedicled and microvascular reconstruction, transoral robotic, salivary, endocrine and cutaneous malignancy surgery, including Bellevue Head & Neck Clinic.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Board eligible in Otolaryngology–Head & Neck Surgery (International Track per AHNS rules)
International applicants
Accepted via the AHNS 'International Track': applicants who trained outside the US/Canada must have completed (or be completing) a non-ACGME/non-RCPSC residency in otolaryngology, general surgery or plastic surgery and hold ECFMG certification
Visa support
Institution policyNYU Langone does not offer H-1B sponsorship for residents or fellows; J-1 sponsorship available through ECFMG
Licence requirement
Institution policyFor NYU-sponsored ACGME/AOA programmes no NY licence is required by state law (resident exemption, Educ. Law 6526(1)), though individual programmes may require a NY licence or limited permit; trainees in non-accredited programmes MUST hold a NY licence or limited permit before starting; USMLE Step 3 required before final year in ACGME programmes

How to apply

Deadline
15 March 2027 (AHNS match, July 2028 start)
Application method
AHNS central fellowship match (Advanced Training Council); for July 2028 start: applications 21 Dec 2026 – 15 Mar 2027, interviews Mar–Jun 2027, results 9 Jul 2027; AHNS membership (or pending application) required
