Overview

A GOSH international fellowship in paediatric cochlear implantation for overseas doctors with at least four years of specialty training, offering structured subspecialist training mapped to the Royal College curriculum.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
GMC-acceptable medical degree; 4+ years of specialty training; Part 1 College exam or equivalent; postgraduate specialty qualification (e.g. UK Royal College membership or home-country equivalent); recent full-time clinical work; IELTS 7.5 overall (7.0 each) or OET grade B
International applicants
Designed for international doctors resident overseas; must return home after visa expires
Visa support
GOSH is an official UK visa sponsor
Licence requirement
GMC registration via GOSH GMC sponsorship
