Overview

FK UNHAS in Makassar offers an internal medicine subspecialist (Sp2) track in nephrology and hypertension for Indonesian internists, admitted through the Mandiri Sp-2 PPDS pathway.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Indonesian specialist (Sp1) in internal medicine; legalised Sp1 diploma and transcript; valid specialist STR from KKI; BPJS card; current employment letter and superior's permission; accreditation certificates of the Sp1 programme (LAM-PTKes) and university (BAN-PT) - foreign graduates submit accreditation of the home or adaptation university; written references from colleagues in the chosen subspecialty; health-office/local-government recommendation if sponsored. Maximum age 45 at start; Sp1 GPA >= 3.00 from an A/B-accredited institution; KIPD competence certificate and mandatory Internal Medicine Collegium (KIPD) recommendation; TOEFL >= 450 or IELTS >= 6; physical and mental health certificate; 3 personal references; 2 years' experience as an internist in a hospital or secondary care (FK UNHAS staff: 6 months).

How to apply

Deadline
Latest cycle published on the page (2025/2026): online registration and fee payment 15 April - 17 May 2025, documents received by 20 May 2025; 2026/2027 dates not stated on official page
Application method
Jalur Mandiri Sp-2 PPDS via regpmb.unhas.ac.id: pay Rp 1,200,000 registration fee, complete online form and uploads, post two document sets to the FK UNHAS PPDS Central Office; health test, MMPI and selection exams

Funding

Funding
Self-funded or sponsored (Ministry of Health, LPDP, military/police scholarships accepted with documents); fees: UKT Rp 17,500,000 per semester plus one-off IPI Rp 40,000,000
