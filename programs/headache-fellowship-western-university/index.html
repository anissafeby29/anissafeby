Overview

A headache neurology fellowship combining clinical care, research and education at LHSC.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
Typically 1 year
Start
July (IMG deadline 31 August of the preceding year for a July start)
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Western PGME clinical fellowship (not RCPSC-accredited)
Department
Department of Clinical Neurological Sciences, Schulich School of Medicine & Dentistry

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed accredited neurology residency; eligible for licensure in Ontario
International applicants
IMGs welcome if funding is secured through their sponsoring bureau
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
CPSO licence eligibility (educational licence for IMGs)

How to apply

Deadline
IMGs: August 31 of the preceding year; Canadian graduates per Canadian Headache Society
Application method
Send CV, medical degree and specialty certificate (English translations), three references and proof of citizenship to the fellowship director; sponsored international fellows' sponsors submit to the PGME Office

Funding

Funding
Canadian graduates via Canadian Headache Society; IMGs must secure sponsor funding
