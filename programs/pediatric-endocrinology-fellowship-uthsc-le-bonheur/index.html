Overview

Long-established ACGME-accredited pediatric endocrinology fellowship supporting clinical services at Le Bonheur and Regional One Health, with adjunct faculty at St. Jude.

International applicants
Institution policyIMGs eligible with a valid ECFMG certificate (or Fifth Pathway) and passed USMLE Steps 1-2 (Step 3 for entry at PGY3+); must be J-1 eligible or permanent residents (H-1B only by GME exception)
Visa support
Institution policy (UTHSC GME Policy #140, rev. 7/2025): non-US-permanent-resident IMGs in ACGME programmes must train on an ECFMG J-1 visa; J-1 holders are not permitted in non-ACGME or non-standard training programmes; H-1B only with Department Chair and Associate Dean for GME approval (department pays fees)
