Overview

University of South Alabama's Mobile programme appears in the Society of Gynecologic Oncology's national participating-program list for advanced multidisciplinary training in gynaecologic cancer care.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years (year 1 research; years 2-3 clinical)
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Listed by the Society of Gynecologic Oncology; confirm current ACGME status
Department
Department of Obstetrics & Gynecology; training at USA Health Mitchell Cancer Institute

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed Obstetrics and Gynaecology residency; ERAS, NRMP and institution-specific requirements apply
International applicants
Institution policyYes - IMGs need a valid ECFMG certificate (or unrestricted licence/Fifth Pathway) and permanent residency or J-1 status
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 (ECFMG) only for foreign nationals (or permanent residence/EAD; other statuses case by case); H-1B visas will not be sponsored for trainees
Licence requirement
Institution policytrainees without a full Alabama licence must apply for a limited Alabama medical licence within six months of starting and maintain it until fully licensed

How to apply

Deadline
Follows the SGO Program Directors Network common application deadline (ERAS); virtual interviews
Application method
ERAS (NRMP Match)

Funding

Funding
Salaried per USA GME scale: PGY-5 $72,420; PGY-6 $75,378; PGY-7 $78,645; relocation allowance
