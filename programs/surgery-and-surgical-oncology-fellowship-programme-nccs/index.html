Overview

Professional oncology training for overseas doctors, training surgeons to work in multidisciplinary teams providing comprehensive tertiary cancer care (theatre, clinics, tumour boards).

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed accredited specialist training in General Surgery; proficient in English. Basic medical degree; 12 months housemanship; registered in home country; eligible for SMC Temporary Registration and MOM Training Employment Pass; 2 recommendation letters; service testimonial; English proficiency; 3 years post-housemanship experience or postgraduate diploma/degree; clinical fellows must be sponsored by government, regional health authority or home institution (full-time employed)
International applicants
Yes - 'Non-Local Doctors Fellowships' offered by the NCCS Post Graduate Education Unit
Visa support
Training Employment Pass from Ministry of Manpower (MOM) required for clinical fellowship
Licence requirement
SMC Temporary Medical Registration plus malpractice indemnity cover (MPS)

How to apply

Deadline
At least 8 months before targeted commencement (clinical fellowship); at least 3 months before (hands-off observership)
Application method
Send CV specifying desired attachment period and division to pgeu@nccs.com.sg using the NCCS application form; no concurrent applications
