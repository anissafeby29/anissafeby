Overview

SNUH-sponsored observational international fellowship available in ophthalmology and paediatric ophthalmology, with daily allowance, visa assistance and certificate.

The programme

Training type
Observational fellowship
Duration
1 month to 1 year
Start
Per departmental approval
Positions
Approximately 25 sponsored trainees per year across SNUH
Accreditation
Certificate of completion (institutional)
Department
Department of Ophthalmology (incl. Pediatric Ophthalmology)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Medical doctors (medical licence, certificate of employment, degree certificate in English)
International applicants
Yes - programme for international medical doctors
Visa support
Yes - visa assistance provided
Licence requirement
Home-country medical licence (observational only)

How to apply

Deadline
Applications accepted annually
Application method
Online application via SNUH Global education page; reviewed and approved by department

Funding

Funding
Sponsored programme: KRW 60,000 per day (KRW 1.8 million per month); lodging, meals, insurance and transport at trainee's own cost
