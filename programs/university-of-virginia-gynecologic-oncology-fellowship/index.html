Overview

University of Virginia's Charlottesville programme appears in the Society of Gynecologic Oncology's national participating-program list for advanced multidisciplinary training in gynaecologic cancer care.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed accredited OBGYN residency; board eligible with ABOG application filed >=90 days before entry; 3 letters (PD SLOE, Gyn Onc SLOE), CREOG scores
International applicants
Accepted; must be eligible for ECFMG certification before matriculation and show English proficiency
Visa support
J-1 sponsored
Licence requirement
Institution policyAll medical house staff must hold a Virginia Intern and Residency Training License (issued annually; fee paid by the hospital, coordinated by the GME Office) - or a permanent Virginia licence

How to apply

Deadline
2026 cycle: 6 April 2026; invitations 11 May 2026; virtual interviews 8 and 22 July 2026; rank list deadline 9 Sept 2026
Application method
ERAS and NRMP Match

Funding

Funding
Employed as UVA House Staff; salary/benefits per UVA GME
