Overview

Clinical training spans cardiac, surgical, neurological and medical ICUs with substantial transplant and mechanical-support experience.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed ACGME anesthesiology or general surgery residency. Exceptional ECFMG-certified international graduates with home-country anesthesiology residency may be considered case by case.
International applicants
Exceptional ECFMG-certified IMGs with home-country anaesthesiology residency considered case by case (contact programme first)
Visa support
J-1 via ECFMG sponsorship; Mayo Clinic may also sponsor H-1B if all eligibility requirements are met

Not stated on official page

Application method
