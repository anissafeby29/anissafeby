Overview

University of Chicago's Chicago programme is included in the American Urogynecologic Society's 2026 national guide to Urogynecology and Reconstructive Pelvic Surgery fellowships.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years for OBGYN pathways; confirm urology-entry options
Start
August (fellows join as PGY-5 in August)
Positions
1 position in the published 2026 cycle
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited (entering third year of accreditation)
Department
Not stated on official page

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Graduates of OB/GYN or Urology residencies (fellows enter at PGY-5)
International applicants
Institution policyForeign nationals accepted with visa sponsorship, primarily J-1 (ECFMG); H-1B case by case
Visa support
Institution policyPrimarily J-1 via ECFMG (preferred); H-1B case by case only with programme and GME Office approval (programme bears H-1B costs)
Licence requirement
Institution policyIllinois temporary (training) or permanent medical licence required before any patient care; cost borne by trainee

How to apply

Deadline
2026 cycle: ERAS opened 12 Nov 2025; first-round interview deadline 26 Jan 2026; interviews 2 Mar–1 Jun 2026 (closed)
Application method
ERAS; AUGS common dates

Funding

Funding
Institution policyUniform UCMC GME stipend scale FY26: PGY4 $86,036; PGY5 $89,776; PGY6 $91,647; PGY7 $95,154; PGY8 $96,824 (PGY9+ by approved letter)
