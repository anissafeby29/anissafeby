Overview

One-year fellowship at UCSF Benioff Children's Hospitals San Francisco and Oakland covering airway, paediatric otology, head and neck neoplasms, complex sleep surgery, VPI and endoscopic sinus/skull base surgery.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed an otolaryngology residency accredited by ACGME, AOA, ACGME-International (Advanced Specialty Accreditation) or RCPSC
International applicants
Open to graduates of ACGME-I (Advanced Specialty Accreditation) or RCPSC-accredited residencies as well as ACGME/AOA
Visa support
Institution policyIMGs in graduate medical training hold J-1 (Alien Physician) visas sponsored by ECFMG, with the UCSF ISSO as Training Program Liaison (request 6 months before start); H-1B for clinical trainees only via exception request
Licence requirement
California medical licence

How to apply
