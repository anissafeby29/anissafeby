Hepatobiliary and Upper Gastrointestinal Surgery Anaesthesia Fellowship

Fiona Stanley Fremantle Hospitals Group

AnaesthesiologyOncological anaesthesiaClinical Fellowship
Destination

Overview

A 12-month fellowship promoting transition to consultant-level practice in anaesthesia for major liver resection, Whipple's procedure and minimally invasive oesophagectomy.

The programme

Training type

12 months

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
FANZCA or overseas equivalent with a keen interest in the subspecialty
International applicants
Yes — fellowship positions are open to local and international candidates
Visa support
Institution policyApplicants must be eligible to work in Australia; those requiring visa sponsorship indicate this in their MedCareersWA application
Licence requirement
Institution policyMust be eligible for registration with the Medical Board of Australia (Ahpra); IMGs need an approved pathway (e.g. short-term training in a medical specialty, specialist, competent authority or standard pathway)

How to apply

Deadline
Recruitment yearly via MedCareersWA; formal recruitment pool opens approximately March/April; roles may also arise during the year
Application method
Apply via MedCareersWA recruitment pool; enquiries to the FSFHG fellowship recruitment team

Funding

Funding
Salaried per WA Health System – Medical Practitioners – AMA Industrial Agreement 2022, with salary packaging
