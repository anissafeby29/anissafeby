Clinical Joint Replacement Surgery Fellowship

Department of Orthopaedics and Traumatology, The University of Hong Kong (Queen Mary Hospital)

OrthopaedicsAdult reconstruction / arthroplastyClinical Fellowship
Destination
HKG
Hong Kong, Hong Kong Island, Hong Kong SAR
Open official programme page ↗

Overview

Three-month clinical and research fellowship in hip and knee replacement surgery for overseas orthopaedic surgeons.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 months
Start
Four 3-month fellowships per year, one starting 1 January
Positions
4 per year
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Division of Joint Replacement Surgery

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Registered medical practitioner in good standing in country of origin; completed orthopaedic training; basic oral and written English
International applicants
Yes - the division welcomes overseas doctors as fellows
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Offer contingent on limited registration being granted by the Medical Council of Hong Kong

How to apply

Deadline
One year prior to the starting date
Application method
Personal statement to the division chief, CV, best first-author paper, two recommendation letters from orthopaedic consultants, proof of full registration and completed training - to Ms Julia Chan (jchanlaina@ortho.hku.hk)

Funding

Funding
HK$20,000 per month honorarium depending on experience; travel, accommodation and insurance not included
