Overview

Trains physicians in diabetes, endocrinology, metabolism and nutrition across UW Medical Center, Harborview and VA Puget Sound, with clinical scholar and research scholar tracks.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
2 years (Clinical Scholar) or 3 years (Research Scholar)

Not stated on official page

Accreditation
ACGME-accredited
Department
Division of Metabolism, Endocrinology & Nutrition, Department of Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of ACGME- or RCPSC-accredited internal medicine residency; eligible for VA Puget Sound appointment (incl. Selective Service where applicable). Research track open only to US citizens/permanent residents.
International applicants
Institution policyYes - international medical graduates must hold valid ECFMG certification as a condition of eligibility for fellowship application; non-US citizens need permanent residence, a valid EAD, or eligibility for J-1, F-1 OPT or H-1B
Visa support
J-1 and H-1B sponsorship for the two-year clinical scholar track only (per UW GME Visa Policy)
Licence requirement
Institution policyactive Washington State provider licence required throughout training (limited/resident licence or full physician licence as specified by the programme)

How to apply
