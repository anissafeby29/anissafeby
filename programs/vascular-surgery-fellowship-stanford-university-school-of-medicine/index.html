Overview

Two-year RRC-accredited vascular surgery fellowship across Santa Clara Valley Medical Center, VA Palo Alto and Stanford, leading to ABS vascular surgery board eligibility.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
2 years
Start
Institution policyStanford GME contracts are issued per academic year (1 July - 30 June); standard start late June/1 July
Positions
2 per year (NRMP match)
Accreditation
ACGME (RRC) accredited
Department
Division of Vascular & Endovascular Surgery

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Generally at least 4 years of an ACGME-approved general surgery training programme completed before enrolling
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs welcome to apply; must hold a valid ECFMG certificate and be able to obtain Medical Board of California licensure; non-ACGME fellowships need ACGME Non-Standard Training recognition before J-1 sponsorship
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 (ECFMG) is the primary visa; H-1B permitted only in some circumstances with GME approval; O-1 rarely, varies by department
Licence requirement
Institution policyMedical Board of California Postgraduate Training License required within 180 days of starting an ACGME programme (USMLE Steps 1 & 2 CK passed); non-ACGME programmes require a full California licence by the start date

How to apply

Deadline
Not stated on official page
Application method
ERAS; NRMP match registration required

Funding

Funding
Salaried; California licensure cost, $2,000 annual educational stipend and $3,000 moving stipend provided
