Breast/Cross-Sectional Imaging Fellowship

McMaster University

RadiologyBreast imagingClinical Fellowship
Destination
CAN
Hamilton, Ontario, Canada
Open official programme page ↗

Overview

12-month fellowship combining six months of breast imaging and six months of cross-sectional (oncologic body) imaging.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
12 months (July 1 – June 30)
Start
July
Positions
1 position annually per component (per site)
Accreditation
McMaster PGME clinical fellowship (one year subspecialty training by RCPSC-certified faculty)
Department
Department of Radiology (Medical Imaging), McMaster University — St. Joseph's Healthcare Hamilton / Hamilton Health Sciences — Juravinski Hospital and Cancer Centre / St. Joseph's Healthcare Hamilton

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Medical degree and specialty certificate (MCCQE1 results if no specialty certificate); valid educational (or independent) licence required for clinical functions
International applicants
Yes — portal has a separate International Applicants stream; passport and funding documentation requested
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Valid CPSO educational (or independent) licence

How to apply

Deadline
2028–2029 portal closed (April 1 – August 15, 2026); applications for 2029–2030 open April 1, 2027; IR follows national Canadian offer dates in January
Application method
McMaster Faculty of Health Sciences postgraduate application portal (Forms Manager) or via sponsoring bureau; CV, degree, specialty certificate, transcripts, 3 reference letters, passport copy, funding; up to 3 fellowships; do not email supervisors

Funding

Funding
Open to candidates with funding and those who require departmental funding
