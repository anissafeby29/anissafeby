Overview

Cardiac imaging is one of the specialties offered through GOSH's International Medical Fellowship Programme, typically delivered over twelve months for doctors with at least four years of specialty training.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Medical degree acceptable to the GMC; four or more years of specialty training at entry; passed Part 1 College exam or UK equivalent; postgraduate specialty qualification (UK Royal College membership/fellowship or equivalent home-country qualification); recent full-time clinical work; IELTS (7.0 each band, 7.5 overall) or OET (grade B each) within two years before GMC application; resident overseas when applying. Alternatively, already GMC-registered with at least four years of specialty training.
International applicants
Designed for international doctors; applicants must be resident overseas when applying and willing to return home after the visa expires.
Visa support
Successful candidates complete visa applications as part of pre-employment checks; GOSH can provide GMC sponsorship for entry onto the UK Medical Register.
Licence requirement
GMC registration required (GOSH can sponsor GMC registration).

How to apply

Deadline
Not stated on official page
Application method
Online application portal managed by the GOSH Learning Academy; eligible applications reviewed by the specialty, followed by a virtual interview and conditional offer.

Funding

Funding
Self/home-organisation funded: funding must be agreed with the home organisation to cover a placement fee; GOSH does not provide scholarships.
