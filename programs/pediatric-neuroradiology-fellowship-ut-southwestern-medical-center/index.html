Overview

A Texas Medical Board-approved paediatric neuroradiology fellowship at Children's Health Children's Medical Center Dallas; fellows typically have completed a neuroradiology or paediatric radiology fellowship.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
1 July
Positions
Up to 3 per year
Accreditation
Texas Medical Board (TMB) approved; non-ACGME
Department
Department of Radiology, UT Southwestern Medical Center

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of an accredited diagnostic radiology residency; board certified or board eligible in diagnostic radiology; eligible for Texas medical licensure.
International applicants
Department states it only accepts permanent residents or J-1 ECFMG-certified visa holders; H-1B or other visas are not routinely offered.
Visa support
J-1 (ECFMG) only; H-1B not routinely offered.
Licence requirement
Must be eligible for Texas medical licensure.

How to apply

Deadline
Recommended by 1 October (21 months before start).
Application method
Department fellowship application form, three letters of recommendation (one from current Program Director) and original medical school transcript sent by mail or email to the Radiology Education Office.

Funding

Funding
Salary by PGY level (2025: PGY-6 $86,503; PGY-7 $91,451; PGY-8 $95,787) plus benefits.
