Overview

A three-year ACGME-accredited programme with a categorical clinical/endoscopy track and an NIH T32 translational research track.

The programme

Focus
Gastroenterology
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years (categorical); 4-5 years (T32 track)
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Three to four fellows selected each year for the categorical track
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited
Department
Gastroenterology & Hepatology Fellowship Program (Cleveland Clinic digestive disease department)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Institution policyLCME/AOA graduates or IMGs with valid ECFMG certificate; ACGME fellows must have completed specialty prerequisite training (exceptional-candidate route where the RC allows)
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs eligible with a current valid ECFMG certificate; Cleveland Clinic holds ACGME Non-Standard Training recognition so approved non-accredited fellowships can take ECFMG J-1 trainees
Visa support
Cleveland Clinic accepts H-1B or ECFMG-sponsored J-1 visas for clinical fellowship; does not sponsor permanent residency petitions
Licence requirement
Institution policyPermanent or training medical licence/certificate from the state board where training (State Medical Board of Ohio training certificate for Ohio; Florida Board of Medicine for Florida) required before starting; some advanced/non-accredited fellowships require a permanent licence

NIH-funded T32 track; salaries based on level of training
