Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Medical licence held ≥3 years; degree certificate and transcripts (notarised translations); TOEFL-iBT 64 / IELTS 5.0 or above; health and immune examination form required for Department of Radiology applicants
International applicants
Yes — international trainees accepted in the Thai-language programme; should study Thai for 3–6 months before the course

How to apply

Deadline
Submit at least 6 months before the beginning of the programme's academic year
Application method
Siriraj postgraduate online Forms A and B, two recommendation forms, passport, degree, transcript, English test score

Funding
