Advanced Adult Clinical Cardiology Fellowship

University of Ottawa Heart Institute

CardiologyGeneral cardiologyClinical Fellowship
Destination
CAN
Ottawa, Ontario, Canada
Open official programme page ↗Compare saved →

Overview

A primarily inpatient fellowship providing experience in the overall care of cardiac patients and practical experience of practising cardiology in a Canadian context.

The programme

Focus
General / clinical cardiology
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
2 years (minimum)
Start
1 July
Positions
1–2 per year
Accreditation
University of Ottawa clinical fellowship (does not lead to Canadian certification)
Department
Division of Cardiology, University of Ottawa Heart Institute / Department of Medicine, University of Ottawa

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
MD with training in internal medicine or cardiology, eligible for an Ontario educational licence
International applicants
Yes — listed among uOttawa fellowships accepting International Medical Graduates; proof of English proficiency (e.g., IELTS 7 in each component) required
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Ontario educational licence (CPSO); internationally trained clinical fellows complete a 4–12 week Pre-Entry Assessment Program; three-year limit of licensure for clinical fellowships

How to apply

Deadline
uOttawa fellowship documents uploaded 1 May – 30 September for a start the following July
Application method
Online application through the University of Ottawa PGME fellowship portal (personal statement, CV, degrees, proof of English proficiency, three letters of recommendation, proof of sponsorship if applicable)
