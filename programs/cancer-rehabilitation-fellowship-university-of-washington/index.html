Overview

One-year non-ACGME fellowship with Fred Hutch Cancer Center and UW Medical Center in diagnosing and treating neurological and musculoskeletal impairments related to cancer and its treatment.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Board eligible or board certified in PM&R in the year of application
International applicants
Applicants must be US citizens, US permanent residents, or eligible for H-1B visa sponsorship
Visa support
H-1B (applicants must be eligible for H-1B sponsorship)
Licence requirement
Eligible for full medical licence or limited teaching and research licence in Washington State
