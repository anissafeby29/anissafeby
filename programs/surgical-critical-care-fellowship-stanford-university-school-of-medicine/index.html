Overview

One-year ACGME-accredited surgical critical care fellowship leading to ABS certification eligibility, with optional second-year acute care surgery or research instructorship.

The programme

Focus
Trauma & acute care surgery / surgical critical care
Training type

1 year (optional second year)

2027–2028 fellowship year
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
ACGME

Department of Surgery, Division of General Surgery

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
At least three years of ACGME-accredited general surgery training in the United States
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs welcome to apply; must hold a valid ECFMG certificate and be able to obtain Medical Board of California licensure; non-ACGME fellowships need ACGME Non-Standard Training recognition before J-1 sponsorship
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 (ECFMG) is the primary visa; H-1B permitted only in some circumstances with GME approval; O-1 rarely, varies by department
Licence requirement
Institution policyMedical Board of California Postgraduate Training License required within 180 days of starting an ACGME programme (USMLE Steps 1 & 2 CK passed); non-ACGME programmes require a full California licence by the start date

How to apply

Deadline
1 July 2026 (2027–2028 cycle; match announced 26 August 2026)
Application method
