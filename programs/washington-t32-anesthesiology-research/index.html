Overview

Postdoctoral training supports research careers across anesthesiology and perioperative medicine, spanning biomedical, clinical, population and health-services approaches.

The programme

Training type
Research Fellowship
Duration
At least 2 years; extension to a third year is possible
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
3 supported positions; typically 1–2 openings annually.
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Department of Anesthesiology & Pain Medicine, University of Washington

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
MD or comparable degree from an accredited domestic or foreign institution; US citizenship or permanent residency required.
International applicants
Not eligible: US citizens or permanent residents only (NIH regulations)
Visa support
None: US citizenship or permanent residency required
Licence requirement
Institution policyactive Washington State provider licence required throughout training (limited/resident licence or full physician licence as specified by the programme)

How to apply

Deadline
Application deadline for the latest posted cycle: 28 February 2026
Application method
Application form, research/career statement, CV and three letters (one from proposed NIH-funded mentor)

Funding

Funding
NIH training-grant salary uses the NIH scale according to prior training.
