ESOR Clinical Fellowship in Neuroradiology (Ottawa)

Overview

ESOR-sponsored neuroradiology position in uOttawa's Royal College-accredited two-year subspecialty programme covering advanced MRI, CT, myelography and diagnostic and interventional neuroangiography.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
2 years (RCPSC-accredited subspecialty residency)
Start
July 1 (last published cycle: July 1, 2025)
Positions
1 ESOR place
Accreditation
Neuroradiology: RCPSC-accredited subspecialty programme
Department
Department of Radiology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Recently board-certified ESR full members residing within Europe; completed accredited radiology training and certified/eligible with RCPSC, ABR or equivalent; proficient English
International applicants
ESOR place reserved for radiologists residing within the boundaries of Europe (non-European IMGs should see the regular uOttawa fellowship listings)
Visa support
Successful applicants requiring a visa and/or sponsorship to Canada receive assistance from uOttawa
Licence requirement
Not stated on official page
