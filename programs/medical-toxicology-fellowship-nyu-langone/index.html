Overview

Two-year ACGME medical toxicology fellowship with the New York City Poison Center, with fellows following 500–1,000 cases a year and completing a scholarly project.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Board-eligible or board-certified in emergency medicine, paediatrics, internal medicine or preventive medicine
International applicants
Visa-dependent: J-1 sponsored; H-1B not sponsored
Visa support
J-1 visas can be sponsored; unable to sponsor H-1B
Licence requirement
Must meet requirements for a New York State licence and US DEA registration before starting
