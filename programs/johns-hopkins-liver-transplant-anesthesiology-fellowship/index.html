Overview

Trains academic leaders in liver transplantation and complex surgical patients through advanced perioperative management, TEE training and QI projects across about 100 liver transplants a year plus thoracic and vascular cases.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed ACGME-accredited anesthesiology residency; eligible for unrestricted Maryland licence, Maryland CDS and DEA registration and full faculty appointment; J-1 candidates cannot be considered
International applicants
Institution policyJohns Hopkins SOM accepts international residents/fellows via J-1 ECFMG Alien Physician Exchange Visitor sponsorship (applies to those joining from abroad or already in the US in another status); H-1B only in limited circumstances
Visa support
Institution policyJohns Hopkins SOM sponsors incoming residents/fellows for J-1 (ECFMG); H-1B only in limited cases (US MD graduates on OPT in ACGME training, or trainees already on H-1B in ACGME-accredited/recognised training with sufficient H-1B time remaining) - GME policy P&F028
Licence requirement
Fellows practise as independently licensed physicians
