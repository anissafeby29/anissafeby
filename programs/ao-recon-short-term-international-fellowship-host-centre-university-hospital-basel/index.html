Overview

AO-funded short-term adult reconstruction / arthroplasty fellowship placement at University Hospital Basel (Basel), a department performing about 2169 surgical cases a year.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Arthroplasty is the main focus of practice; working knowledge of English; under 45 in year of application; AO profile required
International applicants
Yes – AO global programme; fellows from any country are placed by AO regional fellowship coordinators (membership and course prerequisites apply)
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Scrub-in: Yes; additional licence/requirement to scrub in: none additional stated. Dominant OR language: German (team English level (4)/5). Documents required: Health insurance coverage in Switerland MRSA-Screening in nose &amp;amp; throat Vaccines: TB, Tetanus, Diphteria, Polio, Hepatitis B

How to apply

Deadline
2027 call closed; 2028 call opens spring 2027
Application method
Online application to the AO Foundation during the open call (host centres allocated by AO); not directly to the hospital

Funding

Funding
Small stipend towards accommodation and living (benchmark USD 550/week in the US, PPP-adjusted); travel and insurance paid by fellow
