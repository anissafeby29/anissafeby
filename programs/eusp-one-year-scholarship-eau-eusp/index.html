EUSP One-Year Scholarship

European Association of Urology – EUSP (Certified EUSP Host Centres)

Overview

Year-long research project under expert guidance at a certified European host centre, with projects proposed by host centres or by the applicant.

The programme

Focus
Urology research / clinical scholarship
Training type
Research Fellowship (scholarship)
Duration
1 year
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
One EUSP scholar per host centre at a time
Accreditation
EUSP; as of 2026 must be performed in a Certified EUSP Host Centre
Department
Not stated on official page

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
EAU member for at least one year; urologist, urologist-in-training, researcher or scientist; preferably 3+ peer-reviewed PubMed publications with at least one as first author
International applicants
Open to EAU members (membership for at least one year before applying; 2026 is a transition year); the page sets no nationality restriction, but all visits must take place at a European institution
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Not stated on official page

How to apply

Deadline
Three deadlines per year: 1 January, 1 May and 1 September
Application method
Online application with CV, publications, motivation letter, recommendation letter, host centre confirmation, project proposal (proforma) and itemised budget; shortlisted candidates interviewed by EUSP Board

Funding

Funding
Maximum grant €30,000 (paid in three instalments); no other salary or sponsorship allowed during the scholarship
