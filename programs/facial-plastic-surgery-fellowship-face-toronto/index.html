Facial Plastic Surgery Fellowship (FACE Toronto)

FACE Toronto / University of Toronto

CAN

Overview

Hands-on fellowship in the breadth of facial plastic and reconstructive surgery including deep-plane facelift, forehead lift and fat grafting, affiliated with the University of Toronto.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
1 July each year
Positions
1 per year
Accreditation
AAFPRS Educational and Research Foundation fellowship (ABFPRS examination pathway)
Department
Division of Facial Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed an ACGME-accredited residency in otolaryngology or plastic surgery, or board-certified by ABOto or ABPS
International applicants
AAFPRS directory marks this programme as accepting Canadian applicants; not marked as accepting IMGs
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Educational licence supplied by the University of Toronto

How to apply

Deadline
1 February (AAFPRS application database)
Application method
AAFPRS Foundation Fellowship Database (AAFPRS membership required; $225 fee covers 10 programmes) by 1 February; interviews by 31 May; matched via SF Match in early June

Funding

Funding
$80,000
