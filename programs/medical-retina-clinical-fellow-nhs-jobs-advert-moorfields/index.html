Overview

Twelve-month medical retina fellowship built from three rotations chosen by the fellow from six subspecialty-biased timetables in the Moorfields Medical Retina Service.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
MRCOphth or equivalent; completed higher surgical training (or overseas equivalent) and working in ophthalmology for last 12 months; independent laser and intravitreal injection experience
International applicants
Yes - applicants from outside UK with equivalent training; Dual Sponsorship Scheme and self-funded applicants welcome; IELTS required for doctors from abroad
Visa support
Dual Sponsorship Scheme (RCOphth) encouraged
Licence requirement
Full GMC registration with licence to practise (or eligibility)
