Overview

The CU Anschutz PAG Fellowship, affiliated with NASPAG, trains pediatric and adolescent gynecologists who are outstanding clinicians, surgeons, teachers and researchers.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
2 years
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
1 per year
Accreditation
NASPAG-affiliated (15th recognized program in North America)
Department
Department of Obstetrics and Gynecology, University of Colorado Anschutz School of Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Graduated from an ACGME-accredited OB/GYN residency
International applicants
Institution policyGraduates of non-US/Canadian schools eligible with valid ECFMG certificate (or full Colorado licence); must hold or obtain legal work authorization; USMLE Step 3 required before starting a fellowship
Visa support
Institution policyClinical J-1 via ECFMG is the standard route; H-1B for residents/fellows only if CU GME agrees to waive its J-1 requirement
Licence requirement
Institution policyColorado Medical Board physician training licence (or full Colorado licence) required
