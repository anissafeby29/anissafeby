Overview

Cooper University Hospital's Camden programme appears in the Society of Gynecologic Oncology's national participating-program list for advanced multidisciplinary training in gynaecologic cancer care.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years (second year is 12 months of research)
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
1 fellow per year
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited
Department
Division of Gynecologic Oncology, Department of Obstetrics and Gynecology, Cooper University Hospital

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Successful completion of a four-year Obstetrics and Gynecology residency
International applicants
Accepted applicants must be a US citizen, resident alien, or hold a J-1 visa
Visa support
J-1 visa only (no other visa types stated)
Licence requirement
Institution policyNew Jersey medical permit is a state requirement for GME trainees at PGY-2 and above (or NJ medical licence where required); fees reimbursed

How to apply

Deadline
6 April 2026 (published deadline for the current ERAS cycle; next cycle not yet posted)
Application method
ERAS only (3 letters incl. one from training director; SGO SLOEs accepted; personal statement; CREOG scores emailed to programme)
