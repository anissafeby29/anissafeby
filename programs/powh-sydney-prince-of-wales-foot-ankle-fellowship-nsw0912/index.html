Overview

Foot and ankle fellowship across Prince of Wales public and private, Sydney Children's and other Sydney hospitals.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
12 months for international fellows; 6 or 12 months for Australian/NZ fellows
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
AOA (Australian Orthopaedic Association) accredited fellowship
Department
Not stated on official page

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed orthopaedic training in country of origin; eligible for registration with the NSW Medical Board (Australia); fluent English with IELTS 7 in all modules
International applicants
Yes - international fellows (12 months); IELTS 7 in all modules or English-medium schooling
Visa support
Occupational Training Visa required (arranged after appointment)
Licence requirement
Eligible for registration with the NSW Medical Board

How to apply

Deadline
Applications accepted 24 months in advance
Application method
Email CV, three recent referees and a short paragraph on fellowship goals to Dr Jeff Ling; applications accepted 24 months in advance

Funding

Funding
AU$90,000 p.a. plus assist fees
