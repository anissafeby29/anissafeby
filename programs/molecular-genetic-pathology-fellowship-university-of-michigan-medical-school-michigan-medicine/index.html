Overview

A one-year MGP fellowship spanning molecular oncology, infectious disease, genetics, tissue typing, pharmacogenomics and cytogenetics in labs performing about 70,000 molecular tests a year.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Board-certified or eligible in AP and/or CP or medical genetics
International applicants
Non-US/Canadian graduates must hold a valid ECFMG certificate and be eligible for a Michigan licence
Visa support
Applicants must be US citizens, permanent residents or J-1 visa holders
Licence requirement
Eligible for State of Michigan professional licence
