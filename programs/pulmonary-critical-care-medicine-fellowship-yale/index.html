Overview

A three-year PCCM fellowship with at least 18 months of clinical training and 18 months of scholarly activity, supported by an NIH T32 grant.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years (36 months; option to extend for research)
Start
Institution policyYale-New Haven Hospital resident/fellow appointments run for one year beginning 1 July and ending 30 June (sample appointment agreement)
