Pediatric Radiology Fellowship

McGill University Health Centre (McGill University)

RadiologyPaediatric radiologyClinical Fellowship

Overview

A pediatric radiology fellowship giving increasing responsibility across ultrasound, GI/GU, MSK, chest, neuroradiology and basic interventional imaging in children.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 or 2 years
Start
Institution policyMcGill PGME academic year starts 1 July and ends 30 June
Positions
Not stated on official page

Not stated on official page
Department
Department of Diagnostic Radiology, McGill University

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed an accredited Diagnostic Radiology training programme by the fellowship start date and hold a specialist certificate from country of origin.
International applicants
Eligibility is based on a specialist certificate from the candidate's country of origin; no further international-specific conditions stated.
Visa support
Institution policyNon-Canadian fellows need an LMIA-exempt employer-specific work permit (code C45), initiated by McGill PGME after the CMQ Letter of Eligibility; no CAQ/study permit
Licence requirement
Institution policyCollège des médecins du Québec Letter of Eligibility and training card required (source verification via physiciansapply.ca)

How to apply

Deadline
Official application page states: 'Application process for 2026-2027 is now open up until September 1, 2024' (confirm current cycle with the department).
Application method
McGill on-line application form; maximum of 2 fellowships applied for simultaneously.

Funding

Funding
Institution policyUnfunded and self-funded training not permitted; proof of non-profit sponsorship of at least the R-1 salary equivalent (~CAD 60,000/yr) plus tuition/student fees required unless programme offers internal funding
