Visa support
Institution policyUAMS ACGME programmes sponsor J-1 physician visas (ECFMG) for IMGs outside the US; H-1B considered case by case only for applicants already in the US in (or eligible to change to) H-1B status - no new consular H-1B petitions
Licence requirement
Institution policytrainees in Arkansas residency/fellowship programmes do not need an Arkansas licence while working within the training programme (sports medicine fellows do require one)
