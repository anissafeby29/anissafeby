Chest and Body Fellowship

University of Texas Medical Branch (UTMB)

Destination

Overview

A one-year body imaging fellowship from plain films and fluoroscopy to CT, MRI and PET/CT that qualifies for the ABR alternate pathway; foreign medical graduates are encouraged to apply.

The programme

Focus
Cross-sectional imaging

1 year

Not stated on official page

Not stated on official page
Department

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Board-certified or board-qualified in Diagnostic Radiology; eligible for Texas licence.
International applicants
Foreign medical graduates with radiology training outside the US are encouraged to apply; qualifies for the ABR alternate pathway.
Visa support
J-1 sponsorship available; UTMB supports J-1 or H-1B if needed.
Licence requirement
Must be eligible to obtain a Texas medical license.

How to apply

Deadline
Applications accepted from 1 July; deadline 15 August; interviews early September.
Application method
Application with CV, personal statement and three letters by mail, fax or email to the programme.

Funding

Funding
Competitive salary and benefits.
