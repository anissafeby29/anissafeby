Overview

AO-funded short-term craniomaxillofacial surgery fellowship placement at Charité- Universitätsmedizin Berlin (Berlin), a department performing about 1155 surgical cases a year.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Graduate of a training programme in a CMF specialty; completed an AO CMF MFT or other hands-on AO CMF course; at least two years' AO membership; under 40 in year of application (exceptions possible); AO Access 'Breaking Biases' pathway completed
International applicants
Yes – AO global programme; fellows from any country are placed by AO regional fellowship coordinators (membership and course prerequisites apply)
Visa support
Not stated on official page

Scrub-in: No; additional licence/requirement to scrub in: none additional stated. Dominant OR language: German (team English level (5)/5).
