Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Board-certified anaesthesiologist (home country); Belgian medical registration; Dutch or B2-level English; valid BLS (can be obtained on site); at least 2 reference letters
International applicants
Accepted; non-Belgian applicants must obtain Belgian medical registration (and any work visa) at their own expense before patient care
Visa support
Not provided; any working visa must be obtained by the fellow at own expense
Licence requirement
Board certification in anaesthesiology (home country) plus Belgian national medical registration, obtained at own expense before patient care

How to apply

Deadline
Not stated on official page
Application method
Email scanned CV, MD diploma, anaesthesiology certification, national registration (non-Belgian applicants), B2 English certificate if non-Dutch speaking and at least 2 recent reference letters to stefaan.bouchez@azorg.be and koen.de.decker@azorg.be
