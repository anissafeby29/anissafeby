Overview

UCNS-accredited one- or two-year fellowship at the University of Utah training fellows in autonomic testing, autonomic neurology and complex dysautonomia care, with clinical, research or combined tracks.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year, with an optional second year (clinical, research or combined tracks)
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
UCNS-accredited Autonomic Disorders fellowship (listed in the UCNS Fellowship Directory)
Department
Department of Neurology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Graduate of an ACGME-, RCPSC- or CanERA-accredited residency in adult neurology, child neurology, cardiology or internal medicine; must be a US citizen or permanent resident
International applicants
Not open to non-US citizens/non-permanent residents: page states applicants must be US citizens or permanent residents (ECFMG certificate requested if applicable)
Visa support
No visa route: US citizenship or permanent residency required
Licence requirement
Not stated on official page

How to apply

Deadline
Applications open 1 March of PGY-3 year; deadline 1 October of PGY-4 (final residency) year; offers made on a rolling basis 31 October-31 December
Application method
Contact the programme (neuro.fellowship@hsc.utah.edu) to apply; CV, personal statement, three letters of recommendation, USMLE/COMLEX scores and ECFMG certificate if applicable
