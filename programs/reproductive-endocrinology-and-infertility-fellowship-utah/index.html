Overview

The University of Utah's three-year ACGME-accredited REI fellowship trains clinical experts in reproductive endocrinology and infertility for academic medicine, with a fully research-dedicated second year.

The programme

Training type

Duration

Institution policyUniversity of Utah GME academic year begins 1 July

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Institution policygraduate of LCME/AOA-accredited school or ECFMG-certified IMG; PGY2+ must have passed USMLE Steps 1, 2CK (2CS) and 3 (or COMLEX/LMCC) and be eligible for a Utah medical licence by hire date
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs may apply on the same basis as US/Canadian graduates but need ECFMG certification; non-US citizens/non-permanent residents need an institutionally sponsored visa
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 clinical visa (ECFMG-sponsored) for non-US-citizen trainees providing patient care; H-1 visas not accepted for residencies or fellowships (GME IMG eligibility policy)
Licence requirement
Institution policyPGY2+ trainees must have passed USMLE Step 3 and be eligible for a Utah medical licence by the contracted hire date
