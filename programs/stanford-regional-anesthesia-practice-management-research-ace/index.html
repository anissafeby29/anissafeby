Overview

An ACE for anesthesiologists seeking scholarship and management skills to build, direct and innovate regional anesthesia programmes, with an individualised curriculum in practice management, leadership, teaching and research.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed an ACGME-accredited (or equivalent) anesthesiology residency with a solid foundation in regional anesthesia
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs welcome to apply; must hold a valid ECFMG certificate and be able to obtain Medical Board of California licensure; non-ACGME fellowships need ACGME Non-Standard Training recognition before J-1 sponsorship
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 (ECFMG) is the primary visa; H-1B permitted only in some circumstances with GME approval; O-1 rarely, varies by department
Licence requirement
Proof of medical licensure required with application
