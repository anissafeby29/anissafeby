Pediatric Emergency Medicine Subspecialty Program

Children's Hospital of Eastern Ontario (CHEO) / University of Ottawa

Overview

Two-year RCPSC-accredited PEM programme based at CHEO, a level-one paediatric trauma centre with about 70,000 annual emergency visits, with trauma and toxicology rotations in Toronto.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
2 years

Not stated on official page

Accreditation
Fully accredited by the Royal College of Physicians and Surgeons of Canada; graduates eligible for the PEM subspecialty examination
Department
Division of Emergency Medicine, Department of Pediatrics

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed three years of core training in pediatrics or adult emergency medicine
International applicants
Institution policyYes - IMGs eligible for listed fellowship programmes; must be certified (RCPSC, CFPC or recognised international board) or residency-complete, meet English proficiency requirements and complete PEAP
Visa support
Institution policyinternational trainees need an IRCC work permit (LMIA-exempt); the uOttawa PGME/immigration advisor provides the documents needed to apply (CPSO letter of eligibility and LMIA-exemption number)
Licence requirement
Institution policyCPSO postgraduate education (educational) certificate of registration required, renewed annually; internationally trained clinical fellows must complete a 4-12 week Pre-Entry Assessment Program (PEAP); three-year licensure limit for clinical fellowships

How to apply

Deadline
Per CaRMS subspecialty match (applications due the year preceding entry)
Application method
CaRMS subspecialty match

Funding

Funding
Institution policyInternational fellows without Canadian status must be funded by an official home-country sponsor (government agency or health-related charity; private funds not accepted) unless fellowship funding is available through an Ottawa hospital
