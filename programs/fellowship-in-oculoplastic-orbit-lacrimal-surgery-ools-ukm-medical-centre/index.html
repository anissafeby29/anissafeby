Overview

UKM fellowship in oculoplastic, orbital and lacrimal surgery with graded surgical responsibility and supervisor evaluations.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
12, 24 or 36 months (1 year for international candidates)
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
1 per year
Accreditation
UKM certificate of completion (Course Director and Dean); UKM Fellowship Exit Examination
Department
Department of Ophthalmology, Faculty of Medicine UKM - Hospital Canselor Tuanku Muhriz (HCTM)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Recognised medical degree and postgraduate ophthalmology qualification registrable with MMC; at least 1 year active clinical practice post specialist qualification; 2 referees; list of courses attended; letter of offer/financial warranty from employer
International applicants
Yes - separate 1-year track for international candidates trained exclusively at UKMMC; tele-interview option for international candidates; HCTM credentialing flowchart for international candidates
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Postgraduate ophthalmology qualification registrable with the Malaysian Medical Council (MMC); evaluation by UKM and MMC
