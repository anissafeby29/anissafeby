Clinical Paediatric Orthopaedics Fellowship

Overview

Six-month clinical fellowship in paediatric orthopaedics with full hospital duties and research participation.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
6 months
Start
Two intakes: 1 January and 1 July
Positions
2 six-month fellowships per year
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Division of Paediatric Orthopaedics

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Medical degree recognised by the Medical Council of Hong Kong; completion of specialist orthopaedic training or equivalent
International applicants
Yes - the division welcomes overseas doctors as fellows
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Offer contingent on limited registration being granted by the Medical Council of Hong Kong

How to apply

Deadline
One year before intended start (e.g. 31 December for the January intake of the following year)
Application method
CV, covering letter with preferred timing and two reference letters to ngmym@ha.org.hk

Funding

Funding
HK$20,000 per month honorarium depending on experience
