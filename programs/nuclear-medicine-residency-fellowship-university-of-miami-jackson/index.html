Overview

An ACGME nuclear medicine programme offering a one-year fellowship for radiology graduates plus two- and three-year residency tracks.

The programme

Focus
Nuclear medicine / Molecular imaging
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (fellowship); 2–3 years (residency tracks)
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
4 per year
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited
Department
Department of Radiology, University of Miami Miller School of Medicine (Jackson Health System)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
One-year fellowship: completed ACGME-accredited radiology residency; USMLE Steps 1 and 2 passed.
International applicants
Foreign medical graduates are eligible with ECFMG certification; Jackson Memorial Hospital sponsors J-1 visas (ECFMG) for qualified applicants.
Visa support
J-1 (ECFMG-sponsored) for qualified applicants.
Licence requirement
Not stated on official page

How to apply

Deadline
Applications accepted 1 July–31 January; interviews year-round.
Application method
ERAS or email to Program Coordinator.

Funding

Funding
Funded by the Department of Diagnostic Radiology, University of Miami.
