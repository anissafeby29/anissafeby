Overview

The aim of the programme is to equip the Fellow with standard techniques of renal transplantation and safe practices in laparoscopic donor nephrectomy.

The programme

Focus
Renal transplantation and laparoscopy
Training type
Clinical Fellowship (attachment for foreign doctors)
Duration
6-12 months depending on: - Candidate’s existing experience and clinical skills in transplantation/laparoscopy - A 12-month program will provide more ...
Start
Subject to SMC registration and work pass approval (clinical fellowship processing takes approx. 6-9 months after provisional offer)
Positions
1
Accreditation
Certificate of Training
Department
Surgery & Surgical Oncology/ Urology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Practicing Urologists with an interest in gaining experience in renal transplantation and establishing or expanding the transplant service in their institution.; Department requirement: Postgraduate qualification in Urology (FRCS Urology or equivalent) ; Minimum 2 years of postgraduate training experience in relevant specialty ; Has at least basic laparoscopic skills ; Preferably has some experience with renal transplantation
International applicants
Yes - SGH fellowship and attachment programmes are offered to foreign doctors for short-term clinical training (more than 1000 international doctors since 2000)
Visa support
Training Employment Pass application with Ministry of Manpower (MOM) upon successful SMC Temporary Registration
Licence requirement
Temporary Registration (for training) with the Singapore Medical Council

How to apply

Deadline
Rolling - applications reviewed monthly; cut-off is the last day of each month (Singapore time)
Application method
Apply through SGH Postgraduate Medical Institute (PGMI) with CV/posting history, case summary and two endorsed recommendation letters (one from Head of Institution/Department); online interview for clinical fellowship; queries pgmi.fellowships@sgh.com.sg

Funding

Funding
Training Fee: S$3,000 (before prevailing GST) per month Programme Funding source: Self-funded
