Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
English and French required; plus McGill PGME requirements (specialist recognition, FAIMER-listed school, proof of funding)
International applicants
Open to physicians recognized as specialists in Canada or abroad (McGill PGME admissions)
Visa support
Institution policyNon-Canadian fellows need an LMIA-exempt employer-specific work permit (code C45), initiated by McGill PGME after the CMQ Letter of Eligibility; no CAQ/study permit
Licence requirement
Must be eligible for a training card from the Collège des Médecins du Québec

How to apply

Deadline
2027-2028 cycle: applications opened May 15, 2026; deadline September 1, 2026
Application method
McGill PGME online application (non-refundable fee $143.82); up to two programs per fee

Funding

Funding
Candidates must secure funding from a non-profit source (minimum equivalent of R-1 salary, approx. $60,000/yr, plus tuition and fees); self-funding refused (McGill PGME admissions)
