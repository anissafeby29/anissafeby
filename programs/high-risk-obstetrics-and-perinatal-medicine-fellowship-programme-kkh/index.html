High Risk Obstetrics and Perinatal Medicine Fellowship Programme

Overview

Training in high-risk obstetric clinics, delivery suite management and perinatal medicine with a project leading to publication.

The programme

Focus
Maternal-fetal medicine (high-risk obstetrics)
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
6 months
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Two positions per year
Accreditation
Perinatal Medicine and High Risk Obstetrics Certification
Department
Department of Maternal Fetal Medicine (Peripartum Unit)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
English proficiency (TOEFL paper-based 600); M Med (O&G), MRCOG, MRANZCOG or ACOG Board equivalent; recognised O&G specialist; accreditation by MOH, SMC and KKH; currently practising in country of origin
International applicants
Yes - for foreign/local doctors interested in perinatal medicine and high-risk pregnancies
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Temporary Registration for training with the Singapore Medical Council (SMC)

How to apply

Deadline
Not stated on official page
Application method
Write officially with detailed CV to the Head, Peripartum Unit, Department of Maternal Fetal Medicine, KKH

Funding

Funding
Fees determined upon application; all funding at applicant's expense
