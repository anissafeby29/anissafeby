Orthopaedic Foot and Ankle Fellowship

McGill University / Jewish General Hospital

OrthopaedicsFoot & ankleClinical Fellowship
Destination
CAN
Montreal, Quebec, Canada
Open official programme page ↗

Overview

One-year clinical fellowship at the Jewish General Hospital covering adult foot and ankle sports medicine, trauma and reconstruction under two fellowship-trained surgeons.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Institution policyCompleted residency plus Royal College or Board specialty certification; must be eligible for a CMQ training card (European Board certificate not accepted)
International applicants
Institution policyYes - foreign citizens may apply (max 3 years of training in Quebec); must be recognized specialists with proof of non-profit funding
Visa support
Institution policyNon-Canadian fellows need an LMIA-exempt employer-specific work permit (code C45), initiated by McGill PGME after the CMQ Letter of Eligibility; no CAQ/study permit
Licence requirement
Institution policyCollège des médecins du Québec Letter of Eligibility and training card required (source verification via physiciansapply.ca)

Funding

Funding
Institution policyUnfunded and self-funded training not permitted; proof of non-profit sponsorship of at least the R-1 salary equivalent (~CAD 60,000/yr) plus tuition/student fees required unless programme offers internal funding
