Overview

A one-year non-invasive vascular medicine fellowship drawing on cardiology, cardiac surgery, vascular surgery and interventional radiology at the Frankel CVC.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
Not stated on official page

Accreditation
Non-invasive fellowship preparing for American Board of Vascular Medicine and RPVI examinations
Department
Division of Cardiovascular Medicine, Department of Internal Medicine, Frankel Cardiovascular Center

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Board-eligible/certified in internal medicine and/or cardiovascular medicine
International applicants
Not eligible if requiring any visa
Visa support
No — applicants with any type of visa cannot be considered
Licence requirement
Institution policyState of Michigan medical licence and controlled substance licence (Educational Limited or Permanent) required; permanent-licence holders also need a personal DEA registration

How to apply

Deadline
2027–2028 applications reviewed on a rolling basis from August 2026
Application method
Email CV, personal statement and three letters of recommendation to the programme administrator; Zoom interviews

Funding

Funding
Salaried; salary commensurate with a House Officer at equivalent level of training (HOA contract)
