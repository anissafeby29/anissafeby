Overview

Chulalongkorn subspecialty residency in sleep medicine offering 1-year and 2-year tracks for academic year 2027.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship (Thai subspecialty residency, 'Phaet Prachamban To Yot')
Duration
1-year track (for internists already boarded in neurology or pulmonary/critical care) or 2-year track
Start
Academic year 2027
Positions
7 (4 on the 2-year track, 3 on the 1-year track) for AY2027
Accreditation
Medical Council of Thailand / Royal College of Physicians of Thailand subspecialty training (Diploma / Thai Board)
Department
Division of Sleep Medicine, Department of Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Thai medical licence; 1-year track: Thai board in internal medicine plus neurology or pulmonary & critical care (or final year/eligible); 2-year track: Thai board in internal medicine or neurology (or final year/eligible)
International applicants
Not stated on official page
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Thai medical licence (registration with the Medical Council of Thailand) required

How to apply

Deadline
Applications until 31 October 2026 (interview date to be announced)
Application method
Online application form via QR code on the announcement; interview; appeals to the Sleep Society of Thailand
