Overview

A one-year ACGME-accredited pediatric pain medicine fellowship based in the interdisciplinary pain team at Seattle Children's Hospital, covering acute, recurrent, chronic and cancer pain in infants, children and adolescents and leading to pain medicine sub-board eligibility.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of an ACGME-, RCPSC- or CFPC-accredited residency; open to fellowship-trained pediatric anesthesiologists, pediatricians, pediatric neurologists, family practice physicians and child psychiatrists
International applicants
Foreign national physicians who meet eligibility requirements are accepted (J-1 via ECFMG)
Visa support
J-1 (ECFMG) accepted; H-1B generally not sponsored unless the fellow is otherwise ineligible for J-1
Licence requirement
Must be eligible for a Washington State provider license
