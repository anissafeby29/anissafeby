Overview

Three-year neonatal-perinatal fellowship with rotations at Lurie Children's and Prentice Women's Hospital, training fellows as excellent clinicians and academic neonatologists.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
4 fellows each year
Accreditation
Accredited by the Residency Review Committee for Pediatrics
Department
Division of Neonatology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed ACGME-accredited pediatric residency; ABP board-certified or board-eligible; sufficient NICU experience (at least three neonatology rotations highly recommended)
International applicants
Graduates of schools outside the US and Canada need qualifying examinations (USMLE, FMGEMS, TOEFL, FLEX) and translated credentials; non-US citizens need a valid visa and work authorisation
Visa support
Non-US citizens need a valid visa and authorisation to work in the US
Licence requirement
Must meet eligibility requirements for a temporary Illinois medical license
