Overview

A one-year CAST-accredited fellowship in surgery for movement disorders, chronic pain, psychiatric disorders, neurostimulation, focused ultrasound and radiosurgery.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed or projected to complete neurosurgical residency at time of application; passed USMLE Steps 1, 2CK and 2CS
International applicants
International medical graduates must hold a currently valid ECFMG certificate
Visa support
Applicants requesting an H-1B visa must also have passed USMLE Step 3
Licence requirement
Institution policyPermanent or training medical licence/certificate from the state board where training (State Medical Board of Ohio training certificate for Ohio; Florida Board of Medicine for Florida) required before starting; some advanced/non-accredited fellowships require a permanent licence

How to apply

Deadline
Encouraged to apply by 1 September to be included in the autumn interview day
Application method
Fellowship application, CV and cover letter, letter from residency programme director, at least two letters of recommendation, USMLE/COMLEX scores and medical school diploma
