Overview

Duke offers a traditional one-year clinical neuroradiology fellowship and a two-year pathway combining mentored research with clinical training for future academic neuroradiologists.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
USMLE required.
International applicants
Non-US citizens require a J1 or H1-B visa; a student visa is unacceptable.
Visa support
J1 or H1-B visa required for non-US citizens.
Licence requirement
North Carolina Medical Board license (full or training) and Duke credentialing required before training.
