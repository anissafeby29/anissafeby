EACTS Travelling Leadership Fellowship for Women in Cardiothoracic Surgery

Erasmus MC Rotterdam, German Heart Centre Munich, Annunziata Hospital Calabria, University Hospital of Wales (EACTS Francis Fontan Fund)

Overview

Three one-week shadowing visits with women leaders in cardiothoracic surgery at three of four European centres, covering clinical, strategic and leadership activities.
