Overview

One-year clinical fellowship in a programme with over 3,500 outpatient cancer visits and more than 150 major ablative–free flap cases a year plus endocrine, salivary and transoral surgery.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Board eligible for the American Board of Otolaryngology–HNS, American Board of Surgery or American Board of Plastic Surgery (or Canadian equivalent); International Track applicants as per AHNS rules
International applicants
Accepted via the AHNS 'International Track': applicants who trained outside the US/Canada must have completed (or be completing) a non-ACGME/non-RCPSC residency in otolaryngology, general surgery or plastic surgery and hold ECFMG certification
Visa support
Institution policyUF-COM sponsors J-1 via ECFMG; new H-1B only in limited case-by-case circumstances for select specialties (requires department approval, USMLE Steps 1-3 and Florida licence)
Licence requirement
Institution policyMust meet Florida Board of Medicine licensure (training registration) requirements

How to apply

Deadline
15 March 2027 (AHNS match, July 2028 start)
Application method
AHNS central fellowship match (Advanced Training Council); for July 2028 start: applications 21 Dec 2026 – 15 Mar 2027, interviews Mar–Jun 2027, results 9 Jul 2027; AHNS membership (or pending application) required
