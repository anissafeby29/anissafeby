Overview

CareFlight's retrieval registrar programme offers helicopter, fixed-wing and international jet retrieval experience in NSW and the NT, with up to five weeks of initial medical and aviation training.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship (registrar post)
Duration
Rotational registrar terms (6-month college-recognised training)
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
ACEM: 6 months advanced training; ANZCA: 6 months; CICM on request; accredited training site for the Associateship in Prehospital and Retrieval Medicine
Department
CareFlight aeromedical operations (NSW Ambulance Toll Aeromedical Base, CareFlight Rapid Response Helicopter Westmead, Top End Medical Retrieval Service Darwin)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Minimum 5 years postgraduate; Australian trainees in final year of advanced training/provisional fellows/specialists in EM, anaesthesia or ICM; UK trainees completed ST4 (plus FRCA for anaesthesia); international senior registrars/consultants in critical care; EMST/ATLS; fitness assessment; HUET
International applicants
Yes – prospectus states CareFlight assists overseas applicants with visa, college accreditation and Australian medical registration
Visa support
CareFlight assists overseas registrars with visa requirements (per 2025 prospectus)
Licence requirement
Eligible for medical registration in Australia (AHPRA)
