Overview

A 12-month subspecialty programme in regional anesthesiology and acute pain medicine across UPMC hospitals, selecting up to 15 fellows a year.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of an ACGME/ACGME-I accredited anesthesiology residency, or a non-ACGME residency with certificate and summative evaluation letter
International applicants
Applicants from non-ACGME accredited anesthesiology residencies may apply with completion certificate and summative evaluation
Visa support
Institution policyUPMC ME programmes may (not required to) offer J-1 via ECFMG and/or H-1B; programme can offer either or both; H-1B requires USMLE Steps 1-3 passed by match date
Licence requirement
Institution policyPennsylvania graduate medical training licence required (within 60 days of contract start)

How to apply

Deadline
Applications accepted on a continual (rolling) basis
Application method
SF Match, or out-of-match via ASRA common application (rolling)
