Overview

Three-year training in paediatric gastroenterology, hepatology, nutrition and liver transplantation with clinical responsibility, scholarly research and medical education.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed paediatric residency and eligible for a California medical licence
International applicants
Limited: US citizen or green card required (NIH grant rules); J-1 holders accepted only if in a US/Canadian residency for 2 years; no H-1B
Visa support
J-1 transfer only (from US/Canadian residency); H-1B not accepted
Licence requirement
Must be eligible for a California medical licence (medical school recognised by Medical Board of California)
