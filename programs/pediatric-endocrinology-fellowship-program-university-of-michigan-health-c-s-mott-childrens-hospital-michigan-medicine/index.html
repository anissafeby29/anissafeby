Overview

Three-year pediatric endocrinology fellowship with inpatient and outpatient training, endocrine and diabetes continuity clinics and broad research opportunities at Mott Children's Hospital.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed three-year pediatric residency at an ACGME-accredited institution
International applicants
Programme can only accept US citizens or permanent residents because of funding sources; otherwise fellows must provide funding from other sources for the three years
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 sponsored by ECFMG (or permanent residence / qualifying EAD); other visas only in very limited circumstances, case by case at Program Director discretion with prospective GME Committee approval
Licence requirement
Institution policyState of Michigan medical licence and controlled substance licence (Educational Limited or Permanent) required; permanent-licence holders also need a personal DEA registration

Funding
Institution policyhouse officers paid on the UM/HOA scale; FY27 (from 1 July 2026) HO I $77,049, HO IV $86,670, HO V $90,137, HO VI $93,742, HO VII $97,492 up to HO IX $105,447, plus annual lump-sum savings payment and benefits
