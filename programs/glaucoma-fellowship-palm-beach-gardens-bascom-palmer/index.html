Overview

One-year advanced clinical glaucoma fellowship at Bascom Palmer Palm Beach Gardens with comprehensive medical, laser and surgical glaucoma training plus complex and refractive cataract surgery.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
Not stated on official page

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Eligibility for an unrestricted Florida medical licence; all USMLE steps; no visa requirements (US citizen or permanent resident)
International applicants
Only US citizens or permanent residents
Visa support
No - must not have any visa requirements
Licence requirement
Unrestricted Florida medical license required before starting; page states all 3 USMLE steps passed and 2 years of supervised US training are needed for a Florida licence
