Overview

A one-year ACPNF-accredited fellowship preparing fellows to be board-certified paediatric neurosurgeons, with 300+ operative cases per year.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Institution policygraduate of an accredited medical/osteopathic school and residency meeting ACGME requirements; ECFMG certification where applicable; eligible for Ohio training licence/medical licence; US work eligibility
International applicants
Institution policyYes - international graduates must hold ECFMG certification and obtain an appropriate visa and I-9 work eligibility before the start date

Not stated on official page
