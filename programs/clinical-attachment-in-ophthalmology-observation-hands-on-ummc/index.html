Clinical Attachment in Ophthalmology (Observation / Hands-on)

University Malaya Medical Centre (UMMC)

OphthalmologyMultiple / comprehensive ophthalmologyClinical attachment / observership
Destination
MYS
Kuala Lumpur, Federal Territory of Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia
Open official programme page ↗

Overview

UMMC clinical attachment (observation or limited hands-on) in glaucoma, paediatric ophthalmology & strabismus, medical retina & uveitis, cornea and vitreoretinal services.

The programme

Focus
Multiple ophthalmic subspecialties
Training type
Clinical attachment / observership
Duration
Observation up to 90 days; hands-on attachment 1-12 months
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
UMMC fellowship; can count toward Malaysian National Specialist Register subspecialty training
Department
Department of Ophthalmology, Faculty of Medicine, Universiti Malaya

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Master's/clinical master's recognised by MMC; MMC registration; PLIK pass; head of department support; C&P approval
International applicants
Yes - fee schedule for non-Malaysian citizens
Visa support
Applicants must be approved for a Professional Visit Pass (PLIK) by the Immigration Department of Malaysia (no sponsorship stated)
Licence requirement
Registration with the Malaysian Medical Council; approval by UMMC Credentialing & Privileging Committee

How to apply

Deadline
At least 2 months before start (observation)
Application method
Online via the UMMC Fellowship/Training Attachment Programme e-services portal; non-Malaysians apply at least 6 months before start

Funding

Funding
Non-Malaysian: observation RM2,500 (up to 2 weeks) or RM5,000 (15 days-4 weeks); hands-on RM36,000 per year pro-rated
