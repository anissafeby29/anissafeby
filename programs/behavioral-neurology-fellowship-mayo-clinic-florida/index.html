Behavioral Neurology Fellowship

Mayo Clinic — Florida

NeurologyBehavioural neurology & dementiaClinical Fellowship
Destination
USA
Jacksonville, Florida, United States
Open official programme page ↗

Overview

A one-year UCNS fellowship in Jacksonville offering clinical, educational and research training in neurodegenerative diseases and neurobehavioural syndromes.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (optional second research year)
Start
July
Positions
1 per year
Accreditation
UCNS-certified programme
Department
Department of Neurology, Mayo Clinic School of Graduate Medical Education

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
ACGME-accredited neurology or psychiatry residency; ABPN board eligible/certified; Florida licence or eligibility (all three USMLE steps passed in three attempts within seven years)
International applicants
Open to international applicants; graduates of medical schools outside the US/Canada must hold ECFMG certification before appointment
Visa support
Mayo Clinic supports ECFMG J-1 and H-1B sponsorship (J-1 requires an ACGME-accredited/recognised programme; no new H-1B petitions requiring the US$100,000 payment); final visa type at Mayo's discretion
Licence requirement
Florida medical licence or eligibility required

How to apply

Deadline
November 30, 18 months before start (rolling review)
Application method
Online application via Mayo's Recruit system

Funding

Funding
Salaried trainee position (stipend and benefits per Mayo Clinic School of GME)
