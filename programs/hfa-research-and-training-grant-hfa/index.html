HFA Research and Training Grant

Heart Failure Association (HFA/ESC) – HFA ICARe-HF Accredited Centres or other host centres

CardiologyHeart failure, transplant & cardiomyopathyResearch / Training Grant

Overview

HFA grant for young investigators and clinicians to undertake heart failure research or training for at least three months at a centre in another ESC member country.

The programme

Focus
Heart failure
Training type
Research / Training Grant
Duration
Minimum 3 months (funding up to 4 months)
Start
Exchange must start by May of the year after application
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
HFA (ESC); preference for ICARe-HF Accredited Centres
Department
Not stated on official page

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Under 40; HFA member or fellow; MD not yet in a permanent/senior/consultant post with demonstrated HF expertise, or PhD holder; project prepared with host centre
International applicants
Training must be outside own country in an ESC member country; citizenship or residence in a low/middle-income country is weighted (20%) in selection
Visa support
Grant validation subject to visa approval; fellow arranges visa and paperwork
Licence requirement
Not stated on official page

How to apply

Deadline
Application period 10 April – 30 September; results in December
Application method
Online application with project proposal prepared with the host centre

Funding

Funding
€2,500 per month, maximum €10,000
