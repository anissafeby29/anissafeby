Obstetrical Anesthesia Fellowship

Mount Sinai Hospital (Sinai Health)

AnaesthesiologyObstetric anaesthesiaClinical Fellowship
Destination
CAN
Toronto, Ontario, Canada
Open official programme page ↗Compare saved →

Overview

Advanced clinical training, teaching and research in obstetric anaesthesia at a tertiary centre delivering more than 7,000 women per year, approximately 50% high-risk.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 or 2 years
Start
July
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
University of Toronto PGME-registered fellowship; does not count towards Royal College of Physicians and Surgeons of Canada specialty certification
Department
Department of Anesthesia and Pain Management, Mount Sinai Hospital / University of Toronto

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Minimum 30 months of clinical anaesthesia training and all requisite anaesthesia certification exams passed in home country (University of Toronto departmental requirements)
International applicants
Yes — University of Toronto fellowships accept international graduates; English proficiency required where training was not in English
Visa support
Institution policyForeign nationals need an LMIA-exempt work permit; U of T PGME issues the IRCC offer-of-employment number and CPSO letter of eligibility about 5 months before start
Licence requirement
Eligibility for registration with University of Toronto PGME and a postgraduate education certificate of registration from the College of Physicians and Surgeons of Ontario

How to apply

Deadline
July 2027 intake closed 31 May 2026; July 2028 intake opens 1 January 2027 and closes 31 May 2027
Application method
Online application platform on the University of Toronto Department of Anesthesiology and Pain Medicine website

Funding

Funding
Institution policyFellowships funded by department, sponsoring agency or external source; PGME guidelines recommend at least PARO PGY1-equivalent remuneration and pay commensurate with a Canadian in the same role for visa trainees
