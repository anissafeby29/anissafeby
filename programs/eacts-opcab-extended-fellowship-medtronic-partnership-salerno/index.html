EACTS OPCAB Extended Fellowship (Medtronic partnership)

San Giovanni di Dio e Ruggi d'Aragona Hospital (EACTS Francis Fontan Fund)

Overview

Six-week fellowship under high-volume OPCABG surgeons (around 300 cases/year) focused on total arterial off-pump revascularisation.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
EACTS member; first five years of independent practice; partnership with anaesthesiologists and cardiologists at own centre; access to up-to-date coronary imaging; fluent English
International applicants
Open to EACTS members; the page sets no nationality or EU-residency restriction (EACTS membership is international). Fluent English required.
