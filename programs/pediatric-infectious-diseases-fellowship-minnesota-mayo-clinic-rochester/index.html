Overview

A three-year pediatric infectious diseases fellowship at Mayo Clinic Rochester recruiting two fellows every three years.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years
Start
1 July
Positions
2 positions every three years (1 position recruited for 2025-2026 cycle)
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited
Department
Department of Pediatric and Adolescent Medicine, Mayo Clinic

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Accredited general pediatrics or med-peds residency in the US or Canada; exceptional graduates of other programmes considered case by case
International applicants
Page contains a section for international medical graduates (exceptionally qualified IMGs considered); exceptional graduates of other training programmes considered case by case
Visa support
Institution policyMayo Clinic School of Graduate Medical Education supports ECFMG J-1 and H-1B visas (Mayo selects visa type). Since the Sept 2025 $100,000 H-1B fee, Mayo will not file new H-1B petitions that trigger the fee - H-1B only for change/extension of status (USMLE Steps 1-3 required); otherwise ECFMG J-1 (Steps 1-2, ACGME-accredited/recognised programme). F-1 OPT accepted for first year for US-school graduates
Licence requirement
Institution policyMinnesota-based MCSGME trainees must hold a Minnesota residency permit, and all eligible trainees must obtain and maintain a full Minnesota medical licence (USMLE/COMLEX/MCCQE final exam deadlines apply); Mayo reimburses fees

How to apply

Deadline
1 September of the year preceding appointment
Application method
ERAS; NRMP Pediatric Specialties Match; interviews August-October (virtual per APPD/CoPS recommendations)
