Overview

A one-year immersive fellowship across the breadth of hospital-based neurology practice in a multi-campus tertiary medical centre.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
July 1
Positions
1 per year
Accreditation
Accredited by the Neurohospitalist Society
Department
Department of Neurology, Mayo Clinic School of Graduate Medical Education

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of an accredited adult neurology residency in the United States or Canada
International applicants
Open to international applicants; graduates of medical schools outside the US/Canada must hold ECFMG certification before appointment
Visa support
Page notes IMGs on J-1 visas may complete only one year of fellowship; Mayo sponsors ECFMG J-1 and (limited) H-1B
Licence requirement
Fellows must obtain and maintain an appropriate medical licence while enrolled

How to apply

Deadline
Applications open December 1, 2025 for July 2027 start; due March 31, 2026; offers May 10, 2027
Application method
Online application via Mayo's Recruit system; interviews in person or virtual; direct appointment

Funding

Funding
Salaried trainee position (stipend and benefits per Mayo Clinic School of GME)
