Overview

Australia's largest paediatric gastroenterology fellowship program with liver and multivisceral transplantation, intestinal failure, IBD and 1500–2000 endoscopies per year.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
MBBS or equivalent; completed written and clinical FRACP (paediatrics) or equivalent
International applicants
International medical graduates must meet AHPRA English language requirements (IELTS 7 in all bands or equivalent) for medical registration
Visa support
Institution policyAll RCH employees need Australian citizenship or a visa with work rights; where RCH agrees to sponsor, it engages migration lawyers for a Temporary Skill Shortage (subclass 482) employer-sponsored visa (usually 2-4 years, dependants included); other routes include 417/482 working holiday, 189/190 skilled visas
Licence requirement
AHPRA (Medical Board of Australia) registration required
