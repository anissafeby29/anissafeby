Overview

Body imaging and non-vascular intervention fellowship at St Paul's Hospital that has trained fellows from all over the world.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (a second year possible with Fellowship Director approval; third year only under extenuating circumstances, subject to CPSBC approval)
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
3 fully funded positions (one January and two July starts); 1 sponsored or self-funded position
Accreditation
UBC clinical fellowship under CPSBC policy (not an RCPSC-accredited residency)
Department
UBC Department of Radiology, Faculty of Medicine — St Paul's Hospital

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Medical degree and specialty diploma in radiology, or completing an accredited diagnostic radiology residency in home country, to be eligible for licensure by the College of Physicians and Surgeons of British Columbia (CPSBC); MCCEE not required
International applicants
Yes — all qualified applicants encouraged to apply; Canadian citizens and permanent residents given priority
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Eligibility for licensure by the College of Physicians and Surgeons of British Columbia

How to apply

Deadline
September 30, 2026 (for 2028–2029)
Application method
UBC Radiology online Fellowship Application Form (Step-by-Step Application Guide); referees email letters to ubcrad.fellowship@ubc.ca; maximum 2 subspecialties per academic year (IR applicants may apply to IR only)

Funding

Funding
Funded and/or sponsored/self-funded positions as listed: 3 fully funded positions (one January and two July starts); 1 sponsored or self-funded position
