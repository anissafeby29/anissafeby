Overview

A one-year McGill clinical fellowship for haematology clinician-scientists directed by Dr Kelly Davison; candidates must secure funding.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
1-2
Accreditation
McGill PGME fellowship; does not lead to RCPSC/CFPC certification (McGill PGME admissions)
Department
Department of Medicine / PGME, Hematology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed residency with Royal College or Board certification; graduate of a FAIMER-listed medical school; recognized specialist in Canada or abroad; proof of non-profit funding; eligible for a CMQ training card
International applicants
International medical graduates (Canadian, US and foreign citizens) may apply for up to three years of training in Quebec; applicants sponsored under McGill contracts apply via their sponsoring agency (McGill PGME admissions)
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Must be deemed eligible for a training card from the Collège des Médecins du Québec

How to apply

Deadline
2027-2028 cycle: online application opens 15 May; deadline 1 September (McGill PGME application timelines)
Application method
McGill PGME online application form; non-refundable application fee $143.82

Funding

Funding
Candidates must hold non-profit funding of at least the R-1 salary equivalent (approx. $60,000/yr) plus tuition and student services fees; self-funding refused (McGill PGME admissions)
