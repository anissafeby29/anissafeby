Neonatal Neurology and Neurocritical Care Fellowship

Overview

A UCNS-accredited 1-year clinical fellowship in neonatal and fetal neurology and neonatal neurocritical care, running since 2000.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year clinical (extendable to 3 years with external research funding)
Start
Institution policyMost UCSF residents/fellows are appointed for June-August start dates (off-cycle starts possible)
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
UCNS-accredited (since 2023)
Department
Department of Pediatrics, UCSF School of Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Child neurology residency, or pediatrics residency plus neonatal-perinatal medicine fellowship
International applicants
Foreign medical graduates must hold an ECFMG certificate
Visa support
Institution policyUCSF sponsors J-1 (ECFMG) and H-1B (incl. OPT) visas; H-1B/OPT require pre-approval by UCSF OGME; non-ACGME fellowships can take J-1 trainees only if ACGME Non-Standard Training (NST) recognised
Licence requirement
All applicants must be eligible for a California state licence

How to apply

Deadline
Not stated on official page
Application method
Direct to programme: CV, 3 letters (incl. programme director), statement of interest; interview in person or video

Funding

Funding
Funding available every other year
