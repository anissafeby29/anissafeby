Overview

Competitive international fellowship scheme placing overseas paediatricians in the Oxford Newborn Care Unit or acute children's services at senior (ST level 2/3) standard for up to two years.

The programme

Training type

Duration
Maximum 24 months (3–6 months induction then 18–21 months at senior level)
