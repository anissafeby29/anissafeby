Overview

A clinician-educator fellowship scaffolding a Master's in medical/health professions education, run jointly with the Centre for Innovation in Medical Education.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
12 months (optional second year)
Start
July
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Institution policyUniversity of Ottawa clinical fellowship - does not lead to any Canadian (RCPSC/CFPC) certification
Department
Department of Emergency Medicine, University of Ottawa

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Emergency physicians (PGY5 ACE trainees or licensed locum staff); Master's-level medical education enrolment highly encouraged
International applicants
IMGs must first complete a year of Clinical Emergency Medicine fellowship and apply after arrival
Visa support
Institution policyinternational trainees need an IRCC work permit (LMIA-exempt); the uOttawa PGME/immigration advisor provides the documents needed to apply (CPSO letter of eligibility and LMIA-exemption number)
Licence requirement
Eligible for CPSO licensure in Ontario and CMPA liability coverage; international clinical fellows complete a 4–12 week Pre-Entry Assessment Program

How to apply

Deadline
September (aligned with CiMED first-round deadline)
Application method
Letter of interest, research statement, CV and 3 references via uOttawa fellowship form plus CiMED application

Funding

Funding
Institution policyinternational fellows without Canadian status must be funded by an official home-country sponsor (government agency or health-related charity; private funds not accepted) unless fellowship funding is available through an Ottawa hospital
