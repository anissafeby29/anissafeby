Overview

A one-year lung radiation oncology fellowship seeing over 1,000 new patients a year with prospective SBRT and stage III NSCLC databases for research.

The programme

Training type
Clinical & Research Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
January and July intakes (January 2028 intake currently open)
Positions
2–3 per year
Accreditation
University of Toronto PGME clinical fellowship
Department
Radiation Medicine Program; University of Toronto Department of Radiation Oncology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Holds, or expects to hold, radiation oncologist specialty certification in own country; recently completed radiation oncology specialty training
International applicants
Yes — programme open to radiation oncologists certified in their home country; fellows have come from Australia, Europe, Asia, South America, the USA and Canada
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Postgraduate educational licence from the College of Physicians and Surgeons of Ontario (via U of T PGME)

How to apply

Deadline
18 December 2026 for the January 2028 intake
Application method
Online via U of T PGME Postgraduate Applications System (PASS) with personal statement (programme type and sites of interest), CV and 3 confidential reference letters sent to radonc.fellowship@utoronto.ca

Funding

Funding
Funded by supervisors and hospital departments; fellows are remunerated; extra positions possible for candidates bringing grant/home-centre/government funding; personal self-funding not permitted
