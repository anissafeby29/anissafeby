Overview

A 12-month clinical fellowship for regional pathologists in general anatomical pathology at SGH, which processes over 45,000 specimens a year, covering grossing, diagnosis, ancillary tests and frozen sections.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Pathologists meeting SMC Temporary Registration requirements (basic medical degree from accredited school, national licensing exam passed where applicable, internship, registration and good standing at home)
International applicants
Yes — designed for foreign doctors; target audience includes pathologists from regional countries
Visa support
Training Employment Pass (MOM) application required
Licence requirement
Temporary Registration with the Singapore Medical Council (SMC); then Training Employment Pass (MOM)
