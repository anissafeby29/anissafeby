Overview

A 12-month fellowship based in Freeman Hospital's cardiothoracic theatres and cardiac intensive care unit, tailored for clinicians in cardiothoracic anaesthesia, general anaesthesia or critical care.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
GMC-recognised primary medical qualification; board certification/eligibility or completed residency; 3–5 years' specialty experience; IELTS 7.5 overall (7.0 each) or OET B; working towards FFICM/FRCA/MRCP or similar
International applicants
Yes — international clinical fellowship requiring support of an overseas employer, government or recognised sponsoring body
Visa support
Royal College sponsorship for GMC registration and Tier 5 Government Authorised Exchange visa
Licence requirement
Full GMC registration (or eligible within 3 months)
