Complex Radiation Oncology Fellowship

Overview

A one- to two-year non-standard clinical fellowship with 8–10 months of clinical service and dedicated research, focusing on techniques such as proton therapy, brachytherapy or SBRT, or specific disease sites.

The programme

Focus
Complex radiation oncology (proton, brachytherapy, SBRT, site-specific)
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1–2 years
Start
1 July
Positions
2–4 per year
Accreditation
Texas Medical Board Non-Standard Training (NST) programme
Department
Division of Radiation Oncology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed a radiotherapy residency of at least three years; at least one year of US clinical training preferred
International applicants
Yes — international graduates accepted (current fellows include radiation oncologists from Thailand and Lebanon)
Visa support
J-1 visa sponsored by ECFMG; valid ECFMG certificate required
Licence requirement
Texas Medical Board training permit

How to apply

Deadline
30 June for a start on 1 July of the following year
Application method
Contact the programme coordinator (FESmith@mdanderson.org) between April and end of June to start the pre-application process
