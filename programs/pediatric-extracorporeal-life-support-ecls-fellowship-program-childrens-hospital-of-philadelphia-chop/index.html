Pediatric Extracorporeal Life Support (ECLS) Fellowship Program

Children's Hospital of Philadelphia (CHOP)

PaediatricsCritical careClinical Fellowship
Destination
USA
Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, United States
Open official programme page ↗

Overview

One-year clinical fellowship mentoring board-certified/eligible pediatric intensivists and cardiac intensivists in the techniques, management and decision-making of extracorporeal life support.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Board eligible/certified in Pediatric Critical Care Medicine, or Pediatric Cardiology with an additional 1-year Pediatric Cardiac Critical Care fellowship; board certified in General Pediatrics; USMLE certification or equivalent; PALS and ACLS
International applicants
Institution policyCHOP common GME application requires ECFMG certification for graduates of medical schools outside the US, Canada or Puerto Rico (with USMLE/COMLEX score reports); applicants indicate whether they need J-1 (ECFMG) or H-1B sponsorship
Visa support
Applicants must have visa eligibility for training, if needed
