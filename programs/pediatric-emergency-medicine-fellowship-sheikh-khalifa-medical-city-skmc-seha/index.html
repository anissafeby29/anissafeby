Overview

DoH-approved paediatric emergency medicine fellowship at SKMC, home to Abu Dhabi's largest paediatric ED.

Department of Health – Abu Dhabi Medical Fellowship Program

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Valid primary specialty board certification (Tier 1 or 2); less than 5 years between residency completion and board certification; no practice gap over 3 years; age below 36 for international applicants (below 40 for UAE nationals) on 1 September
International applicants
Open to UAE nationals, UAE-trained graduates and eligible international graduates (international applicants must be under 36)
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Applicants must be licensed medical specialists: valid professional licence and Good Standing Certificate required with the DoH Abu Dhabi fellowship application

How to apply

Deadline
Applications annually 1–31 March (fellowship starts in January)
Application method
DoH Abu Dhabi e-portal (select training facility); employment contract signed directly with the training facility; no DoH application fee
