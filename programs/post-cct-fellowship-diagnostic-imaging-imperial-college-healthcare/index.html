Overview

Post-CCT fellowships providing advanced subspecialty diagnostic imaging training in paediatric, gynaecological, urological imaging or neuroradiology across Imperial's three West London sites.

The programme

Focus
Diagnostic imaging (paediatric, gynaecological, urological or neuroradiology)
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
Up to 12 months (fixed term, until August 2027)
Start
Two posts August 2026, one November 2026
Positions
3 posts (up to 1 per subspecialty: paediatric imaging, gynaecological imaging, urological imaging, neuroradiology)
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Department of Radiology (Hammersmith, Charing Cross and St Mary's hospitals)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
On GMC register/licensed medical practitioner; FRCR or equivalent; completed formal radiology training with pre-CCT subspecialty training in the chosen subspecialty.
International applicants
NHS Jobs advert: applications from job seekers who require Skilled Worker sponsorship are welcome and will be considered alongside all other applications.
Visa support
NHS Jobs advert: applications from job seekers who require Skilled Worker sponsorship are welcome and will be considered alongside all other applications.
Licence requirement
GMC registration with licence to practise.

How to apply

Deadline
Advert posted 27 March 2026.
Application method
Online application via NHS Jobs only.

Funding

Funding
Salary £44,170–£67,610 per annum depending on experience, plus London weighting and 1B banding (40%).
