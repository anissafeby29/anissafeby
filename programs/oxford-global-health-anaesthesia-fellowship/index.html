Overview

Oxford-based clinical work is combined with a supervised global-health project and collaboration with the Oxford Global Surgery Group.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Minimum 36 months clinical anaesthesia training and all specialty certification exams passed in own country (applying page); department overview states at least five years of anaesthesia training and practice; English proficiency evidence if training not in English; demonstrated interest in global health / safe anaesthesia for underserved populations
International applicants
Yes - overseas applicants via RCoA Medical Training Initiative (MTI) or Tier 2/Skilled Worker sponsorship
Visa support
MTI route: Tier 5 visa with Certificate of Sponsorship from the Academy of Medical Royal Colleges; or Tier 2/Skilled Worker CoS from OUH
Licence requirement
GMC registration required (sponsored by RCoA for MTI appointees)
