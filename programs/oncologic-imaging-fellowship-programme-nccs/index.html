Oncologic Imaging Fellowship Programme

National Cancer Centre Singapore (NCCS, SingHealth)

RadiologyOncologic imagingClinical Fellowship (attachment)
Destination
SGP

Overview

Clinical fellowship attachment strengthening experience in oncological radiology, neuro/head and neck, oncologic body and breast imaging including image-guided breast biopsies.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship (attachment)
Duration
6-12 months (clinical fellowship)

Not stated on official page

Not stated on official page

Not stated on official page
Department
Division of Oncologic Imaging

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed basic radiology training with a postgraduate degree; full FRCR (UK) or equivalent (FRANZCR, ABR); proficient in English (TOEFL may be needed for SMC if medical education not in English); knowledge of CT, MRI and US
International applicants
Yes - 'Non-Local Doctors Fellowships' offered by the NCCS Post Graduate Education Unit
Visa support
Training Employment Pass from Ministry of Manpower (MOM) required for clinical fellowship
Licence requirement
SMC Temporary Medical Registration plus malpractice indemnity cover (MPS)

How to apply

Deadline
At least 8 months before targeted commencement (clinical fellowship); at least 3 months before (hands-off observership)
Application method
Send CV specifying desired attachment period and division to pgeu@nccs.com.sg using the NCCS application form; no concurrent applications
