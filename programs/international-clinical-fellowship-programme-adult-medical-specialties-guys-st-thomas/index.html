Overview

A GMC-certified one- or two-year structured clinical fellowship for international doctors sponsored by their Ministry of Health, government, hospital or training body, available in all adult and paediatric specialties.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 or 2 years
Start
No fixed start date; applications considered year-round
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Certified by the UK General Medical Council (GMC sponsorship)
Department
Guy's and St Thomas' Education (all adult specialties)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Primary medical degree and internship; completed or near completion of 4/5-year national residency; GMC English standards; clinical practice 3 of last 5 years; certificate of good standing
International applicants
Designed for international doctors; must be sponsored by overseas employer, government or sponsoring body (self-funded not accepted)
Visa support
Programme assists with visa application and sponsors GMC registration
Licence requirement
GMC registration sponsored through the programme

How to apply

Deadline
Rolling
Application method
Email application with CV, passport copy and IELTS/OET results; video interview via Microsoft Teams

Funding

Funding
Sponsor-funded; non-refundable administration fee after conditional offer
