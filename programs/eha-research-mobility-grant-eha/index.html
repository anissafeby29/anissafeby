Overview

Supports young haematology researchers to visit a complementary research group in another country for up to three months to build collaboration.

The programme

Training type
Research Visit Fellowship (grant)
Duration
Up to 3 months (extension up to 6 months possible)
Start
As early as mid-March 2027
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Society research grant (not a specialist training programme)
Department
EHA Fellowships and Grants Committee

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
EHA member; PhD student, PhD within one year, or MD in training (within one year of haematology accreditation) with research interest; home and host institutes in different countries, at least one in Europe; lab-based research
