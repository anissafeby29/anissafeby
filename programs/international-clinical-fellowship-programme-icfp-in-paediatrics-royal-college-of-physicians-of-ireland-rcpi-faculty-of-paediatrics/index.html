Overview

RCPI Faculty of Paediatrics programme offering sponsored international doctors structured clinical training in leading Irish hospitals across paediatric specialties including PEM, neonatology, cardiology, endocrinology, gastroenterology, ID, respiratory and rheumatology.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
Varies by specialty: e.g. General Paediatrics and PEM up to 3 years; Endocrinology, Gastroenterology, Allergy & Immunology, Developmental Paediatrics 2 years; Infectious Diseases and Respiratory 12-24 months; Rheumatology up to 3 years

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Primary medical degree taught in English; structured internship; at least one year post-residency experience in chosen specialty; completed 4-5 year national residency programme with exams (e.g. Saudi or Arab Board); eligible for Irish Medical Council Supervised Division registration; IELTS Academic 7.0 overall (6.5 each) or OET B
International applicants
Yes - programme is for international doctors who are citizens of Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia or the UAE; must be government-sponsored (self-sponsored candidates not accepted); MRCPI/MRCP UK holders given priority
