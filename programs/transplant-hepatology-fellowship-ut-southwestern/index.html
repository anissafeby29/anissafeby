Overview

A one-year ACGME-accredited clinical liver transplant fellowship based at William P. Clements Jr. University Hospital, leading to transplant hepatology board eligibility and UNOS certification.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Gastroenterology board eligible.
International applicants
Institution policyYes - IMGs need ECFMG certification; non-US citizens train on ECFMG J-1 (or H-1B where sponsored)
Visa support
UT Southwestern accepts permanent residents or ECFMG-sponsored J-1 visas; does not sponsor H-1B.
Licence requirement
State requirement: Texas Medical Board Physician-in-Training (PIT) permit for participation in a Board-approved GME/fellowship programme (supervised practice only)
