Overview

Fellows assess transplant candidates and living donors and provide anaesthesia for kidney, pancreas, islet, intestinal, multivisceral and uterine transplants at the Oxford Transplant Centre.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Institution policyminimum 36 months training in clinical anaesthesia and passed all certification exams required for the specialty in own country
International applicants
Yes — international doctors may be appointed via the Medical Training Initiative (MTI) or a Skilled Worker (Tier 2) Certificate of Sponsorship
Visa support
MTI route: RCoA-sponsored GMC registration and Tier 5 visa via Academy of Medical Royal Colleges CoS; alternatively Skilled Worker (Tier 2) CoS assigned by OUH
Licence requirement
GMC registration with licence to practise required (sponsored by RCoA for MTI appointees)
