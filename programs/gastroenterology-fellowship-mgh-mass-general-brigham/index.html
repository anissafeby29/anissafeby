Overview

Provides a rigorous foundation in patient care, research and teaching for physicians committed to academic gastroenterology, with access to opportunities across the integrated Mass General Brigham division.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Enrolled in or completed an ACGME-accredited internal medicine residency; minimum 18 months clinical curriculum.
International applicants
Graduates of international medical schools (except Canadian) need 6-12 months of US-based hands-on clinical experience and three letters from US medical school faculty.
Visa support
J-1 and H-1B visa applicants considered on occasion
Licence requirement
Licence issued by the Medical Board of Massachusetts required

How to apply

Deadline
Third Wednesday in July (ERAS); interviews September-October
Application method
ERAS only; NRMP Match
