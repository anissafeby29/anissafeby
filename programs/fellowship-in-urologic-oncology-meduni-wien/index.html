Fellowship in Urologic Oncology

Medical University of Vienna / Vienna General Hospital (AKH) – Department of Urology

UrologyUrologic oncology & roboticsClinical and Research Fellowship
Destination
AUT
Vienna, Austria
Open official programme page ↗

Overview

One-to-two-year academic urologic oncology fellowship with laparoscopic, robotic and open surgical training, GU oncology rotations and a dedicated research year.

The programme

Focus
Urologic oncology
Training type
Clinical and Research Fellowship
Duration
1 to 2 years (one year dedicated to basic/clinical research)
Start
Rolling
Positions
Not stated on official page

Medical University of Vienna postgraduate training (International Affairs)

Department of Urology, Comprehensive Cancer Center

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Urologists who have completed or are completing a general urology residency
International applicants
Yes – applications reviewed year-round; MedUni Vienna fellowship rules apply (cooperation agreement with home institution, processing up to 9 months)
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Supervised involvement under an individual training plan; not equivalent to residency; minimum Master's degree

How to apply

Deadline
Rolling – apply about 12 months in advance
Application method
Send CV, exact fellowship dates and passport copy to the Chairman's office (Prof. S. F. Shariat) for pre-application

Funding

Funding
Not employment. Clinical fellowship tuition €29,000 per 12 months (may be waived on justification) plus €700 processing fee; home institution or grant must finance the fellow
