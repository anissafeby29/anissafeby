Overview

A one-year ACGME-accredited hand surgery fellowship run jointly with orthopaedics, covering adult and paediatric hand surgery, brachial plexus and microsurgery.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed residency in plastic surgery, orthopaedic surgery and/or general surgery
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs from non-LCME schools must hold ECFMG certification before entry; non-US citizens need employer-sponsored visa (J-1/H-1B per programme)
Visa support
Institution policyUPMC ME programmes may (not required to) offer J-1 via ECFMG and/or H-1B; programme can offer either or both; H-1B requires USMLE Steps 1-3 passed by match date
Licence requirement
Institution policyPennsylvania graduate medical training licence required (within 60 days of contract start)
