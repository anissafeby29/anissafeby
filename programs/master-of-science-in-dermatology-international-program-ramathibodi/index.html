Overview

Two-year international programme combining clinical dermatology (general and subspecialty clinics, dermatologic surgery, laser) with research, open to foreign medical graduates.

The programme

Focus

Training type
Postgraduate clinical training (2-year international MSc programme)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Medical degree from a recognised university with GPA 3.0 or above; completed one year of internship or general practice; no other medical obligations; English proficiency (TOEFL paper >550, IELTS >5.5 or MUTEP/CUTEP)
International applicants
Yes - international programme; foreign graduates apply via the Mahidol University Faculty of Graduate Studies
Visa support
Not stated on official page

Not stated on official page

How to apply

Deadline
Applications October to February; interviews in November and February
Application method
Online application through Mahidol Graduate Studies (separate links for Thai and foreign graduates)

Funding

Tuition approximately THB 500,000 for 2 years
