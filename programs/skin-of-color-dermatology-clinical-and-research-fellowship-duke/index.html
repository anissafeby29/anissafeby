Overview

A one-year paid fellowship training in health-disparities research and clinical care of skin-of-colour diseases, with hidradenitis suppurativa as the platform disease.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
MD or equivalent; at least one year of residency; USMLE Steps 1 and 2; eligible for NC trainee licence
International applicants
Applicants must be US citizen/permanent resident/refugee/asylee or hold a non-immigrant visa status authorising US employment
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 alien physician status sponsored by ECFMG with Duke's assistance; Duke is H-1B cap-exempt and H-1B is possible for clinical trainees who have passed USMLE Steps 1-3 and hold an ECFMG certificate (individual programmes decide which visas they accept)
Licence requirement
Eligible for North Carolina trainee licence
