Overview

Royal College of Physicians of Thailand-supervised subspecialty residency (fellowship) in pulmonary and critical care medicine at Maharat Nakhon Ratchasima Hospital, listed with 2 approved position(s) for training year 2027.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship (Thai subspecialty residency, 'Phaet Prachamban To Yot')
Duration
Not stated on official page
Start
Training year 2570 (2027); selected trainees must start training by 1 July 2027
Positions
2 (Medical Council-approved Plan A positions at this institution, training year 2570/2027 round 1)
Accreditation
Medical Council of Thailand-approved training institution; subspecialty training supervised by the Royal College of Physicians of Thailand
Department
Not stated on official page

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Category 3 subspecialty (อายุรศาสตร์โรคระบบการหายใจและภาวะวิกฤตโรคระบบการหายใจ): must hold a Thai medical licence, have completed the Medical Council internship (increased-skills) programme, and have completed training in / hold the Thai Diploma or Board in the prerequisite specialty (or be eligible for its board exam); final-year residents must be free of government service obligation or have employer approval to continue immediately
International applicants
Not stated on official page
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Thai medical licence (Medical Council of Thailand) required; licence must be held before training starts on 1 July 2027

How to apply

Deadline
Round 1: applications 1–31 October 2026 (Royal College of Physicians of Thailand / Medical Council TCGME system); Medical Council announces registered trainees by March 2027
Application method
Apply online via the Medical Council of Thailand TCGME system (tmc.or.th/tcgme), choosing the training institution; pay THB 1,400 fee; email application, documents and payment proof as one PDF to rcpt.fellow2570@gmail.com; other documents (references, CV) go directly to the chosen institution
