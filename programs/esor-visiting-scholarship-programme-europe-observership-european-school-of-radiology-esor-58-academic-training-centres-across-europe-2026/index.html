Overview

Three-month structured, modular visiting scholarship for senior radiology residents at a pre-selected academic training centre in Europe, supervised by a subspecialty tutor.

The programme

Focus
Diagnostic and interventional radiology (subspecialty fellowships)
Training type
Observership / visiting scholarship
Duration
3 months (consecutive, cannot be split or extended)
Start
Second half of the year; must be completed by end of the year
Positions
Limited (58 training centres enrolled in 2026)
Accreditation
ESOR programme; scholars observe clinical activities without direct patient care responsibility
Department
ESOR Visiting Scholarship Programme

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Radiology residents in 3rd, 4th or 5th year of training throughout the scholarship period; proof of ≥2 years of residency signed by Head of Department; fluent English; active ESR membership; not previously selected (Europe or USA programme)
International applicants
Open to ESR members (ESR membership is available to radiologists worldwide); candidates may only apply to training centres outside their own country of residence/work; Brazilian applicants must also upload Annual Progression Exam results
Visa support
Selected candidates must apply for their own work permits and insurance; training centres will provide assistance (long visa waiting times should be considered)
Licence requirement
Not required — observer status (no direct patient care)

How to apply

Deadline
March 23, 2026 (2026 cycle); training centres for 2027 not yet selected
Application method
MyESR portal → 'Visiting Scholarship Programme': choose topic (chest, CEUS, emergency, head & neck, IR, molecular imaging, MSK, neuro, oncologic, paediatric, urogenital) and 1st–3rd choice centres; single PDF incl. ESOR-format CV

Funding

Funding
€3,500 grant on completion (towards travel/accommodation); training free; scholar covers own expenses and health insurance
