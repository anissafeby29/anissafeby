Overview

State-of-the-art academic neuroradiology training across all subspecialty areas, with one- and two-year positions; second-year fellows subspecialise in spine intervention, head and neck, paediatric or vascular imaging.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 or 2 years
Start
1 July
Positions
8 (one- and two-year positions)
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited (first year)
Department
Department of Radiology and Imaging Sciences, University of Utah School of Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Must meet Utah medical licensure requirements and be a graduate of an LCME-accredited US/Canadian school, an AOA-accredited US osteopathic college, or a non-US school with a valid ECFMG certificate before appointment.
International applicants
IMGs with a valid ECFMG certificate are eligible to apply; visa policy not stated on this page.
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 clinical visa (ECFMG-sponsored) for non-US-citizen trainees providing patient care; H-1 visas not accepted for residencies or fellowships (GME IMG eligibility policy)
Licence requirement
Must meet Utah state medical licensure requirements.

How to apply

Deadline
Preferred deadline 10 December; virtual interviews mid-January to February
Application method
ASNR Neuroradiology Fellowship Match (NRMP)

Funding

Funding
Appointed as Visiting Instructor (typical); salary, conference/vacation time and benefits competitive.
