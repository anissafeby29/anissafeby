Overview

RCOG subspecialty training (SST) in Reproductive Medicine, one of four O&G subspecialty programmes completed in the final years of UK training to develop consultant-level skills.

The programme

Training type
Subspecialty Training Programme
Duration
Indicative 24 months; up to 36 months for a full-time trainee entering at ST5 with minimal relevant experience (competency-based)
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
RCOG Subspecialty Curriculum 2024 (leads to RCOG subspecialty accreditation; part of CCT in O&G)
Department
RCOG Subspecialty Committee (posts run by UK Deaneries/Trusts)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
UK NTN (or equivalent) with clinical training to ST4/ST5 and ARCP outcome 1; or UK CCT/Portfolio Pathway and on (or entitled to) the UK Specialist Register in O&G; or EEA/non-UK applicants listed on the UK Specialist Register in O&G
International applicants
Only non-UK applicants already on the UK Specialist Register in O&G are eligible; those requiring a work permit only if no other suitable candidates
Visa support
Non-UK/non-EEA nationals requiring a work permit are subject to the resident labour market test and considered only if no other suitable candidates; individual Deaneries decide immigration evidence required
Licence requirement
GMC registration with a licence to practise (UK NTN or UK Specialist Register in O&G)

How to apply

Deadline
Per individual post advert (usually advertised in the BMJ)
Application method
Apply directly to the Deaneries/Trusts as set out in adverts (usually in the BMJ); open competition with structured interview; register with the RCOG Subspecialty Committee on appointment
