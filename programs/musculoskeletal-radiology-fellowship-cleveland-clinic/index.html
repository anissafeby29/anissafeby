Musculoskeletal Radiology Fellowship

Cleveland Clinic

RadiologyMusculoskeletal imagingClinical Fellowship
Destination
USA
Cleveland, Ohio, United States
Open official programme page ↗

Overview

A one-year programme offering comprehensive diagnostic and interventional musculoskeletal imaging training in a section performing over 200,000 procedures annually, with strengths in sports medicine, orthopaedic oncology and rheumatology.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (1 July – 30 June)
Start
1 July
Positions
6 per year (2 ACGME-accredited, 4 clinical non-accredited)
Accreditation
2 ACGME-accredited positions plus 4 clinical non-accredited positions
Department
Imaging Institute, Cleveland Clinic (Section of Musculoskeletal Imaging)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Enrolled in or completed an ACGME-accredited US residency or RCPSC/CFPC-accredited Canadian residency.
International applicants
Exceptional international candidates enrolled in or having completed a diagnostic radiology residency will be considered.
Visa support
Sponsors applicants with J and H visas.
Licence requirement
Institution policyPermanent or training medical licence/certificate from the state board where training (State Medical Board of Ohio training certificate for Ohio; Florida Board of Medicine for Florida) required before starting; some advanced/non-accredited fellowships require a permanent licence
