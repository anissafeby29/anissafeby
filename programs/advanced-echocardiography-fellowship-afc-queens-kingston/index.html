Advanced Echocardiography Fellowship (AFC)

Queen's University / Kingston Health Sciences Centre

CardiologyCardiac imagingClinical Fellowship
Destination
CAN
Kingston, Ontario, Canada
Open official programme page ↗Compare saved →

Overview

A Royal College-accredited year achieving Level III echocardiography competence in a high-volume lab (1,000+ stress echos, 400–500 TEEs per year) including structural and intraoperative guidance.

The programme

Focus
Cardiac imaging (echocardiography)
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
12 months
Start
July (Block 1) or the following January (Block 8)
Positions
Not stated on official page

Eligibility and visa

Cardiology training; international applicants need specialist certification (or MCCQE Part 1 where applicable) and proof of English proficiency

Yes – self-funded/unfunded international applicants apply directly (MCCQE1 if applicable, specialist certification, English proficiency); applicants funded by listed sponsoring countries cannot apply directly

How to apply

Deadline
About 18 months before start (e.g., 1 January of the preceding year for a July start); accepting applications for 2028–2029
Application method
Submit application package to the division (CV, degree, transcripts, three references, personal statement, passport copy)

Funding
