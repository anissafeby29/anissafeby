Overview

Two-year surgical retina fellowship founded in 1977 with procedural volumes above the 80th percentile nationally.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
2 years
Start
July
Positions
1 two-year position per year (alternating 1 and 2 fellows from 2027)
Accreditation
Meets AUPO FCC compliance criteria
Department
Department of Ophthalmology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed accredited ophthalmology residency; ABO certification eligibility
International applicants
International applicants must have equivalent training and have passed the ECFMG examination
Visa support
Institution policyECFMG J-1 sponsorship and H-1B supported (final visa type chosen by Mayo); new H-1B petitions requiring the $100,000 payment not pursued - H-1B only for change/extension of status, USMLE Steps 1-3 and ECFMG certification required
Licence requirement
Institution policyMinnesota-based programmes: Minnesota residency permit required, and eligible trainees must obtain and maintain a full Minnesota medical licence

How to apply

Funding
