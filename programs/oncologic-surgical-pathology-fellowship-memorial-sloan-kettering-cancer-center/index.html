Overview

A one-year ACGME-accredited tumour pathology fellowship examining over 60,000 surgical specimens a year across breast, bone and soft tissue, GI, GU, gynaecologic, head and neck, neuro and thoracic pathology.

How to apply

Deadline
Rolling; early application encouraged (positions for 2026-27 and 2028-29)
Application method
Apply online (MSK Embark portal); then send three letters of recommendation, CV, copy of medical school diploma (translated if necessary) and ECFMG certificate for IMGs
