Overview

Six-month clinical fellowship in sports and arthroscopic surgery at HKU / Queen Mary Hospital for overseas orthopaedic surgeons.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
6 months
Start
Two intakes a year: 1 January and 1 July
Positions
2 six-month fellowships per year (one starting 1 January, one starting 1 July)
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Division of Sports and Arthroscopic Surgery

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Registered medical practitioner with full registration; completed orthopaedic training in home country; basic English
International applicants
Yes - the division welcomes overseas doctors as fellows
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Offer contingent on limited registration being granted by the Medical Council of Hong Kong

How to apply

Deadline
Decisions made one year in advance
Application method
Covering letter with requested timing, CV, first-author published paper, two recommendation letters, proof of registration and orthopaedic training to Ms Eva Liu (evatyliu@hku.hk)

Funding

Funding
HK$20,000 per month honorarium, depending on experience
