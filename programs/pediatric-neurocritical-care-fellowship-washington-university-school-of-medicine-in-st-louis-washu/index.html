Overview

One-year WashU pediatric neurocritical care fellowship with separate tracks for pediatric intensivists, neurologists and pediatric neurosurgeons.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (separate tracks for pediatric intensivists, neurologists and pediatric neurosurgeons; may be linked for two-year training)
Start
1 July
Positions
2 positions per year
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Department of Neurology, Division of Pediatric & Developmental Neurology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Board eligible in a pediatric subspecialty (neurology, critical care medicine, neurosurgery or emergency medicine); eligible for Missouri licensure
International applicants
Applicants with H-1 or J-1 visas considered if eligible for Missouri licensure
Visa support
H-1 or J-1 visa holders considered (per programme page)
Licence requirement
Must be eligible for licensure in Missouri

How to apply

Deadline
Rolling: applications accepted on a rolling basis; interviews in the fall before the July start
Application method
Send short personal statement, CV and 2 letters of reference to the programme coordinator
