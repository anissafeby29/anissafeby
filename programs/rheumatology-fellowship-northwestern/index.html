Rheumatology Fellowship

Northwestern University Feinberg School of Medicine (McGaw Medical Center)

Internal MedicineRheumatologyClinical Fellowship
Destination
USA

Open official programme page ↗

Overview

A two-year curriculum preparing fellows for ABIM rheumatology certification, with research or medical education emphasis in year two and an optional T32-supported research year.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
2 years (optional non-ACGME third research year)
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited (optional third research year is non-ACGME)
Department
Division of Rheumatology, Department of Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed ACGME-accredited Internal Medicine residency; ABIM exam eligible; US citizen/national or permanent resident (T32 support).
International applicants
US citizenship, non-citizen national or permanent residence required (NIH T32).
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 (ECFMG) and H-1B; McGaw sponsors H-1B only for candidates of exceptional academic potential with prior approval before ranking/offer (cost borne by the programme)
Licence requirement
Eligible for unrestricted Illinois medical license
