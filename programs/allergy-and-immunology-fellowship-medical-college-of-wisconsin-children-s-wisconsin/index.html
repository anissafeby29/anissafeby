Overview

A two-year allergy and immunology fellowship training future allergists and immunologists in a collaborative environment at MCW and Children's Wisconsin.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
2 years
Start
Institution policyMCWAH academic year begins 1 July
Positions
2 fellows per year
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited (reaccredited 2024)
Department
Department of Pediatrics, Medical College of Wisconsin

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
US-trained pediatric, internal medicine or medicine-pediatrics residents, and international residents
International applicants
Applications accepted from international residents (per official page); USMLE or ECFMG scores required
Visa support
Institution policyMCWAH accepts J-1 (ECFMG-sponsored); some programmes pursue H-1B (requires USMLE Step 3 passed) - check with programme; no permanent-residency sponsorship
Licence requirement
Institution policyMust maintain proper Wisconsin medical licensure per MCWAH licensure policies
