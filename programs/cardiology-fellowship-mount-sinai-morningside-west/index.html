Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai (Mount Sinai Morningside and Mount Sinai West)

Overview

A three-year programme with strong interventional/structural, critical care cardiology, EP, heart failure and imaging training across two Manhattan hospitals and a VA track.

The programme

Focus
General cardiovascular disease
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years
Start
Institution policyMount Sinai house staff appointments commence 1 July (house staff contract)
Positions
11 per year
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited
Department
Division of Cardiology, Mount Sinai Fuster Heart Hospital

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
ACGME eligibility qualifications; ERAS application and three letters
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs accepted with valid ECFMG certificate; may not begin training until an appropriate visa is obtained
Visa support
Institution policyMount Sinai Health System offers J-1 (ECFMG) and H-1B (categorical candidates in programmes of 2+ years, ECFMG-certified with USMLE Step 3 passed by Match) plus E-3 for Australians; programme director decides which is offered
Licence requirement
Institution policyResidents/fellows in ACGME-accredited programmes do not need a New York State licence (supervised practice exemption); non-ACGME fellows need a valid NY licence or limited permit

How to apply

Deadline
Interviews in September and October (ERAS cycle)
Application method
ERAS; NRMP (programme code 1413521251); participates in signalling

Funding

Funding
Institution policyACGME fellows paid by PGY level (Mount Sinai Hospital stipend effective January 2026: PGY4 $106,313, PGY5 $110,565); non-ACGME fellow pay set by programme funds
