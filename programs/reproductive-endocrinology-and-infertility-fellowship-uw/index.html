Overview

The Reproductive Endocrinology & Infertility Fellowship at the University of Washington is a three-year program accepting one applicant per year for a maximum of three fellows at any given time.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years
Start
Institution policyUW GME academic year runs 1 July - 30 June (standard start 1 July)
Positions
1 fellow per year (maximum 3)
Accreditation
ABOG certified; initial ACGME accreditation
Department
Department of Obstetrics & Gynecology, University of Washington School of Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Institution policyIMGs need valid ECFMG certification; applicants must be eligible for a Washington (or other applicable) state licence and for appropriate work authorisation/visa
International applicants
Applications from H1B and J1 visa holders cannot be accepted
Visa support
Applications from H1B and J1 visa holders cannot be accepted (due to the nature of fellowship funding)
Licence requirement
Institution policyactive Washington State provider licence required throughout training (limited/resident licence or full physician licence as specified by the programme)

How to apply

Deadline
1 May 2026, 12 PM CST (per SREI guidelines)
Application method
ERAS application with personal statement and three letters of recommendation (one from programme director)

Funding

Funding
Institution policyACGME residents/fellows (CIR NW members) paid per UW compensation schedule; AY27 (1 July 2026-30 June 2027) R4 $92,976, R5 $95,352, R6 $98,160, R7 $104,208 (R1 $87,708 to R9 $112,620), including an $11,000 allowance
