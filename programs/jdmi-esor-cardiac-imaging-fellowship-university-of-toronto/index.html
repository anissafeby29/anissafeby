Overview

A funded collaboration between JDMI and the European School of Radiology offering cardiac MR/CT and thoracic imaging training to late radiology trainees or recently board-certified radiologists.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
12 months (9 months cardiac, 3 months thoracic imaging)
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
1 funded position per year
Accreditation
JDMI / European School of Radiology (ESOR) fellowship
Department
Joint Department of Medical Imaging (UHN, Sinai Health, Women's College Hospital) with the European School of Radiology (ESOR)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Late radiology trainees or recently board-certified radiologists (within the first three years after board certification).
International applicants
Applicants apply through ESOR; open to late radiology trainees or radiologists within three years of board certification.
Visa support
Institution policyinternational clinical fellows need an IRCC work permit; the PGME Office issues an LMIA-exempt offer of employment number and supports the work permit application
Licence requirement
Institution policyclinical fellows must hold a CPSO certificate of registration authorising postgraduate education (educational licence); research fellows do not need CPSO registration

How to apply

Deadline
Applications open one year in advance and remain open until 30 June each year.
Application method
Apply through ESOR (European School of Radiology).

Funding

Funding
One funded position per year.
