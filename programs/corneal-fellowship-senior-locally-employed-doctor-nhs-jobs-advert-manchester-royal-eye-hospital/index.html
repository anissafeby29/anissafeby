Overview

12-month corneal fellowship with three supervised operating sessions, two specialist clinics and weekly refractive laser sessions per week, plus leading an acute corneal clinic.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Minimum four years postgraduate training (two in specialty programme); at least 400 independent phaco cataract operations in last 3 years; CCT or equivalent desirable
International applicants
Yes - GMC registration may be obtained via the Manchester International Fellowship Scheme
Visa support
Advert states applicants requiring Skilled Worker sponsorship are welcome and will be considered alongside all other applications
Licence requirement
Active GMC registration and licence to practise at appointment (via Manchester International Fellowship Scheme)
