Overview

Three-month research fellowship at a leading European training centre working on a chosen research project, with instruction in data acquisition, analysis, bias management and scientific writing.

The programme

Focus
Diagnostic and interventional radiology (subspecialty fellowships)
Training type
Research Fellowship
Duration
3 months
Start
First half of the year (January–June); must be completed by end of June 2027
Positions
45 research projects across 11 subspecialties available for 2027
Accreditation
ESOR programme supported by Bracco (2026–2028)
Department
ESOR / Bracco Fellowships

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Young radiologists, residents in their last years of training, or recently board-certified radiologists; aged 35 or younger at application; fluent English; Full Member or Member-in-Training of ESR
International applicants
Open to ESR members (ESR membership is available to radiologists worldwide); candidates may only apply to training centres outside their own country of residence/work
Visa support
Selected candidates apply for their own work permits and insurance and find their own accommodation; training centres may provide assistance
Licence requirement
Not stated on official page

How to apply

Deadline
7 September (for fellowships January–June 2027)
Application method
Choose a listed research project and apply via the MyESR portal as one PDF (max 8MB); one ESOR programme at a time

Funding

Funding
€3,500 grant on completion; training free; fellow covers own expenses and health insurance
