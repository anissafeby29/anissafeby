Overview

The largest and oldest urologic oncology fellowship in the world, established in 1951, providing rigorous clinical and research training.

The programme

Focus
Urologic oncology
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
Two years (one research year then one clinical year)
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Society of Urologic Oncology
Department
Urology Service, Department of Surgery

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed urology residency, board-eligible, passed USMLE (or equivalent) and hold a valid ECFMG certificate
International applicants
Candidates must be visa-eligible
Visa support
Institution policyMSK sponsors GME trainees as J-1 (ECFMG Alien Physician) or H-1B; H-1B only for applicants already in the US on H-1B in GME or in a status allowing in-country change/extension - MSK will not file H-1B petitions for trainees residing outside the US; O-1 rarely, needs GME review 6 months ahead
Licence requirement
Must obtain a New York State limited permit

How to apply

Deadline
See Society of Urologic Oncology for application deadline
Application method
Society of Urologic Oncology Fellowship Match (AUA-sponsored)

Funding

Funding
Salary and benefits by PGY level; one fellow per year awarded an endowed Rona Jaffe Foundation fellowship
