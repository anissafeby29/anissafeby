G. Raymond Chang Forensic Pathology Fellowship

University of Toronto – Laboratory Medicine and Pathobiology / Ontario Forensic Pathology Service

PathologyForensic pathologyClinical Fellowship
Destination

Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Overview

A one-year forensic pathology fellowship for anatomical pathologists from low- and middle-income countries, based on supervised medicolegal autopsies, to build forensic capacity at home.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
Normally July (selected fellows may wait 1–3 years)
Positions
1 per year
Accreditation
University of Toronto PGME clinical fellowship (not Royal College certification)
Department
Department of Laboratory Medicine and Pathobiology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
From a low- or middle-income country (preference for Jamaica, the Caribbean and Africa); MD/MBBS; recognised postgraduate certification in anatomical pathology; CPSO-eligible; fluent English; able to build forensic capacity at home
International applicants
Yes — designed exclusively for pathologists from low- and middle-income countries
Visa support
Work permit required for non-Canadians; Temerty PGME Office provides documents for the Canadian visa application (applicant pays processing fee)
Licence requirement
CPSO Certificate of Registration for Postgraduate Education (or independent licence) and CMPA malpractice cover

How to apply

Deadline
Online applications 1 January – 31 August each year (rolling)
Application method
Online form with letter of interest, CV, MD and specialty certificate copies (translated), letter of good standing if in training; three references emailed to lmp.pg@utoronto.ca

Funding

Funding
Financial support from the Raymond Chang Foundation for trainees whose countries cannot fund training in Canada
