Overview

A 2-year academic Complex Family Planning (Contraception, Abortion, Advocacy, Research and Education) fellowship with concurrent master's degree, offered every two years.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
2 years, with concurrent completion of a master's degree or equivalent
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Objectives aligned with Society of Family Planning (SFP) Complex Family Planning fellowships
Department
Department of Obstetrics and Gynaecology, Temerty Faculty of Medicine, University of Toronto

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Fulfilled FRCS(C) requirements and an independent medical licence from the province of Ontario; preference for those planning an academic career
International applicants
Exceptional candidates from outside Canada considered if they show potential and are self-funded
Visa support
Institution policyinternational clinical fellows need an IRCC work permit; the PGME Office issues an LMIA-exempt offer of employment number and supports the work permit application
Licence requirement
Certificate of registration with the College of Physicians and Surgeons of Ontario (CPSO); internationally trained physicians must complete the 4–12-week Pre-Entry Assessment Program (PEAP) before full acceptance (department application guidelines)

How to apply

Deadline
January 14, 2026 (application form to obgyn@utoronto.ca, subject "CAARE Fellowship"); program offered every two years
Application method
Fellowship Application Form (PDF) submitted to the program director; appointment processed through U of T PGME (OATS). Allow 5–6 months processing for international applicants.

Funding

Funding
Institution policyclinical fellows must be funded (department or sponsoring agency); FEAC recommends pay at least equal to PGY1 on the PARO salary scale, and foreign nationals must be paid at a rate commensurate with a Canadian in the same role
