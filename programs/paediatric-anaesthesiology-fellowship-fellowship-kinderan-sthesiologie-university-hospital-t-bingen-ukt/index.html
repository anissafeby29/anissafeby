Paediatric Anaesthesiology Fellowship (Fellowship Kinderanästhesiologie)

University Hospital Tübingen (UKT)

AnaesthesiologyPaediatric anaesthesiaClinical Fellowship
Destination
DEU
Tübingen, Baden-Württemberg, Germany
Open official programme page ↗

Overview

A structured, competency-based paediatric anaesthesia fellowship of at least 12 months covering premature infants, complex malformations, congenital heart disease, regional techniques and transplant/bypass surgery, ending in a UKT certificate.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
Minimum 12 months full-time equivalent (up to 3 months paediatric/neonatal ICU may be credited)
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
2 per year
Accreditation
Structured competency-based programme following the 'Arbeitsgemeinschaft Fellowship Kinderanästhesiologie' curriculum; certificate after final oral assessment with two fellowship directors
Department
Universitätsklinik für Anästhesiologie und Intensivmedizin
