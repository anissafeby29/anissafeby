Overview

A large general cardiology fellowship with four training tracks ranging from 2-year clinical core to 4–5-year physician-scientist pathways.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3–5 years depending on track (Clinical Scholar/Educator, Physician Scientist, Research Training)
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
35 general fellows total
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited
Department
Division of Cardiology, Department of Internal Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed internal medicine training; three letters (one from PD), CV, personal statement, USMLE Steps I and II
International applicants
Yes – programme states it is committed to recruiting qualified international trainees
Visa support
Accepts permanent residents or J-1 ECFMG-sponsored visas; does not sponsor H-1B
Licence requirement
Texas Medical Board licence or training permit

How to apply

Deadline
25 August (ERAS opens July) for July start the following year
Application method
ERAS; NRMP Match; virtual interviews
