Overview

Twelve-month ESR fellowship in clinical MRI and MRI research honouring Prof. Alexander R. Margulis, for young radiologists from Eastern and South-Eastern Europe.

The programme

Training type
Clinical and Research Fellowship
Duration
12 months
Start
Last published cycle: 15 September 2024 – 31 August 2025
Positions
1
Accreditation
ESR certificate on completion and submission of research report
Department
Radiology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
ESR resident members in 4th/5th year of training or full members within 3 years of board certification from Eastern and South-Eastern Europe; English fluency (basic German desirable); area of MRI interest; basic database and research skills
International applicants
Restricted to applicants from Eastern and South-Eastern Europe; apply only to institutions outside own country of residence/work
Visa support
Fellow responsible for own visa, work permit, insurance and accommodation; training centre helps with organisational details
Licence requirement
Not stated on official page

How to apply

Deadline
Not stated on official page
Application method
Email single PDF to ESOR Office (esor@myesr.org): motivation letter, ESOR CV, recommendation letters, certificates, language certificate, insurance, 5 publications, references, ESR membership, passport; online interview for shortlisted

Funding

Funding
Alexander R. Margulis grant of €3,000 per month
