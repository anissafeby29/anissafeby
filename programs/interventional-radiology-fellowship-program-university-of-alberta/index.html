Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Successful completion of (or progression towards) a recognised radiology postgraduate qualification (FRCPC, FRCR, etc.); English language proficiency test (academic) within 24 months before CPSA application unless exempt
International applicants
Yes — around 10 fellows selected each year from Canada and worldwide
