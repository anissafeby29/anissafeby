Overview

A one-year ACGME-accredited fellowship providing interventional, multimodal and multidisciplinary training in acute and chronic pain across multiple clinical sites.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
One year
Start
Institution policyMount Sinai Health System resident/clinical fellow appointments commence 1 July
Positions
Four fellows per year
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited
Department
Department of Anesthesiology, Perioperative and Pain Medicine (Mount Sinai West / Mount Sinai Morningside)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Not stated on official page
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs accepted with valid ECFMG certificate; may not begin training until an appropriate visa is obtained
Visa support
Institution policyMount Sinai Health System offers J-1 (ECFMG) and H-1B (categorical candidates in programmes of 2+ years, ECFMG-certified with USMLE Step 3 passed by Match) plus E-3 for Australians; programme director decides which is offered
Licence requirement
Institution policyResidents/fellows in ACGME-accredited programmes do not need a New York State licence (supervised practice exemption); non-ACGME fellows need a valid NY licence or limited permit
