KSR International Fellowship Program (funded visiting fellowship)

Korean Society of Radiology (KSR) — host Korean academic institutions

RadiologyInterventional radiologyVisiting Fellowship (clinical training and/or research)
Destination
KOR
Various (Korea), Seoul, South Korea
Open official programme page ↗

Overview

KSR-funded 1–3 month visiting fellowship placing foreign radiologists at a Korean institution for clinical training and/or research, with a grant for airfare and living costs.

The programme

Focus
Diagnostic and interventional radiology (subspecialty fellowships)
Training type
Visiting Fellowship (clinical training and/or research)
Duration
1–3 months
Start
Within the applicable year (notification in October)
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
KSR certificate of completion issued after report from recipient and tutor
Department
KSR International Liaison Committee

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Radiology specialists with sufficient training and experience, graduates of medical colleges, aged up to 40, fluent in English or Korean; must become an international member of KSR
International applicants
Yes — programme is designed for foreign radiologists; requires recommendation letter from the president of the applicant's national radiological society; recipients must return home after training
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Licences as medical doctor and radiologist (home country) required for application

How to apply

Deadline
August 30/31 of the previous year
Application method
Send application form, recommendation (from national radiological society president to KSR President), CV, passport copy, medical report, written pledge, MD and radiologist licences and employment certificate to KSR International Liaison Committee (office@radiology.or.kr)

Funding

Funding
KSR grant covering airfare and minimum living costs: max 5,500,000 KRW per recipient, max 1,500,000 KRW per month, economy airfare up to 1,000,000 KRW
