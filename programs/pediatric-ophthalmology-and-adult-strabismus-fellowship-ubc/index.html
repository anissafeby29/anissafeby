Overview

Fellowship at BC Children's Hospital with complex pediatric ophthalmology and adult strabismus surgery at Vancouver Hospital.

The programme

Focus
Paediatric ophthalmology
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
1 July
Positions
2 (McCormick Fellowship; John Pratt-Johnson Fellowship primarily for applicants from developing countries)
Accreditation
Not evaluated by the Royal College of Physicians and Surgeons of Canada (university clinical fellowship)
Department
Department of Ophthalmology & Visual Sciences

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Licensed medical practitioner; Canadian residency graduates must complete PGY1-5; US graduates ABO certified/eligible; other foreign graduates must show completion of an approved programme; CPSBC English proficiency
International applicants
Yes - one endowed position primarily aimed at applicants from developing countries; English language requirements apply to all IMGs
Visa support
Visa required; department issues acceptance letter and required documentation (UBC immigration process for foreign clinical fellows)
Licence requirement
Licensure with the College of Physicians and Surgeons of British Columbia; English language proficiency

How to apply

Deadline
1 October 2026 for 2027 positions
Application method
Not stated on official page

Funding

Funding
Funded by two endowments, paid by UBC
