Overview

An intense fellowship at the largest paediatric centre in North America with exposure to all subspecialties within paediatric ophthalmology and strabismus.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
1 July (3 positions) or 1 January (1 position)
Positions
4 per year
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Department of Ophthalmology & Vision Sciences

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Institution policymust be eligible for a CPSO postgraduate education certificate, including recognition as a medical specialist/family physician in the jurisdiction where the applicant practised before the fellowship
International applicants
Foreign applicants may apply
Visa support
Institution policyinternational clinical fellows need an IRCC work permit; the PGME Office issues an LMIA-exempt offer of employment number and supports the work permit application
Licence requirement
CPSO educational licence after 4-6 week pre-fellowship evaluation

How to apply

Deadline
Second Friday in September of the year prior
Application method
Email Fellowship Coordinator; North American Match registration required; in-person interview
