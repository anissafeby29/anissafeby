Overview

A breast oncology clinical research fellowship (offered since 2011) with two full-day clinics weekly, multidisciplinary sessions and expected international abstract and publication.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Research Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
1 July 2027
Positions
1
Accreditation
Western University fellowship; not recognised as training towards RCPSC/CFPC certification
Department
Department of Oncology, Division of Medical Oncology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
FRCPC or equivalent training; completed an oncology subspecialty programme; licensed with or eligible for a CPSO educational licence; IELTS 7 in each band / TOEFL-iBT 93 (speaking 24) / OET-Medicine B if medical school not taught in English
International applicants
Fellowship training available to Canadian and non-Canadian citizens, including international medical graduates (Western oncology fellowship page)
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
CPSO licence or eligibility for CPSO educational licence
