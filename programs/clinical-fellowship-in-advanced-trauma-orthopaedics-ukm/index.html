Overview

Clinical Fellowship in Advanced Trauma Orthopaedics at UKM / Hospital Canselor Tuanku Muhriz: this clinical fellowship offers operative exposure to complex pelvis and acetabular fractures, corrective deformities, and ring fixator techniques.
