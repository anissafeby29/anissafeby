Overview

Twelve-month basic interventional cardiology training at the University Medical Center HCMC run by UMP's social-demand training centre.

The programme

Focus
Interventional cardiology
Training type
Clinical training course (certificate; Vietnamese continuing medical education / professional technique certificate)
Duration
12 months
Start
Most recent published intake: Course 04, 25 September 2023 - 20 September 2024 (Course 03: 27 March 2023 - 22 March 2024); later intakes announced by UMP CHPT
Positions
6 trainees per course
Accreditation
Certificate issued under Vietnamese Ministry of Health continuing-training regulations
Department
Center for Health Workforce Training on Social Demand (CHPT), UMP

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
General medical doctor with either at least 2 years working in cardiology (or a clinical department caring for cardiac patients) or a cardiology specialty certificate of at least 1 year
International applicants
Not stated on official page
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Vietnamese medical practising certificate (chứng chỉ hành nghề / giấy phép hành nghề) required

How to apply

Deadline
Course 04: until 15 September 2023 or when 6 applications received (closed)
Application method
Registration form, notarised MD degree, photos and CV to the UMP CHPT centre (chpt@ump.edu.vn)

Funding

Funding
Self-funded - tuition 65,000,000 VND per trainee
