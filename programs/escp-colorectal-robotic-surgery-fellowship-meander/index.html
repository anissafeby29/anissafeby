ESCP Colorectal Robotic Surgery Fellowship

Meander Medical Center

Overview

Six-month ESCP robotic colorectal fellowship training fellows to perform standard robotic procedures (rectopexy, sigmoidectomy, right colectomy, low anterior resection) at an approved host centre.

The programme

Focus
Colorectal surgery (robotic)
Training type
Clinical Fellowship (society-funded)
Duration
6 months
Start
Start date defined by ESCP; must commence within 12 months of award
Positions
Four six-month fellowships across all host centres for 2026
Accreditation
European Society of Coloproctology (ESCP) fellowship host centre (supervisor: Esther Consten)
Department
Not stated on official page

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
ESCP member (fully paid) for at least 12 months; board-certified in general surgery (e.g. CCT) with at least five years of higher surgical training; senior trainee or early-career consultant (ideally ≤2 years as consultant); evidence of basic robotic training (full simulation certificate or ≥2-day robotic course); demonstrated interest in coloproctology
International applicants
Open to ESCP members meeting the criteria; page does not restrict nationality, but host-country licence, work permission and language are the candidate's responsibility
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Valid licence to practise colorectal surgery in the host country required; candidates secure their own licence and work permission (fellowship forfeited if not obtained within four months); must communicate and practise in the host centre's native language

How to apply

Deadline
Applications closed for 2026
Application method
Online application to ESCP; possible interview; fellows provide a video and written report
