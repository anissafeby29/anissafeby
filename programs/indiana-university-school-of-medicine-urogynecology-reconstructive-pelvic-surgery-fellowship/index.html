Overview

Indiana University School of Medicine's Indianapolis programme is included in the American Urogynecologic Society's 2026 national guide to Urogynecology and Reconstructive Pelvic Surgery fellowships.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years for OBGYN pathways; confirm urology-entry options
Start
Early August 2027 for the published cycle; confirm with programme
Positions
2 positions in the published 2026 cycle
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited (established 1988)
Department
Department of Obstetrics and Gynecology and Department of Urology (integrated)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed eligible Obstetrics and Gynaecology or Urology residency; ERAS, NRMP and institution-specific requirements apply
International applicants
Institution policyYes - international graduates need ECFMG certification (or unrestricted US licence or Fifth Pathway) and US work authorisation; J-1 preferred
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 (ECFMG) is the preferred training visa; J-2 with EAD, F-1 (OPT), H-1B or O-1 may be considered under special circumstances by the Program Director with GME approval (extra costs borne by the programme)
Licence requirement
Not stated on official page

How to apply

Deadline
Participated in the 2026 ERAS and NRMP cycle; confirm the next cycle
Application method
ERAS and NRMP Match

Funding

Funding
Institution policyIU GME stipends July 2026-June 2027: PGY-4 $75,100, PGY-5 $78,200, PGY-6 $81,100, PGY-7 $84,600, PGY-8 $88,200 (PGY-1 $68,100), with no-premium health and dental insurance
