Overview

UCNS-accredited one- to two-year Penn fellowship in behavioral neurology and neuropsychiatry based in the Penn Memory Center and Frontotemporal Degeneration Center, with clinical trials and optional research year.

The programme

Eligibility and visa

Licence requirement
Must be eligible for Pennsylvania medical licensure, including passing USMLE Step 3 (or COMLEX Level 3)

How to apply

Deadline
Application cycle opens 1 September; submission deadline 1 December; interviews begin 15 January (apply in penultimate year of residency)
Application method
Send CV, personal statement, professional photo, USMLE Step 1-3 scores and three letters of recommendation to the fellowship directors (apply in penultimate year of residency)
