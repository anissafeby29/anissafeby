Uveitis Fellowship

University of Ottawa Eye Institute (The Ottawa Hospital)

OphthalmologyUveitis & ocular immunologyClinical Fellowship
Destination
CAN
Ottawa, Ontario, Canada
Open official programme page ↗

Overview

One-year uveitis fellowship across the Ottawa Hospital General and Riverside campuses and CHEO with two uveitis specialists, covering adult and paediatric ocular inflammation.

The programme

Focus
Ocular immunology & uveitis
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
1 July
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Institution policyUniversity of Ottawa clinical fellowship - does not lead to any Canadian (RCPSC/CFPC) certification
Department
Department of Ophthalmology, University of Ottawa

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed Canadian/US residency or international clinical and surgical ophthalmology training; English proficiency; CV and 2 letters (one from residency programme director); interview
International applicants
Yes - international clinical and surgical training in ophthalmology accepted
Visa support
Institution policyinternational trainees need an IRCC work permit (LMIA-exempt); the uOttawa PGME/immigration advisor provides the documents needed to apply (CPSO letter of eligibility and LMIA-exemption number)
Licence requirement
Educational licence from the College of Physicians and Surgeons of Ontario (stated for uveitis; applies to trainees generally)

How to apply

Deadline
30 September
Application method
CV and two letters of recommendation to the coordinator

Funding

Funding
Salary $70,000 CAD; $1,000 conference sponsorship
