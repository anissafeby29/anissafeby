Overview

ESOR-sponsored 12-month cardiothoracic fellowship at The Ottawa Hospital split six months cardiac and six months chest imaging, including cardiac CT/MR and image-guided chest procedures.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
12 months
Start
July 1 (last published cycle: July 1, 2025)

1 ESOR place
Accreditation
University of Ottawa clinical fellowship (ESOR one-year fellowship)
Department
Department of Radiology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Recently board-certified ESR full members residing within Europe; completed accredited radiology training and certified/eligible with RCPSC, ABR or equivalent; proficient English
International applicants
ESOR place reserved for radiologists residing within the boundaries of Europe (non-European IMGs should see the regular uOttawa fellowship listings)
Visa support
Successful applicants requiring a visa and/or sponsorship to Canada receive assistance from uOttawa
Licence requirement
Not stated on official page
