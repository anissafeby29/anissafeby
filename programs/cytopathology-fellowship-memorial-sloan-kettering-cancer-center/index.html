Overview

A one-year ACGME-accredited cytopathology fellowship with about 38,000 specimens a year including 6,700 image-guided and 1,300 pathologist-performed FNAs.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
1 July
Positions

Department
Department of Pathology and Laboratory Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed ACGME-accredited pathology residency; board-eligible in AP or AP/CP by start
International applicants
Accepted if eligibility met — IMGs must submit an ECFMG certificate marked 'valid indefinitely'
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
New York State licence or limited permit

How to apply

Deadline
30 December 2026 (for 2028); NRMP match from 2028-29
Application method
Apply online (MSK Embark portal); then send three letters of recommendation, CV, copy of medical school diploma (translated if necessary) and ECFMG certificate for IMGs

Funding

Funding
Stipend commensurate with the applicant's year of postgraduate training
