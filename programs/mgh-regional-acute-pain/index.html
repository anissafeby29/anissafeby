Regional Anesthesiology and Acute Pain Medicine Fellowship

Overview

Regional and acute pain training covers adult and pediatric techniques, inpatient consultation and ambulatory catheter care.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
1 July 2027 (final MGH cohort); integrated MGB programme from July 2028
Positions
2 per year (MGH programme); integrated MGB programme offers 6 positions per year from 2028
Accreditation
ACGME
Department
Not stated on official page

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed ACGME or RCPSC-accredited anesthesiology residency; institutional selection requirements also apply.
International applicants
Yes - non-LCME/AOA graduates eligible with applicable USMLE requirements; ACGME-accredited anesthesiology residency required (integrated programme criteria)
Visa support
Mass General Brigham sponsors H-1B and J-1 visas for eligible applicants
Licence requirement
Not stated on official page

How to apply

Deadline
2027-28: SF Match registration opened 5 January 2026, deadline 10 May 2026. Next cycle: integrated MGB RAPM fellowship (2028-29) via SF Match - see SF Match calendar
Application method
SF Match

Funding

Funding
Paid at PGY-5 level; starting PGY-5 salary in 2026 is $102,466
