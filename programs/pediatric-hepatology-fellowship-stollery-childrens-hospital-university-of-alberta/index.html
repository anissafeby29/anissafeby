Pediatric Hepatology Fellowship

Stollery Children's Hospital / University of Alberta

Overview

Two-year fellowship enhancing expertise in pediatric hepatology and liver transplantation at the pediatric liver transplant centre for Western Canada.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
2 years (clinical first year of 13 four-week blocks; research second year)
Start
Institution policyPreferred start 1 July; otherwise must start on the first of a month
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Not accredited by the Royal College of Physicians and Surgeons of Canada
Department
Division of Pediatric Gastroenterology and Nutrition

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Competent pediatric gastroenterologist certifiable by their training body (e.g. RCPSC, AAP or RACP); endoscopic skills preferred but not required
International applicants
Open to gastroenterologists certifiable by bodies such as RCPSC, AAP or RACP; Department of Pediatrics fellowship page: sponsored international trainees must apply via their sponsoring agency to PGME (pgspon@ualberta.ca); applications sent directly by trainees are not accepted for the sponsored route
Visa support
Institution policyInternational fellows require a Canadian work permit before starting; PGME issues the offer/FVCA number, documents due to PGME at least 6 months before start
Licence requirement
Institution policyCollege of Physicians & Surgeons of Alberta (CPSA) registration required before clinical fellowship begins, incl. CPSA English language proficiency where applicable

How to apply

Deadline
Not stated on official page
Application method
Institution policyApply directly to the fellowship programme per its guidelines; offers subject to PGME approval (sponsored applicants from Saudi Arabia, Oman, Bahrain, UAE apply via sponsor to PGME)

Funding

Funding
Institution policyFellowships without salary/funding not permitted; from 1 Jan 2026 salary must be at least the PARA R1 level; PGME provides no salary
