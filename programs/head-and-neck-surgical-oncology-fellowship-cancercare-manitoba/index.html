Overview

One-year AHNS-approved clinical fellowship at the tertiary cancer referral centre for Manitoba, Nunavut and north-western Ontario, whose head and neck surgical oncology service sees over 1,200 new patients a year.

Eligibility
Board eligible for the American Board of Otolaryngology–HNS, American Board of Surgery or American Board of Plastic Surgery (or Canadian equivalent); International Track applicants as per AHNS rules
International applicants
AHNS International Track applies to the central match (non-ACGME/non-RCPSC residency in OHNS, general or plastic surgery; ECFMG certification)
