Overview

A 12-month prostate imaging fellowship at Switzerland's highest-volume prostate MRI centre (over 2,500 examinations a year) including supervised CT/MRI reporting, PSMA-PET/CT and uro-oncologic conferences.

The programme

Focus
Abdominal imaging
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
12 months (1 October – 1 October)
Start
1 October (2025/26 cycle)
Positions
1
Accreditation
Certificate from ESR/ESOR and Octorad AG with Hirslanden Clinic on completion.
Department
Radiology (Octorad AG with Hirslanden Clinic and Klinik im Park)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Late radiology trainee or radiologist within three years of board certification, residing in Europe, full ESR member, pursuing abdominal radiology with focus on prostate imaging; advanced German and basic English required.
International applicants
Restricted to applicants residing in Europe; candidates should apply to institutions outside their country of residence/work.
Visa support
Fellow is responsible for visa, work permit, insurance and accommodation; the centre helps with organisational details.
Licence requirement
Not stated on official page

How to apply

Deadline
Applications currently closed (per ESOR page).
Application method
Email application as a single PDF (motivation letter, ESOR CV, recommendation letters, certifications, language certificate, insurance proof, publications, ESR membership, passport); online interview.

Funding

Funding
€23,000 gross grant from the training centre, paid monthly via ESOR; fellow covers own expenses and health insurance.
