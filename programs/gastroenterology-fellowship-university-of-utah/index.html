Overview

A three-year ACGME programme across University Hospital, Huntsman Cancer Hospital and the Salt Lake VA with broad exposure to general GI, IBD, hepatology, transplant hepatology, therapeutics and motility.

The programme

Training type

Duration

Department
Division of Gastroenterology, Hepatology & Nutrition, Department of Internal Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of a 3-year ACGME-accredited internal medicine residency; board certification in internal medicine within the first fellowship year.
International applicants
Welcomes non-U.S. citizens; must hold a J-1 visa with valid ECFMG certificate (with sufficient time left to complete 3 years) or be a U.S. permanent resident.
Visa support
J-1 (ECFMG) only
