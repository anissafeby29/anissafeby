Overview

FK UNAIR's O&G subspecialist (Sp2) programme trains obstetrician-gynaecologists in gynaecologic oncology, FER, urogynaecology, social O&G and fetomaternal medicine.

The programme

Focus

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Indonesian specialist in obstetrics and gynaecology (Sp.OG) (domestic or foreign graduate; foreign-trained specialists need an adaptation certificate from a collegium-recognised institution); legalised specialist diploma; valid specialist STR; collegium recommendation; employer consent if employed; signed statement to comply with RSUD Dr. Soetomo/FK UNAIR rules; family consent. O&G Collegium recommendation; ELPT/TOEFL >= 500; evidence of interest (symposium/workshop/training); age limits by interest (gynaecologic oncology, FER, urogynaecology <= 45; social O&G and fetomaternal <= 50); at least 1 year of specialist practice (except lecturers); workplace recommendation with statement to return; maximum 2 exam attempts.
International applicants
Indonesian citizens only (Warga Negara Indonesia); Indonesian specialists who trained abroad are eligible with an adaptation certificate
Visa support
Not applicable (Indonesian citizens only)
Licence requirement
Valid Indonesian specialist STR (at least 6 months before expiry); RSUD Dr. Soetomo practice permit (SIP) during training where stated

How to apply

Deadline
Even semester 2026/2027: online registration 1 September - 3 November 2026 (19:00 WIB); TPA and English test 7 November 2026 on Campus C. Odd semester 2026/2027 registration ran 1 April - 22 June 2026
Application method
Online registration via PPMB Universitas Airlangga (ppmb.unair.ac.id), selection fee, TPA and English test, then programme subject exam, health test/MMPI and interview; hard-copy documents for stage-1 passers

Funding

Funding
Self-funded or sponsored; fees 2026/2027: UKT Rp 30,000,000 per semester plus one-off IPI Rp 50,000,000. Ministry of Health study-assignment candidates need a letter confirming MoH/health office covers UKT and IPI
