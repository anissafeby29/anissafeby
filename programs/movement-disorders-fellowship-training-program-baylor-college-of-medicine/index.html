Overview

Baylor College of Medicine's two-year movement disorders fellowship, founded by Dr Joseph Jankovic in 1977, training neurologists to become experts in Parkinson's disease and movement disorders.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
2 years (approved as a two-year fellowship by the Texas State Board of Medical Examiners)
Start
1 July (orientation before 1 July)
Positions
Up to five fellows enrolled at any time; two to four new fellows accepted each year depending on funding
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Department of Neurology, Parkinson's Disease Center and Movement Disorders Clinic

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed an ACGME-accredited neurology programme or an equivalent (i.e. foreign training programme)
International applicants
Foreign training equivalent to an ACGME neurology residency accepted; foreign graduates must provide documentation to comply with BCM and Texas Medical Board requirements
Visa support
Foreign medical graduates are referred to BCM's International Services Office regarding qualifications and visa status
Licence requirement
Not stated on official page
