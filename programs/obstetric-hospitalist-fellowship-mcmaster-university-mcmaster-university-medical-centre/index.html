Obstetric Hospitalist Fellowship

McMaster University – McMaster University Medical Centre

Obstetrics & GynaecologyMaternal-fetal medicineClinical Fellowship
Destination

Hamilton, Ontario, Canada
Open official programme page ↗Compare saved →

Overview

A one-year non-RCPSC-accredited program in hospital-based obstetrics, emergency skills, patient safety and quality improvement, training fellows as obstetric safety officers.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
July 1
Positions
Two per year
Accreditation
Not accredited by the Royal College of Physicians and Surgeons of Canada
Department
Department of Obstetrics & Gynecology, Faculty of Health Sciences, McMaster University

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Not stated on official page
International applicants
Yes – TOEFL required if training was not in English; sponsorship letter documenting full salary support required
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
College of Physicians and Surgeons of Ontario (CPSO) licence; accepted clinical fellows trained abroad must complete the CPSO 4–12-week pre-entry assessment program (department fellowship resources)

How to apply

Deadline
September 1 each year (September 1, 2026 for 2027/2028; closed)
Application method
Pre-assessment, then full package (CV, degrees, transcripts, cover letter, three references, TOEFL, sponsorship letter) emailed to the Fellowship Coordinator; interviews in November

Funding

Funding
Must be fully funded by a sponsoring institution/agency; trainee salary no less than CAD 60,000 per annum
