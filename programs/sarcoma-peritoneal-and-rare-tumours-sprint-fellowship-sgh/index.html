Overview

Train fellows to provide comprehensive tertiary multidisciplinary care for patients with complex intra-abdominal, pelvic and peritoneal malignancies.

The programme

Focus
Surgical oncology (sarcoma, peritoneal and rare tumours)
Training type
Clinical Fellowship (attachment for foreign doctors)
Duration
6 months – Clinical Fellowship only 12 months – Clinical Fellowship and offer the fellow a research project
Start
Subject to SMC registration and work pass approval (clinical fellowship processing takes approx. 6-9 months after provisional offer)
Positions
2
Accreditation
Certificate of Training
Department
Surgery & Surgical Oncology/ Sarcoma, Peritoneal and Rare Tumours (SPRinT)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Fellows with accredited specialist training in General Surgery.; Department requirement: Completed accredited specialist training in General Surgery ; Positive evaluation of written application. ; Strong support references from previous mentors. ; Proficient in English. ; Pass an interview by the HOD.
International applicants
Yes - SGH fellowship and attachment programmes are offered to foreign doctors for short-term clinical training (more than 1000 international doctors since 2000)
Visa support
Training Employment Pass application with Ministry of Manpower (MOM) upon successful SMC Temporary Registration
Licence requirement
Temporary Registration (for training) with the Singapore Medical Council

How to apply

Deadline
Rolling - applications reviewed monthly; cut-off is the last day of each month (Singapore time)
Application method
Apply through SGH Postgraduate Medical Institute (PGMI) with CV/posting history, case summary and two endorsed recommendation letters (one from Head of Institution/Department); online interview for clinical fellowship; queries pgmi.fellowships@sgh.com.sg

Funding

Funding
Training Fee: S$3,000 (before prevailing GST) per month Programme Funding source: Self-funded
