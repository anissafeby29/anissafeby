Overview

A short-term interventional post giving high-volume PCI and primary PCI experience for candidates aiming for consultant interventional cardiology appointments.

The programme

Focus
Interventional cardiology
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
4 months fixed term (as soon as possible until 2 February 2027), 40 hours/week, no on-call
Start
As soon as possible
Positions
1
Accreditation
Not formally accredited training post (Trust-appointed fellow post)
Department
Cardiology, Freeman Hospital

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Full GMC registration with licence; MRCP or equivalent; competent in diagnostic catheterisation, TTE and first-operator PCI; nearing end of cardiology specialist training (post-CCT or equivalent suitable); paid NHS work experience
International applicants
Eligible if fully GMC-registered with prior paid NHS experience
Visa support
NHS Jobs advert states applications from job seekers requiring Skilled Worker sponsorship are welcome and will be considered alongside all other applications
Licence requirement
Full GMC registration with a licence to practise
