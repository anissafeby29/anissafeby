International Fellowship - Glaucoma

The Chinese University of Hong Kong / Hong Kong Eye Hospital / Prince of Wales Hospital (CUHK DOVS)

Destination
HKG

Overview

Fellows participate in four glaucoma subspecialty clinics (over 350 patients a week) at HKEH and PWH and act as PI on at least one glaucoma research project.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Under 45; completed general ophthalmology residency; preference for applicants with a completed subspecialty fellowship and research record; fluent English
International applicants
Yes - track of the CUHK-HKEH-PWH International Ophthalmology Fellowship Programme for overseas and mainland Chinese ophthalmologists (recent fellows from the Philippines, Malaysia, Saudi Arabia and others)
Visa support
Training visa required; CUHK-HKEH-PWH assists with training visa and limited medical registration but cannot guarantee outcome (allow ~10 months)
Licence requirement
Limited medical registration (Medical Council of Hong Kong) for clinical fellowships; not required for laboratory fellowships
