Overview

One-year clinical and one-year research ocular oncology fellowships covering ocular surface, adnexal, intraocular and orbital tumours in adults and children.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
For international clinical fellows: WFME-listed medical school diploma, home-country medical licence, 3-year ophthalmology residency diploma, USMLE Steps 1 and 2 CK (2 CS listed), valid ECFMG certificate, J-1 eligibility; documents in English; three letters of recommendation (one from department chair)
International applicants
Yes - open to international medical graduates; Yes - international fellows welcome; IMG positions generally non-funded (self/government/institutional funding encouraged; occasional funding varies by year)
Visa support
J-1 visa only (J-1 Physician eligibility required for clinical fellowship)
Licence requirement
Institution policyState of Michigan medical licence and controlled substance licence (Educational Limited or Permanent) required; permanent-licence holders also need a personal DEA registration
