Overview

A one-year ACGME-accredited neuroradiology fellowship providing comprehensive diagnostic neuroradiology training including advanced post-processing for stroke, tumour and brain injury.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of a four-year radiology residency in the United States or Canada; eligible for a California medical license.
International applicants
Requires residency completed in the US or Canada.
Visa support
Institution policyClinical J-1 (ECFMG) or EAD only; UC Davis Health does not sponsor H-1B visas for residents and fellows
Licence requirement
Eligible for a California medical license.
