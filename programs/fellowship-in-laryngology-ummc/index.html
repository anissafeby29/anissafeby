Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
General: Master's degree/clinical master or equivalent recognised by the Malaysian Medical Council (MMC); registration with MMC; Professional Visit Pass (PLIK) approval; supported by Head of Clinical Department; approved by UMMC Credentialing and Privileging Committee
International applicants
Yes - UMMC offers its Clinical Fellowship Programme to both local and overseas candidates
Visa support
Professional Visit Pass (PLIK) approved by the Immigration Department of Malaysia
Licence requirement
Registration with the Malaysian Medical Council

How to apply

Deadline
Non-Malaysians: apply at least 6 months before training start date; accept offer within 1 month
Application method
Apply online via the UMMC Fellowship/Training Attachment Programme e-services portal (register and log in)

Funding

Funding
Non-Malaysian training fee RM36,000 per year (prorated monthly) plus RM100 management fee; government agencies/university teaching hospitals pay management fee only; 15% discount for Lower Middle Income and 30% for Low Income Countries (World Bank)
