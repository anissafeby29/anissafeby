Overview

A combined two-year ACGME CNP and epilepsy fellowship with three Level 4 EMUs, 50+ sEEG surgeries and 5,000+ LTM studies a year.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed ACGME-accredited neurology residency; current Texas medical licence
International applicants
IMGs accepted with ECFMG certificate (application lists ECFMG certificate and J-1 status if applicable)

Foreign medical graduates must be ECFMG-certified and eligible for J-1 sponsorship
