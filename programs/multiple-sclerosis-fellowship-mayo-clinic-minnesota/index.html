Multiple Sclerosis Fellowship

Mayo Clinic — Minnesota

NeurologyNeuroimmunologyClinical Fellowship
Destination
USA
Rochester, Minnesota, United States
Open official programme page ↗Compare saved →

Overview

One year of advanced training in the diagnosis, treatment and clinical research of immune-mediated CNS disorders at Mayo Clinic in Rochester.

The programme

Focus
Neuroimmunology / multiple sclerosis
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (second year may be considered)
Start
July
Positions
2 per year
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Department of Neurology, Mayo Clinic School of Graduate Medical Education

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Successful completion of a neurology residency
International applicants
Open to international applicants; graduates of medical schools outside the US/Canada must hold ECFMG certification before appointment
Visa support
Mayo Clinic supports ECFMG J-1 and H-1B sponsorship (J-1 requires an ACGME-accredited/recognised programme; no new H-1B petitions requiring the US$100,000 payment); final visa type at Mayo's discretion
Licence requirement
Fellows must obtain and maintain an appropriate medical licence while enrolled

How to apply

Deadline
December 31 of the previous year
Application method
Mayo Recruit application plus SF Match neuroimmunology match (ACTRIM-sponsored); interviews January–February

Funding

Funding
Salaried trainee position (stipend and benefits per Mayo Clinic School of GME)
