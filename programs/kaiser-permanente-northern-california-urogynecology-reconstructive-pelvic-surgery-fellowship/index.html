Overview

Kaiser Permanente Northern California's Oakland programme is included in the American Urogynecologic Society's 2026 national guide to Urogynecology and Reconstructive Pelvic Surgery fellowships.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years (2 clinical years plus equivalent of 12 non-consecutive research months) for OB/GYN or Urology graduates
Start
Early August 2027 for the published cycle; confirm with programme
Positions
1 fellow per year
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited (Kaiser Permanente East Bay with UCSF)
Department
Not stated on official page

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed eligible Obstetrics and Gynaecology or Urology residency; ERAS, NRMP and institution-specific requirements apply
International applicants
Accepted with ECFMG certificate and ECFMG-sponsored J-1; no H-1B sponsorship
Visa support
J-1 accepted via ECFMG EVSP (KP does not itself sponsor); H-1B not sponsored
Licence requirement
Not stated on official page

How to apply

Deadline
Participated in the 2026 ERAS and NRMP cycle; confirm the next cycle
Application method
ERAS (interview dates via AUGS)

Funding

Funding
Paid; KP NCAL 2026-27 salary: Fellow I $125,270.73, Fellow II $128,570.69, Fellow III $131,871.79 plus stipends
