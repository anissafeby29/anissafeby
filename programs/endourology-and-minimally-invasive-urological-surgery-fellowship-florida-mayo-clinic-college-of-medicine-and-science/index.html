Overview

A one-year fellowship in advanced robotic oncology and holmium laser enucleation with six to seven two-month mentor-based rotations.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed an ACGME-accredited five- or six-year urology programme
International applicants
International graduates eligible if ECFMG-certified, meeting visa sponsorship requirements and eligible for a Florida licence (minimum one year of US/Canadian accredited training)
Visa support
Institution policyMayo Clinic School of Graduate Medical Education supports ECFMG J-1 and H-1B visas (Mayo selects visa type). Since the Sept 2025 $100,000 H-1B fee, Mayo will not file new H-1B petitions that trigger the fee - H-1B only for change/extension of status (USMLE Steps 1-3 required); otherwise ECFMG J-1 (Steps 1-2, ACGME-accredited/recognised programme). F-1 OPT accepted for first year for US-school graduates
Licence requirement
Institution policyFlorida fellows must hold a full Florida Board of Medicine (or Osteopathic) licence before starting (3-6 months to obtain); residents need Florida registration as an unlicensed physician; Mayo reimburses fees
