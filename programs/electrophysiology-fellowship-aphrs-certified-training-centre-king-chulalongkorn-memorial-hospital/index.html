Overview

APHRS-certified EP and CIED training centre with about 402 ablations and 312 device implantations a year (Carto, EnSite, PFA), offering vein of Marshall ethanol ablation, conduction system pacing, S-ICD, EV-ICD and leadless pacemaker experience.
