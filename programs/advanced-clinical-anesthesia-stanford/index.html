Overview

Individualised advanced experience across difficult airway, thoracic, neuro, high-risk obstetric, regional, transplant, bariatric and paediatric anaesthesia.

The programme

Training type
Advanced Training Programme
Duration
Individualised - rotation schedule and length chosen jointly by attending and programme director
Start
By arrangement
Positions
1–2 per year
Accreditation
Not applicable — faculty ACE appointment
Department
Department of Anesthesiology, Perioperative and Pain Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Qualified anaesthesiologists seeking additional subspecialty experience
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs welcome to apply; must hold a valid ECFMG certificate and be able to obtain Medical Board of California licensure; non-ACGME fellowships need ACGME Non-Standard Training recognition before J-1 sponsorship
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 (ECFMG) is the primary visa; H-1B permitted only in some circumstances with GME approval; O-1 rarely, varies by department
Licence requirement
California clinical appointment requirements apply
