Overview

A visiting fellowship programme allowing doctors to train with CUHK cardiologists at Prince of Wales Hospital in a chosen subspecialty for 3–12 months.

The programme

Focus
Multiple cardiology subspecialties (by supervisor)
Training type
Clinical Fellowship (visiting attachment)
Duration
3–12 months
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Division of Cardiology, Department of Medicine and Therapeutics, CUHK

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
MBChB or equivalent and at least 2 years of clinical experience in current institution/hospital
International applicants
Yes – programme is for visiting doctors; overseas doctors need Limited Registration
Visa support
Division assists with training visa application after signed letter of appointment (training visa valid up to 12 months, no extension)
Licence requirement
Limited Registration with the Medical Council of Hong Kong before clinical/patient contact (approx. 12 weeks to process)

How to apply

Deadline
Not stated on official page

Email CV, recommendation letter from unit head, certificates/transcripts and travel document copy to joanneling@cuhk.edu.hk naming the chosen professor

Funding

Funding
Unfunded – no stipend; candidates bear all expenses
