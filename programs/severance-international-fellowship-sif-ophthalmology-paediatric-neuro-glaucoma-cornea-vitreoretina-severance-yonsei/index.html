Overview

Yonsei Severance international training in paediatric/neuro-ophthalmology, glaucoma, external eye & cornea and vitreoretina, as observer or (with MOHW approval) limited hands-on practice.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
International medical doctors; hands-on track requires >=3 years clinical experience after licensure
International applicants
Yes - programme for international physicians; preference for discounts/waivers to physicians from developing (OECD DAC) countries
Visa support
Yes - visa support included in administration fee
Licence requirement
Observation only unless Korean Ministry of Health and Welfare approves limited hands-on practice (requires >6 months training, >=3 years post-licence experience, government recommendation letter and the 4-week SIF Prime Course)
