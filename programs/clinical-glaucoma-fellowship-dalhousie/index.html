Clinical Glaucoma Fellowship

Dalhousie University Department of Ophthalmology & Visual Sciences

OphthalmologyGlaucomaClinical Fellowship

Overview

One-year AUPO-approved clinical glaucoma fellowship at the Eye Care Centre, Halifax, with heavy hands-on surgical experience including trabeculectomy, tubes and MIGS.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
1 July
Positions
1 per year

Department
Department of Ophthalmology & Visual Sciences (Eye Care Centre, QEII)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed ophthalmology residency; must qualify for a full, defined or academic licence in Nova Scotia
International applicants
Yes - page states the fellowship has trained many Canadian and international ophthalmologists
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Full, Defined or Academic licence from the College of Physicians & Surgeons of Nova Scotia

How to apply

Deadline
15 September
Application method
Submit CV, three reference letters (one from department head or residency director) and cover letter; virtual interview

Stipend CAD 90,000 per year; 2 weeks vacation plus statutory holidays
