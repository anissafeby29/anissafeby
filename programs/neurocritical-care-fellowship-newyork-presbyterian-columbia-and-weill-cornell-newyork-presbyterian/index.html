Overview

Two-year joint NewYork-Presbyterian neurocritical care fellowship at Columbia and Weill Cornell, equally balanced between neurological and medical critical care, with neuromonitoring, TCD and POCUS training.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Graduate of an ACGME-accredited neurology, emergency medicine, anesthesiology, internal medicine or neurosurgery residency; must be eligible for and hold a New York State medical licence on the 1 July start date
International applicants
Graduates of US and foreign medical schools are eligible; non-US medical school graduates must have completed at least one year of US GME training in internal medicine or surgery to meet New York State licensure on the start date
Visa support
NewYork-Presbyterian Hospital only sponsors J-1 visas through ECFMG for foreign applicants (per Weill Cornell page)
Licence requirement
New York State medical licence required on the fellowship start date
