Overview

Fellowship at Asia's first urogynaecology centre covering lower urinary tract disorders and pelvic floor dysfunction, clinical work, surgery and research.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
2 years post-membership; able to perform common gynaecological surgery (THBSO, LSCS, simple vaginal surgery); sponsored by government bodies or companies; minimum 12-month attachment; fulfil SMC requirements
