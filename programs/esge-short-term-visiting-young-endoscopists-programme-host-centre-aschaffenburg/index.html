Overview

Short-term ESGE visiting placement to observe laparoscopy, hysteroscopy and robotic gynaecological surgery at Klinikum Aschaffenburg-Alzenau – Department of Gynaecology and Obstetrics (areas: gynaecological endoscopy).

The programme

Focus
Gynaecological endoscopy / minimally invasive gynaecological surgery
Training type
Observership (short-term visiting programme)
Duration
2–12 weeks (agreed between applicant and host)
Start
By agreement with host
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
European Society for Gynaecological Endoscopy (ESGE) selected host centre
Department
Department of Gynaecology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Resident/trainee or young specialist in obstetrics and gynaecology (or board-certified); ESGE member; minimum GESEA Level 1 certification (Level 2 preferred); English C1 or host-country language
International applicants
Open to ESGE members meeting the criteria; no nationality restriction stated
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
English C1 or host-country language; observational (live surgery observation)
