Overview

A fellowship building competency in multimodality cardiac imaging with a focus on imaging-guided structural intervention alongside LHCH's imaging and TAVI/structural teams.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
MBBCh or equivalent; eligible for full GMC registration with licence; BSE TTE accreditation or equivalent; ALS; ideally post-CCT with imaging interest; CT level 2/3 and TOE accreditation desirable
International applicants
Eligible if GMC-registrable; must be eligible to work in the UK
Visa support
NHS Jobs advert states applications from job seekers requiring Skilled Worker sponsorship are welcome and will be considered alongside all other applications
Licence requirement
Full GMC registration with licence at time of appointment
