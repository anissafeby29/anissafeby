Neuroanesthesia Fellowship

Toronto Western Hospital (University Health Network)

Destination
CAN
Toronto, Ontario, Canada
Open official programme page ↗

Overview

Experience in anaesthetic management of a wide spectrum of neurosurgery including awake craniotomy, cerebrovascular, functional and spine surgery at Toronto Western Hospital.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
Not stated on official page
Start
February/January and July/August intakes
Positions
4 per year (2 January, 2 July)
Accreditation
University of Toronto PGME-registered fellowship; does not count towards Royal College of Physicians and Surgeons of Canada specialty certification
Department
Department of Anesthesia and Pain Management, UHN / University of Toronto

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
University of Toronto departmental requirements: minimum 30 months of clinical anaesthesia training and all requisite certification exams passed in home country
International applicants
Yes — University of Toronto fellowships accept international graduates; English proficiency required where training was not in English
Visa support
Institution policyinternational clinical fellows need an IRCC work permit; the PGME Office issues an LMIA-exempt offer of employment number and supports the work permit application
Licence requirement
Institution policyclinical fellows must hold a CPSO certificate of registration authorising postgraduate education (educational licence); research fellows do not need CPSO registration

How to apply

Deadline
August 2027 closed 1 August 2026; February 2028 closes 1 February 2027; August 2028 closes 1 August 2027
Application method
Online application form on the University of Toronto Department of Anesthesiology and Pain Medicine website

Funding

Funding
Institution policyclinical fellows must be funded (department or sponsoring agency); FEAC recommends pay at least equal to PGY1 on the PARO salary scale, and foreign nationals must be paid at a rate commensurate with a Canadian in the same role
