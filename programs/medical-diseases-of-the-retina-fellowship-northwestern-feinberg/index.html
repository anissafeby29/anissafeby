Northwestern University Feinberg School of Medicine

Destination

Overview

One-year medical retina fellowship with advanced imaging (OCT-A, autofluorescence, widefield angiography) and retinal electrophysiology.

The programme

Training type

Start

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed ophthalmology residency; ABO board-eligible; US citizen, permanent resident or appropriate visa status
International applicants
Institution policyYes - IMGs require ECFMG certification and appropriate visa documentation (J-1, or H-1B for exceptional candidates)
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 (ECFMG) and H-1B; McGaw sponsors H-1B only for candidates of exceptional academic potential with prior approval before ranking/offer (cost borne by the programme)
Licence requirement
Institution policyIllinois temporary (training) medical licence from IDFPR required before any clinical activity or pay (issued up to 3 years, extendable)
