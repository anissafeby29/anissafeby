Overview

A three-year programme with traditional dual-board and research-focused career tracks, training physicians for independent academic or clinical careers with NIH training-grant support.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed or completing ACGME-accredited IM or Med-Peds residency; US citizen, permanent resident or J-1; passed USMLE Steps I and II (and III if completed); ECFMG certificate for non-US/Canadian graduates
International applicants
Non-citizens/permanent residents must obtain an ECFMG-sponsored J-1 visa (programme page)
Visa support
J-1 only; does not sponsor H-1B (programme page)
