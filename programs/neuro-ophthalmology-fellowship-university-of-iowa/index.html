Overview

AUPO-approved programme providing clinical and research education to train independent neuro-ophthalmologists for academic or community practice.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (1 July-30 June)
Start
1 July (orientation); runs 1 July-30 June
Positions
1-2 per year
Accreditation
AUPO approved
Department
Department of Ophthalmology & Visual Sciences

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Not stated on official page
International applicants
Institution policyGraduates of non-US/Canadian schools eligible with a valid ECFMG certificate (or full unrestricted US licence, or Fifth Pathway); visa trainees accepted at programme discretion
Visa support
Institution policyAcceptable visas: J-1 (ECFMG), J-2 with EAD, H-1B, H-4 with EAD, E-3 (Australian citizens), F-1 OPT (first residency year only); no department is required to accept visa trainees; J-1 requires USMLE Steps 1-2 passed, H-1B requires Steps 1-3 passed before offer
Licence requirement
Must be licensed in Iowa by 30 June before start

How to apply

Deadline
31 August
Application method
SF Match CAS with supplemental form, personal statement and three letters

Funding

Funding
Institution policySalaried on UI Health Care GME stipend scale 2026-27: PGY-4 $80,800; PGY-5 $84,100; PGY-6 $87,300; PGY-7 $92,100 (PGY-1 $71,100)
