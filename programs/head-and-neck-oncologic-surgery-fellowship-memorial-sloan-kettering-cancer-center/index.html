Head and Neck Oncologic Surgery Fellowship

Overview

North America's largest advanced head and neck surgery fellowship, offering a one-year ablative clinical fellowship, a one-year oncologic and microvascular reconstructive fellowship, and a three-year combined clinical and translational research fellowship.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (clinical or clinical+microvascular) or 3 years (clinical + research)
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
3-5 per year
Accreditation
American Head and Neck Society (AHNS) accredited
Department
Head and Neck Service, Department of Surgery

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Surgeons with otolaryngology, general surgery or plastic surgery backgrounds
International applicants
Institution policyInternational trainees accepted with J-1 (ECFMG) sponsorship, or H-1B only if already in the US in a transferable status; eligibility must be confirmed with the programme coordinator
Visa support
Institution policyMSK sponsors GME trainees as J-1 (ECFMG Alien Physician) or H-1B; H-1B only for applicants already in the US on H-1B in GME or in a status allowing in-country change/extension - MSK will not file H-1B petitions for trainees residing outside the US; O-1 rarely, needs GME review 6 months ahead
Licence requirement
Institution policyMSK does not require licensure for trainees in ACGME-accredited programmes unless the programme director specifies; trainees in non-ACGME programmes must hold a NY licence or limited permit; work outside the training programme requires a full NY licence

How to apply

Deadline
Per AHNS match timeline (set annually)
Application method
North American residency graduates via the AHNS match

Funding

Funding
