Overview

University of Southern California's Los Angeles fellowship is one of the 39 US training sites listed by the Society of Family Planning for advanced clinical care, research and teaching in complex contraception and abortion care.

Page states: accredited by the National Fellowship in Family Planning
Department
Department of Obstetrics and Gynecology, Keck School of Medicine of USC (training at Los Angeles General Medical Center)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed ACGME-accredited OBGYN residency; must meet Los Angeles General Medical Center GME eligibility requirements
International applicants
IMGs may apply but must obtain California licensure, which can be especially time-consuming
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 (ECFMG) processed through the Keck/LAC+USC Training Program Liaison; Keck/USC and LAC+USC do not sponsor H-1B visas; O-1 and TN not accepted for trainees
Licence requirement
California medical licence required by the start of fellowship
