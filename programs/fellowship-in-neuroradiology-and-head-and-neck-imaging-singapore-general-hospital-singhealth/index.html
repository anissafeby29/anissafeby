Overview

Adult neuroradiology and head & neck fellowship working with NNI, SNEC, NCCS and SGH ENT, aiming for independent reporting of neurological and head/neck examinations.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
6 months to 1 year
Start
Institution policycommencement subject to SMC registration and work pass approval (clinical fellowship processing approx. 6-9 months after the review outcome)
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
SGH PGMI clinical fellowship (SMC temporary registration)
Department
Division of Radiological Sciences

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Postgraduate medical qualification in radiology (e.g. FRCR, MMed or equivalent); 2 years of postgraduate training in the relevant specialty; proven English competency (Singapore Medical Council criteria)
International applicants
Yes — international doctors attach via SGH Postgraduate Medical Institute (PGMI); more than 1,000 international doctors since 2000
Visa support
Training Employment Pass from the Ministry of Manpower required (arranged with offer)
Licence requirement
Temporary Registration with the Singapore Medical Council (clinical fellowship)

How to apply

Deadline
Institution policyrolling - SGH PGMI reviews applications monthly; cut-off is the last day of each month (Singapore time)
Application method
Online application via SGH PGMI (www.pgmi.com.sg); minimum 9 months between application closing date and commencement; enquiries pgmi.fellowships@sgh.com.sg

Funding

Funding
Stipend may be given on a case-by-case basis if funding is requested on the application form (SGH-PGMI funded Clinical Fellowship Award)
