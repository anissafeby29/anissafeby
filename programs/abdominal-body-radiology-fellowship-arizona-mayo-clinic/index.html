Abdominal/Body Radiology Fellowship (Arizona)

Overview

Covers all facets of abdominal and pelvic imaging, including MRI, CT and ultrasound, with emphasis on gastrointestinal and genitourinary disease.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year

2028–2029 academic year (interviews 13 January 2027)
Positions
Up to 2
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Department of Radiology, Mayo Clinic School of Graduate Medical Education

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed an ACGME-accredited diagnostic radiology residency; board-certified or board-eligible in diagnostic radiology at start.
International applicants
IMGs generally not eligible unless they have completed at least three years of ACGME-accredited postgraduate clinical training in the US or Canada (occasional exceptions).
Visa support
Institution policyMayo Clinic School of Graduate Medical Education supports ECFMG J-1 and H-1B visas (Mayo selects visa type). Since the Sept 2025 $100,000 H-1B fee, Mayo will not file new H-1B petitions that trigger the fee - H-1B only for change/extension of status (USMLE Steps 1-3 required); otherwise ECFMG J-1 (Steps 1-2, ACGME-accredited/recognised programme). F-1 OPT accepted for first year for US-school graduates
Licence requirement
Institution policyArizona trainees must hold a full Arizona medical licence (if eligible: 12 months ACGME training for US/Canadian grads, 36 months for IMGs, USMLE 1-3) or an Arizona postgraduate training permit before day one; Mayo pays/reimburses

How to apply

Deadline
Applications accepted 1 October – 1 December two years before the start of the program
Application method
Recruit; virtual interview; no matching program services used (SCARD non-match timeline).
