Overview

Duke University's Durham fellowship is one of the 39 US training sites listed by the Society of Family Planning for advanced clinical care, research and teaching in complex contraception and abortion care.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed Obstetrics and Gynaecology residency; ERAS, NRMP and institution-specific requirements apply
International applicants
Institution policyYes - graduates of medical schools outside the US/Canada are eligible if they hold a valid ECFMG certificate or a full unrestricted US licence
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 alien physician status sponsored by ECFMG with Duke's assistance; Duke is H-1B cap-exempt and H-1B is possible for clinical trainees who have passed USMLE Steps 1-3 and hold an ECFMG certificate (individual programmes decide which visas they accept)
Licence requirement
Institution policyNorth Carolina full medical licence or Resident Training License (RTL) required before clinical duties
