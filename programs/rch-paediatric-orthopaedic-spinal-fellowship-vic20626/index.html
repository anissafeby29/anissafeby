Overview

Year-long clinical fellowship in paediatric spinal deformity at the Royal Children's Hospital, with research through the Murdoch Children's Research Institute.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed (or in final year of) specialist orthopaedic training in home country / accredited orthopaedic surgery residency
International applicants
Institution policyInternational candidates welcome; they must obtain AHPRA registration (including English-language requirements) and a visa with work rights (RCH may sponsor a 482 TSS visa) before starting
Visa support
Institution policyAll RCH employees need Australian citizenship or a visa with work rights; where RCH agrees to sponsor, it engages migration lawyers for a Temporary Skill Shortage (subclass 482) employer-sponsored visa (usually 2-4 years, dependants included); other routes include 417/482 working holiday, 189/190 skilled visas
Licence requirement
Institution policyAHPRA (Medical Board of Australia) registration required before starting at RCH; in-principle approval must be finalised by presenting identity documents to Ahpra within 6 weeks of arrival; IMGs must meet AHPRA English-language standards
