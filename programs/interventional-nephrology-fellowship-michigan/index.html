Interventional Nephrology Fellowship

University of Michigan Medical School (Michigan Medicine)

Destination

Overview

A one-year fellowship in comprehensive dialysis vascular access care at a dedicated interventional nephrology unit, meeting ASDIN certification requirements.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed nephrology fellowship; ABIM-certified in internal medicine and certification-eligible in nephrology
International applicants
Institution policyYes - non-US medical graduates must hold a current ECFMG certificate and be eligible for a State of Michigan licence; non-US citizens need ECFMG J-1, permanent residence or qualifying EAD
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 sponsored by ECFMG (or permanent residence / qualifying EAD); other visas only in very limited circumstances, case by case at Program Director discretion with prospective GME Committee approval
Licence requirement
Institution policyState of Michigan medical licence and controlled substance licence (Educational Limited or Permanent) required; permanent-licence holders also need a personal DEA registration
