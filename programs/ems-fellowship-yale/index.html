Overview

A one-year ACGME EMS fellowship emphasising operational and academic EMS, with field response on a regional team and extensive research opportunities.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
As outlined by ABEM; background and interest in EMS, education, research and administration
International applicants
Institution policyForeign medical graduates accepted into accredited residencies/fellowships; J-1 requires valid ECFMG certificate and Ministry of Health statement of need
Visa support
Institution policyYNHH/YSM GME accepts foreign nationals on J-1 (ECFMG-sponsored), F-1, or H-1B transfers from a current programme
Licence requirement
Must be eligible for an unrestricted Connecticut medical licence
