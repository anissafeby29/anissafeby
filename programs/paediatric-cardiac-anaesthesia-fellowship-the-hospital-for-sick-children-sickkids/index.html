Paediatric Cardiac Anaesthesia Fellowship

The Hospital for Sick Children (SickKids)

AnaesthesiologyPaediatric cardiac anaesthesiaClinical Fellowship
Destination
CAN
Toronto, Ontario, Canada
Open official programme page ↗

Overview

An advanced specialty training programme in perioperative care of children with congenital and acquired heart disease at the Labatt Family Heart Centre, with rotations through echocardiography and critical care.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
Typically 12 months
Start
July
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
University of Toronto-affiliated fellowship; does not count towards Royal College of Physicians and Surgeons of Canada specialty certification
Department
Department of Anesthesia and Pain Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Anaesthetists with prior specialist training in paediatric anaesthesia
International applicants
Yes — open to candidates with fellowship qualification outside North America; NRMP match policy applies to US applicants
Visa support
Institution policyinternational clinical fellows need an IRCC work permit; the PGME Office issues an LMIA-exempt offer of employment number and supports the work permit application
Licence requirement
Institution policyclinical fellows must hold a CPSO certificate of registration authorising postgraduate education (educational licence); research fellows do not need CPSO registration

How to apply

Deadline
Applications for July 2027 accepted January–April 2026
Application method
Online University of Toronto PASS application form (pass.postmd.utoronto.ca); enquiries to anesthesia.fellowship@sickkids.ca

Funding

Funding
Institution policyclinical fellows must be funded (department or sponsoring agency); FEAC recommends pay at least equal to PGY1 on the PARO salary scale, and foreign nationals must be paid at a rate commensurate with a Canadian in the same role
