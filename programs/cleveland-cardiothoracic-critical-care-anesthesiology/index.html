Overview

A newly developed advanced fellowship in Cleveland Clinic's 95-bed CVICU providing training in cardiac, vascular and thoracic critical care, mechanical circulatory support and heart and lung transplant critical care.

The programme

Training type
Advanced Clinical Fellowship (non-ACGME)
Duration
52 weeks (13 four-week modules)
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Up to ten clinical fellows per year
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Anesthesiology Institute — Department of Intensive Care and Resuscitation

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Minimum five years of postgraduate training and completion of a fellowship in Critical Care Medicine, Critical Care Anesthesiology, Critical Care Anesthesiology/Emergency Medicine, Adult Cardiothoracic Anesthesiology, Cardiovascular Disease or Pulmonary Diseases/Critical Care
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs eligible with a current valid ECFMG certificate; Cleveland Clinic holds ACGME Non-Standard Training recognition so approved non-accredited fellowships can take ECFMG J-1 trainees
Visa support
Institution policyH-1B (requires USMLE Steps 1-3 passed; Step 3 results by 30 April for NRMP programmes) and J-1 (ECFMG); O-1 for fellows case by case; no permanent-residence sponsorship for trainees
Licence requirement
Must be eligible for a temporary unrestrictive Ohio medical license
