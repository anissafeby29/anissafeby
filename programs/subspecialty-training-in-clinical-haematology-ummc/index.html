Overview

Clinical haematology training set up by the Haematology Subspecialty Training Board of the Malaysian Society of Haematology, with supervisor, logbook and oral examinations.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
MMed/MRCP or equivalent internal medicine postgraduate degree; minimum 3 years internal medicine post-housemanship; registered with the MOH Haematology Fellowship Programme and/or the Malaysian Society of Haematology subspecialty training programme; General UMMC requirements: Master's degree/clinical master or equivalent recognised by the Malaysian Medical Council (MMC); registration with MMC; Professional Visit Pass (PLIK) approval; support of Head of Clinical Department; approval by UMMC Credentialing and Privileging Committee
International applicants
Yes - UMMC states its Clinical Fellowship Programme is offered to both local and overseas candidates; non-Malaysian fees apply
Visa support
Professional Visit Pass (PLIK) approved by the Immigration Department of Malaysia is a stated requirement
Licence requirement
Registration with the Malaysian Medical Council

How to apply

Deadline
Non-Malaysians: apply at least 6 months before the training start date; accept offer within 1 month of offer letter
Application method
Apply online via the UMMC Fellowship/Training Attachment Programme e-services portal (register and log in)

Funding

Funding
Self-funded training fee: non-Malaysian RM36,000 per year (Malaysian RM18,000) plus RM100 management fee, prorated monthly; 15% discount for Lower Middle Income and 30% for Low Income Countries (World Bank)
