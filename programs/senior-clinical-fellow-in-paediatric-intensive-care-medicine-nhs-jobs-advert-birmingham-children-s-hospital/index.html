Overview

Senior clinical fellow posts in the UK's largest single-centre PICU covering cardiac ICU with ECMO, neuro, liver transplant, burns and trauma critical care and retrieval.

Eligibility
MRCPCH or FRCA or equivalent; APLS; at least 6 months UK (or 2 years international) experience in PICU, cardiac ICU, anaesthesia, PEM or retrieval
