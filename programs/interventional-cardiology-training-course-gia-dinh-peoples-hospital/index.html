Overview

Twelve-month hands-on interventional cardiology course at Gia Dinh People's Hospital for internists working in cardiology or cardiac critical care.

The programme

Focus
Interventional cardiology
Training type
Clinical training course (certificate; Vietnamese continuing medical education / professional technique certificate)
Duration
12 months
Start
Course 02 expected start 5 May 2025 (later intakes announced by the hospital)
Positions
3 trainees (course 02)
Accreditation
Certificate issued under Vietnamese Ministry of Health continuing-training regulations
Department
Training and Referral Direction Office (Phong chi dao tuyen)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Regular-track general medical graduate with a practising certificate in general internal medicine plus either 2 years in cardiology/cardiac ICU/ICU, or 1 year there plus a cardiology practice certificate (at least 1 year) or CK1/MSc in internal medicine
International applicants
Not stated on official page
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Vietnamese medical practising certificate (chứng chỉ hành nghề / giấy phép hành nghề) required

How to apply

Deadline
Rolling - from announcement until enough applications received
Application method
Paper dossier (notarised degrees, practising certificate, CV, ID, photos) to the Training and Referral Direction Office; fee paid after dossier accepted

Funding

Funding
Self-funded - tuition 72,000,000 VND per trainee
