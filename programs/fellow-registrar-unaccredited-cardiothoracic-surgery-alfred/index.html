Overview

A 12-month position for additional training in adult cardiac surgery, heart transplantation, adult and paediatric lung transplantation and mechanical circulatory support at a unit performing over 100 lung transplants a year.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Suited to applicants who have completed cardiothoracic surgery training (specialist examination not necessarily sat)
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs accepted with end-to-end recruitment support (AHPRA registration, college, visa); IMG fellows typically use the competent authority or short-term specialist training pathway
Visa support
Institution policyEmployer-sponsored Training visa (subclass 407) is Alfred Health's preferred visa for IMGs (granted 13 months); Medical Workforce team supports visa nomination, starting at least 6 months before commencement
Licence requirement
Institution policyMedical Board of Australia registration via AHPRA required (competent authority, standard, specialist or short-term training in a medical specialty pathway)
