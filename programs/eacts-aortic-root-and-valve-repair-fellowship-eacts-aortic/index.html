EACTS Aortic Root and Valve Repair Fellowship

High-volume aortic centres in Paris, Rome, Madrid, Brussels and Kaiserslautern (EACTS Francis Fontan Fund)

Cardiothoracic SurgeryAortic surgeryTravelling Fellowship (society-funded clinical placement)
Destination

Overview

Three one-week internships in high-volume valve-sparing aortic root centres plus the EACTS 'Fundamentals in Aortic Surgery' and 'Aortic Forum' courses.

How to apply

Deadline
Application window 1 March – 1 May 2026 (2026/27 cycle closed)
Application method
Online application to the EACTS Francis Fontan Fund with letter of support, letter of interest, CV with publications and operative experience (documents in English, Word or PDF)

Funding

Funding
Grant of €6,200 towards living and travel costs plus EACTS Annual Meeting bursary
