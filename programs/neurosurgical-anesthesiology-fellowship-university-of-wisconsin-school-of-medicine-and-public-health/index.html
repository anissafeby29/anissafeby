Overview

A non-ACGME University of Wisconsin neurosurgical anesthesia fellowship combining complex neurosurgical, neurovascular and neuromonitoring experience with about one-third time as an attending clinical instructor.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
12-24 months (individualised clinical neuroanesthesia and neuroscience research)
