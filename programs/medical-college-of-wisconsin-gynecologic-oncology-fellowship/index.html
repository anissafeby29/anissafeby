Overview

Medical College of Wisconsin's Milwaukee programme appears in the Society of Gynecologic Oncology's national participating-program list for advanced multidisciplinary training in gynaecologic cancer care.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed Obstetrics and Gynaecology residency; ERAS, NRMP and institution-specific requirements apply
International applicants
Yes - IMGs must follow ECFMG guidelines and apply through ERAS; prior US clinical experience not essential
Visa support
Institution policyMCWAH accepts J-1 (ECFMG-sponsored); some programmes pursue H-1B (requires USMLE Step 3 passed) - check with programme; no permanent-residency sponsorship
Licence requirement
Institution policyMust maintain proper Wisconsin medical licensure per MCWAH licensure policies

How to apply

Deadline
6 April 2026 via ERAS (published cycle); interviews June-August
Application method
ERAS only; CREOG scores sent separately to programme
