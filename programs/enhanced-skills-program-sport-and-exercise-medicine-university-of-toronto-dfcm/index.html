Overview

CFPC-accredited PGY3 sport and exercise medicine year run by the Toronto Primary Care Sport Medicine Program with MSK ultrasound, event coverage and paediatric and orthopaedic sport medicine.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
PGY2 residents in a CFPC-accredited Canadian family medicine programme holding an educational licence (or Canadian FPs with Ministry re-entry funding)
International applicants
Only via the international Sponsored Visa Trainee route: trainees sponsored by countries with a UofT training agreement (Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, UAE) apply through the Post MD Education Office; otherwise restricted to Canadian FM residents (CaRMS Family Medicine/Enhanced Skills Match)
Visa support
