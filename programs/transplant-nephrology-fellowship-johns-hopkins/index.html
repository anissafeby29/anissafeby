Overview

A one-year ASN/AST-accredited transplant nephrology fellowship complementary to the ACGME general nephrology fellowship, based at the Johns Hopkins Comprehensive Transplant Center.

The programme

Focus
Nephrology
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (1 July – 30 June); off-cycle applicants considered case by case
Start
1 July
Positions
1–2 per academic year
Accreditation
Accredited by the American Society of Nephrology / American Society of Transplantation (since 2003)
Department
Division of Nephrology, Department of Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Board-certified or board-eligible in internal medicine and nephrology (ABIM or equivalent for international scholars); completed or completing an ACGME nephrology fellowship or equivalent
International applicants
International scholars may apply with equivalent board certification and nephrology training
Visa support
Institution policyJohns Hopkins SOM sponsors incoming residents/fellows for J-1 (ECFMG); H-1B only in limited cases (US MD graduates on OPT in ACGME training, or trainees already on H-1B in ACGME-accredited/recognised training with sufficient H-1B time remaining) - GME policy P&F028
Licence requirement
Institution policy (Maryland Board of Physicians): residents and fellows train as registered unlicensed practitioners through their programme; a full Maryland licence requires 1 year (US/Canadian grads) or ECFMG + 2 years (IMGs) of accredited postgraduate training plus licensing exams
