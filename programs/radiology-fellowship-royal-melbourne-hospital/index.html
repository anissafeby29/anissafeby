Overview

A fellowship-level position assisting qualified radiologists with reporting and procedures and becoming progressively responsible for them under supervision in RMH's comprehensive diagnostic and therapeutic imaging service.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Current registration with the Medical Board of Australia (AHPRA); MBBS or equivalent; completion of radiology training with RANZCR or equivalent for international applicants.
International applicants
International applicants eligible with radiology training equivalent to RANZCR.
Visa support
Institution policyRMH may sponsor eligible international candidates where operationally supported - most commonly Training visa (subclass 407) or Skills in Demand visa (subclass 482); ENS 186 only in exceptional cases
Licence requirement
Current registration with the Medical Board of Australia (AHPRA).
