Overview

Two-year structured paediatric cardiology fellowship split between Amrita (Kochi) and Institut Jantung Negara (Kuala Lumpur) to build paediatric cardiac capacity in underserved regions.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Postgraduate degree in general paediatrics with basic training in paediatric cardiology
International applicants
Yes - joint Children's HeartLink programme designed for trainees from low- and middle-income countries; selected applicants complete each country's formalities for local permissions and practice privileges

Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
