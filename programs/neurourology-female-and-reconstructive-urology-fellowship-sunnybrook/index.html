Overview

A two-year fellowship in functional urology covering urodynamics, neurourology, overactive bladder, female urology, pelvic floor dysfunction and male urethral reconstruction.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Certified urologist in Canada or country of origin, or passed MCCQE
International applicants
Fellows come from across Canada and the world; IMG fellows must complete a 4–12 week Pre-Entry Assessment Period (PEAP); TOEFL/TSE or TOEFL-iBT (93, speaking 24) required if medical school instruction was not in English
Visa support
University of Toronto PGME Office assists non-Canadian fellows with a work permit
Licence requirement
Must be licensed by the College of Physicians and Surgeons of Ontario (CPSO) with CMPA coverage
