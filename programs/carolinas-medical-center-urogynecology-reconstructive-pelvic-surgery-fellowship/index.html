Overview

Carolinas Medical Center's Charlotte programme is included in the American Urogynecologic Society's 2026 national guide to Urogynecology and Reconstructive Pelvic Surgery fellowships.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years
Start
Early August 2027 for the published cycle; confirm with programme
Positions
1 per year
Accreditation
ACGME
Department
Not stated on official page

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed accredited OB/GYN or urology residency; USMLE Steps 1-3; 3 most recent CREOG scores; eligibility for North Carolina medical licensure
International applicants
No visa sponsorship ("We do not sponsor Visas")
Visa support
None - programme does not sponsor visas
Licence requirement
Eligibility for and maintenance of North Carolina medical licensure

How to apply

Deadline
Not participating in 2026 Match; programme normally runs a December application cycle (applications accepted from 1 December)
Application method
ERAS with NRMP participation (not participating in 2026 Match)

Funding

Funding
Paid: Carolinas Medical Center 2026-2027 salary scale (PGY-5 $88,025.60; PGY-6 $91,332.80; PGY-7 $94,660.80) plus $2,500/yr scholarly expense allowance
