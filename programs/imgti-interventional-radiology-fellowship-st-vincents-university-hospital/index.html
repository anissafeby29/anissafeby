Overview

A tailored interventional radiology fellowship, with optional cardiovascular CT/MRI, at a 500-bed Dublin teaching hospital with one IR fellow per year and no competition for cases.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Fellowship of the Faculty of Radiologists and Radiation Oncologists or equivalent qualification (e.g. Saudi Boards); four years of accredited training completed before commencement; registered or eligible for registration on the Supervised Division of the Irish Medical Council register; IELTS 7.0 overall (6.5 in each band) or OET grade B in all categories, test-centre results only.
International applicants
Delivered through the International Medical Graduate Training Initiative (HSE / Forum of Irish Postgraduate Medical Training Bodies), aimed primarily at doctors from countries with less developed health sectors; trainees are expected to return to their country of origin after training.
Visa support
Successful candidates must obtain an atypical visa and work permit before appointment.
Licence requirement
Supervised registration with the Irish Medical Council; medical indemnity certificate; registration with the Faculty's Professional Competence Scheme.
