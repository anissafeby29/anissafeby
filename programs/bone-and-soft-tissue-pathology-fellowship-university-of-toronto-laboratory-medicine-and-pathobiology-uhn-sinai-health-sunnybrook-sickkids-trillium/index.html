Overview

A one- to two-year University of Toronto LMP clinical fellowship in bone and soft tissue pathology, organised and funded through affiliated Toronto teaching hospitals, combining subspecialty diagnostic training with applied or basic research.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (some 2 years)
Start
July (academic year)
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
University of Toronto PGME clinical fellowship (not Royal College certification)
Department
Department of Laboratory Medicine and Pathobiology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed residency in the base specialty (IMGs must hold a specialty certificate before applying); meet CPSO postgraduate licensure requirements
International applicants
Yes — IMGs accepted with specialty certification; TOEFL iBT 93 (Speaking 24) if training was not in English/French; IMGs complete a 4–12 week Pre-Entry Assessment Program (PEAP)
Visa support
Work permit required for non-Canadians; Temerty PGME Office provides documents for the Canadian visa application (applicant pays processing fee)
Licence requirement
CPSO Certificate of Registration for Postgraduate Education (or independent licence) and CMPA malpractice cover

How to apply

Deadline
Applications usually open June–July each year (2027-28 round closed)
Application method
Online LMP application with three reference letters sent directly to pathology.residency@utoronto.ca; sponsored applicants from agreement countries apply via PGME STAR system

Funding

Funding
Funded by affiliated teaching hospitals or external sponsors (governments, institutions); foreign nationals must be paid at Canadian-equivalent rates
