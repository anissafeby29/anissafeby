Overview

ASTS-accredited abdominal transplant fellowship in kidney transplant, liver transplant and hepatobiliary surgery offering two positions per year.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Board eligible or certified (five years of US/Canadian residency at fellowship start); must meet Georgia licensure requirements
International applicants
Foreign medical graduates eligible only if they attended a Medical Board of California–approved school and completed at least 1 year of ACGME/Canadian-accredited training
Visa support
H-1B sponsored for qualified foreign medical graduates; J-1 applicants not considered
Licence requirement
Georgia medical licence
