Hematopathology Fellowship

Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center

PathologyHaematopathologyClinical Fellowship
Destination
USA
New York, United States
Open official programme page ↗Compare saved →

Overview

A one-year ACGME haematopathology fellowship in a service accessioning over 30,000 haematologic malignancy and flow cytometry cases a year, with an optional second neoplastic haematopathology year.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
1 July
Positions
3 (plus 1 optional second-year Neoplastic Hematopathology position)
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited; NRMP code 1466311F0
Department
Department of Pathology and Laboratory Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
MD or MD/PhD with AP, CP or AP/CP residency in the US (ACGME) or Canada; ABPath certified or eligible
International applicants
Only if residency completed in the US (ACGME) or Canada; IMGs need ECFMG certificate
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
New York State licence or limited permit

How to apply

Deadline
31 December (2028-29 academic year)
Application method
Apply online (MSK Embark portal); then send three letters of recommendation, CV, copy of medical school diploma (translated if necessary) and ECFMG certificate for IMGs

Funding

Funding
Stipend commensurate with the applicant's year of postgraduate training
