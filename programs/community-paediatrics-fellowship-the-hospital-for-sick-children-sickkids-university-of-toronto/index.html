Community Paediatrics Fellowship

The Hospital for Sick Children (SickKids), University of Toronto

PaediatricsGeneral paediatricsClinical Fellowship
Destination
CAN
Toronto, Ontario, Canada
Open official programme page ↗

Overview

One-year community paediatrics fellowship focusing on acute neonatology, outpatient mental health and development and teaching, with rotations through community hospitals.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (PGY5)
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Not accredited by the Royal College of Physicians and Surgeons of Canada
Department
Division of Paediatric Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Four years of core paediatric training, FRCPC eligible; apply during third year of residency
International applicants
Open to international trainees; citizens sponsored by Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia or UAE apply via University of Toronto Fellowship Training (STAR)
Visa support
Institution policyinternational clinical fellows need an IRCC work permit; the PGME Office issues an LMIA-exempt offer of employment number and supports the work permit application
Licence requirement
Institution policyclinical fellows must hold a CPSO certificate of registration authorising postgraduate education (educational licence); research fellows do not need CPSO registration

How to apply

Deadline
Applications currently closed
Application method
Paediatric Medicine Fellowship Application form plus CV, cover letter, degree and paediatric specialty certificate, 3 reference letters (one from current Program Director) emailed to paedmed.fellowship@sickkids.ca

Funding

Funding
Institution policyclinical fellows must be funded (department or sponsoring agency); FEAC recommends pay at least equal to PGY1 on the PARO salary scale, and foreign nationals must be paid at a rate commensurate with a Canadian in the same role
