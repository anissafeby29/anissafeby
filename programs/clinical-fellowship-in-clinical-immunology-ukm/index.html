Overview

Clinical Fellowship in Clinical Immunology at UKM / Hospital Canselor Tuanku Muhriz: aims to produce clinical immunologists with detailed knowledge in clinical immunology.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
Minimum of 1 years for international candidate and up to 3 years for local Malaysian candidate

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Recognised postgraduate training in the field of medicine or master equivalent with interest in clinical immunology (MRCPCH/ MRCP/ Masters equivalent).
International applicants
Yes - page sets out the Malaysian Medical Council Temporary Practising Certificate (TPC) route used by non-Malaysian doctors, applied for via UKM; page specifies arrangements for international candidates (e.g. 1-year training, tele-interview)
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
