Overview

Six-month fellowship for international ophthalmologists to enhance clinical and surgical skills in orbital and oculoplastic disorders.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
6 months; intakes 1 January and 1 July each year
Start
1 January or 1 July
Positions
2 per year
Accreditation
Institutional fellowship (Aravind Eye Care System)
Department
Aravind Eye Care System - Postgraduate Institute of Ophthalmology (Education & Training)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
International ophthalmologists with a degree in ophthalmology from a recognised university
International applicants
Yes - listed under 'Long term Fellowship Programmes (International)'
Visa support
Page states international trainees must obtain a student/entry/internship visa (not tourist visa) for the entire training period; FRRO registration on arrival. No sponsorship stated.
Licence requirement
Temporary medical licence from India's National Medical Commission (NMC) is mandatory for international ophthalmologists undergoing surgical training; NMC application processed by the institute

How to apply

Deadline
Not stated on official page
Application method
Application via Aravind course page; course fee paid by wire transfer after confirmation of admission

Funding
