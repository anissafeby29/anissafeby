Overview

Cleveland Clinic's Level 4 Epilepsy Center offers six ACGME epilepsy fellowship positions for adult or paediatric neurologists with extensive video-EEG, surgical epilepsy and neuromodulation training.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (optional second year in intracranial neurophysiology or ACGME clinical neurophysiology)
Start
July 1
Positions
6 per year (typically 1 paediatric, 5 adult track)

ACGME-accredited
