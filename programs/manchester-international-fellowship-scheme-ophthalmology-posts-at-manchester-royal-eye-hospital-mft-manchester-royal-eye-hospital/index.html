Overview

MFT's GMC-sponsored pathway bringing doctors from outside the UK and EU for a fixed period of supervised training, used for MREH ophthalmology fellowships.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
Fixed period (posts typically 12 months)
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
GMC-approved sponsorship scheme
Department
Manchester Royal Eye Hospital and other MFT hospitals

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Doctors from outside the UK and EU; posts require ophthalmology training appropriate to the advertised fellowship
International applicants
Yes - designed for doctors from outside the UK and EU who will return to their home country
Visa support
MFT is a GMC-approved sponsoring body; NHS Jobs fellowship adverts welcome Skilled Worker sponsorship applicants
Licence requirement
GMC registration obtained through MFT sponsorship

How to apply

Deadline
Rolling, linked to individual adverts
Application method
Apply to individual MREH fellowship adverts on NHS Jobs referencing the scheme

Funding

Funding
Salaried NHS fellowship posts (e.g. £67,325 for 2026 MREH fellowships)
