Overview

One-year clinical head and neck fellowship at the UCSF Helen Diller Family Comprehensive Cancer Center covering oncologic, microvascular reconstructive, endoscopic skull base, transoral robotic and endocrine surgery, with a Clinical Instructor appointment.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility

International applicants
Institution policyYes - international medical graduates train on ECFMG-sponsored J-1 visas coordinated by the UCSF ISSO
Visa support
Institution policyIMGs in graduate medical training hold J-1 (Alien Physician) visas sponsored by ECFMG, with the UCSF ISSO as Training Program Liaison (request 6 months before start); H-1B for clinical trainees only via exception request
Licence requirement
California medical licence
