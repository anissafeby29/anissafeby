Subspecialty Training in Palliative Medicine

University Malaya Medical Centre (UMMC)

Internal MedicinePalliative medicineSubspecialty Training

Overview

Three-year competency-based subspecialty programme preparing trainees to practise as palliative medicine subspecialists across hospital, community and hospice settings.

The programme

Training type
Subspecialty Training
Duration
3 years full time (competency based): 12 months palliative medicine, 6 months community hospice, 6 months oncology, 12 months fellowship preferably at a recognised overseas centre
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Department of Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Postgraduate qualification recognised by the Malaysian National Specialist Registry, preferably in a medical specialty (MMed, MRCP); others considered with 24 months internal medicine training; General UMMC requirements: Master's degree/clinical master or equivalent recognised by the Malaysian Medical Council (MMC); registration with MMC; Professional Visit Pass (PLIK) approval; support of Head of Clinical Department; approval by UMMC Credentialing and Privileging Committee
International applicants
Yes - UMMC states its Clinical Fellowship Programme is offered to both local and overseas candidates; non-Malaysian fees apply
Visa support
Professional Visit Pass (PLIK) approved by the Immigration Department of Malaysia is a stated requirement
Licence requirement
Registration with the Malaysian Medical Council

How to apply

Deadline
Non-Malaysians: apply at least 6 months before the training start date; accept offer within 1 month of offer letter
Application method
Apply online via the UMMC Fellowship/Training Attachment Programme e-services portal (register and log in)

Funding

Funding
Self-funded training fee: non-Malaysian RM36,000 per year (Malaysian RM18,000) plus RM100 management fee, prorated monthly; 15% discount for Lower Middle Income and 30% for Low Income Countries (World Bank)
