Paediatric Cardiology Observership Program

The Hospital for Sick Children (SickKids), University of Toronto

Destination
CAN
Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Overview

Observerships in echocardiography, fetal echo, electrophysiology, intervention, cardiac MRI and heart function/transplant for internationally certified paediatric cardiologists.

The programme

Training type
Observership
Duration
Not stated on official page

By agreement (observership applications accepted up to two months before the agreed start date)

Not stated on official page

Not stated on official page

Labatt Family Heart Centre – Division of Cardiology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
International medical graduates board-eligible or certified in paediatric cardiology and not licensed in Canada
International applicants
Designed for international medical graduates
Visa support
Institution policyinternational clinical fellows need an IRCC work permit; the PGME Office issues an LMIA-exempt offer of employment number and supports the work permit application
Licence requirement
No CPSO licence needed (no patient contact)

How to apply

Deadline
Up to two months before agreed start date
Application method
International Medical Observer Application via SickKids International Learners Program (ilp.requests@sickkids.ca)

Funding

Funding
Institution policyclinical fellows must be funded (department or sponsoring agency); FEAC recommends pay at least equal to PGY1 on the PARO salary scale, and foreign nationals must be paid at a rate commensurate with a Canadian in the same role
