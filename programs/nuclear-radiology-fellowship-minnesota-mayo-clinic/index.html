Nuclear Radiology Fellowship (Minnesota)

Mayo Clinic — Minnesota

RadiologyNuclear medicine / Molecular imagingClinical Fellowship
Destination
USA
Rochester, Minnesota, United States
Open official programme page ↗

Overview

A 12-month fellowship teaching the use of diagnostic and selective therapeutic radionuclides and radiopharmaceuticals across a wide spectrum of disease.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
12 months
Start
Institution policyMayo Clinic School of GME academic year begins 1 July (matched trainees report to MCSGME orientation on 1 July)
Positions
3
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited
Department
Department of Radiology, Mayo Clinic School of Graduate Medical Education

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Board certified or eligible for certification, with completion of a four-year accredited radiology residency program.
International applicants
Open to US citizens, permanent US immigrants and international applicants; graduates of medical schools outside the US and Canada must hold ECFMG certification (visa sponsorship information via ECFMG).
Visa support
Institution policyMayo Clinic School of Graduate Medical Education supports ECFMG J-1 and H-1B visas (Mayo selects visa type). Since the Sept 2025 $100,000 H-1B fee, Mayo will not file new H-1B petitions that trigger the fee - H-1B only for change/extension of status (USMLE Steps 1-3 required); otherwise ECFMG J-1 (Steps 1-2, ACGME-accredited/recognised programme). F-1 OPT accepted for first year for US-school graduates
Licence requirement
Institution policyMinnesota-based MCSGME trainees must hold a Minnesota residency permit, and all eligible trainees must obtain and maintain a full Minnesota medical licence (USMLE/COMLEX/MCCQE final exam deadlines apply); Mayo reimburses fees
