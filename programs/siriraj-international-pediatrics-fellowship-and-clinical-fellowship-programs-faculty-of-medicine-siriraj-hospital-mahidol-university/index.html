Overview

International-track paediatric fellowship and clinical fellowship programmes at Thailand's largest university hospital for foreign doctors.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
Varies by programme (academic year 1 July – 30 June)
Start
1 July (some fellowships accept applicants year-round)
Positions
1–2 foreign seats per programme per year
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Department of Pediatrics

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Graduated in the relevant field with a valid medical licence from country of origin; TOEFL-iBT 64 or IELTS 5.0+ (within 2 years)
International applicants
International track with 1–2 seats for foreign applicants per programme per year; Thai language not mandatory at fellowship level
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Not stated on official page

How to apply

Deadline
Apply at least six months before the course starts
Application method
Download forms from sirirajgrad.com and email to siint.recruit@gmail.com; departmental review and possible online interview

Funding

Funding
Tuition about 350,000 THB per academic year plus 4,500 THB processing fee (self/sponsor-funded)
