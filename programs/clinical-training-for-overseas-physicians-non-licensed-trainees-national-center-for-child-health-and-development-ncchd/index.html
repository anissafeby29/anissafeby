Overview

Japan's national centre for child health accepts overseas physicians for observational training alongside its 2–3 year subspecialty fellow programmes.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Physician licence from home country, recommendation letter, CV, immunisation and health certificates, passport copy
International applicants
Only holders of the Japanese national medical licence may practise; NCCHD accepts and mentors non-licensed overseas trainees through demonstrations and lectures (hands-off)
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Japanese medical licence required for hands-on practice (NCCHD is also a designated hospital for Advanced Clinical Training permission)
