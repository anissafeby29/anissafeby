Overview

A one-year (optional second year) advanced clinical fellowship in acute and chronic leukaemias, MDS and MPN in MD Anderson's Department of Leukemia.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year, optional 2nd year
Start
1 July
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Non-standard fellowship approved by the Texas Medical Board (not ACGME-accredited); affiliated with the ACGME-accredited Hematology/Oncology Fellowship
Department
Department of Leukemia

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Per MD Anderson GME eligibility: MD/DO or foreign equivalent, valid ECFMG certificate, USMLE Step 1 and Step 2 CK
International applicants
IMGs eligible with valid ECFMG certificate and USMLE Step 1 and Step 2 CK (each passed within 3 attempts) (MD Anderson GME eligibility page)
Visa support
J-1 visa (ECFMG-sponsored) is the required visa for international trainees needing sponsorship; H-1B not offered (MD Anderson GME eligibility page)
Licence requirement
Texas Medical Board physician-in-training permit or Texas medical licence

How to apply

Deadline
Applications accepted annually 1 August - 1 October
Application method
Contact the Leukemia Training and Education Program Office (leuktrain@mdanderson.org)
