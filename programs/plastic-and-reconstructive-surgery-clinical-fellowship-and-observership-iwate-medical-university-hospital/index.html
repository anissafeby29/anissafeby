Overview

Observation of clinics, rounds and surgeries with scrubbing in possible after MHLW permission.

The programme

Focus
Breast, cleft, craniomaxillofacial, head and neck, microsurgery, trauma
Training type
Clinical Fellowship and Observership
Duration
1-12 months (negotiable)
Start
Any time through the year
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Department of Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Immunisation certificate required for long-term observership
International applicants
Yes - listed by the Japan Society of Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery (JSPRS) as accepting overseas fellows/observers
Visa support
Short-term visa documents for observership; Cultural Activities visa for advanced clinical training
Licence requirement
Scrubbing in available after permission from the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare
