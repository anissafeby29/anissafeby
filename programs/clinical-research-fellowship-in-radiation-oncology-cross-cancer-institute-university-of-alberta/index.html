Overview

A one-year clinical research fellowship split roughly half research and half clinical duties at a centre treating over 4,000 patients a year, with Gamma Knife, MR-Linac development and LDR/HDR, intraoperative and ocular brachytherapy.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Junior faculty or trainees completing radiation oncology specialist training by 30 June 2027, or completed within the last three years; competent in spoken and written English
International applicants
Yes — applicants trained in a non-English language must provide TOEFL, OET or IELTS results meeting CPSA requirements
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Eligible for educational licensure in Alberta (CPSA)

How to apply

15 July 2027
Application method
