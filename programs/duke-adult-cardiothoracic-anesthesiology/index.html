Overview

Training covers complex cardiac and thoracic surgery, echocardiography, perfusion and cardiothoracic intensive care.

The programme

Focus
Adult cardiothoracic anaesthesia
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
12 months; an individualized second clinical or research year may be available
Start
2028-29 fellowship year (current SF Match cycle)
Positions
14 ACGME-accredited positions per year
Accreditation
ACGME
Department
Department of Anesthesiology, Duke University School of Medicine (Cardiothoracic Anesthesiology Division)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Institution policyLCME/AOA-accredited MD/DO graduates, or international graduates with a valid ECFMG certificate or full unrestricted US licence; NC full licence or RTL, I-9 employment eligibility, background and NPDB checks
International applicants
Institution policyYes - graduates of medical schools outside the US/Canada are eligible if they hold a valid ECFMG certificate or a full unrestricted US licence
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 alien physician status sponsored by ECFMG with Duke's assistance; Duke is H-1B cap-exempt and H-1B is possible for clinical trainees who have passed USMLE Steps 1-3 and hold an ECFMG certificate (individual programmes decide which visas they accept)
Licence requirement
Institution policyNorth Carolina full medical licence or Resident Training License (RTL) required before clinical duties

How to apply

Deadline
3 March 2027 for the 2028-29 fellowship (SF Match registration opens 2 November 2026; applications accepted on a rolling basis from November 2026)
Application method
SF Match (sponsored by the Society of Cardiovascular Anesthesiologists); register at sfmatch.org

Funding

Funding
Institution policyGME trainees paid on the Duke stipend scale; FY27 (from 1 July 2026) PGY-1 $71,052 rising to PGY-4 $79,908, PGY-5 $82,992, PGY-6 $86,292, PGY-7 $90,036 (up to PGY-12 $106,800), plus benefits
