Fellowship in Interventional Radiology

Amrita Institute of Medical Sciences (AIMS), Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham

Overview

One-year IR fellowship covering vascular and non-vascular interventions including HCC ablation, TACE/TARE, TIPSS, BRTO, prostate and uterine artery embolisation and lymphatic interventions.
