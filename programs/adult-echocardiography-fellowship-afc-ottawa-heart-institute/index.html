Adult Echocardiography Fellowship (AFC)

University of Ottawa Heart Institute

Overview

An echocardiography fellowship running since 1988 in one of Canada's largest echo laboratories (over 29,000 TTEs and 1,000 TEEs annually).

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1–2 years
Start
July 1 (typical)
Positions
3 internally funded positions
Accreditation
First RCPSC-accredited echocardiography AFC programme in Canada
Department
Division of Cardiology, University of Ottawa Heart Institute / Department of Medicine, University of Ottawa

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Adult cardiology training
International applicants
Yes — listed among uOttawa fellowships accepting International Medical Graduates; proof of English proficiency (e.g., IELTS 7 in each component) required
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Ontario educational licence (CPSO); internationally trained clinical fellows complete a 4–12 week Pre-Entry Assessment Program; three-year limit of licensure for clinical fellowships
