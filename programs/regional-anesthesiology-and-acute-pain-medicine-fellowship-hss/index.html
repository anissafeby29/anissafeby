Overview

A 12-month ACGME-accredited fellowship that has trained more than 200 fellows since 1987, based at the HSS main campus, ambulatory surgery centres, Sports Medicine Institute and Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center.

The programme

Training type

Duration
1 year (12 months); starts after ABA board exams in August

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Graduates of accredited anesthesiology residency programmes
International applicants
Non-US citizens accepted if they maintain appropriate immigration status (ECFMG J-1)
Visa support
Non-US citizens apply for ECFMG J-1 visa sponsorship; non-US citizens may work on a New York State limited permit
Licence requirement
New York State licence required to rotate to the HSS Ambulatory Surgery Center
