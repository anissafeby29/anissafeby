Overview

A three-year ACGME GI fellowship across four University of Miami/Jackson hospitals training consultants in cognitive and endoscopic gastroenterology and hepatology.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship (with separate T32 research track)
Duration
3 years
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
5 trainees selected annually
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited (ERAS 2027 participating programme list)
Department
Division of Digestive Health and Liver Diseases

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed ACGME-accredited internal medicine residency; USMLE Steps 1, 2 and 3 passed before start
International applicants
Non-US citizens/permanent residents train on the ECFMG-sponsored J-1 visa
Visa support
J-1 (ECFMG Alien Physician Program) is the standard visa
Licence requirement
USMLE Steps 1-3 completed before start

How to apply

Deadline
Applications accepted from July (ERAS opens 1 July); no fixed deadline, early application encouraged; interviews September-October
Application method
ERAS; national fellowship Match only (no out-of-match positions)
