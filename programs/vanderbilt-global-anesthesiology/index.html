Global Anesthesiology Fellowship

Vanderbilt University Medical Center

Destination

Nashville, Tennessee, United States

Overview

Clinical service, global-health research and teaching prepare anesthesiologists for work in settings with limited resources, with an optional public-health degree.

The programme

Training type
Combined Fellowship
Duration
1–2 years; the two-year option can include an MPH
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Non-ACGME, as listed in the departmental fellowship index.
Department
Department of Anesthesiology, Vanderbilt University Medical Center

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed approved internship and ACGME-accredited anesthesiology residency before starting; board eligible or board certified.
International applicants
Institutional GME requirement: holders of a foreign medical degree need a valid ECFMG certificate; no programme-specific policy stated
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 via ECFMG; H-1B considered case by case by programme with GME; no new O-1 applications supported
Licence requirement
Institution policyHouse staff train under a Tennessee Board of Medical Examiners licence exemption obtained by GME; full Tennessee licence needed for moonlighting and for non-ACGME clinical fellows with a secondary faculty appointment

Funding

Funding
Salary equivalent to Clinical Instructor rank over 12 months; relocation allowance $2,000 single/$3,000 family; CME fund; supported by the Department of Anesthesiology and research funding
