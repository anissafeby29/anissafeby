Overview

A two-year programme in prehospital care, medical oversight, quality management, research and special operations; the first year is ACGME accredited.

The programme

Focus
EMS
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
2 years (year 1 ACGME-accredited EMS fellowship; year 2 non-ACGME research/education year as clinical instructor)
Start
2027 (2027-2029 fellowship cohort)
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited (first year)
Department
Department of Emergency Medicine (EMS Division), Stanford School of Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Emergency medicine residency; background and interest in EMS, education, research and administration; eligible for unrestricted California medical licence
International applicants
Yes - Stanford Health Care welcomes IMGs to its sponsored training programmes (approved visa and Medical Board of California licensure required)
Visa support
Stanford GME permits J-1 (ECFMG; most common) and H-1B (varies by department); IMGs must hold an approved visa and California licensure; check with programme which visas it permits
Licence requirement
Must be eligible for an unrestricted California medical licence

How to apply
