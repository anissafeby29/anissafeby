Overview

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Must have completed an ACGME-accredited residency by the fellowship start date; 3 letters of recommendation required (preferably including the residency programme director)
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs welcomed; must hold a valid ECFMG certificate (or unrestricted US licence) before starting
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 (ECFMG) preferred; H-1B only with GME approval for candidates with USMLE Step 3 and a MN residency permit meeting set criteria (programme decides); EAD accepted
Licence requirement
Institution policyMinnesota residency permit or unrestricted Minnesota physician licence (as required by the programme); passage of USMLE Step 3/COMLEX Level 3 documented (GME residency/fellowship agreement)
