Overview

Unpaid work-shadowing placement of up to two months on the wards and in the operating theatre of the DHZC cardiac, thoracic and vascular surgery clinic.

The programme

Focus
Adult cardiac, thoracic and vascular surgery
Training type
Observership (Hospitation / work shadowing)

Up to 2 months (rotation on wards and in theatre)

Rolling

2 places

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Medical specialists
International applicants
Yes – international applicants accepted; visa or residence permit required
Visa support
International applicants need a visa or residence permit (not arranged by page)
Licence requirement
No professional licence, licence to practise or work permit required (job shadowing is not a medical employee activity). Proof of English and German at least B2

How to apply

Deadline
Rolling
Application method
Email the department with application letter (German or English, marked 'Hospitant'), CV, B2 English and German certificates, passport copy, reference letter, health insurance proof and proof of financing
