Overview

Annual post-doctoral fellowship in nuclear oncology and theranostics covering PET-CT/PET-MR reporting and radionuclide therapies (I-131, PRRT, Lu/Ac-PSMA, TARE).

The programme

Focus
Nuclear medicine
Training type
Clinical Fellowship (post-doctoral certificate)
Duration
1 year
Start
Admission open throughout the year
Positions
2 national + 2 international
Accreditation
Institutionally approved post-doctoral certificate programme
Department
Department of Nuclear Medicine & Molecular Imaging

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
MD / DNB Nuclear Medicine or equivalent from international universities
International applicants
Yes - open to national and international nuclear medicine physicians (2 + 2 seats); international candidates need approval from their own government/ministry and NMC (New Delhi) approval
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
National Medical Commission (New Delhi) approval required for international candidates
