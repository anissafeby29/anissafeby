Overview

Three-month basic FESS course with theory at UMP and practice at UMC, the HCMC ENT Hospital, Cho Ray and Nguyen Tri Phuong hospitals.

Eligibility and visa

ENT doctors with an ENT practising certificate, or postgraduate ENT qualification (CKI, CKII, MSc, residency), or 18+ months continuous ENT work with nomination letter
