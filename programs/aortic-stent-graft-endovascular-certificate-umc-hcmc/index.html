Overview

Four-month UMC HCMC programme in endovascular stent-graft (EVAR/TEVAR) treatment of aortic disease.

The programme

Training type
Professional technique certificate programme (Vietnamese Ministry of Health continuing training)
Duration
About 4 months (20 July - 6 November 2026, Course 01)
Start
20 July 2026 (Course 01)
Positions
10-20
Accreditation
Certificate of professional technical training (chứng chỉ đào tạo kỹ thuật chuyên môn) issued by UMC HCMC
Department
Cardiovascular surgery / interventional units

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Doctors with a practising certificate in cardiovascular surgery, vascular surgery, interventional cardiology, cardiology or interventional radiology, or another specialty plus 3 years working in those fields
International applicants
Not stated on official page
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Vietnamese practising certificate/licence (chứng chỉ/giấy phép hành nghề) required

How to apply

Deadline
Rolling - from announcement until enough applications received
Application method
Registration form and notarised documents to the Center for Health Workforce Training on Social Demand; no postal applications; fees paid on submission until places are filled

Funding

Funding
Self-funded - 40,000,000 VND
