Overview

An AGES-accredited two-year advanced gynaecological endoscopy training unit (Royal Hospital for Women (Sydney)) under the AGES Accredited Training Program, with training at Royal Hospital for Women and Prince of Wales Private.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
2 years
Start
Training years run in two-year cycles (current recruitment for 2028–2029 positions)
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Australasian Gynaecological Endoscopy & Surgery Society (AGES) accredited training unit; developed with RANZCOG (no degree or subspecialty qualification conferred)
Department
AGES National Training Unit – Royal Hospital for Women (Sydney) (training sites: Royal Hospital for Women and Prince of Wales Private)

How to apply

Deadline
Applications for 2028–2029 trainee positions open 1 November 2026 (central AGES process; interviews at the March AGES Annual Scientific Meeting)
Application method
Central AGES application process; interviews during the March ASM (applicants must be registered delegates); enquiries education@ages.com.au
