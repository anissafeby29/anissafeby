Overview

Interdisciplinary clinical pain training spanning procedural, medical, physical and behavioural approaches to chronic, acute and cancer pain.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
1 July, 1 August or 1 January (multiple start dates)
Positions
7 per year (fully funded)
Accreditation
ACGME
Department
Department of Anesthesiology, Perioperative and Pain Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed ACGME-accredited residency in a pain-board-eligible specialty (anesthesiology, PM&R, neurology, psychiatry, radiology, emergency medicine, family medicine); eligible for unrestricted California licence
International applicants
IMGs accepted only with ECFMG certification, all three USMLE steps, completed ACGME residency, ABA board certification/eligibility and California licence eligibility
Visa support
J-1 only (ECFMG)
Licence requirement
Eligible for an unrestricted California medical licence

How to apply

Deadline
ERAS applications accepted 1 December to 31 May; interviews May-July; NRMP match
Application method
ERAS and NRMP

Funding

Funding
Fully funded positions; Stanford GME salary and benefits
