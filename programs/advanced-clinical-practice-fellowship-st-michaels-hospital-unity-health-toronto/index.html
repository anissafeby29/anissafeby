Advanced Clinical Practice Fellowship

St. Michael's Hospital (Unity Health Toronto)

AnaesthesiologyAdvanced clinical anaesthesiaClinical Fellowship
Destination

Toronto, Ontario, Canada
Open official programme page ↗Compare saved →

Overview

Offers trainees more experience across anaesthesia practice, including high-risk procedures, at the adult trauma centre for downtown Toronto.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
6–12 months (full-time or 60–80% reduced workload on request)
Start
January or July
Positions
4 per year (2 starting each January, 2 each July)
Accreditation
University of Toronto PGME-registered fellowship; does not count towards Royal College of Physicians and Surgeons of Canada specialty certification
Department
Department of Anesthesia, St. Michael's Hospital / University of Toronto

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
University of Toronto departmental requirements: minimum 30 months of clinical anaesthesia training and all requisite certification exams passed in home country
International applicants
Yes — University of Toronto fellowships accept international graduates; English proficiency required where training was not in English
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Not stated on official page
