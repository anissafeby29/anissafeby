Overview

A senior clinical fellow post in a team of 30 registrars supervised by more than 30 haematology consultants, covering inpatients, day care, triage, laboratory and liaison haematology.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Full GMC registration with licence; MRCP(UK) or equivalent; completed core medical training (4 years postgraduate IM experience) or equivalent; clinical and laboratory haematology knowledge
International applicants
Suitable for IMT trainees, senior haematology trainees, and doctors seeking GMC specialist registration via the Portfolio pathway (advert)
Visa support
Applicants requiring Skilled Worker sponsorship are welcome and considered alongside all other applications (NHS Jobs advert)
Licence requirement
Full GMC registration with licence to practise
