Overview

Combined orthopaedic and neurosurgical spine fellowship at Toronto Western Hospital with two required research projects and an optional Masters-track research year.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
12 months (2-year clinical/research option)
Start
July
Positions
Up to 5 per year
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Division of Orthopaedic Surgery, Department of Surgery

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Orthopaedic or neurosurgical certification required
International applicants
Yes - clinical fellows are surgeons from Canada or international countries who already hold orthopaedic specialist status in their country of origin
Visa support
Institution policyinternational clinical fellows need an IRCC work permit; the PGME Office issues an LMIA-exempt offer of employment number and supports the work permit application
Licence requirement
Institution policyclinical fellows must hold a CPSO certificate of registration authorising postgraduate education (educational licence); research fellows do not need CPSO registration

How to apply

Deadline
Not stated on official page
Application method
Institution policyapply to the departmental Fellowship Program Director; sponsored international applicants apply through their sponsoring agency to the PGME Office

Funding

Funding
Institution policyclinical fellows must be funded (department or sponsoring agency); FEAC recommends pay at least equal to PGY1 on the PARO salary scale, and foreign nationals must be paid at a rate commensurate with a Canadian in the same role
