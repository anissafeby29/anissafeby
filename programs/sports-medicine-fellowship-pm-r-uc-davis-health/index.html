Overview

UC Davis PM&R's one-year sports medicine fellowship, ACGME-accredited since 2009, trains physiatrists in sports and musculoskeletal medicine with sports-board-certified physiatrists, orthopaedic surgeons and Sacramento River Cats team physicians.
