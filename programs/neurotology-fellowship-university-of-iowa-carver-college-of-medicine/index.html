Overview

Two-year ACGME-accredited neurotology fellowship progressing from surgical approaches in year one to tumour removal and cranial nerve conservation surgery in year two, with one research day per week.

The programme

Training type

Duration

Department
Department of Otolaryngology–Head and Neck Surgery

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Graduates of an ACGME-accredited otolaryngology–head and neck surgery residency
International applicants
Institution policyInternational graduates may train at UI Health Care on GMEC-approved visas (ECFMG J-1, H-1B, etc.) if they meet Iowa licensure and USMLE requirements; acceptance of visa holders is at department discretion
Visa support
Institution policy (UI Health Care GMEC): acceptable visas are ECFMG J-1, J-2 with EAD, H-1B, H-4 with EAD, E-3 (Australians only) and F-1 OPT (first year only); J-1 requires USMLE Steps 1-2, H-1B requires Steps 1-3; no department is obliged to accept visa holders
Licence requirement
Institution policyAn Iowa resident physician licence (or permanent licence) from the Iowa Board of Medicine is required for any resident/fellow practising in Iowa and must be obtained before training begins
