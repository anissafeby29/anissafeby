Psychosocial Oncology Fellowship

University Health Network (Princess Margaret Cancer Centre) / University of Toronto

PsychiatryPsycho-oncologyClinical Fellowship
Destination
CAN
Toronto, Ontario, Canada
Open official programme page ↗Compare saved →

Overview

One- and two-year fellowships for psychiatrists in the psychiatric care of seriously ill cancer patients, working with palliative care and cancer rehabilitation teams.

The programme

Focus
Psycho-oncology / consultation-liaison
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 or 2 years
Start
July 1 preferred (alternative dates negotiable)
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
University of Toronto PGME-registered fellowship (not RCPSC-accredited subspecialty training)
Department
Department of Supportive Care, University Health Network

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Medical degree from a recognised school and specialist designation in psychiatry; must meet CPSO minimum licensure eligibility
International applicants
Yes — internationally trained specialists eligible if they meet CPSO requirements; English proficiency results may be requested; government-sponsored trainees from Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, UAE and Saudi Arabia apply via STAR
Visa support
Applicants requiring a work permit must apply 6–8 months ahead to allow immigration, PGME and CPSO processing
Licence requirement
CPSO educational (or independent) licence eligibility required

How to apply

Deadline
Rolling; complete package 6–8 months before start for applicants needing a work permit (e.g. January 1 for July 1), 4 months for Canadian citizens/PRs
Application method
University of Toronto Psychiatry Fellowship Program online application (documents.med.utoronto.ca) with CV, MD and specialty certificates and 3 references (one from residency programme director)
