Overview

Observation of clinics, rounds, surgery and laboratories or on-the-job advanced clinical training.

The programme

Focus
Head and neck, burn, trauma, breast, microsurgery, microtia, ptosis
Training type
Observership and Advanced Clinical Training (hands-on)
Duration
Observership 1-8 weeks; advanced clinical training 3 months to 2 years
Start
Anytime through the year

Observership: students, residents or employed doctors; advanced training: see JSPRS sheet
International applicants
Yes - listed by the Japan Society of Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery (JSPRS) as accepting overseas fellows/observers
Visa support
Not stated on official page

Hands-on clinical training requires a Certificate of Permission of Advanced Clinical Training (Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare) (advanced clinical training)
