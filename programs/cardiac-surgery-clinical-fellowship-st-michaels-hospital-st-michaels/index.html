Cardiac Surgery Clinical Fellowship – St. Michael's Hospital

St. Michael's Hospital, Unity Health Toronto (University of Toronto)

Overview

Clinical fellowships at a division performing approximately 1,100 open-heart cases a year, including high-risk coronary, complex mitral repair and endovascular aortic arch surgery.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Certified cardiac surgeon in Canada or country of origin, or passed MCCQE
International applicants
Open to Canadian and international surgeons; IMG fellows must complete a 4–12 week Pre-Entry Assessment Period (PEAP); TOEFL/TSE or TOEFL-iBT (93, speaking 24) if medical school instruction was not in English
Visa support
Institution policyinternational clinical fellows need an IRCC work permit; the PGME Office issues an LMIA-exempt offer of employment number and supports the work permit application
Licence requirement
Institution policyclinical fellows must hold a CPSO certificate of registration authorising postgraduate education (educational licence); research fellows do not need CPSO registration
