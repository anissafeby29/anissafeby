Overview

UCSF Department of Ophthalmology clinical cornea fellowship offered as a fourth or fifth post-residency year, recruited through SF Match. Based at the F.I. Proctor Foundation.

The programme

Focus
Cornea & external disease
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (a fourth or fifth year of training beyond residency)
Start
Institution policyMost UCSF residents/fellows are appointed for June-August start dates (off-cycle starts possible)

Not stated on official page

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed an accredited US or RCPSC/CFPC residency; completed USMLEs or MCCEE; eligible for a US Social Security number and California medical licensure
International applicants
Accepted if eligible: accredited US or Canadian residency, USMLE/MCCEE, US Social Security number eligibility and H-1B eligibility (if applicable) required
Visa support
H-1B (applicants must be H-1B eligible if applicable)
Licence requirement
California medical licensure eligibility
