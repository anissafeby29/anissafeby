Overview

Massachusetts General Hospital's Boston programme is included in the American Urogynecologic Society's 2026 national guide to Urogynecology and Reconstructive Pelvic Surgery fellowships.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years (1 research year, 2 clinical years); OB/GYN and Urology applicants
Start
Early August 2027 for the published cycle; confirm with programme
Positions
1 per year
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited (since 2012)
Department
Not stated on official page

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
ACGME- or RCPSC-accredited OB/GYN or Urology residency; eligible for Massachusetts licensure at matriculation
International applicants
Yes - IMGs must hold valid ECFMG certificate at time of application
Visa support
Visa sponsorship available in accordance with institutional policies
Licence requirement
Eligibility for Massachusetts medical licensure at matriculation
