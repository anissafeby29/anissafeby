Overview

One-year EHRA advanced fellowship in CIED therapy incl. biventricular, conduction-system and leadless pacing, S-ICD and lead extraction at an EHRA training centre abroad.

The programme

Focus
Cardiac devices (pacing, ICD, CRT)

Advanced programme: 1 year
Start
Must start before March 2028 (current cycle)

Not stated on official page

EHRA Training Fellowship Programme
Department
Not stated on official page

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Citizen or permanent resident of an ESC member country; CIED specialist with less than 5 years' practice after fellowship; preferably under 40; EHRA member; certified B2 in host language; >50 CIED implants and ≥50 follow-ups
International applicants
Restricted to citizens or permanent residents of ESC member countries (ESC National Cardiac Societies, which include some non-EU countries; see the rules' appendix). Training must be in an ESC member country other than the applicant's own. Final validation subject to visa approval where needed
Visa support
Grant validation subject to visa approval; fellow arranges visa and paperwork
Licence requirement
Certified B2 level in the language required by the host; training status (hands-on or hands-off) per local regulations
