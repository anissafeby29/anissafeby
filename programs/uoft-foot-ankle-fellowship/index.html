Overview

Citywide foot and ankle fellowship rotating through St. Michael's, Toronto Western, Brampton Civic and Scarborough Grace hospitals.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
12 months (3-month rotations across hospitals)
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Division of Orthopaedic Surgery, Department of Surgery

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed accredited North American orthopaedic residency; foreign fellows with comparable training as determined by CPSO
International applicants
Yes - clinical fellows are surgeons from Canada or international countries who already hold orthopaedic specialist status in their country of origin
Visa support
Foreign fellows require a work visa processed by the University of Toronto (at least six months lead time)
Licence requirement
Must qualify for licensure with the College of Physicians and Surgeons of Ontario (CPSO)

Funding
