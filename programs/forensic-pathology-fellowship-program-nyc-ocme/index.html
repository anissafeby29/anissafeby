Forensic Pathology Fellowship Program

Overview

The nation's largest forensic pathology fellowship, at NYC OCME (about 8,000 examinations a year), with an optional ACGME second year in forensic neuropathology and cardiovascular pathology.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start

ACGME-accredited (listed in ERAS)
