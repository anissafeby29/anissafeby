Overview

Chiang Mai University observership-style clinical short course in any department (OPD, wards and operating rooms, 20-30 hours a week) for international medical staff.

The programme

Focus
Multiple specialties (institution-wide observership)
Training type
Observership / short clinical fellowship
Duration
Flexible; ED visa permits up to 90 days per entry (longer programmes require re-applying for an ED visa outside Thailand)
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
International Relations Office, Faculty of Medicine (host department per specialty)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Medical staff with relevant degrees and medical licence, CV, certification letter from home institution; fluent English (intermediate level for non-native speakers)
International applicants
Yes - programme for international medical staff run by the International Relations Office
Visa support
IRO provides official supporting documents for a Non-Immigrant ED visa (max 90 days per entry, not extendable for training)
Licence requirement
Observers only - no scrubbing in or independent clinical activity

How to apply

Deadline
Apply 3-6 months before intended start
Application method
Email application form, photo, passport copy, CV, degrees, licences and home-institution letter to frmedcmu@cmu.ac.th; IRO forwards to the department for approval

Funding

Funding
Self-funded - fees depend on specialty, activities and MOU/MOA; exclude living costs; health insurance of at least 100,000 USD required
