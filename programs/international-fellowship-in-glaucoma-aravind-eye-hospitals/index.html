Overview

One-year international fellowship covering diagnosis and management of primary, secondary and complicated glaucomas in patients of all ages under faculty supervision.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Postgraduate degree in ophthalmology from a recognised university; nomination of the candidate by the head of their institution is mandatory
International applicants
Yes - listed under 'Long term Fellowship Programmes (International)'
Visa support
Page states international trainees must obtain a student/entry/internship visa (not tourist visa) for the entire training period; FRRO registration on arrival. No sponsorship stated.
Licence requirement
Temporary medical licence from India's National Medical Commission (NMC) is mandatory for international ophthalmologists undergoing surgical training; NMC application processed by the institute
