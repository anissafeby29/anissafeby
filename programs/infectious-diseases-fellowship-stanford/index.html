ACGME-accredited infectious-diseases training with research, clinician-educator, antimicrobial-stewardship and hospital-epidemiology pathways.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
MD/DO; completed accredited internal medicine residency by enrolment; US citizen, permanent resident, H-1B or J-1 holder; FMGs submit ECFMG certificate
International applicants
Accepted if H-1B or J-1 visa holders with ECFMG certificate
Visa support
Applicants should be US citizens, permanent residents, H-1B or J-1 visa holders

California medical licence (Medical Board of California) required; IMGs need ECFMG certification
