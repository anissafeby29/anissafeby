Overview

A 4-semester anaesthesiology subspecialist (Sp2) track in paediatric anaesthesia and critical care at FK-KMK UGM for anaesthesiology and intensive therapy specialists.

Focus
Paediatric anaesthesia and critical care

4 semesters

January 2027 intake (new-student lecture 16 December 2026); FK-KMK UGM runs January and July admission periods

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Indonesian specialist (Sp1) in anaesthesiology and intensive therapy (Sp.An-TI); specialist transcript GPA >= 3.00 (programme accredited A/B); English test AcEPT >= 209 or TOEP PLTI >= 45/450; aptitude test PAPs UGM or TPDA PLTI >= 500; recommendation letter or competence certificate from the relevant collegium; valid STR; certificate of completion of the specialist deployment programme (formerly WKDS) or at least 1 year of clinical service; 2 confidential online references. Maximum age 50 at start (older applicants need a special recommendation from a hospital director, head of health office or the programme head).

How to apply

Deadline
January 2027 intake: online registration 14-27 September 2026 via admissions.ugm.ac.id; documents emailed to the programme 12-23 October 2026; tests 24-30 October 2026; results 4 December 2026
Application method
Online registration at admissions.ugm.ac.id, then email the selection documents (PDF with printed online registration proof) to the programme (khotimah.suci.u@ugm.ac.id; 0878-3981-2240); academic test, MMPI, health test and programme interview
