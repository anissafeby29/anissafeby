Overview

AUPO-accredited training in optic-nerve, visual-pathway, ocular-motility, pupil and higher cortical visual disorders.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
July
Positions
1 or 2 per year depending on qualified candidates and funding
Accreditation
AUPO (first AUPO-accredited neuro-ophthalmology programme)
Department
Not stated on official page

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed ophthalmology or neurology residency; international candidates must meet ECFMG and USMLE requirements
International applicants
Yes - exceptionally qualified FMGs with ophthalmology residency abroad, valid ECFMG certificate and USMLE Steps 1-3 may be considered
Visa support
Institution policyJohns Hopkins SOM sponsors incoming residents/fellows for J-1 (ECFMG); H-1B only in limited cases (US MD graduates on OPT in ACGME training, or trainees already on H-1B in ACGME-accredited/recognised training with sufficient H-1B time remaining) - GME policy P&F028
Licence requirement
Institution policy (Maryland Board of Physicians): residents and fellows train as registered unlicensed practitioners through their programme; a full Maryland licence requires 1 year (US/Canadian grads) or ECFMG + 2 years (IMGs) of accredited postgraduate training plus licensing exams
