Overview

Society of Surgical Oncology–approved one-year breast oncology fellowship at the NCI-designated Stanford Cancer Institute emphasising interdisciplinary and longitudinal breast cancer care.

The programme

Duration

Institution policyStanford GME contracts are issued per academic year (1 July - 30 June); standard start late June/1 July

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of an approved US surgical residency; board-eligible or board-certified
International applicants
Not eligible — applicants must be US citizens or permanent residents
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 (ECFMG) is the primary visa; H-1B permitted only in some circumstances with GME approval; O-1 rarely, varies by department
Licence requirement
Valid California medical licence required before starting
