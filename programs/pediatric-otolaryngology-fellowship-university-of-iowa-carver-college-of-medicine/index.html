Overview

Broad academic paediatric otolaryngology fellowship with in-depth cleft and craniofacial surgery, airway reconstruction, paediatric otology/neurotology including cochlear implantation and head and neck tumours.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
For the ACGME position: graduates of an ACGME-accredited OHNS residency in the US or Canada (incl. osteopathic graduates)
International applicants
ACGME position limited to US/Canadian ACGME-accredited residency graduates
Visa support
Institution policy (UI Health Care GMEC): acceptable visas are ECFMG J-1, J-2 with EAD, H-1B, H-4 with EAD, E-3 (Australians only) and F-1 OPT (first year only); J-1 requires USMLE Steps 1-2, H-1B requires Steps 1-3; no department is obliged to accept visa holders
Licence requirement
Institution policyAn Iowa resident physician licence (or permanent licence) from the Iowa Board of Medicine is required for any resident/fellow practising in Iowa and must be obtained before training begins
