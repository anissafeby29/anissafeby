Sports and Spine Fellowship

University of Toronto (Division of Physical Medicine & Rehabilitation)

Physical Medicine & RehabilitationSports medicineClinical Fellowship
Destination
CAN
Toronto, Ontario, Canada
Open official programme page ↗Compare saved →

Overview

Multidisciplinary fellowship for PM&R physicians in sports medicine and spine care emphasising ultrasound- and fluoroscopy-guided procedures, teaching and research methodology.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
Minimum 1 year (no fellowships shorter than 12 months; some offer a second year)
Start
1 July of the following year
Positions
Varies by year (number of positions may vary)
Accreditation
Non-accredited; does not lead to RCPSC certification
Department
Division of Physical Medicine & Rehabilitation, Department of Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed PM&R residency (some fellowships open to complementary specialties or family medicine); board certified or board eligible before starting
International applicants
Yes — international applicants accepted; positions offered initially to Canadian citizens/PRs per immigration rules. IMGs must pass the CPSO Pre-Entry Assessment Program (PEAP, 4-12 weeks), have degree source-verified via physiciansapply.ca and, if training was not in English, TOEFL-iBT 93 (speaking 24) or IELTS Academic 7.0 in each band. Gulf-sponsored applicants (Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, UAE) apply via UofT PGME STAR.
Visa support
Work permit supported by UofT PGME after acceptance (can take up to 8 months)
Licence requirement
CPSO educational licence (postgraduate certificate of registration) required; CMPA malpractice coverage

How to apply

Deadline
Applications 1 July - 31 August each year for the following academic year
Application method
Email application form, CV, cover letter, passport, degree documents and three structured reference letters to the Fellowship Coordinator (Toronto Rehabilitation Institute, UHN)

Funding

Funding
Limited Division-funded positions; applicants advised to seek own institutional/government funding. Non-Canadian self/sponsor-funded fellows need minimum CAD $85,564.01 per year (PGY-3 scale, from 1 July 2025)
