Overview

Royal College-accredited two-year pediatric critical care programme based at BC Children's Hospital, with training divided between the CICU and MSICU plus research and electives.

Division of Critical Care, BC Children's Hospital

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Canadian trainees who completed a Royal College programme in Anesthesiology, Pediatric Cardiac Surgery, Emergency Medicine or Pediatric General Surgery, or in PGY3 of Pediatrics (via CaRMS); other entry considered individually; PALS and ACLS required
International applicants
International applicants should refer to the programme's Application Process information on categories of training
Visa support
Institution policy (UBC PGME): foreign trainees must hold an IRCC work permit for the whole training period; the sponsoring department/PGME submits an offer of employment to IRCC and the fellow applies under the LMIA-exempt category
Licence requirement
Licensure with the College of Physicians and Surgeons of British Columbia (CPSBC) required (postgraduate/educational licence for fellows; CPSBC English-language proficiency requirements apply)

Funding

Funding
Institution policyInstitution policy (UBC PGME): fellows are not covered by the Resident Doctors of BC agreement; a written Training Agreement with the sponsoring Health Authority/University sets pay; $500 PGME administrative fee plus annual CPSBC licence fee; self-funded or unfunded postgraduate trainees are not eligible
