Overview

The Stanford Abdominal/Body Imaging Fellowship is a one-year clinical fellowship that provides structured training and broad exposure to abdominal/body imaging.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (up to two fellows may train for two years via the Stanford Cancer Imaging Training (SCIT) programme)
Start
2028–2029 academic year (applications open 1 September 2026)
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Non-accredited program (stated on official page)
Department
Department of Radiology, Stanford University School of Medicine (Body Imaging Division)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Successful completion of an ACGME radiology residency in a US or Canadian program.
International applicants
Applicants must have completed an ACGME radiology residency in a US or Canadian program.
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 (ECFMG) is the primary visa; H-1B permitted only in some circumstances with GME approval; O-1 rarely, varies by department
Licence requirement
California Medical License required prior to start of fellowship.

How to apply

Deadline
Not stated on official page
Application method
Online application system; CV, personal statement, USMLE (or equivalent) scores and 3 letters of recommendation (one from program director).
