Overview

A three-year ACGME-accredited programme at an NCI-designated comprehensive cancer centre training clinician-scientists in cancer and blood disorders.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Graduate of a medical school recognised by the Medical Board of California; completed ACGME-approved residency; ABIM board eligible/certified; eligible for California medical licence

Graduates of schools outside US/Canada must submit a valid ECFMG certificate (programme page)
