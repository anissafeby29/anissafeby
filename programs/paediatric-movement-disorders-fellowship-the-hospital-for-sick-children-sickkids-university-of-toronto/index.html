Overview

Fellowship in paediatric movement disorders at SickKids.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (extendable to 2)
Start
Institution policyUniversity of Toronto PGME academic year runs 1 July - 30 June (letters of appointment issued per academic year; off-cycle starts possible)
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Division of Neurology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed neurology/child neurology residency (or PhD), fluent English, interview
International applicants
International applicants accepted; IELTS or TOEFL required if English is not first language; passport required
Visa support
Institution policyinternational clinical fellows need an IRCC work permit; the PGME Office issues an LMIA-exempt offer of employment number and supports the work permit application
Licence requirement
Institution policyclinical fellows must hold a CPSO certificate of registration authorising postgraduate education (educational licence); research fellows do not need CPSO registration

How to apply

Deadline
Not stated on official page
Application method
Online Neurology Fellowship Application Form (surveys.sickkids.ca) with CV, letter of intent, medical degree (notarised translation if needed), transcripts, specialty certificate, English proficiency test if needed, passport, 3 reference letters

Funding

Funding
Institution policyclinical fellows must be funded (department or sponsoring agency); FEAC recommends pay at least equal to PGY1 on the PARO salary scale, and foreign nationals must be paid at a rate commensurate with a Canadian in the same role
