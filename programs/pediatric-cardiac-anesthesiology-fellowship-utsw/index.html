Overview

A 12-month ACGME-accredited fellowship at Children’s Health Dallas covering the perioperative management of congenital heart disease from neonates to adults, including VADs, ECMO and transplantation.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed or enrolled in an ACGME-accredited pediatric anesthesia fellowship; alternative pathways (e.g. after adult cardiothoracic fellowship) considered individually
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs must be ECFMG-certified and hold an ECFMG-sponsored J-1 visa for clinical training at UTSW
Visa support
Not currently accepting or sponsoring training visas
Licence requirement
State requirement: Texas Medical Board Physician-in-Training (PIT) permit for participation in a Board-approved GME/fellowship programme (supervised practice only)
