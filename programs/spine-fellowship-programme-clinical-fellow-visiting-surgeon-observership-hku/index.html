Overview

Spine surgery fellowships, visiting surgeon posts and observerships for overseas surgeons at HKU / Queen Mary Hospital.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship / Visiting Surgeon / Observership
Duration
Clinical fellow 6 months; visiting surgeon/researcher up to 3 months; observership 2-4 weeks
Start
Clinical fellows: 1 January and 1 July; others flexible
Positions
2 six-month clinical fellowships per year
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Division of Spine Surgery

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Medical degree recognised by the Medical Council of Hong Kong; clinical fellows must have completed specialist orthopaedic training or equivalent
International applicants
Yes - welcomes overseas doctors and researchers
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Clinical fellow offers contingent on limited registration by the Medical Council of Hong Kong

How to apply

Deadline
One year before fellowship start
Application method
Full CV, cover letter with requested timing and reference letters

Funding

Funding
Clinical fellow HK$10,000-20,000/month; visiting surgeon HK$100/day plus accommodation; observers pay HK$100 per working day (waiver possible)
