Advanced Interventional Radiology Fellowship in Neurointervention

National University Hospital (NUHS)

RadiologyInterventional neuroradiologyClinical Fellowship
Destination
SGP
Singapore
Open official programme page ↗Compare saved →

Overview

Advanced neurointervention fellowship in a 24/7 endovascular stroke service (>100 thrombectomies and ≈500 therapeutic neurointerventions a year).

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
6 or 12 months
Start
January or July intake
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
NUH clinical fellowship (SMC requirements)
Department
Department of Diagnostic Imaging

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed one year of interventional radiology fellowship or equivalent, or (primary applicants) residency training in radiology, neurology or neurosurgery; plus NUH general eligibility
International applicants
Yes — must be formally sponsored by their government, regional health authority or home institution
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Singapore Medical Council registration (temporary registration for clinical fellowship)

How to apply

Deadline
Apply at least 18 months before preferred attachment date
Application method
Download NUH fellowship application form; submit with CV, photo and supporting documents 18 months before preferred start

Funding

Funding
No financial commitment by the hospital or department; fellows must be sponsored by home government/health authority/institution
