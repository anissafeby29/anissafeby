Overview

Hands-on NNI fellowship exposing the fellow to neuro-oncology, neurovascular, spine, skull base, peripheral nerve, functional neurosurgery, TBI and neurocritical care, with a research project.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Young neurosurgeon residing in a developing country; recently completed residency; accredited basic medical degree (SMC requirement); national licensing exam passed; SMC English requirement; 12 months' internship; minimum 3 years as medical officer; registered and in good standing
International applicants
Yes - targeted at young neurosurgeons from developing countries

Not stated on official page
