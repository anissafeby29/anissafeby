Fellowship in Paediatric Haematology-Oncology (Fellowship en hématologie-oncologie pédiatrique)

CHU Sainte-Justine / Université de Montréal

Overview

Three fellowship tracks: full 3-year paediatric haematology-oncology, 2-year focused track, and 2-year paediatric cellular therapy (HSCT/CAR-T).

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Institution policyMD from FAIMER/IMED-listed school, specialist certificate recognised in home country, sufficient French, CMQ requirements met, and proof of non-private bursary of at least CAD 40,000/yr (CAD 50,000 from 1 Jul 2028)
International applicants
Foreign physicians admitted as 'moniteurs cliniques' via Université de Montréal Postdoctoral Medical Studies (start process at least 12 months ahead); sufficient French is mandatory (TEF or equivalent if not exempt)
Visa support
Institution policyFellows (moniteurs) from outside Canada need a temporary work permit; Université de Montréal provides the job-offer letter submitted to IRCC
Licence requirement
Letter of eligibility from the Collège des médecins du Québec required

How to apply

Deadline
Not stated on official page
Application method
Institution policyContact Université de Montréal Vice-décanat aux études médicales postdoctorales (coordination-moniteurs@med.umontreal.ca) with CV at least 12 months ahead; formal admission via VD-EMPD

Funding

Funding
Fellows are not paid under the FMRQ agreement; CHU Sainte-Justine runs a competitive matching-funds bursary (CAD 40,000 for 12 months) for foreign candidates
