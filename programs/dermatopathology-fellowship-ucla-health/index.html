Overview

One-year jointly sponsored dermatopathology fellowship with daily sign-out of challenging consults and high routine volume, plus immunohistochemistry and molecular pathology.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship

1 year

Institution policy (UCLA GME): appointments run 1 July to 30 June

3 per year

Accredited (ACGME-approved)
Department
Department of Pathology and Laboratory Medicine & Division of Dermatology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Board eligible in AP, AP/CP or dermatology; California medical licence required
International applicants
Institution policy (UCLA GME, ACGME programmes): IMGs eligible with ECFMG certification (USMLE Steps 1-2) or a full California licence; all IMG applicants must be reviewed by the GME office/DIO before an offer and must meet California licensure requirements to continue training
Visa support
Institution policy (UCLA GME, ACGME programmes): ECFMG-sponsored clinical J-1 is the preferred visa; H-1B considered only for US LCME/AOA graduates moving from F-1 OPT, trainees already in clinical training on H-1B with enough time left, or physicians of national/international renown (department-funded, case by case)
Licence requirement
Institution policy (UCLA GME): all residents/fellows must comply with Medical Board of California (or Osteopathic Board) licensure requirements - a PTL or full California licence - at appointment and reappointment; non-compliance means non-appointment; fellows from outside institutions must have passed all USMLE/COMLEX steps
