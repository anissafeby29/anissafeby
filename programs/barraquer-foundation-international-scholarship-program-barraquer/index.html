Overview

Three funded 3-month tailored training stays a year at the Barraquer Ophthalmology Centre with a personal tutor, for young ophthalmologists with limited resources worldwide.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Degree in medicine with ophthalmology specialty (or qualified ophthalmic technician); residency completed within last two years or in final year; good English (Spanish valued); recommendation letter
International applicants
Yes - open to young ophthalmologists with few resources from any country in the world
