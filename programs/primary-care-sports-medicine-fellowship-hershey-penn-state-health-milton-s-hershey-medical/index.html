Overview

Penn State Hershey's one-year ACGME-accredited primary care sports medicine fellowship covers sports, occupational medicine, cardiac rehabilitation and fitness, with Hershey Bears, Harrisburg Senators, Division II/III college and high school coverage.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Board-eligible or board-certified family medicine candidates who have completed (or are completing) an ACGME-accredited family medicine residency and are ABFM certified or scheduled to sit boards before the start; significant commitment to an academic sports medicine career
