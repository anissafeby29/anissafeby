Overview

A 6-12 month clinical attachment at CUHK's Institute of Digestive Disease for clinicians interested in luminal GI disease, participating in research on ulcer bleeding, GI tumours or IBD alongside endoscopy training.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Clinicians with a specific interest in luminal GI disease and at least 2 years of clinical experience in their current institution.
International applicants
Yes - overseas doctors welcomed; Limited Registration with the Medical Council of Hong Kong required before clinical/patient interaction (about 3-4 months to process).
Visa support
CUHK assists with HKSAR training visa application after signed appointment letter (stay up to 12 months, no extension).
Licence requirement
Limited Registration via the Medical Council of Hong Kong
