Neuro-Ophthalmology Fellowship (Minnesota)

Mayo Clinic School of Graduate Medical Education

OphthalmologyNeuro-ophthalmologyClinical Fellowship
Destination
USA

Open official programme page ↗Compare saved →

Overview

One-year neuro-ophthalmology fellowship drawing on over 100 Mayo neurologists' referral base for common and rare diseases.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (7 July-6 July)
Start
7 July (academic year runs July 7 – July 6)
Positions
1 per year
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Department of Ophthalmology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed accredited ophthalmology or neurology residency; ABO or ABPN certification eligibility
International applicants
International applicants must have equivalent training and have passed the ECFMG examination
Visa support
Institution policyECFMG J-1 sponsorship and H-1B supported (final visa type chosen by Mayo); new H-1B petitions requiring the $100,000 payment not pursued - H-1B only for change/extension of status, USMLE Steps 1-3 and ECFMG certification required
Licence requirement
Institution policyMinnesota-based programmes: Minnesota residency permit required, and eligible trainees must obtain and maintain a full Minnesota medical licence

How to apply

Deadline
1 September (SF Match; applications open in June)
Application method
AUPO Ophthalmology Fellowship Match (SF Match) neuro-ophthalmology match

Funding

Funding
Institution policyMayo Clinic School of GME stipends effective 11 March 2026: Graduate Level 4 $85,398; Level 5 $89,036; Level 6 $92,376; Level 7 $95,417 (Level 1 $75,105)
