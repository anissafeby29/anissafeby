Overview

Medical, surgical and academic training in paediatric ophthalmology and adult strabismus at the Wilmer Eye Institute’s tertiary children’s eye centre.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed ophthalmology residency; match and Maryland licensing requirements apply
International applicants
Yes - applications from other countries encouraged; ECFMG certificate and USMLE Steps 1-3 required
Visa support
Institution policyJohns Hopkins SOM sponsors incoming residents/fellows for J-1 (ECFMG); H-1B only in limited cases (US MD graduates on OPT in ACGME training, or trainees already on H-1B in ACGME-accredited/recognised training with sufficient H-1B time remaining) - GME policy P&F028
Licence requirement
US-trained fellows obtain a Maryland medical licence and junior faculty appointment; IMGs need ECFMG and USMLE Steps 1-3
