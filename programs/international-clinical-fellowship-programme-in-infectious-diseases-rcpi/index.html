International Clinical Fellowship Programme in Infectious Diseases

Royal College of Physicians of Ireland (RCPI)

Internal MedicineInfectious diseasesClinical Fellowship
Destination
IRL
Dublin (placements in accredited hospitals across Ireland), Various, Ireland
Open official programme page ↗

Overview

A two-year RCPI curriculum-based clinical fellowship in infectious diseases for government-sponsored international doctors in accredited Irish hospitals.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
2 years
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
RCPI Certificate of Achievement of Clinical Fellowship Training; curriculum approved by RCPI Institute of Medicine
Department
RCPI Institute of Medicine (training in HSE-accredited hospitals)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Primary medical degree through English; structured internship; completed 4/5-year national residency programme and examinations (e.g. Saudi or Arab Board); at least one year post-residency experience in chosen specialty; IELTS Academic 7.0 overall (6.5 each) or OET B in all domains (mandatory after offer)
International applicants
Open only to citizens of Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia and UAE nominated and sponsored by their government body (per RCPI ICFP page)
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Must be eligible for registration with the Irish Medical Council (Supervised Division)

How to apply

Deadline
Recruitment currently closed; register interest via RCPI (as of page)
Application method
Application via RCPI Global Training and Partnerships (internationalaffairs@rcpi.ie)

Funding

Funding
Full government sponsorship required (covering fees and living expenses); self-sponsored candidates not eligible
