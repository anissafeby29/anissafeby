Overview

A UCNS fellowship for neurologists or psychiatrists offering a one-year clinical or two-year clinical/research track in behavioural neurology and neuropsychiatry.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (clinical) or 2 years (clinical/research)
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
1–2 per year
Accreditation
UCNS-accredited
Department
Department of Neurology, University of Washington

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Current unrestricted licence in the US/territories or Canada; ABMS, RCPSC, AOA or CFPC board certified/eligible in neurology, child neurology or psychiatry; full Washington licence before start
International applicants
IMGs may apply with ECFMG certificate (if applicable)
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Full unrestricted Washington medical licence before start

How to apply

Deadline
Rolling until filled (accepting for 2027–2028)
Application method
