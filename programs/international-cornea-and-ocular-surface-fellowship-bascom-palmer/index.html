Overview

One-year advanced clinical programme for international ophthalmologists seeking comprehensive experience in cornea, ocular surface and anterior segment surgery, with four clinic days and one OR day per week.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Valid Florida State medical licence; completion of all three parts of USMLE; ECFMG certification (for J-1 visa applicants)
International applicants
Yes - designed for international ophthalmologists
Visa support
ECFMG certification required for J-1 visa applicants (J-1 implied)
Licence requirement
Unrestricted Florida medical license required before starting; page states all 3 USMLE steps passed and 2 years of supervised US training are needed for a Florida licence
