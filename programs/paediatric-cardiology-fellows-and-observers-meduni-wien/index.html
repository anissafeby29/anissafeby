Overview

Vienna Children's Heart Center welcomes national and international clinical and research fellows and observers in paediatric cardiology.

The programme

Training type
Observership / Clinical or Research Fellowship
Duration
Observer: up to 6 months; Fellow: 3–12 months
Start
Rolling

Not stated on official page

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Medical graduates (minimum Master's degree)
International applicants
Yes – open to medical graduates from around the world; processing up to 4 months depending on nationality and legal requirements
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Observers: no hands-on. Fellows: supervised involvement

How to apply

Deadline
Rolling
Application method
Contact the division; approval via MedUni International Affairs

Funding

Funding
Observers unpaid; fellows: Not employment. Clinical fellowship tuition €29,000 per 12 months (may be waived on justification) plus €700 processing fee; home institution or grant must finance the fellow
