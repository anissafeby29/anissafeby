Vascular Neurology Fellowship

Mayo Clinic — Minnesota

NeurologyStrokeClinical Fellowship
Destination
USA
Rochester, Minnesota, United States
Open official programme page ↗Compare saved →

Overview

A one-year ACGME fellowship in Rochester training neurologists in the evaluation, diagnosis and management of stroke and all types of cerebrovascular disorders.

The programme

Focus
Stroke / vascular neurology
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
July
Positions
Page states two positions in one section and one position offered annually in another — confirm with programme
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited
Department
Department of Neurology, Mayo Clinic School of Graduate Medical Education

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of an accredited neurology or physical medicine residency in the United States or Canada
International applicants
Open to international applicants; graduates of medical schools outside the US/Canada must hold ECFMG certification before appointment
Visa support
Mayo Clinic supports ECFMG J-1 and H-1B sponsorship (J-1 requires an ACGME-accredited/recognised programme; no new H-1B petitions requiring the US$100,000 payment); final visa type at Mayo's discretion
Licence requirement
Fellows must obtain and maintain an appropriate medical licence while enrolled

How to apply

Deadline
January 15, approximately 18 months before the start date
Application method
ERAS application; appointments through the Vascular Neurology Match (NRMP); interviews January–March by invitation

Funding

Funding
Salaried trainee position (stipend and benefits per Mayo Clinic School of GME)
