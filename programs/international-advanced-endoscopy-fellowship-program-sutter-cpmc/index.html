Overview

An international advanced endoscopy programme at CPMC for endoscopists already experienced in diagnostic EUS and ERCP, selected on documented procedure experience and narrated EUS/ERCP videos.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Medical school diploma; diploma in medical or surgical GI subspecialty training; letter of competency in GI endoscopy; home-country medical licence; fluency in English; Ministry of Health good-standing letter; strong foundation and independent primary-operator experience in diagnostic EUS and ERCP.
International applicants
Yes - programme is designed for international gastroenterologists/endoscopists (home-country licence and Ministry of Health letter required).
