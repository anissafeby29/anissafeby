EACTS Pulmonary Thromboendarterectomy Fellowship

European CTEPH centres – Papworth, Leuven, Pavia, Vienna, Bad Nauheim or Paris (EACTS Francis Fontan Fund)

Cardiothoracic SurgeryAdult cardiac surgeryTravelling Fellowship (society-funded clinical placement)
Destination
EUR

Open official programme page ↗

Overview

One-month stay in a European CTEPH centre covering surgical indications, deep hypothermic circulatory arrest technique and perioperative/ICU management.

Focus

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Early-career surgeons with significant experience (not residents or recent graduates); experienced in standard cardiothoracic surgery and extracorporeal circulation; high possibility of creating a CTEPH unit; EACTS member; fluent English
International applicants
Open to EACTS members; the page sets no nationality or EU-residency restriction (EACTS membership is international). Fluent English required.

How to apply

Deadline
Application window 1 March – 1 May 2026 (2026/27 cycle closed)
Application method
Online application to the EACTS Francis Fontan Fund with letter of support, letter of interest, CV with publications and operative experience (documents in English, Word or PDF)

Funding

Funding
Grant of €4,000 plus EACTS Annual Meeting bursary
