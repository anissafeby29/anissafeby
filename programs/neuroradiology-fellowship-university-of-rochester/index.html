Overview

A one-year ACGME-certified neuroradiology fellowship covering brain, spine, paediatric and head and neck imaging and interventional neuroradiology.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed an ACGME-accredited DR residency in the US or RCPSC/CFPC-accredited residency in Canada; ABR board eligible/certified; USMLE Steps 1, 2 (and 3 if eligible); permanent residency or US citizenship; valid NY licence for non-accredited programmes.
International applicants
Department requires US permanent residency or citizenship for its fellowship programmes.
Visa support
Not offered (permanent residency or citizenship required).
Licence requirement
Valid New York State licence required for non-accredited programmes.
