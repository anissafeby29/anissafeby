Overview

A two-year administrative fellowship for board-eligible or certified obstetrician-gynecologists combining a clinical fellowship in general obstetrics or gynecology with the Yale School of Management MBA - Leadership in Healthcare program.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Board eligible or certified obstetrician-gynecologist; completed one year of post-graduate experience by start date; must be accepted to the Yale MBA for Executives: Leadership in Healthcare program and be eligible for Yale New Haven Hospital credentialing
International applicants
Institution policyForeign medical graduates accepted into accredited residencies/fellowships; J-1 requires valid ECFMG certificate and Ministry of Health statement of need
Visa support
Institution policyYNHH/YSM GME accepts foreign nationals on J-1 (ECFMG-sponsored), F-1, or H-1B transfers from a current programme
Licence requirement
Institution policyHospital obtains a Connecticut Department of Public Health residency training permit on the trainee's behalf (trainee supplies documentation)
