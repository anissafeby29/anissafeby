Neurosurgery Spine Fellowship (Minnesota)

Mayo Clinic College of Medicine and Science

NeurosurgerySpineClinical Fellowship
Destination
USA
Rochester, Minnesota, United States
Open official programme page ↗

Overview

A one-year clinical fellowship offering advanced training across the full spectrum of spinal surgery, with more than 1,500 spine procedures performed each year.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
1 July (external candidates begin July 8)
Positions
2 per year (one external candidate, one Mayo Clinic PGY-7 enfolded fellow)
Accreditation
Committee on Advanced Subspecialty Training (CAST) of the Society of Neurological Surgeons (since May 2014)
Department
Department of Neurologic Surgery

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of an ACGME-accredited seven-year neurological surgery programme
International applicants
Institution policyGraduates of non-US medical schools eligible if ECFMG-certified before appointment (applicants still obtaining certification may interview but cannot be ranked or appointed) or holding a full unrestricted licence in the programme state; Canadian graduates after 1 July 2025 also need ECFMG certification
Visa support
Institution policyMayo Clinic School of Graduate Medical Education supports ECFMG J-1 and H-1B visas (Mayo selects visa type). Since the Sept 2025 $100,000 H-1B fee, Mayo will not file new H-1B petitions that trigger the fee - H-1B only for change/extension of status (USMLE Steps 1-3 required); otherwise ECFMG J-1 (Steps 1-2, ACGME-accredited/recognised programme). F-1 OPT accepted for first year for US-school graduates
Licence requirement
Institution policyMinnesota-based MCSGME trainees must hold a Minnesota residency permit, and all eligible trainees must obtain and maintain a full Minnesota medical licence (USMLE/COMLEX/MCCQE final exam deadlines apply); Mayo reimburses fees

How to apply

Deadline
1 February, two years prior to the fellowship start date
Application method
Online application through Recruit (third-party application service), followed by interview

Funding

Funding
Institution policyMCSGME resident/fellow stipend (effective 11 March 2026): Graduate Level 1 $75,105; L2 $77,721; L3 $80,936; L4 $85,398; L5 $89,036; L6 $92,376; L7 $95,417; L8 $99,058; L9 $102,594; L10 $106,096
