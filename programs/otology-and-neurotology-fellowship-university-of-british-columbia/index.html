Otology & Neurotology Fellowship

University of British Columbia / BC Rotary Hearing & Balance Centre (St Paul's)

ENT / OtolaryngologyOtology & neurotologyClinical Fellowship
Destination
CAN
Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada
Open official programme page ↗Compare saved →

Overview

One-year clinical fellowship in medical and surgical care of temporal bone and lateral skull base disease, with endoscopic ear surgery exposure, a temporal bone laboratory and an optional research year.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year clinical (optional additional research year)
Start
1 July
Positions
1 per year
Accreditation
Not accredited (no Canadian accreditation process for otology/neurotology fellowships)
Department
Division of Otolaryngology – Head & Neck Surgery, Department of Surgery

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed otolaryngology residency and passed licensing examinations in country of origin
International applicants
Open to Canadian and non-Canadian trained otolaryngologists able to obtain a Canadian training visa and BC educational licence
Visa support
Non-Canadian fellows must obtain a training visa for Canada
Licence requirement
BC educational licence

How to apply

Deadline
Not stated on official page

Funding
