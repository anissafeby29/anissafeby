Overview

Senior clinical fellow post in the regional paediatric rheumatology unit at Evelina London, a tertiary hub for South London, Kent, Surrey and Sussex covering JIA, connective tissue disease, vasculitis and autoinflammatory disease.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
MRCPCH or equivalent; ST4 and above; completed Level 1 (ST1-3) paediatric training or equivalent; APLS/EPLS and child protection training
International applicants
Previous overseas trainees (e.g. Australia, Switzerland) have had training recognised by home regulators; post approved for the Medical Training Initiative
Visa support
Post approved for the Medical Training Initiative programme
Licence requirement
Regulatory requirement: GMC registration with a licence to practise (appropriate registration for the post); international graduates gain full registration via PLAB, GMC-approved sponsorship, an acceptable postgraduate qualification or other routes
