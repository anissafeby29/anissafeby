Overview

A three-year ACGME-accredited pediatric pulmonology fellowship at UT Southwestern and Children's Medical Center Dallas, enrolling since 2011.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years
Start
July 2027 (NRMP Medicine and Pediatric Specialties Match July 2027 appointments)
Positions
Up to 2 per year
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited
Department
Department of Pediatrics, UT Southwestern Medical Center

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Permanent residents or ECFMG-sponsored J-1 (no H-1B)
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs must be ECFMG-certified and hold an ECFMG-sponsored J-1 visa for clinical training at UTSW
Visa support
Institution policyClinical residents/fellows (ACGME and non-ACGME) on J-1 sponsored by ECFMG; UTSW does not usually sponsor H-1B for clinical training
Licence requirement
State requirement: Texas Medical Board Physician-in-Training (PIT) permit for participation in a Board-approved GME/fellowship programme (supervised practice only)

How to apply
