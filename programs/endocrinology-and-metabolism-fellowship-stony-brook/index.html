Overview

A clinically robust two-year endocrinology fellowship centred on Stony Brook University Hospital and the Northport VA, with continuity, FNA/ultrasound and urgent endocrine clinics.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Successful completion of a US three-year internal medicine programme; ERAS application, personal statement, USMLE/COMLEX scores, transcript, 3 letters plus a letter from the IM programme director
International applicants
Graduates of foreign medical schools must provide FMGEMS scores and a Standard ECFMG certificate
Visa support
US citizenship or a valid visa required; H visas are not supported
Licence requirement
All accepted candidates must obtain a New York medical licence

How to apply

Deadline
ERAS opens to programmes 15 July; all documents should be in ERAS by then; interviews August-September (virtual)
Application method
ERAS only; Match
