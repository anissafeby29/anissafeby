Abdominal Imaging Fellowship

Overview

An ACGME-accredited one-year abdominal imaging fellowship of 13 four-week blocks (MRI, ultrasound, body CT, biopsy/colonography, elective) with up to nine fellows each year.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Satisfactory completion of a diagnostic radiology residency accredited by the ACGME or the Royal College of Physicians and Surgeons of Canada; must meet Pennsylvania licensing requirements.
International applicants
Applicants with an ECFMG certificate may apply (ECFMG certificate requested if applicable); how-to-apply page refers to licensure and any applicable visa requirements.
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Must meet Pennsylvania licensing requirements (fellows licensed at PGY-6 or above).

How to apply

Deadline
Not stated on official page
Application method
Penn Radiology Fellowship Application plus CV, personal statement, three letters (one from current PD), medical school transcripts, dean's letter, USMLE reports and ECFMG certificate (if applicable), emailed to the programme. Follows SCARD embargo.
