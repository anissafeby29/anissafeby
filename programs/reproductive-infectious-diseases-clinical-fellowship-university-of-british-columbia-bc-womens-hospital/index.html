Overview

A two-year clinical, microbiologic and research training program in reproductive infectious diseases, endorsed by the Infectious Diseases Society of Obstetrics and Gynaecology.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1–2 years (normally two years)
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Up to one per year, on a funding-available basis
Accreditation
Not a Royal College subspecialty; endorsed by the Infectious Diseases Society of Obstetrics and Gynaecology
Department
Department of Obstetrics & Gynaecology, Faculty of Medicine, University of British Columbia

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Canadian applicants: completed ObGyn training in an RCPSC-accredited program and eligible for RCPSC ObGyn examinations; confirm eligibility for CPSBC registration
International applicants
IMGs considered only if fully sponsored by an external agency (no fellowship funding available; self-funded fellows not accepted)
Visa support
Institution policy (UBC PGME): foreign trainees must hold an IRCC work permit for the whole training period; the sponsoring department/PGME submits an offer of employment to IRCC and the fellow applies under the LMIA-exempt category
Licence requirement
Must be licensed by the College of Physicians and Surgeons of British Columbia (CPSBC)

How to apply

Deadline
Applications accepted year round
Application method
Personal statement, CV and three reference letters emailed to the Sr. Program Assistant (lauren.ritchie@cw.bc.ca); IMGs via sponsoring agency/UBC PGME

Funding

Funding
Institution policy (UBC PGME): fellows are not covered by the Resident Doctors of BC agreement; a written Training Agreement with the sponsoring Health Authority/University sets pay; $500 PGME administrative fee plus annual CPSBC licence fee; self-funded or unfunded postgraduate trainees are not eligible
