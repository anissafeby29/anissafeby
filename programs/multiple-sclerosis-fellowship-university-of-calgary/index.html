Overview

Individualised one- and two-year MS fellowships for Canadian and internationally trained neurologists combining clinical MS care with research.

The programme

Focus
Neuroimmunology / multiple sclerosis
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (60–70% clinical) or 2 years (~70% research)
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
University of Calgary PGME clinical fellowship (not RCPSC-accredited)
Department
Department of Clinical Neurosciences, University of Calgary

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Canadian or internationally trained neurologists with credentials for a CPSA educational licence; CPSA English proficiency if trained in another language
International applicants
Yes — open to internationally trained neurologists; sponsored fellowships with home-country salary funding considered
Visa support
IMGs who are not Canadian citizens/PRs need a Canadian work permit (processing at least 10 months for international applicants)
Licence requirement
CPSA educational licence (Provisional Register – Postgraduate Trainee) via UCalgary PGME

How to apply

Deadline
Allow at least 10 months (international) / 6 months (domestic) for licensing and permits
Application method
Not stated on official page

Funding

Funding
Limited; Canadian Network of MS Clinics (Canadian-trained), MSIF McDonald Fellowships, sponsor-funded positions
