Overview

A comprehensive abdominal imaging fellowship at Canada's largest regional trauma centre with a strong oncology focus, covering ultrasound, multidetector CT, MRI and US/CT-guided biopsies and drainages.

The programme

Focus
Abdominal imaging
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
2028-2029 fellowship year (current UofT Medical Imaging application cycle: applications 6 April-9 August 2026)
Positions
4 positions annually
Accreditation
University of Toronto Department of Medical Imaging fellowship (certificate)
Department
Department of Medical Imaging, Sunnybrook Health Sciences Centre

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Proficient in written and spoken English; completed an accredited radiology training programme; certified by the Royal College of Physicians and Surgeons of Canada in Diagnostic Imaging, American Board of Radiology certified/eligible, or equivalent qualifications (CPSO uses the GMC directory to recognise specialty certification).
International applicants
Eligible with equivalent radiology certification; Sponsored positions available for trainees funded by agencies from Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia and UAE.
Visa support
Institution policyinternational clinical fellows need an IRCC work permit; the PGME Office issues an LMIA-exempt offer of employment number and supports the work permit application
Licence requirement
Institution policyclinical fellows must hold a CPSO certificate of registration authorising postgraduate education (educational licence); research fellows do not need CPSO registration

How to apply

Deadline
2028–2029 cycle: online applications opened 6 April 2026 and closed 9 August 2026; reference letters due 23 August 2026; interviews September 2026 – January 2027.
Application method
Online Fellowship Application System (University of Toronto Department of Medical Imaging): CV, candidate letter, proof of certification and three reference letters (one from the residency programme director), all in PDF; up to 2 programmes on one form.

Funding

Funding
Applicants must secure CAD $85,564.01/annum as training salary; positions are Funded (supervisor, hospital, grant, endowment or international agency) or Sponsored (agencies from Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, UAE only); self-funding from personal funds is not acceptable.
