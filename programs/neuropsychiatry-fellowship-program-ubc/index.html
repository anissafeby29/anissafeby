Neuropsychiatry Fellowship Program

University of British Columbia Department of Psychiatry

Overview

A two-year neuropsychiatry and behavioural neurology fellowship with rotations at UBC, Vancouver General, St Paul's and GF Strong, then a year at UBC Hospital with Fraser outreach clinics.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Postgraduate clinical fellows: physicians registered as specialists in psychiatry (or related discipline) in their home country
International applicants
Yes — non-Canadian international applicants accepted via UBC Faculty of Medicine PGME (separate process); internationally sponsored (visa) trainee route available where a home-country organisation funds fees and salary
Visa support
Non-Canadians complete the immigration/work permit process after an offer letter (via UBC PGME); up to 6 months
Licence requirement
Licence from the College of Physicians & Surgeons of British Columbia required
