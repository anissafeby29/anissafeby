Overview

A one-year non-tenured breast imaging instructorship offering screening and diagnostic breast imaging, MRI and interventions, with electives January–June.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Passed the ABR Core Exam and eligible for the Certifying Exam (or equivalent); able to obtain a Virginia licence.
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs eligible with a valid ECFMG certificate; J-1 sponsored institutionally (other visas per department)
Visa support
Institution policyUVA institutionally sponsors the J-1 clinical visa; sponsorship of other visa types is decided at department level
Licence requirement
Must be able to obtain a license to practise medicine in Virginia.

How to apply

Deadline
Applications not accepted before 1 November 2026 for 2028-2029 positions (SCARD); interviews 11 January-31 March 2027
Application method
NRMP Radiology Match (Breast Imaging); SBI recommends applying via FellowshipsCAS

Funding

Funding
Instructor salary; optional nightly coverage supplements income (around $2,000 per night).
