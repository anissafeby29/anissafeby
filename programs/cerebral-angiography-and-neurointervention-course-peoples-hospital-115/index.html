Overview

Six-month programme at People's Hospital 115 in cerebral DSA and neurovascular intervention, ending with theory and practical exams for a Ministry of Health professional technique certificate.

The programme

Training type
Clinical training course (certificate; Vietnamese continuing medical education / professional technique certificate)
Duration
6 months (960 periods: 60 theory, 900 practice)
Start
2025 intakes: 17 February 2025 and June 2025
Positions
5-10 per class
Accreditation
Certificate issued under Vietnamese Ministry of Health continuing-training regulations
Department
Training Unit / Department of Diagnostic Imaging

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Doctors in diagnostic imaging, neurology, neurosurgery or emergency/critical care with a practising certificate and an orientation to neurovascular intervention
International applicants
Not stated on official page
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Vietnamese medical practising certificate (chứng chỉ hành nghề / giấy phép hành nghề) required

How to apply

Deadline
Course 1: 30 January 2025 (closed); later intakes per announcement
Application method
Dossier (nomination letter, notarised degree and practising certificate, registration form, photos) to the Training Unit; pre-register by email dtlt.bvnd115@gmail.com
