Overview

A CAST-approved 12-month functional fellowship providing training in the diagnosis and management of movement disorders, epilepsy, spasticity and craniofacial pain.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of an ACGME-approved neurosurgery residency in the United States or Canada before the start of the fellowship, or meet the criteria for an unrestricted (non-training) Missouri medical licence
International applicants
Institution policyInternational graduates accepted via ECFMG J-1 sponsorship (H-1B at programme discretion); Missouri temporary licence requires a permanent ECFMG certificate
Visa support
Institution policyWashU GME Consortium supports ECFMG J-1 visas (managed by institutional Training Program Liaisons); H-1B may be supported at department/programme discretion; WashU holds ACGME Non-Standard Training recognition so non-accredited fellowships can host J-1 trainees
Licence requirement
ACGME-approved US/Canadian neurosurgery residency or eligibility for an unrestricted Missouri medical licence

How to apply
