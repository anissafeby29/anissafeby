Overview

Oculoplastic fellowship/observership emphasising East Asian patients with monthly oculoplastics-radiology-pathology conferences and research opportunities; APSOPRS certificate for APSOPRS fellows.

The programme

Training type

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Medical degree and licence, working in an overseas hospital, proficient in English or Korean; APSOPRS track: completed ophthalmology residency, under 40, >=1 first/corresponding-author OPRS article
International applicants
Yes - physicians working in overseas hospitals; APSOPRS fellowship track for applicants from APSOPRS member countries
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Medical degree and licence (home country)

How to apply

Deadline
Year-round; at least 3 months before the start of training
Application method
Via Asan International Visiting Scholars (IViS) programme application; contact preceptor Prof. Ho-Seok Sa
