Overview

Two one-year combined clinical-research PET fellowships in the Division of Nuclear Medicine and Molecular Imaging, with an optional second year; research is required.

Visa support
Institution policyIMGs must be ECFMG-certified and enter training on a J-1 (ECFMG-sponsored) or H-1B visa unless US citizen/green-card holder (Emory GME housestaff policy 5.12)

Eligible for an unrestricted Georgia medical licence
