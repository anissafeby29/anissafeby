Overview

The pediatric radiology fellowship at UCSF Benioff Children's Hospital provides comprehensive training centred on the Mission Bay campus, with additional rotation time at UCSF Benioff Children's Hospital Oakland.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of a four-year diagnostic radiology residency from an accredited program.
International applicants
Graduates of medical schools outside the US or Canada must hold a valid ECFMG certificate at the time of application, be eligible for a California Medical License and have passed USMLE Steps I, II and III (or COMLEX/LMCC equivalent).
Visa support
The University sponsors J-1 ECFMG visas for fellowship positions in the majority of cases.
Licence requirement
Radiology board certification (or board eligibility) and a California Medical License are required prior to appointment.
