Overview

A one-year clinical fellowship in diagnosis and management of primary CNS tumours in children and young adults.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship

1 year clinical (optional 2nd/3rd research year with approval and funding)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed ACGME-accredited pediatric hematology-oncology or pediatric neurology training; international applicants with equivalent qualifications eligible if all USMLE steps completed
International applicants
International applicants with equivalent qualifications eligible if they have passed USMLE Steps 1-3 and CSA and hold a valid ECFMG certificate
Visa support
Institution policyNYU Langone does not offer H-1B sponsorship for residents or fellows; J-1 sponsorship available through ECFMG
Licence requirement
New York State medical licence or limited permit required before start
