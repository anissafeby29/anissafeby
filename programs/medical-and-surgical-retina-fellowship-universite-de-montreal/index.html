Overview

Two-year clinical medical and surgical vitreoretinal fellowship alternating 6-month blocks between HMR and CHUM, with ROP and electrophysiology training.

The programme

Training type

Eligibility and visa

International applicants
Yes - foreign-student tuition stated; French proficiency assessed 1 month before start
