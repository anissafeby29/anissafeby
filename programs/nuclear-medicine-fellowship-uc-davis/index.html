Nuclear Medicine Fellowship

UC Davis Health

RadiologyNuclear medicine / Molecular imagingClinical Fellowship
Destination
USA
Sacramento, California, United States
Open official programme page ↗

Overview

A traditionally one-year nuclear medicine programme after DR residency, with optional rotations at regional PET and military centres; has hosted ABR Alternate Pathway trainees.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year
Start
July
Positions
1 or more per year (offered through the match at PGY-5 level)
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
Department of Radiology, UC Davis School of Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
ACGME internship (PGY-1) plus completion of a full DR residency (reverse NM3 pathway exception).
International applicants
Has included ABR Alternate Pathway (IMG) trainees.
Visa support
Institution policyClinical J-1 (ECFMG) or EAD only; UC Davis Health does not sponsor H-1B visas for residents and fellows
Licence requirement
Eligible for a California medical license.

How to apply

Deadline
Preferably by 1 November
Application method
Application form, CV, three letters (one from residency PD), USMLE transcripts and personal statement via the UC Davis Radiology Fellowship Applicant Portal.

Funding

Funding
Institution policySalaried on UC Davis housestaff scale effective 1 Sep 2026: PGY4 $101,002; PGY5 $104,792; PGY6 $108,377; PGY7 $111,703; PGY8 $117,140
