Radiation Oncology Fellowship Programme

National Cancer Centre Singapore (NCCS)

Radiation OncologyGeneral radiation oncologyClinical Fellowship
Destination
SGP

Open official programme page ↗Compare saved →

Overview

A clinical fellowship giving experience with standard and new radiotherapy technologies, including brachytherapy, and management of cancers common in Asian populations, with tumour boards and NCCS grand rounds.

The programme

Focus
General radiation oncology (multi-site)
Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
Clinical fellowship 6–12 months (clinical observership up to 6 months)
Start
Not stated on official page

Division of Radiation Oncology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
MBBS, proficient in English, oncology trainee or practising oncologist; 12 months' internship; registered at home; 3 years' post-housemanship experience or postgraduate qualification
International applicants
Yes — offered under NCCS Non-Local Doctors Fellowships; clinical fellows must be sponsored by their government, regional health authority or home institution (full-time employed)
Visa support
Training Employment Pass from Ministry of Manpower (applicant initiates entry visa if required)
Licence requirement
Singapore Medical Council Temporary Registration

How to apply

Deadline
At least 8 months before the targeted start date (clinical fellows)
Application method
Email CV with desired period and division to pgeu@nccs.com.sg and submit the NCCS application form

Funding

Funding
Self/sponsor-funded; fees include NCCS admin SGD 160.50, SMC registration SGD 750, malpractice SGD 410–820, TEP SGD 330
