Overview

One-year ACGME-approved surgical critical care fellowship with four positions per year, including trauma/acute care surgery operative blocks.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
At least three years of ACGME-approved general surgery, neurosurgery, urology or OB/GYN residency
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs from non-LCME schools must hold ECFMG certification before entry; non-US citizens need employer-sponsored visa (J-1/H-1B per programme)
Visa support
Institution policyUPMC ME programmes may (not required to) offer J-1 via ECFMG and/or H-1B; programme can offer either or both; H-1B requires USMLE Steps 1-3 passed by match date
Licence requirement
Pennsylvania graduate medical training licence
