Pediatric Emergency Medicine Fellowship

Phoenix Children's Hospital

PaediatricsEmergency medicineClinical Fellowship
Destination
USA

Overview

A three-year ACGME-accredited PEM fellowship with fellows covering the Phoenix Children's ED and outside rotations in adult EM, trauma, toxicology and EMS.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years (research year distributed across fellowship)
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
4 positions through the NRMP (12 fellows in programme)
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited (established 2004)
Department
Graduate Medical Education, Phoenix Children's

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completion of an ACGME-accredited pediatrics residency
International applicants
US citizenship, permanent residency, or J1 visa sponsored by ECFMG required (per official page)
Visa support
J1 visa sponsored by ECFMG accepted
Licence requirement
Institution policyPhoenix Children's trainees (residents and fellows) must hold and maintain a current Arizona medical licence or training permit for the whole appointment
