Precision Radiation Oncology Fellowship (PROF)

UT Southwestern Medical Center

Radiation OncologyAdaptive & MR-guided radiotherapyClinical Fellowship
Destination
USA

Overview

A one-year ACGME-accredited fellowship in highly customised radiotherapy, including daily adaptive radiotherapy, across UTSW, Parkland and Children's Health.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (1 July – 30 June)
Start
1 July
Positions
2
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited
Department
Department of Radiation Oncology

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed ACGME-accredited (or equivalent) radiation oncology residency; eligible for full Texas licensure; USMLE 1, 2CK, 2CS and 3 (or COMLEX equivalents)
International applicants
Yes — must hold a valid ECFMG certificate; at least one year of US clinical training preferred
Visa support
J-1 visa sponsored by ECFMG
Licence requirement
Full Texas Medical Board licence

How to apply

Deadline
1 October 2026
Application method
Email photo, CV, personal statement, three recent references, medical school transcript and USMLE/COMLEX scores to Sandra.Jeffery@UTSouthwestern.edu

Funding

Funding
PGY-6 stipend USD 86,503 (2025-26) plus health cover, malpractice, leave and professional allowance
