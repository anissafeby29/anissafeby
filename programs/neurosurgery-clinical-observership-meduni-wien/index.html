Overview

Clinical observership covering TBI and neuro-ICU management, skull base and vascular surgery, multimodal monitoring, intraoperative imaging and Gamma Knife at Vienna's university neurosurgery department.

The programme

Focus
General neurosurgery (TBI, neuro-ICU, skull base, vascular, radiosurgery)
Training type
Observership

Typically 1–5 weeks (maximum 6 months within 12 months per MedUni rules)

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Physicians; minimum Master's degree (MedUni requirement)
International applicants
Yes – open to medical graduates from around the world; processing up to 4 months depending on nationality and legal requirements (recent observers from Europe, Brazil, Uganda and elsewhere)
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Strict 'no hands-on' policy; minimum Master's degree; Gamma Knife observation only
