Overview

Cross-campus diagnostic neuroradiology fellowship at Royal Perth and Sir Charles Gairdner Hospitals with advanced CT/MR neuroimaging and spinal/paraspinal percutaneous interventions, run by Australasia's first specialist neuroradiology department.
