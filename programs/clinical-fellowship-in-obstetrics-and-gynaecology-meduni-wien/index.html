Overview

Clinical fellowship of up to 12 months in Vienna's university obstetrics and gynaecology department under MedUni Vienna fellowship rules.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
University graduates in medicine; German at least B1; recommendation letter from head of home department
International applicants
Yes – doctors from all over the world; processing up to 9 months depending on country of origin; requires cooperation agreement with home institution guaranteeing continued salaried employment
Visa support
Not stated on official page
Licence requirement
Supervised involvement under an individual training plan; not equivalent to residency; minimum Master's degree; German B1

How to apply

Deadline
Rolling
Application method
Email the department office; MedUni clinical fellow application with training plan and cooperation agreement

Funding

Funding
Not employment. Clinical fellowship tuition €29,000 per 12 months (may be waived on justification) plus €700 processing fee; home institution or grant must finance the fellow
