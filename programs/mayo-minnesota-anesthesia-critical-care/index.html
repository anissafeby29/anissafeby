Overview

Critical care training in Rochester provides multispecialty ICU exposure, research mentorship and optional rotations at other Mayo campuses.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Board-eligible or certified in anesthesiology
International applicants
Institution policyGraduates of non-US medical schools eligible if ECFMG-certified before appointment (applicants still obtaining certification may interview but cannot be ranked or appointed) or holding a full unrestricted licence in the programme state; Canadian graduates after 1 July 2025 also need ECFMG certification
Visa support
J-1 via ECFMG sponsorship; Mayo Clinic may also sponsor H-1B if all eligibility requirements are met
Licence requirement
Institution policyMinnesota-based MCSGME trainees must hold a Minnesota residency permit, and all eligible trainees must obtain and maintain a full Minnesota medical licence (USMLE/COMLEX/MCCQE final exam deadlines apply); Mayo reimburses fees

How to apply

Deadline
Via SF Match; page still shows 12 January 2025 deadline for the 2026-27 year (registration opened 4 Nov 2024); confirm dates for next cycle
Application method
SF Match (Common Application Service); off-cycle and exception-to-the-match positions possible

Funding

Funding
Paid; Mayo Clinic GME stipend by graduate level (effective 11 March 2026: e.g. Graduate Level 5 $89,036/yr); benefits include 20 working days' vacation; conference attendance support
