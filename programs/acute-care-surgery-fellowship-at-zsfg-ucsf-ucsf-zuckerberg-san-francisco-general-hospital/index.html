Overview

AAST-approved 12-month non-ACGME acute care surgery fellowship for surgeons completing the ZSFG surgical critical care year, with fellows appointed as UCSF clinical instructors.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Board-eligible/certified general surgeon who has completed the ZSFG ACGME surgical critical care fellowship
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs eligible if their school is in the World Directory/FAIMER and they hold a valid ECFMG certificate at application; non-US citizens need permanent residence, an appropriate visa or DACA
Visa support
Institution policyUCSF sponsors J-1 (ECFMG) and H-1B (incl. OPT) visas; H-1B/OPT require pre-approval by UCSF OGME; non-ACGME fellowships can take J-1 trainees only if ACGME Non-Standard Training (NST) recognised
Licence requirement
Institution policyCalifornia Postgraduate Training Licence (PTL) from the Medical Board of California required (full CA licence needed once accredited-training thresholds are reached)
