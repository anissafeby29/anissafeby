Pediatric Anesthesiology Fellowship (Minnesota)

Mayo Clinic — Minnesota

AnaesthesiologyPaediatric anaesthesiaClinical Fellowship
Destination
USA
Rochester, Minnesota, United States
Open official programme page ↗

Overview

A pediatric anesthesia year provides experience across ages and acuity levels, supported by simulation and multidisciplinary teaching.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
12 months; an additional research year is available
Start
1 August (academic year begins 1 August)
Positions
2 per year
Accreditation
ACGME
Department
Not stated on official page

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Completed an accredited three- or four-year anesthesiology residency; Mayo GME general admission requirements (ECFMG certification for international graduates)
International applicants
Yes - graduates of non-US medical schools eligible if ECFMG-certified before appointment (or hold a full unrestricted US licence in the program state)
Visa support
ECFMG J-1 sponsorship supported; H-1B only where it can be obtained by change/extension of status without the new $100,000 petition payment
Licence requirement
Not stated on official page

How to apply

Deadline
27 February of the year preceding the programme start date; interviews March-May
Application method
SF Match Central Application Service (Pediatric Anesthesiology Fellowship Match); ABA-ASA in-training exam scores emailed to programme

Funding

Funding
Paid stipend; Mayo Clinic GME annual stipend equivalents (effective 11 March 2026) range from $75,105 to $106,096 by training level, plus benefits incl. 20 days vacation
