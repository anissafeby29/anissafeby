Oculoplastics and Orbit Fellowship

University of British Columbia Department of Ophthalmology & Visual Sciences

OphthalmologyOculoplasticsClinical Fellowship
Destination
CAN
Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada
Open official programme page ↗Compare saved →

Overview

One-year fellowship covering eyelid, lacrimal and orbital pathology including endoscopic DCR, orbital decompression and periocular cancer reconstruction.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year (two 6-month rotations)
Start
1 July or 1 January
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Not evaluated by the Royal College of Physicians and Surgeons of Canada (university clinical fellowship)
Department
Department of Ophthalmology & Visual Sciences

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Licensed medical practitioner; Canadian residency graduates must complete PGY1-5; US graduates ABO certified/eligible; other foreign graduates must show completion of an approved programme; CPSBC English proficiency
International applicants
Yes - graduates of foreign ophthalmology programmes eligible with proof of completion; accepted candidates need a visa/work permit and medical exam (start at least 6 months ahead)
Visa support
Visa required; department issues acceptance letter and required documentation (UBC immigration process for foreign clinical fellows)
Licence requirement
CPSBC licence before start; IELTS/OET/CELPIP minimum 7 in each component if English not language of instruction

How to apply

Deadline
30 April (for July start) / 30 September (for January start) of the preceding year
Application method
Application with reference letters and confirmation of external funding

Funding

Funding
Must be funded by an external institution at Canadian-equivalent level; self-funding not permitted
