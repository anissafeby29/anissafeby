Overview

A two-year programme with Stanford Emergency Medicine International partnering with global organisations to strengthen emergency care systems, education and research.

The programme

Focus
Global health
Training type
Clinical Fellowship (Stanford 'advanced mentorship' programme)
Duration
2 years (optional Master's degree: MPH, MEHP or MS-PopH)
Start
Institution policyStanford GME contracts are issued per academic year (1 July - 30 June); standard start late June/1 July
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
SAEM-approved fellowship
Department
Department of Emergency Medicine, Stanford University School of Medicine

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Board-eligible/certified EM physician from a four-year ACGME EM residency, or a three-year programme plus at least one year post-graduate experience
International applicants
Institution policyIMGs welcome to apply; must hold a valid ECFMG certificate and be able to obtain Medical Board of California licensure; non-ACGME fellowships need ACGME Non-Standard Training recognition before J-1 sponsorship
Visa support
Institution policyJ-1 (ECFMG) is the primary visa; H-1B permitted only in some circumstances with GME approval; O-1 rarely, varies by department
Licence requirement
Institution policyMedical Board of California Postgraduate Training License required within 180 days of starting an ACGME programme (USMLE Steps 1 & 2 CK passed); non-ACGME programmes require a full California licence by the start date
