Overview

Hands-off observership for foreign doctors in NUH clinical departments, strictly for observation.

The programme

Focus
Multiple specialties (anaesthesia, cardiology, CTVS, imaging, EM, medicine, O&G, ophthalmology, orthopaedics, ENT, paediatrics, surgery, urology and others)
Training type
Observership (hands-off)
Duration
Maximum 28 days (4 weeks)
Start
Not stated on official page
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
Not stated on official page
Department
NUH Medical Affairs - Education

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Recognised medical degree; registration as a medical practitioner in current country; immunity documentation (varicella, MMR, Tdap) and chest X-ray
International applicants
Yes - foreign doctors may observe in a clinical department with no hands-on involvement
Visa support
Applicants arrange their own visa
Licence requirement
Not required (strictly observation; no patient care, case notes or prescriptions)

How to apply

Deadline
Minimum lead time of 8 weeks before proposed start date
Application method
Email completed application form and CV to observer_application@nuhs.edu.sg

Funding

Funding
Processing fee S$170 (GST incl.); no stipend or accommodation; miscellaneous departmental fees may apply
