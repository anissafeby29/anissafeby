Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Royal College or CFPC certification (or eligibility/registration in an RC residency); good standing; CPSO licence eligibility; CMPA coverage; basic sonography training; English proficiency
International applicants
Yes – sponsored applicants apply via their sponsorship bureau; non-sponsored international applicants via McMaster PGME; certified degree, translated transcripts and proof of funding required

Not stated on official page
