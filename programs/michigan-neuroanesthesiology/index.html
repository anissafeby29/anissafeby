Overview

Neurosurgical and neurointerventional anesthesia training includes neurocritical care, neuromonitoring and perioperative neuroscience research.

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
US citizen or permanent resident, enrolled in or graduated from an accredited anesthesiology residency; graduating residents and board-eligible/certified anesthesiologists may apply.
International applicants
No - applicants must be US citizens or permanent residents
Visa support
Not offered - US citizenship or permanent residency required
Licence requirement
Institution policyState of Michigan medical licence and controlled substance licence (Educational Limited or Permanent) required; permanent-licence holders also need a personal DEA registration
