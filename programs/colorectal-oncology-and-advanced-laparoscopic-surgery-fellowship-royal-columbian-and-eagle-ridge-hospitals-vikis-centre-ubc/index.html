Colorectal Oncology and Advanced Laparoscopic Surgery Fellowship

Royal Columbian & Eagle Ridge Hospitals (Vikis Centre), UBC

General SurgeryColorectal surgeryClinical Fellowship
Destination
CAN
New Westminster, British Columbia, Canada
Open official programme page ↗Compare saved →

Overview

One- to two-year colorectal, minimally invasive and endoscopic surgery fellowship using the Royal College Competence by Design model.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
1 year clinical; optional second research/clinical year
Start
1 July annually
Positions
Not stated on official page
Accreditation
SLS fellowship; RCPSC CBD framework
Department
Department of Surgery

Eligibility and visa

Eligibility
Graduation from an accredited general surgery residency (Canada, US or international)
International applicants
International general surgery graduates eligible
Visa support
Institution policy (UBC PGME): foreign trainees must hold an IRCC work permit for the whole training period; the sponsoring department/PGME submits an offer of employment to IRCC and the fellow applies under the LMIA-exempt category
Licence requirement
Must be eligible for a BC (CPSBC) independent, restricted or educational licence

How to apply

Deadline
Applications open 1 July 2026; deadline 15 October 2026; documents due 18 December 2026
Application method
Online SLS fellowship application (sls.org/sls-fellowship-programs/sls-fellowship-application/)

Funding

Funding
Institution policy (UBC PGME): fellows are not covered by the Resident Doctors of BC agreement; a written Training Agreement with the sponsoring Health Authority/University sets pay; $500 PGME administrative fee plus annual CPSBC licence fee; self-funded or unfunded postgraduate trainees are not eligible
