Overview

A three-year ACGME-accredited developmental-behavioral pediatrics fellowship preparing trainees to diagnose, treat and advocate for children with developmental and behavioural difficulties.

The programme

Training type
Clinical Fellowship
Duration
3 years
Start
Institution policyYale-New Haven Hospital resident/fellow appointments run for one year beginning 1 July and ending 30 June (sample appointment agreement)
Positions
1 first-year position per year
Accreditation
ACGME-accredited
Department
Department of Pediatrics, Yale School of Medicine
