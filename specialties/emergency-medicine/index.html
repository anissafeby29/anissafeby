Emergency Medicine fellowships

258 emergency medicine fellowships in 10 countries. Compare eligibility, visa support, funding and deadlines from official pages.

The Fellowship Portal lists 258 emergency medicine fellowships at 82 institutions. The main training cities are New York, Houston and Boston. 40% say they accept international medical graduates and 37% describe a visa route. The most common length is 2 years. Every listing links to the programme's official page, where you should confirm dates and requirements.
