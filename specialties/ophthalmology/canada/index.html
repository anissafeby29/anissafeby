Ophthalmology fellowships in Canada

49 ophthalmology fellowships in Canada, including Montreal, Toronto, Ottawa. Eligibility, visa and deadline details from official pages.

The Fellowship Portal lists 49 ophthalmology fellowships in Canada at 12 institutions. The main training cities are Montreal, Toronto and Ottawa. 80% say they accept international medical graduates and 51% describe a visa route. The most common length is 1 year. Every listing links to the programme's official page, where you should confirm dates and requirements.
