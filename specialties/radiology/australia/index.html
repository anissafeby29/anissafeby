Radiology fellowships in Australia

39 radiology fellowships in Australia, including Melbourne, Perth, Melbourne (Clayton). Eligibility, visa and deadline details from official pages.

The Fellowship Portal lists 39 radiology fellowships in Australia at 24 institutions. The main training cities are Melbourne, Perth and Melbourne (Clayton). 31% say they accept international medical graduates and 13% describe a visa route. The most common length is 1 year. Every listing links to the programme's official page, where you should confirm dates and requirements.
