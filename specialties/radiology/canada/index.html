Radiology fellowships in Canada

108 radiology fellowships in Canada, including Toronto, Vancouver, Ottawa. Eligibility, visa and deadline details from official pages.

The Fellowship Portal lists 108 radiology fellowships in Canada at 13 institutions. The main training cities are Toronto, Vancouver and Ottawa. 76% say they accept international medical graduates and 40% describe a visa route. The most common length is 1 year. Every listing links to the programme's official page, where you should confirm dates and requirements.
