Paediatrics fellowships in Canada

131 paediatrics fellowships in Canada, including Toronto, Montreal, Calgary. Eligibility, visa and deadline details from official pages.

The Fellowship Portal lists 131 paediatrics fellowships in Canada at 12 institutions. The main training cities are Toronto, Montreal and Calgary. 57% say they accept international medical graduates and 88% describe a visa route. The most common length is 2 years. Every listing links to the programme's official page, where you should confirm dates and requirements.

Frequently asked questions

How many paediatrics fellowships are there in Canada?

131 programmes at 12 institutions are listed, each researched from the institution's official page.

Can international medical graduates apply?

75 of the 131 programmes say they accept international applicants, and 115 describe a visa route such as J-1, H-1B or a work permit. Requirements differ by programme, so check eligibility on the official page.
