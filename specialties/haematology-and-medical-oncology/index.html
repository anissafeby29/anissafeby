Haematology & Medical Oncology fellowships

172 haematology & medical oncology fellowships in 16 countries. Compare eligibility, visa support, funding and deadlines from official pages.

The Fellowship Portal lists 172 haematology & medical oncology fellowships at 94 institutions. The main training cities are Toronto, Montreal and New York. 46% say they accept international medical graduates and 55% describe a visa route. The most common length is 3 years. Every listing links to the programme's official page, where you should confirm dates and requirements.
